### **What is Voice Actions?**

**Voice Actions** let you control Latchel using nothing but your voice — jump straight to a property's open work order, cancel a job, or create a brand-new work order, all by saying what you need out loud. It's faster than our traditional click-and-type flow, and it isn't just for property managers: vendors and in-house team members out in the field can use it too, so they don't have to stop what they're doing to tap through screens.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to request access to Voice Actions?">
    No. This is already live on every account — just look for the microphone icon in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can vendors and in-house techs use Voice Actions too, or is this just for property managers?">
    Both. Voice Actions are open to property managers, vendors, and in-house team members, so anyone in the field can use their voice instead of typing on a phone screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if Latchel misunderstands what I said?">
    You'll always see a short confirmation screen before the action is finalized, so you can catch and correct anything before it's submitted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is creating a work order by voice different from the regular work order form?">
    It's a faster path to the same result — you're simply speaking the details (property, issue, unit) instead of clicking through the traditional work order creation flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will more voice actions be added over time?">
    Yes. Latchel is using how customers, vendors, and techs use Voice Actions today to shape which actions and controls get added next.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I ask for a voice action that isn't supported yet?">
    Latchel lets you know the action isn't available via voice yet, rather than guessing at what you meant — so you can fall back to the regular click-and-type flow for anything outside the current supported list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
