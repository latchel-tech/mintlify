### **What is Voice Actions?**

**Voice Actions** let you control Latchel using nothing but your voice — jump straight to a property's open work order, cancel a job, or create a brand-new work order, all by saying what you need out loud. It's faster than our traditional click-and-type flow, and it isn't just for property managers: vendors and in-house team members out in the field can use it too, so they don't have to stop what they're doing to tap through screens.

## Steps to Activate

Voice Actions are already live — there's nothing to turn on in your settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the microphone icon">
    Look for the microphone icon in your Latchel dashboard, wherever you'd normally click or type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap it and say what you need">
    Speak naturally — for example, "Take me to two Avery Street's open work order" or "Create a new work order for 2 Avery Street to replace the cabinets under the kitchen sink."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the action">
    A short confirmation screen shows you what Latchel understood before anything is finalized, so you always get a chance to double-check before it goes through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Voice is often the fastest way to get somewhere specific in Latchel — if you already know exactly where you're headed, saying it can beat clicking through menus.
</Tip>
