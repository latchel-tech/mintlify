### **Questions & Answers**

## **General Questions on what it is and how it works**

#### **1) What is Latchel and how do I use your services?**
**Answer:**Latchel is a technology company that handles maintenance coordination for Property Managers.
To use our services, residents can call or text us with their requests. Each Property Manager has their specific Latchel phone number and each Resident has their unique dashboard, where they can also submit requests
**Note:**Use this SOP to walk you through how to get the link to the resident dashboard and educate them on what they can do in that page: [How to: Navigate the Resident Interface](/How-to-Navigate-the-Resident-Interface)

#### **2) What is the 24/7 Home Assistant program? What are the benefits I have with it and how I use them?**
**Answer:**It is a 24/7 concierge to help residents to schedule work on their home, troubleshoot in-home problems, ask rental questions (like lease renewals, adding other residents, reimbursements on rent due to high water bills), and fulfill repairs and maintenance. Residents can call the home assistant to help troubleshoot issues that are normally*their*responsibility, or when they need help with any in-home issue. We also offer Appointment Cancellation Fee Reimbursement, when the resident cancels an appointment 4hrs in advance
**(NOT LIVE YET /****Coming****soon)**The Home Assistant also provides reimbursements to the resident for accidental repairs that they may have caused, like drain or toilet clogs, and supports residents with coordinating move-out repairs
**Note 1**: See this SOP for more details on which features are available for each product: [Job Aid: Latchel Product Features, Pricing, and KPIs](/Job-Aid-Latchel-Product-Features-Pricing-and-KPIs)
**Note 2:** See the Resident page on our website to see the information we are currently sharing with our customers.  [https://latchel.com/24-7-home-assistant/](https://latchel.com/24-7-home-assistant/). You will notice this page may show fewer features than our internal page as some of the new features have not been implemented yet.

**Answer:**They can submit their maintenance requests directly with your Property Manager; however, they may not get the same speed on addressing their requests or get support with issues or damages they have caused.The Home Assistant program provides them with troubleshooting support to avoid maintenance costs and also provides reimbursements for certain issues.

## **Move out Assistance**

#### 1) **What is the benefit of using the Move Out Assistance?**
**Answer:**The goal is to enable residents to get those items repaired before they move out which could potentially bring two benefits:
(1) speed up the return of the portion of their security deposit that was unused to fix resident caused repairs
(2) reduce costs associated with property management fees coordinating those repairs
**Note**: For more information on this process, see this SOP: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Handle **Move out Assistance** Requests from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/999358706/HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Handle+Move+out+Assistance+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)

#### 2) **Will I be charged for the repairs requested in the Move Out Assistance?**
**Answer:**Residents will be charged for repairs that are assessed as resident caused or that were included in the lease agreement as resident responsibility. Before submitting a work order, we will request residents to acknowledge that. The decision on which repair will be charged to the resident will be made by the Property Manager.
**Note**: For more information on this process, see this SOP: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Handle **Move out Assistance** Requests from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/999358706/HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Handle+Move+out+Assistance+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)

## **Reimbursement Requests**

#### **1)  How will I get reimbursed for the repairs I am eligible for?**
**Answer:**Residents will receive a check in the mail within 5-9 business days after the check has been issued. Our team will let the resident know when the check has been issued.
****Note:********For more information on this process, see this SOP:[ARCHIVED --- (HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Process **Reimbursements Requests** from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/983763472/ARCHIVED+---+HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Process+Reimbursements+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)

#### **2) I have requested reimbursement for a repair covered under the Home Assistant Program, but I have not received that yet. Where is my money?**
**Answer:**Let the resident know you will need some time to research.
Review the notes in the work order, or the property profile page, to confirm the resident request was approved and when the check was issued.
1. Verify if the request was approved and a check has been issued by searching the notes  for "CHECK ISSUED"
  1. If you did NOT find the note confirming the request was approved and the check has been issued, search if there is a comment for "REIMBURSEMENT DENIED".
    1. If the reimbursement was denied,let the resident know the request was denied and provide details on when and how this was communicated to them
    2. If there is no comment about this request neither in the work order nor in the property profile, let the resident know we will escalate to our leadership to do deeper research and we will contact the resident back within 2 business days
      1. Create a task in the work order and assign it to T2 due immediately and set expectation to follow-up with the resident in 24hrs
      2. Let the resident know we will contact them back within 2 business days
  2. If you found the note confirming the request was approved but the check has not been issued
    1. Follow-up with Josie as described in the SOP  [ARCHIVED --- (HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Process **Reimbursements Requests** from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/983763472/ARCHIVED+---+HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Process+Reimbursements+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)
    2. Let the resident know the reimbursement was approved, but the check has not been issued yet and you are following-up on it.
    3. Let the resident know you will let them know when the check is issued
  3. If you found the note confirming the request was approved and the check was issued, proceed to the next step below
2. Verify if 9 business days (not including weekend and holidays) has passed since the date the check was issued
  1. If yes, let the resident know they will need to contact the bank directly to inquire about the status of the check. Provide the bank phone number(1-800-935-9935) and the check confirmation number
    1. Before contacting the bank, it is a good practice for the resident to verify the mail box and also talk to other residents and neighbors to check if they have the check
    2. let the resident know we will escalate to our leadership to do deeper research and we will contact the resident back within 2 business days
      1. Create a task in the work order and assign it to T2 due immediately and set expectation to follow-up with the resident in 24hrs
      2. Let the resident know we will contact them back within 2 business days
  2. If not, let the resident know when the 9th business day is (i.e. the date) and request them to wait until them. If they have not received the check by that time, they should contact the bank directly. Provide the bank phone number (1-800-935-9935)and the check confirmation number
