## Purpose

<Info>
  **Purpose of this document** This document provides guidance on how to handle appointment cancellation fee charge backs to Property Managers when preferred service providers charged us an inappropriate fee
</Info>

<Info>
  **Who should read this document?** Customer Success Team
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Process Overview

<Accordion title="What is happening prior to this process?">
  Tier 1 or Tier 2 agent is working on issuing a reimbursement for an appointment cancellation fee and determined that fee was charged by a Preferred Service Provider, or they are unable to determine if the Service Provider is a Preferred Service Provider. 

  If the appointment cancellation fee was issued by a Preferred Service Provider and that should not have happened, Customer Success will contact the Property Manager to discuss the charge back
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Process Overview">
  1. Verify the reimbursement request is valid
  2. Verify the fee is coming from a Preferred Service Provider
  3. If the request is valid and coming form a Preferred Service Provider, proceed to contact the Property Manager to discuss issuing them a charge back
  4. If the request is NOT valid and / or NOT coming form a Preferred Service Provider, no charge back should be issued. Leave notes in the work order explaining the reason for not issuing the charge back
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Customer Success will do further research to verify / determine if we should or not issue the reimbursement to the resident and charge back the Property Manager">
  1. If Customer Success determines the Property Manager should be charged back, go to step 3 in this SOP
  2. If Customer Success is still unable to determine if this charge came from a preferred service provider, or decides to not issue the charge back, Customer Success will make a note to the work order and NOT charge anything to the Property Manager.  Use the “Notes Template” below:
  3. CHARGE BACK NOT ISSUED
     1. Name of the Preferred Service Provider:
     2. Name of Property Manager:
     3. Reason for charge back request:
     4. Charge back amount:
     5. Date charge back was requested to be issued

</Accordion>

#### Step 3 - Contact Property Manager to discuss the charge back

<Accordion title="1. Customer Success should contact the Property Manager. Click here to see topics to be discussed in this conversation:">
  1. Review our 4hr cancellation policy and the expectation that their Preferred Service Providers shouldn't charge a cancellation fee when the appointment is cancelled/rescheduled 4hrs\+ from the scheduled start time of the appointment
  2. Discuss with Property Manager the policy to charge the Property Manager back for improper charges we received from their preferred service providers
  3. Get an agreement from the Property Manager that they understand this policy and agree with us charging them back
  4. Instruct Property Managers to review this policy with their Prefer Service Providers
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Make notes of the conversation. Click here for more details:">
  1. Customer Success will make notes of this conversation in the work order in case there are questions in the future:
     1. Use this template:
        1. CHARGE BACK - APPOINTMENT CANCELLATION FEE
           1. Name of the Preferred Service Provider:
           2. Reason for charge back:
           3. Charge back amount:
           4. Date charge back was issued:
           5. Name of Property Manager that accepted the charge back:
           6. Notes from conversation with Property Manager:
     2. Note: Make sure you are making the call to the Property Manager from the work order so the call gets recorded and we can easily find it!
</Accordion>
