## Purpose

Describe how Latchel structures quarterly reviews and Interview debriefs. Introduce the Superpower Pack(tm) and how Latchel uses it.

## The Superpower Pack(tm)

Latchel uses a deck of cards (yes, really) to guide hiring decisions and quarterly reviews. The Superpower Pack was developed by one of Latchel's investors and is very closely correlated with our Leadership Principles. There are two key components:

### Superpowers

These are categories of high level skills necessary to be successful in a business (e.g. Coaching, Service). These are further organized into "Tracks", which simply group superpowers into related skills.

### Powers

These are contributing attitudes, abilities, and assets that make up a superpower (e.g. Mentorship, Empathy). These are organized by Superpower and ranked (i.e. the ranks are determined by the suit Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs). These ranks are subjective, so we only use the ranking loosely.

## How we use the Superpower Pack at Latchel

For every job role, we have taken the Superpowers and selected 3 "Required" or "Must have" skills and 3 "Desired" or "Nice to have" skills. These are the essential traits a person performing this job must have.

During quarterly reviews, we assess each employee's skills along these Superpowers and their component Powers to assess their aptitude. We use the following:

- **Danger** - This person is so weak in this skill that it is a liability to the team or the company.
- **Challenge** - This person is not proficient in this skill and struggles to demonstrate it consistently.
- **Potential** - This person is proficient in this skill, but sometimes falters or has not demonstrated true mastery.
- **Role Model** - This person is a role model when demonstrating this skill--they are one of the top people in the company for this skill, not just their role or level.

Your relative mix of skills relevant to your job role will determine where you land in quarterly reviews (i.e. Needs Improvement, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Outstanding).

## Latchel Job Roles & Superpowers

These are the Required and Desired Superpowers for each job role. Note: the superpowers themselves are not listed in a particular order beyond the Required/Desired classification. These classifications and categories may change as companies needs change.

- **Manager, Customer Success**
  - Required: Service, Coaching, Direction
  - Desired: Acuity, Judgment, Charisma
- **Customer Success Manager**
  - Required: Service, Integrity, Pathfinding
  - Desired: Suasion, Judgment, Execution
- **Launch Manager**
  - Required: Service, Execution, Suasion
  - Desired: Pathfinding, Acuity, Tenacity
- **Customer Success Assistant**
  - Required: Service, Pathfinding, Integrity
  - Desired: Charisma, Judgment, Execution
- **Program Manager**
  - Required: Pathfinding, Acuity, Service
  - Desired: Execution, Direction, Suasion
- **Product Manager**
  - Required: Vision, Service, Suasion
  - Desired: Pathfinding, Judgment, Acuity
- **Product Analyst**
  - Required: Pathfinding, Integrity, Execution
  - Desired: Service, Acuity, Tenacity
- **Operations Manager**
  - Required: Direction, Suasion, Service
  - Desired: Coaching, Charisma, Execution
- **Operations Associate & Specialists**
  - Required: Service, Integrity, Charisma
  - Desired: Tenacity, Suasion, Pathfinding
- **Shift Support**
  - Required: Pathfinding, Service, Suasion
  - Desired: Charisma, Integrity, Execution
- **Sales Development Rep / Sales Engineer**
  - Required: Suasion, Integrity, Tenacity
  - Desired: Service, Pathfinding, Charisma
- **Marketing Manager**
  - Required: Suasion, Tenacity, Execution
  - Desired: Pathfinding, Service, Direction
- **Software Engineer**
  - Required: Pathfinding, Execution, Acuity
  - Desired: Service, Judgment, Tenacity
- **Designer (Product/UX/UI)**
  - Required: Pathfinding, Execution, Service
  - Desired: Suasion, Judgment, Acuity

## How to use these in interview debriefs

Each person should be assigned 1-2 superpowers related to the role they are interviewing for. Based on the interview, they can rank the candidate along Danger, Challenge, Potential, Strength. Use the Powers and Superpowers to guide the hiring discussion.
