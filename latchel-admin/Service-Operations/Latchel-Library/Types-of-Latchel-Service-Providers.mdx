There are 3 different types of vendor classifications in the system

## 1. Preferred Service Providers
Service Providers that were brought into the console by the property manager directly. This vendor type tends to need support as they were not sold on the console by a Latchel representative. They require the most amount of education to be successful Latchel users

### Invoicing
We collect the invoice but do not remit payment.
We can even disable the invoicing altogether per the request of the management company in charge of paying the vendor

## 2. Latchel Affiliates
These service providers were sourced out of immediate necessity and have the least amount of experience with the Latchel console. Most of these providers are sourced from cold calls and told very little about Latchel process because all we want is the job to be completed. They will need the most amount of up front support to be successful in the platform.

Ideally we are on-boarding these service providers so they can become In-Network, but we aren’t forcing them through the process and can just pay them with a virtual card to prevent barriers to pay invoices

This vendor type is extremely volatile, we don’t know how much they’ve

### Invoicing
Ideally we are on-boarding these service providers so they can become In-Network, but we don’t want to force them to lose a percentage since they know the least amount of information about us.
We pay them out via virtual Card issuing via the Latchel Console

### 3. In-Network Service Provider
Service providers that work directly with Latchel and not the property manager. The expectation is that they communicate with a Latchel representative or use the console to update any work order. We are also in charge of the financial relationship and should be paying these providers directly for 100% of the jobs they accept. 

This vendor type is generally very interested in console features and tends to be the most willing to adapt to changes in the console and changing internal processes to schedule and bill with Latchel

### Invoicing
Direct deposit ONLY
