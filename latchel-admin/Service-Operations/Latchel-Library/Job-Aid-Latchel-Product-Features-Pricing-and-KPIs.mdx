## Key Performance Indicators

### 1. Service Level Agreement (SLAs) for our Products

Here is a quick reference of Latchel's service level agreements. P90 means our goal is to achieve the metric on 90% of cases. Another way to look at this - we expect only 10% of instances to miss the SLA. For every SLA you see, our average and median number is far better. For example, our SLA for hold time is 60 seconds, but in practice our average hold time is only 4 seconds. Generally, we'll only reach the 90th percentile SLA during peak times.

#### **Emergency Products**

See below the SLA metrics we commit to delivering to our customers for the "After hours" and "24/7 Emergency" products:

| # | Emergency SLA Metrics | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hold Time (60 sec P90) | This is the hold time for residents when they call in a maintenance request. |
| 2 | Tier 2 Callback (20 min P90) | This is the amount of time it takes for our experienced troubleshooting team to call the resident back and attempt to fix the issue. |
| 3 | Confirmed Appt (4 hr P90) | This is the time between the request being submitted and Latchel having a confirmed work appointment from a service provider. |
| 4 | Troubleshoot Success Rate (40% P90) | This is the % of time that our experienced troubleshooting team is able to fix or de-escalate issues. |

#### **Non-Emergency Product (Premium)**

| # | Experience NPS (\>80%) | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  | These regular surveys help us track the efficacy of our Premium service on your operations. |
| 1 | Web Chat and SMS Hold Time (2 min P90) | This is the time it takes us to respond to an inbound SMS conversation from you, residents, and service providers. |
| 2 | Phone/Voicemail Response Time (4 min P90) | This is the time it takes us to answer a phone call or respond to a voicemail from you and your service providers. We may respond with a SMS in some cases. |
| 3 | Email Response (4 hrs P90) | This is the time it takes us to respond to emails. |
| 4 | Time to Assign Latchel Service Providers (24hrs P90) | This is the amount of time it takes us to assign a Latchel Service Provider when your preferred vendors are not available. |
| 5 | Time to Switch Preferred Vendors (12hrs P90) | We give your preferred vendors a full business day before switching to another vendor (unless otherwise instructed by you). |
| 6 | Time to Schedule with Resident (48hrs P90) | We give all residents in a unit a full 48 hours to provide availability before escalating the issue. |
| 7 | Time to Complete Non-Emergency Requests (5 days P90) | We complete non-emergency requests on a 5-day SLA assuming that issues do not require escalation. If escalated, we reset this SLA. |
