## Purpose

<Info>
  This is an external-facing Tech Update that is used by Residents. This tool provides flexibility for scheduling, updates and communication with our team. Our team has full visibility of Resident history and actions, allowing us to make appropriate choices according to our Latchel Operational Processes. All Latchel Teams may become well-versed with its various functionalities to be of assistance to Residents if they have any questions about getting faster updates or interacting with their Work Order. ([Informational: Latchel Customers & Customer Subscriptions](/Informational-Latchel-Customers-Customer-Subscriptions))
</Info>

<Info>
  When the Resident clicks on any Work Order link within any of our automated messages, they are directed to the Resident Interface.
</Info>

<Info>
  - After the Work Order is created, the resident and Service Provider should use self-service tools as much as possible, allowing our Latchel software to complete the Work Order in an automated workflow, rather than relying on Associates to manually adjust Work Order details. Operations Associates can now access and provide the direct URL link to residents and customers for their respective Latchel Customer Interface.
  - If a customer contacts you to do something that they should be able to do on the dashboard, you can take care of that, but educate the customer on being able to complete actions themselves, for example: Set availability,  create a new work order,  add images and files, cancel the job. For more information see: [How to: Navigate the Resident Interface](/How-to-Navigate-the-Resident-Interface) and [How to: Navigate the Service Provider Interface](/How-to-Navigate-the-Service-Provider-Interface)  for details on tool capabilities.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. Here's a video of Ethan giving a high-level overview of its interface functionality: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N08Ar9eOEtI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N08Ar9eOEtI)
2. The Resident Interface has both desktop and mobile functionality
3. Built for mobile functionality, but the Resident Dashboard (Click "My Maintenance" button to view all of their jobs) is best viewed from a Desktop computer.
4. Summary of functionalities (View the video for visuals)
   1. This is a screenshot of what Residents will see from their resident dashboard (desktop version).
      <Frame>
        ![Image](/images/LL-38.png)
      </Frame>
   2. To the top right, there is a button is used by residents to create a new maintenance request: "\+ New Maintenance Request".
      <Frame>
        ![Image](/images/LL-39.png)
      </Frame>
      1. The resident is directed to the Create Maintenance Request form.
         <Frame>
           ![Image](/images/LL-40.png)
         </Frame>
   3. To the top right, there is a button is used by residents to send a message to their Property Manager: "Message Property Manager".
      <Frame>
        ![Image](/images/LL-41.png)
      </Frame>
      1. The resident is prompted to submit a message to the property manager OR to be redirected to "Create a Maintenance Request" (this will direct the user to Create Maintenance Request form described above).
         <Frame>
           ![Image](/images/LL-42.png)
         </Frame>
   4. Click on "My Maintenance" button to view all jobs (categorized by Status Columns)
      1. 'Action Required' Column
         1. Per applicable Work Order, click on the button that leads the Resident to fulfill the required action for that job.
         2. Click on the Work Order card to view the Job Details Interface
      2. 'Scheduled Today' Column
      3. 'In Progress' Column
         1. Anything that doesn't require their action, they can provide full updates on
            1. (Full Options for "What would you like to do?" screen)
      4. 'Done' Column
         1. Click "Provide Job Update"
            1. Options for "I need help"
            2. Reopen a Job if the issue was no solved (also known as a Recall Work Order)
   5. From the individual Job Details Page, Click "Provide Update" for more options:
      1. "What would you like to do?"
         1. "Update Availability"
            1. More available time slots, which will automatically send the the Service Provider
               1. This allows Service Providers to schedule an appointment
         2. "Mark job as complete"
            1. "Report an incomplete Request"
               1. Service provider didn't show up.
               2. Job not yet resolved (Why isn't the job resolved?)
                  - A service provider visited, but the issue is still not fixed
                  - I had an appointment but the service provider missed it
                  - Other
         3. "Add images and files"
         4. "Cancel Job Request"
            1. Notifies the Service Provider automatically of the job cancellation
         5. "I need help"
            1. Time Sensitive
               1. The Resident is offered the Property Manager's Emergency Hotline
            2. Not Time Sensitive
               1. "We're here to help"
                  1. Options to write a note about the Work Order, which then becomes a task for Operations team, logged into Notes in Work Order
