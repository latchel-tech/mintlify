#### **(PM Customer Guide) How to Add or Remove Properties**
| **Q:**The property manager asked me to **add a property** and I completed that, but how can I advise them to be self-directed and complete that action on their own next time?**A:**Your response may advise the Property Manager to use their dashboard to fulfill these actions next time. Here's an example of what you could say -Dear *(Property Manager name here)*,As requested, I just added/removed *(property address here)* for you. Did you know that you can Remove or Add Properties via your own Latchel Dashboard? Check out step-by-step instructions here: [https://latchel.com/blog/how-to-remove-properties-and-add-properties/](https://latchel.com/blog/how-to-remove-properties-and-add-properties/)[Associate facing SOP (click here)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/464388136/TECH+101+How+to+Navigate+Properties+Tab+in+the+Latchel+Console#%5BhardBreak%5D(PM-Guide-to-Remove-and-Add-Properties)) ✅ |
