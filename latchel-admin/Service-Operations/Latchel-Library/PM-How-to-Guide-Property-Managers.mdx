    ### **(PM Customer Guide) How to Add or Remove Properties**

    **Q: **The property manager asked me to **add a property** and I completed that, but how can I advise them to be self-directed and complete that action on their own next time?

    **A: **Your response may advise the Property Manager to use their dashboard to fulfill these actions next time. Here's an example of what you could say -

    <Check>
      Dear _(Property Manager name here)_,

      As requested, I just added/removed _(property address here)_ for you.

      Did you know that you can Remove or Add Properties via your own Latchel Dashboard? Check out step-by-step instructions here: [https://latchel.com/blog/how-to-remove-properties-and-add-properties/](https://latchel.com/blog/how-to-remove-properties-and-add-properties/)
    </Check>
