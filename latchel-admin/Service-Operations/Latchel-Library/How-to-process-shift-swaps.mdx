## Purpose

<Info>
**Purpose of this document**

Give guidance to associates and supervisors on how to coordinate and process shift swaps between associates.
</Info>

<Info>
**Who should read this document?**

Supervisors, Operations Support Specialists, Ops Associates, and Ops Troubleshooters.
</Info>

## Step-by-Step Guide

**What happened prior to this step?**

The associate expresses the need to work a different schedule as they have no more available PTO and/or has the possibility to prevent any deductions by requesting an unpaid time leave.

### Process Overview

<Note>
The associate requesting a shift swap will be identified as the "requester".
</Note>

### Step 1 - Associate initiates shift swap request.

1. The requester will contact the supervisor and will communicate the date they need to work on a different schedule.
2. The requester must request the shift swap at least 3 days in advance. This is to guarantee that all associates with a compatible schedule can review their agendas.

### Step 2- The requester's supervisor initiates the shift swap process.

1. The supervisor will review the schedules of the associates with a compatible schedule (and trained in the same skills) and provide their name(s) to the requester.
2. The requester will contact his/her peers and negotiate the shift swap.
3. If the associates with compatible schedules cannot swap their shifts, the associate requesting the shift swap will work with the supervisor and seeks other options based on our attendance policies. End of process.

### Step 3 - The requester's supervisor completes the shift swap process.

1. The requester presents proof of another associate accepting the swap arrangement.
2. The supervisor will coordinate the shift updates in Deputy. This way each associate involved in the shift swap will show in their Deputy session the agreed schedule, on the date requested.
3. If the associates report to different supervisors, the requester's supervisor will own the schedule updates in Deputy, prior to coordination with the other supervisor involved.

<Warning>
**Key reminders:**

- Any shift swap must happen in the same pay period.
- Associates with a 4x10 schedule cannot swap the shift with a person in an 8x5 schedule, and vice-versa.
- The shift swap must not put both associates in a position where he/she will work more than 6 days in a row.
- The shift swap must not put the requester in a position where he/she will work more than 12 hours a day
</Warning>
