## Purpose

<Info>
The purpose of this document is to provide step-by-step guidelines on how to resolve Service Provider payment issues in stripe, this should only be done by a Sr. Service Provider Specialist or above. If you do not have access, escalate the work order to them.
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1
- Log In to[dashboard.stripe.com](http://dashboard.stripe.com)
  - If you do not have an account or access, ask your supervisor for help.
Step 2
- From the main dashboard select Connect
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/LL-140.png" alt="Image" />
</Frame>


#### Step 3
- This should automatically put you in accounts

#### Step 4
- From there select the “Restricted” tab. 
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/LL-141.png" alt="Image" />
</Frame>


#### Step 5
- This will show you a snapshot of each Service Provider with issues and what they are

#### Step 6
- Select your Service Provider

#### Step 7
- Each missing or incorrect field will be outlined in red, in the right corner there is a “pencil” which will allow you to edit the field.
- IMPORTANT: BANK ACCOUNT INFORMATION MUST BE PROVIDED THROUGH THE LATCHEL HOMEPAGE. 
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/LL-142.png" alt="Image" />
</Frame>

Step 8
- Once updated the payment should be reprocessed at the top of the next available hour and removed from the Operations Task Sheet.

#### Step 9
- Notify the Service Provider that the issue is resolved and resolve the ticket in Zendesk
