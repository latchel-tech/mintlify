## Purpose

<Info>
The Customer Love Program is a tangible way for Latchel team members to demonstrate Customer Obsession to all Latchel customers (including all Residents, Service Providers, or Property Managers alike). The processes and guidelines for sending "Customer Acknowledgement" or "Customer Apologies" are outlined in this document.
💌💌💌💌💌
**Question:**Why does the Customer Love program exist?
**Answer:**
"Building process mechanisms to help us be more customer obsessed. One of the hard parts is that when you don't have simple processes for showing you care, it makes being customer obsessed hard, so how can we break down those barriers?"
- Ethan Lieber,
Latchel CEO
</Info>

<Info>
Prior to submitting a Customer Acknowledgement or a Customer Apology, the Latchel agent has had an experience with a customer that prompts you two want to send a special gift to that customer. The special gift will be a hand written note and a gift (approximately $20 value for the gift).
By submitting a request for acknowledgement or apology, the Latchel agent's submission **may or may not** be chosen. The sender will receive a receipt of acceptance if the submission was chosen.
</Info>

#### GROWTH TEAM - Prospective Customer - Customer Love Submission Guidelines

<Frame>
  <img src="https://images.assetsdelivery.com/compings_v2/catalin205/catalin2051508/catalin205150800082.jpg" alt="Image" />
</Frame>

1. If a Sales Business Representative would like to submit Customer Love gift and note to a prospective customer, the customer must be **far enough in the sales process for us to have built a relationship with them.**
  1. For an extreme example, having a 9th or 10th meeting with Company XYZ, a company the SDR has been speaking with for about 9 months, is considered to be eligible.
  2. On the opposite extreme, even if we've just met with them a few times but have shared texts or emails back and forth, the SDR may be enough to have developed a relationship.
2. Please build rapport with the customer prior to submitting.
  1. Cold call prospective customers are not eligible for Customer Love submissions.

#### OPTION 1  - Customer Acknowledgement
1. Become a member of the #customer-love Slack channel.
2. *"Wish I could send a little something extra to make you smile."*Have an awesome experience with a customer and decide to submit an Acknowledgement!
3. **First come, first served. Only one submission per day (among all Acknowledgement submissions) will be chosen.**
4. Navigate to the lightening bolt icon, which indicates the use of Slack Workflows
  1. Choose **"Customer Acknowledgement"** flow
  2. Fill in each criteria of the nomination form.
    1. "Name of the Latchel customer?"
    2. "Why are you nominating this customer?"
    3. "Please provide the Latchel customer profile URL."
      1. **Note:** Gifts will be sent as per below guidelines -
        1. **Resident** - the property address on the customer Profile
        2. **Service Provider** - the Service Provider Company address
        3. **Property Manager** - the office of the Property Manager
    4. "Which gift would the customer prefer?" (Choose an option)
      1. At the end of the form, click "Submit".
5. The Customer Love Liaison will send a receipt that your nomination was accepted.
  1. The customer should receive the gift within 7 days of the original submission.
6. 🔃 Do it all over again whenever the circumstances arise!

#### OPTION 2 - Customer Apologies
1. Become a member of the #customer-love Slack channel.
2. *"Wish I could send a professional apology."* Have a less than optimal experience with a customer, decide to take ownership of the customer relationship, and submit an Apology.
3. ****First come, first served.**Only one submission per day (among all Apology submissions) will be chosen.**
4. Navigate to the lightening bolt icon, which indicates the use of Slack Workflows
  1. Choose **"Send a Customer Apology"** flow
  2. Fill in each criteria of the nomination form.
    1. "Name of the Latchel customer?"
    2. "Briefly explain why Latchel owes this apology?"
    3. "Please provide the Latchel customer profile URL."
      1. **Note:** Gifts will be sent as per below guidelines -
        1. **Resident** - the property address on the customer Profile
        2. **Service Provider** - the Service Provider Company address
        3. **Property Manager** - the office of the Property Manager
    4. "Which gift would the customer prefer?" (Choose an option)
      1. At the end of the form, click "Submit".
5. The Customer Love Liaison will send a receipt that your request was accepted.
  1. **Note:** The customer should receive the gift within 7 days of the original submission.
6. 🔃 Do it all over again whenever the circumstances arise!
