## Objective

<Info>
Each Service Provider is able to provide a list of services and those are described as "skills" in the Latchel Console. "Skills" refer to the abilities or services that a Service Provider offers to their customers. Skills are very specific to what needs to be done to resolve the maintenance request and will be grouped into categories.
If you are in contact with a Service Provider and they let you know they can do certain types of repairs or services that we don't have listed in their account (i.e. we don't have those skills added to their profile), you will need to add those skills to their profile so that Service Provider will appear when you are assigning a Service Provider to a work order that requires that skill.
</Info>

<Note>
When sourcing and creating a new Service Provider to be assigned to an Emergency work order, you will also need to add the relevant skill, so this Service Provider will be automatically listed in the tool to assign a Service Provider to an work order.
</Note>

<Note>
If a Property Manager asks you to change prioritization of their Preferred Service Providers or to edit the skills they want those service providers to execute on their accounts, let them know you will forward the request to our operations management team.
Escalate as per instructions in the Emergency Escalation SOP [(⛑EMERGENCY) How to: Escalate and Ask for Help](/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=LTG&title=%28%E2%9B%91EMERGENCY%29%20How%20to%3A%20Escalate%20and%20Ask%20for%20Help)
</Note>

#### **Adding or Removing Skills to a Service Provider**
1. To add skills to this Service Provider, click on "+Add Skills" at the bottom of the section

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/LL-136.png" alt="Image" />

a. A new menu will open to guide you to select the skill category:

  <img src="/images/LL-137.png" alt="Image" />
</Frame>

b. Next, you will see the list of skills under that category. You can "Check or Uncheck" all of the skills, or you can check or uncheck each skill individually. Once you are done selecting the applicable skills for this Service Provider, click on "Save"

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/LL-138.png" alt="Image" />
</Frame>
