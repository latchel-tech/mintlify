### **General Questions on what it is and how it works**

#### **1) What is the Home Assistant Program?**
**Answer:**The Home Assistant program is a resident concierge service, available 24/7 during 365 days of the year. The concierge includes damage reimbursements, $0 appointment cancellation, move-out assistance, expedited maintenance coordination and support for taking other non-maintenance related questions.
In addition to saving Property Managers' time, the Home Assistant program provides an additional revenue stream for Property Managers via profit-share and cash-backs. See more details on the Property Manager benefits on this Pamphlet: [Latchel PM Pamphlet.pdf](https://hubs.ly/H0nRfQY0)

We charge you a highly discounted rate, which is 70% off the normal resident rate.If the Resident decides to opt-out, they will fill out the[opt-out](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe6P0xd0Hyr9lB5KC7HMxo3seMv8KDd_x8Xx43pkyKTEqSA9g/viewform) form and we will assess if they are eligible to opt-out, i.e. check if they have or not a valid lease including Latchel Lease Addendum. #### **4) Who is in charge of opt-outs?**
**Answer:**Property Managers are responsible for defining if the Home Assistant program will be a mandatory feature in their Resident Benefit Package or not, for communicating the program to their residents, and for including the program in their lease contracts.
Latchel will opt residents in or out in our system based on information received from the Property Manager. Opt-outs will depend on having or not a valid lease with the resident including the program.
As Property Managers subscribe to our program, two scenarios will happen:
1. Residents with existing leases:
  1. The Property Manager will communicate this program is being added to their Resident Benefit Package and they will be opted-in as a default
  2. Because the current lease agreement doesn't have Latchel services included on it, Residents will be able to opt-out of the program by filling out this form: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe6P0xd0Hyr9lB5KC7HMxo3seMv8KDd_x8Xx43pkyKTEqSA9g/viewform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe6P0xd0Hyr9lB5KC7HMxo3seMv8KDd_x8Xx43pkyKTEqSA9g/viewform)
2. Residents with new leases signed after the Property Manager subscribed to our program:
  1. The Property Manager is responsible for including the Latchel services in their lease by having the Resident signing our Lease Addendum
  2. The Property Manager is responsible for sending Latchel the lease information including property address, resident name, and contact information, lease contract dates (start and end), and specifying if the resident has opted-in or out
**Note**: See this SOP on how to opt-out properties: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How To: Opt Properties In or Out of the Resident Benefit Package](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/965574844/HOME+ASSISTANT+How+To+Opt+Properties+In+or+Out+of+the+Resident+Benefit+Package)

### **Move out Assistance**

#### 1) **How do I benefit from my Residents using the "Move Out Assistance"?**
**Answer:**The "Move Out Assistance" benefit helps to reduce vacancy time as the unit could be ready to re-rent earlier that it would if those repairs were only identified after the resident moves out and the property manager inspects the unit.
**Note**: For more information on this process, see this SOP: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Handle **Move out Assistance** Requests from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/999358706/HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Handle+Move+out+Assistance+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)

#### 2) **How will I know which repairs should be paid by the owner or by the resident?**
**Answer****:**Service providers are able to mark certain repairs as "tenant caused"; however, you may also be able to charge the resident for repairs that you have as "resident owned" in your lease agreement. With the Home Assistant subscription, residents are also able to request repairs or services for items that are clearly their responsibility, like move out cleaning, so you will need to review the invoice to assess each repair and decide which ones they should be charged for.
The work orders related to this type of request will have  "Move Out Assistance" on their title. When residents request service via the "Move Out Assistance" process they acknowledge they may be charged for some or all of the repairs they're requesting.
**Note**: For more information on this process, see this SOP: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Handle **Move out Assistance** Requests from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/999358706/HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Handle+Move+out+Assistance+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)

**Answer:** We reimburse residents for the appointment cancellation fee when they have canceled or rescheduled an appointment via our system at least 4hrs before the appointment start time. If a preferred service provider has improperly charged an appointment cancellation fee, we contact the Property Manager to discuss it and agree on a charge-back to the Property Manager, who should discuss this further with their Preferred Service Provider.
1. If the Property Manager is aware of this policy and this charge, and just wants us to provide more details on this specific work order,let the Property Manager know we will escalate to leadership to get those details and contact them back within 2 business days
  1. Ask the Property Manager if they are looking for some specific information or if they just want to get more details on how/when the cancellation was done
  2. Create a task in the work order and assign it to T2 due immediately and set expectation to follow-up with the resident in 24hrs
  3. Let the Property Manager know we will provide the details via email within 2 business days via email
2. If the Property Manager is not aware of this policy or of the charge, or disagrees with it, or seems upset about it, **escalate**directly to **Customer Success** and let them know they will call them back still today
