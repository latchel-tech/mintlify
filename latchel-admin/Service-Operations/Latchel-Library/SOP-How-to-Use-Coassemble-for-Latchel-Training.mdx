### Step 2 - What's expected with Coassemble courses (versus SOPs)

1. **Required Completion of the Course** 
   1. You are required to complete the course within 2 weeks after it was first published. 
   2. Finish your assigned courses the sooner the better. It's like eating hot food that is ideally eaten when it's still hot! You will have 2 weeks to complete the module. You'll receive notifications on your dashboard and in your email.
2. **Time spent is recorded**
   1. Highly recommended to allot time to focus on completing without distractions. If you need to stop to do something else, exit out of the course, and the course will pause for you.
