## Purpose

<Info>
  **Purpose of this document**

  The goal of this document is to coordinate attendance of the ops troubleshooting team to meetings that involves the whole team, so we avoid gaps in coverage and can guarantee we will always have part of the team supporting the customer with their emergency situations
</Info>

<Info>
  **Who should read this document?**

  - Ops Troubleshooters
  - Shift-support
  - Supervisors
</Info>

## Step-by-Step Guide

**What happened prior to this step?**

You will be invited to two meetings that involves the whole team:

1. Ops Troubleshooter All Hands
2. Company Town Hall

### Ops Troubleshooter All Hands

You will receive an invitation for both meetings, which will be on (1) Wednesday at 11:30am PST and (2) Friday at 11:30am PST. However, you should attend only one of them. Please follow the instructions below to know which meeting you should attend. The same instructions will be included in the meeting invites.

1. You will attend the meeting time that matches your schedule
2. If both meeting times match your schedule, then follow this rule:
3. If your first name starts with letters A to I -- Attend the Wednesday meeting
4. If your first name starts with letters J to Z -- Attend the Friday meeting
5. If your schedule doesn't match any meeting time, you can watch the meeting recording on your first day of your schedule week when you arrive to the shift, excluding weekends. Please let shift-support know you will be watching the recording
6. If you miss the meeting for any other reason, please coordinate with shift-support a time that you can watch the recording. We have also been posting meeting notes in the #operations channel.

### Company Town Hall

We will split the attendance based on the first letter of your name:

- If your first name starts with letters A to I -\> You will attend the 1st week and 3rd week of each month
- If your first name starts with letter J to Z -\> You will attend the 2nd week and last week of each month.
- If you miss a meeting or your schedule does not match with the current meeting time, please watch the video. Please plan to watch the video on the last day of your shift, except weekends or when we have volume spikes. Shift-support will let you know you should watch another time.
