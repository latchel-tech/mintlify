#### 2**) Can I get more information on the cashback portion of the program?**
**Answer:**Benefits of the Home Assistant program include $2/month/unit (24/7 Emergency Assistant) or $4/month/unit (24/7 Home Assistant) in new profit, 3% (ACH transaction) or 1% (credit cards) cashback on maintenance invoicesprocessed through the Latchel portal. Cashbacks are paid out via direct deposit to the bank account of your choosing. See this customer guide on how to set up your deposit account for profit share****

If the customer has concerns that their 3% or 1% cashback payment was incorrect, please reach out to Will Gordon directly for a deep dive into this request.

#### 6**) I manage section 8 properties. What is your process for these properties and how will the program affect these residents?**
**24/7 Home Assistant Answer:** Section 8 Residents most likely won't be able to pay extra fees on the top of their lease, so we will segregate those properties in a separate portfolio and opt them out. This is configured during the Phase 1 onboarding process. If a section 8 resident calls in, Latchel will still handle all emergency andnon-emergency work orders per usual.
**24/7 Emergency Assistant Answer:**Section 8 Residents most likely won't be able to pay extra fees on the top of their lease, so we will segregate those properties in a separate portfolio and opt them out. This is configured during the Phase 1 onboarding process. If a section 8 resident calls in, Latchel will still handle all emergency work orders per usual, however, these residents will not receive the resident benefits of the program.
*See*[*Step 4 - How to Configure a "Blanket Portfolio"*](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CS/pages/796623197/CS+Admin+How+to+Create+Configure+a+Portfolio#id-(CS/Admin)Howto:Create&ConfigureaPortfolio-step5Step4-HowtoConfigurea%22BlanketPortfolio%22)*for configuration steps.*

#### 7) **Why would I ever choose Premium service over Home Assistant service and pay extra?**
**Answer:**You could decide to choose Premium services if you are in a market that you don't believe can support charging residents or owners an extra fee. Although there are fewer benefits with the Premium service compared to the Home Assistant Service, you will still save a lot of time with full maintenance coordination and Latchel will still create a scalable maintenance process that helps you grow. With the Home Assistant program, you have pricing flexibility, as you can opt-in/opt-out units. Additionally, there are more moving parts to manage (lease addendum, resident offer letters, etc.) whereas the Premium plan eliminates some of the management required for Home Assistant and there is one standard fee for all units.
**Internal Note:**If this is a potential upsell, notify the #growth_team slack channel immediately for assistance from sales.****

#### 8) **Why am I being charged back for an appointment cancellation that I have just charged my resident for?**
**T1 Answer:** [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Answer **Property Manager** Questions About the Program (Associates FAQ)](/HOME-ASSISTANT-How-to-Answer-Property-Manager-Questions-About-the-Program-Associates-FAQ) In summary, this question will be escalated **IF** the preferred Service Provider charged a fee that should not have been charged. In other words, the resident did in fact, cancel 4+ hours before the appointment time.
**Internal Note:**T1 will inform the Property Manager that Customer Success will call them that same day. Call the Property Manager after receiving this complaint to review the process and next steps. First, see an overview of reimbursement types and detailed process here: [(HOME ASSISTANT) How to: Process **Reimbursements Requests** from Home Assistant Residents](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/983763472/ARCHIVED+---+HOME+ASSISTANT+How+to+Process+Reimbursements+Requests+from+Home+Assistant+Residents)
**CS Answer:**
1. Customer Success will verify if the charge is correct:
  1. Review the question from the Property Manager
    1. Double check the validity of the reimbursement issued to the resident, if any
      1. Verify if the Service Provider is a preferred service provider of this Property Manager
        1. Check validity of the charge issued to the Property Manager
      2. Review if that charge was properly communicated by looking for Customer Success notes in the work order.
        1. Customer Success should have contacted the Property Manager prior to issuing the charge back and should have left notes in the work order (SOP-[(HOME ASSISTANT)(CS/Admin) How to: Handle Appointment Cancellation Charge Backs to Property Managers](/HOME-ASSISTANTCSAdmin-How-to-Handle-Appointment-Cancellation-Charge-Backs-to-Property-Managers))
        2. By the notes, you should be able to get information about the decision to charge back, who CS talked to, when that conversation happened
        3. Also, listen to call recording to get more information on what was communicated, how it was communicated, and any other comment from the Property Manager
2. If confirmed that there was a charge to the property manager, and the charge was appropriate according to our policy, and it was handled correctly, Customer Success will say:
  1. *"Latchel reimbursed your resident for the appointment cancellation fee that was charged to them as they requested the cancellation in accordance with our policy. Our policy is to issue a reimbursement when the resident cancels an appointment 4+ hours before the appointment start time. In this situation, the preferred service provider should not have charged this fee, and therefore we will have a chargeback to you for this reimbursement. Because the resident took action according to our policy and the preferred vendor still charged a fee, we ask that you now discuss this policy with your preferred service provider. We recommend you to chargeback your preferred service provider to recover this chargeback we will charge you."*
3. If Customer Success is unable to confirm the preferred service provider, or discovered that we made a mistake charging the Property Manager  or handled this poorly, Customer Success will:
  1. Revert the charge back
    1. Escalate to Customer Success ([How to Escalate to Customer Success](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1485373477/How+to+Escalate+to+Product+Support)) to request a refund to be issued to the Property Manager
    2. Call the Property Manager to confirm the refund was issued. Leave notes in the account specifying the refund issued and the reason for it
  2. Apologize for the mistake
