## Purpose

Describe Latchel's policies for opening new positions, making hiring decisions, internal job transfers, and promotions.

## New Job Positions

Any people manager can request a new position. To submit a request for a position, submit the following via email to Elisa Rhynedance.

1. A formal job description including: a. Opportunity and Impact b. Job Responsibilities c. Minimum Qualifications
   1. How work will be routed to this role.
   2. The metrics this position will impact.
   3. A Job Posting
   4. A description of why this position is required.
   5. The number of positions and a timeline to fill.

Example Job Description: [Operations Trainer Job Description](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1L_yXSK37C0K6VW3mBafno5DCXDuy8jfHyJDqRR80a6Q/edit#heading=h.r88ntf9jfsub) 

Example Job Posting: [Operations Trainer Job Posting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GiCm1sr-xdJgDSRdGh86XXFyZBbYWRpv9vyMHfTn4LI/edit)

The position must be approved by the Latchel Leadership team in the Weekly Business Review.

## Promotions

1. Promotion decisions (including both job level increases and job role changes) are made during the Quarterly Review Process.
2. Job Level increases are made after the following:
   1. Any review with "Outstanding" performance.
   2. 3 consecutive reviews with "Excellent" performance (or 3 of 4 consecutive reviews).
3. All promotions must be approved by the manager and a majority of the Latchel Leadership team (all attendees of the Weekly Business Review Meeting).
4. All "Excellent" performance reviews must be approved by both the direct manager and the manager's manager.
5. The Head of HR will update all job roles in TriNet and the Latchel Org chart in a single batch update timed with the next All-Hands after the Leadership team approves all promotions.
   1. Any Job Role changes require a new signed offer letter and any updated employment agreements.

## Market Rate Adjustments

At Latchel we typically review market rates (for both salary and equity) for all roles at the end of Q2. We make appropriate adjustments for anyone receiving a "Great work" rating or above.

## Internal Job Transfers

1. Anyone may apply to an open role on their same team at any time.
2. If you have been in your current role for less than 6 months, you are not allowed to apply to roles outside of your team. If you have been in your role for 6 months or longer, you can apply to any open position on any team in any department.

<Note>
  **Example 1 - Same team**

  A Business Development Rep can apply to be an Account Executive any time a new Account Executive position is opened. This is because both roles are on the Sales Team within the Growth Department.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Example 2 - Different Teams**

  Suppose a new Customer Success Manager position is opened. A Business Development Rep (BDR) who just joined the company less than 6 months ago is NOT eligible to apply. This is because the BDR has been in her role for less than 6 months AND the Customer Success Team is different from the Sales team (even though they are both in the Growth Department).
</Note>

3. To apply to a role, submit your updated resume and your cover letter to [jobs@latchel.com](mailto:jobs@latchel.com) and cc your current manager (even if the current manager is the hiring manager).
4. The hiring manager will review all internal candidates as a priority over external candidates.
5. If the hiring manager is interested in the candidate:
   1. If the hiring manager is different from the current manager:
      1. The hiring manager will meet with the current manager to confirm the candidate is eligible to transfer (6\+ months in role and most recent performance review was Great Work or better).
      2. If the hiring manager is still interested in interviewing the candidate whose most recent performance review was below Great Work, written approval to proceed with an interview must be obtained by at least 1 Cofounder.
      3. If the hiring manager can continue with the process, they will interview the candidate. If after the interview, the hiring manager still wants to proceed, continue with step 7
   2. If the hiring manager is the same as the current manager:
      1. Check to make sure the candidate is eligible to transfer (6\+ months in the role).
      2. The hiring manager may choose to interview the candidate or not. The hiring manager must create a written document explaining why this candidate should be considered for the open position, using both the demonstrated performance against the responsibilities and Latchel's Leadership Principles.
      3. Continue with step 7.
6. If the hiring manager is NOT interested in the candidate:
   1. If the hiring manager is different from the current manager:
      1. The hiring manager will email or Slack the current manager explaining why the candidate was not selected for an interview and what would need to change to be eligible in the future.
      2. The current manager will explain in the next 1:1 why they were not selected for an interview.
   2. If the hiring manager is the same as the current manager:
      1. The current manager will explain in the next 1:1 why they were not selected for an interview and work on a development plan to help the candidate become qualified the next time the job is open.
7. Second interview: The hiring manager will schedule a 20 minute interview with a member of the Latchel Leadership team. This interview will be recorded.
8. Debrief: The hiring manager will schedule a debrief with the second interviewer.
   1. If the second interviewer is inclined, then they will notify the hiring manager and Elisa via Slack about the job change. The hiring manager will schedule the formal job change with Elisa. This will include a new signed offer letter and any updated employment agreements.
   2. If the second interviewer is NOT inclined, then they must write down the reasons (s)he is not inclined.
      1. The hiring manager may appeal the decision to either the CEO or the Head of HR.
      2. The CEO/Head of HR will review the reasons and the recorded interview.
      3. The CEO/Head of HR will choose whether or not to conduct a follow-up interview or maintain the second interviewer's decision.
         1. If there is a follow-up interview, the CEO/Head of HR will coach the second interviewer on better interviewing techniques.
         2. If the CEO/Head of HR conducts a follow-up interview and is not inclined, they will add to the second interviewer's decision.
         3. This decision is final and cannot be appealed.
      4. In the case of a not inclined outcome, the hiring manager will explain the documented reasons.
      5. The candidate's current manager will help create a development plan to help the candidate become qualified the next time the job is open
