<Info>
  The below Tips for Remote Working come from a February 2020 “About Me” survey. In total, 24 people responded to this question: **_“If you work for Latchel, then you work remotely! What do you do in your remote-working life to keep your sanity? In other words, what's your remote-working "vice"?”_** See summary results below!
</Info>

| **Themes** | **Raw Comments (anonymous)** |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Taking breaks** Latchel team members take breaks throughout the day which either release the mental stress and/or give themselves a moment to focus on something outside of work. Notable activities include singing while you work, doing crosswork puzzles, or watching/listening to something on the internet for a brief moment. Feel free to post in #random when you’re taking one of those inspirational breaks. | <ul><li>I take a 5 min break. Read something nice online such as trivia, inspirational stories and some jokes too.</li><li>10 minute breaks and do online crosswords to keep my mind fresh, I usually do brain exercises every day to stay mentally healthy</li><li>Depending on the day and my schedule, I'll sometimes take a break to noodle on the guitar.</li><li>Reading Mein Kampf, reading Emma Goldman's anarchism and other essays</li><li>Physically get up and move around. When I grow tired of being in my office I sometimes work out on my porch.</li><li>I'll pick up my guitar or play on the keyboard for a quick mental break, but end up playing for way too long.</li><li>I listen to music while I work. And occasionally watch YouTube videos.</li><li>Spotify and a little bit of Neflix when there's too much avail time.</li><li>Music when not in a call and sing with it from time to time.</li></ul> |
| **Having Others Around you** Latchel team members appreciate their at-home companions, whether they be of human or animal form. Make sure you join the Slack channel #pets to share your pets with us. | <ul><li>Finding ways to remain social and make in-person connections throughout the day</li><li>my fur-child</li><li>Get outside and take the dog for a walk!</li><li>\[While on a Sales call\], I'm prob petting a dog</li><li>Feeding my gold fishes just beside my desk, lol</li><li>Having my animals around all the time helps too. :)</li></ul> |
| **Office situation** Latchel team members think about their working environment, setting it up for an optimal lifestyle. Feel free to post in #general when you want to share your cool new office gadgets. | <ul><li>Electric stand-up desk is great - significant increase in my energy level and overall feeling of well-being. Keeping a dedicated office is crucial (google stimulus control)</li><li>Defined space to work and I don't enter my office during my weekends, so I do feel my home office is separate from the rest of my home</li><li>I also try not to use my computer, phone, and TV during my days off to eliminate too much screen time.</li><li>I took Slack off of my phone!</li><li>\[Less frequently\] I'll have a beer, whiskey, or wine while working. Even less frequently than that, I'll get completely smashed and do really boring work like taxes and bookkeeping.</li></ul> |
| **Outside hobbies** Embracing your favorite activites outside of work is another vice for team members. Noteable activity that sometimes at a high-growth start-up may sometimes warrant stress-relieving activities such as going to a gun range… Post in #adventure about your… adventures! | <ul><li>Gym and video games</li><li>Go out to a gun range :)</li><li>Rearrange my schedule so that I have time to go on a run or something like that instead.)</li><li>Making sure I get a few minutes of work out in.</li><li>Massages</li></ul> |
| **Coffee** Coffee is its own category because, well, just look at us. | <ul><li>Six team members mentioned drinking coffee in their responses.</li><li>One team member mentioned drinking “5 cups of coffee per day”</li><li>One team member embraces their cowboy nature and has “a big cup of black coffee before starting work”</li></ul> |
| **Treat yoself** Latchel team members know how to dress the part while also treating themselves well while doing remote work. | <ul><li>I always start my day here in Latchel at 11pm our time which is really awkward at first, but I don't do coffee. I always make sure that I get a 30-minute nap before my 10-hour shift just so my eyes can relax first and my brain would function well.</li><li>Face masks while on sales calls</li><li>Start many of my days in my pajamas.</li></ul> |
| **Body wellness** Take some time to center your body awareness before sitting down in front of a screen for X amount of hours. | <ul><li>Breathing exercises</li><li>Stretch before starting the workday.</li><li>Meditation before starting the workday.</li></ul> |
