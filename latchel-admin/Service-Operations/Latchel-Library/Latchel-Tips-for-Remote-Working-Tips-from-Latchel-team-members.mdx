<Info>
The below Tips for Remote Working come from a February 2020 “About Me” survey.
In total, 24 people responded to this question:
***“If you work for Latchel, then you work remotely! What do you do in your remote-working life to keep your sanity? In other words, what's your remote-working "vice"?”***
See summary results below!
</Info>
