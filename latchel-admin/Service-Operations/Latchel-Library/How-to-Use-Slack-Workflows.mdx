## Objective

<Info>
  A Slack workflow is a predetermined set of questions or actions that can be prompted within slack channels.
</Info>

<Info>
  The purpose of this document is to give a step by step guide on how to use the workflows available in Slack.
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1 - **Select the lightning bolt to see available macro options, this is referred to as the "Action Menu"**

#### **Step 2 -** Select the applicable macro

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-27.png)

#### **Step 3 -** Fill out the form with your answers

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-28.png)

#### **Step 4 -** Upon submission, your answers will automatically post to the channel and notify the administrator responsible

  ![Image](/images/LL-29.png)
