### **How to: Add/Update your own Service Provider address**

**Q: **The Service Provider asked me to update the company address and I completed that on their behalf, but how can I advise them to be self-directed and complete that action on their own next time?

**A: **Your response (via whatever medium) can advise the Service Provider to use their dashboard to fulfill these actions next time. Here's an example of what you could say -

Dear _(Service Provider name here)_,

As requested, I just changed the original contact information from (_insert original info here) _to the new requested information (_insert requested information here)_ for you.

Did you know that you can make these changes via your own Latchel Dashboard? Navigate to (_insert [Public Dashboard URL](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/781713836/How+to+Navigate+the+Service+Provider+Interface "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/781713836/How+to+Navigate+the+Service+Provider+Interface") here)._
