## Purpose

<Info>
  The purpose of this document is to provide a step-by-step guide on how to resolve Bank Account issues for Service Providers at Latchel.
</Info>

<Info>
  Prior to this process, the agent has completed processes for “Service Provider Needs to complete Bank Information”
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

#### Step 1

- Locate on the [“Operations Task Sheet”](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1t4acQ6w83RgjA8GnqDenrqM-w4Bo9R_Rq29k8fKWEsg/edit#gid=107512268)

#### Step 2

- Go to [latchel portal](https://stage.latchel.com/admin/jobs) Step 3
- Select "Service Providers" under the “People” tab

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-143.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 4

- Search for the applicable Service Provider via name or phone number

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-144.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 5

- Check if they have bank information under their profile
  - If they do not, they must add it in
  - If they do then there is an issue in Stripe [(How to resolve issues in stripe)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1drFQHQpb4fu2AjzK7oKE4mCiybG2XSOQ1DItct0Uqug/edit)

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-145.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 6

- Check if the work order has them listed as “Latchel Service Provider” and compare that to tagged status under their profile.
  - If they do not match then the Service Provider has not been onboarded.
    - Re-onboard via (Latchel Service Provider Network Onboarding Guide- link pending)

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-146.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-147.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 7

- If they do match, Ask Service Provider to input information in Latchel Portal Via their Service Provider homepage which can be reached by going to [latchel.com](http://latchel.com) and selecting login in the upper right

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/LL-148.png)
</Frame>
