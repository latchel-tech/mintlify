## Purpose

<Info>
  This is an external-facing Tech Update that is used by Preferred and Latchel Sourced Service Providers. This tool provides flexibility for scheduling, updates and communication with our team. Our team has full visibility of Service Provider history and actions, allowing us to make appropriate choices according to our Latchel Operational Processes. Latchel Associates and/or Service Provider Managers may become well-versed with its various functionalities to be of assistance to Service Providers during Onboarding or if they have any questions about working together with Latchel. ([Informational: Latchel Customers & Customer Subscriptions](/Informational-Latchel-Customers-Customer-Subscriptions))
</Info>

<Info>
  When the Service Provider clicks on any Work Order link within any of our automated messages, they are directed to the Service Provider Interface. As you know, automated messages require an action on the part of the Service Provider
</Info>

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. Here's a video of Ethan giving a high-level overview of its interface functionality: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oBUsNwKNp1A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oBUsNwKNp1A)
2. The Service Provider Interface has both desktop and mobile functionality
3. Built for mobile functionality to provide convenience to Service Providers who do not typically work from stationary desktops, but instead typically work "out in the field"
4. Summary of functionalities (View the video for visuals)
   1. Click on "Set a new work appointment"
      1. Arrival Window Time Slots (as they match with Resident availability)
      2. "I spoke to the resident, we have agreed upon a different time"
         1. Set a new time on a provided calendar
      3. "None of these work, I have not spoken to the Resident"
         1. This action triggers an action back to the Resident
      4. Also available as a button on the top right (from the Job Details Interface)
   2. Click on "Show me job details"
      1. Job Details Interface
         1. Individual Work Order details
      2. Service Provider Feedback
         1. Within the Service Provider Interface, the Service Provider may click the link that states: **_"Are you happy with Latchel? Provide feedback"_**
            <Frame>
              ![Image](/images/LL-35.png)
            </Frame>
         2. The Service Provider will be linked to an anonymous Google Form that collects feedback.
   3. Click on "My Jobs" Interface to view all jobs (categorized by Status Columns)
      1. 'Action Required' Column
         1. Per applicable Work Order, click on the button that leads the Service Provider to fulfill the required action for that job.
         2. Click on the Work Order card to view the Job Details Interface
      2. 'Scheduled Today' Column
      3. 'In Progress' Column
      4. 'Done' Column
   4. Click on the three dots at the top (More options: "What would you like to do?")
      1. "Set a New Work Appointment"
      2. "Mark job as complete"
      3. "Update job progress"
         1. "Provide job update"
      4. "Budget or estimate was denied"
      5. "Add invoice"
      6. "Add images and files"
      7. "Remove Me from This Job"
         1. Available as an option after the Service Provider has been Assigned to the job.
         2. "Are you sure you want to be removed from this job?"
            1. "Yes, remove me now"
               1. Returns to "Finding Vendor" status
            2. "No, cancel"
      8. "I need help"
         1. Time Sensitive
         2. Not Time Sensitive
            1. "We're here to help"
               1. Options to write a note about the Work Order
