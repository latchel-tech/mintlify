## Purpose

<Info>
**Purpose of this document**

The purpose of this SOP is to explain the process to handle outages in our Latchel application, also known as the Latchel App, Latchel Console, or Latchel Portal
</Info>

<Info>

## Step-by-Step Guide

**What happened before this step?**

Someone has been experiencing an issue with the Latchel Application

### Process Overview

Here is the process (text version):

1. Service Operations staff reproduces the bug and then reports bugs in the #bug-report channel →
2. Service Operations leadership validates if the bug is an outage or degradation of service. The bug is then escalated in the #tech-high-sev-escalations channel →
3. If Customer Success received a communication from a customer-facing an issue, they will also report it in the #tech-high-sev-escalations channel
4. Engineering assesses the issue and determines if it is an outage →
5. Engineering keeps the internal team and customer-facing page updated on the status of the outage →
6. Engineering resolves the issue and communicates the resolution and post-mortem →
7. Customer Success assesses if they should or not send an additional customer communication, depending on duration and/or need for PMs to take action

### What is an outage?

**Outage**

An outage means that multiple people can't access the app or reports it is not running (blank, stuck, unresponsive).

**Degradation of service**

- It means the app is working but it is slow (is the app taking more than 10 secs to load info?)
- It means the app is overall working but impacting certain customers
  - Duplicates being created by an integration
  - Can't create a WO
  - Can't save a setting in the user interface
  - Vendor auto queue
  - Communication features
  - Invoicing or payment features

<Note>
Remember to reproduce the error/bug and record a video. If you cannot reproduce it, it is ok to record a video explaining how the customer navigates the app and where the issue appears.
</Note>

### What should I do throughout an outage?

**Service Operations staff**

| | |
|---|---|
| Having a tech issue | Check Latchel Status Page or #core-team channel to see if there is an issue already identified that tech is working on.<br/>Attempt the troubleshooting steps outlined in the troubleshooting link provided in the Latchel Status Page before escalating.<br/>Escalate to the Service Operations Leadership. |
| Tech issue has been validated | Monitor #core-team slack channel for potential updates. |
| During the outage | Acknowledge the outage message posted on the #core-team channel.<br/>Monitor #core-team slack channel for potential updates.<br/>If we get emails from customers reporting an outage or issue using our system, escalate those to Customer Success.<br/>If customers are asking for support because they have system issues that we are aware of, help the customer with their request and share the link to the Latchel Status page, where they can see instructions for troubleshooting and monitor the status of the issue.<br/>If customers are asking for support because they have system issues that we are NOT aware of, help the customer with their request, say you are reporting this issue, and monitor the Latchel Status Page for updates. Escalate this to Customer Success.<br/>If we are also having system issues internally and cannot take action to help the customer, reply to the customer apologizing and saying we will take care of it when the system is back. Share the link to the Latchel Status page. See blurb in this page [Job-Aid: Blurbs or Templates to Communicate with Customers](#)<br/>Create a task due in 1hr to take the action you were unable to do. If you are unable to create a task, send an email to team@latchel.com with a link to the work order and the action that needs to be taken |
| End of the outage | Resume normal work |

## Appendix:

### Appendix 1 - Slack Workflows

**"Internal Outage"**

- What is happening?
- When did it start?
- How long to resolve?
- What should ops do in the meantime?

**"Outage Post-Mortem"**

- What happened?
- What is the Root Cause?
- Is there anything PMs need to do to restart normal operations? If yes, what
- Is there anything Vendors need to do to restart normal operations? If yes, what
- Is there anything Residents need to do to restart normal operations? If yes, what

### Appendix 2- Customer Success Email Templates

Customer Success will communicate the outage to Customers in two situations:

1. When the outage/issue lasted less than 3hrs and we require any of our customers to take an action to resume normal operations. Send this message to the customer that needs to take action:

**Subject Title:** (Latchel / Latchel Feature) is Back!

**Content:**

Hi there,

We are very sorry about the (feature) (outage/service degradation) we had today. Our tech team worked super hard to get this quickly fixed and (Latchel/feature) is back!

Because this issue impacted (xyz), it is necessary for you to (action) to avoid any issues when you resume using (Latchel/feature). In order to do that, you can follow the instructions in this guide (link).

If you have any questions, please contact us (via email at success@latchel.com / Webchat - if ops has received guidance on how to help customers with this)

Thank you,
The Latchel Team

2. When the outage takes 3hrs+ to get resolved, we will send an apology email to PMs? or also residents?

**Subject Title:** (Latchel/Feature) is Back!

**Content:**

Hi there,

We are very sorry about the (feature) (outage/service degradation) we had today. We appreciate your patience while we got this fixed and apologize for the poor experience you have had. Our tech team worked super hard to fix the issue and (Latchel/feature) is back!

(Because this issue impacted (xyz), it is necessary for you to (action) to avoid any issues when you resume using (Latchel/feature). In order to do that, you can follow the instructions in this guide (link).)

If you have any questions, please contact us (via email at success@latchel.com / Webchat - if ops has received guidance on how to help customers with this)

Thank you,
The Latchel Team
