## Purpose

<Info>
  A basic understanding of Latchel's Four Customer Types and Subscription packages of Latchel services help the Latchel team member understand their role with customers and our services.
</Info>

### **Section 1 - Three Types of Customers**

1. **Residents (including tenants)** - Latchel manages maintenance requests that residents would traditionally ask their landlords (Owners) and Property Managers to take care of. When something goes wrong within the home that's owned or rented by the resident, we step in to assist, ensuring the issue is resolved efficiently and professionally.
   1. We are also support residents with non-maintenance requests and offer additional perks. This SOP provide more details on each subscription we offer: [Job Aid: Latchel Product Features, Pricing and KPIs](/Job-Aid-Latchel-Product-Features-Pricing-and-KPIs)
      <Frame>
        ![Image](/images/LL-23.png)
      </Frame>
   2. **Note: Emergency Services -** As you can see in the above infographic, Latchel _"Screens for Emergencies"_ and completes _"Deep Dive Troubleshooting"_ for residents too. When the resident has an emergency in the home, they can call us to help them solve the problem or get a professional out there to help them. These are referred to as Emergency Services at Latchel. We offer the service 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, but some customers are only subscribed to this service during their "after business hours" time (after 5pm and before 9am). For more information on what maintenance requests we consider to be "emergencies", see: [How to: Decide the Job Category: Emergency Definitions](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/745504771/How+to+Identify+Property+Damage+and+Extreme+Habitability+Situations)
      1. To check the Property Manager's Subscription with Latchel, navigate to the work order’s **“Subscriptions” tab**, located in the lower-right section of the work order. Below is an example for reference:
         <Frame>
           ![Image](/images/LL-24.png)
         </Frame>
2. **Property Managers (including landlords and property owners)** - Property Managers sponsor Latchel services to their residents and property owners. Launched in March 2020, the Home Assistant Program offers an alternative payment option.
   1. Instead of Property Managers covering Latchel fees from their budget or charging property owners, the program integrates Latchel into Resident Benefit Packages.
   2. These packages provide enhanced services for residents, who are charged an additional monthly fee by the Property Manager.
      1. **How it Works:**
         1. Residents sign a "Lease Addendum" explaining Latchel’s services and the associated fee.
         2. Property Managers manage the leases, collect fees from residents, and pay Latchel.
         3. To incentivize participation, Latchel shares profits with Property Managers and provides marketing materials and contract templates to help sign up residents.
   3. To check if a Property Manager has signed up for the Home Assistant program, look for **"Addons:"** located below the base subscription section of the work order.
3. **Service Providers (including vendors, technicians, contractors) -** Latchel connects local professional technicians to maintenance requests from residents across the United States. By facilitating communication and scheduling, Latchel ensures that both residents and Service Providers can efficiently coordinate job appointments.
   1. Service Providers are categorized based on how they are assigned to jobs and how they receive payment. The three types of Service Providers are:
      1. **Preferred Service Providers**
      2. **Latchel Network Service Providers** (part of the Latchel Service Provider Network)
      3. **Latchel Vendor Network**
   2. For more information, see: [How to: Assign a Service Provider](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/752943255/Non-Emergency+How+to+Assign+a+Latchel+Network+Service+Provider), and [Job Aid: Service Provider Payments (Issue a Card)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/FwBDLQ).
   3. Latchel Service Providers across the U.S. sign a contract and uphold a working contractor agreement with Latchel. They are provided with access to their own **Latchel Console Interface**, which enables them to:
      - View and schedule jobs
      - Upload invoices (available on both desktop and mobile)
   4. This system allows Service Providers to manage their tasks efficiently and ensures seamless service delivery to residents.

<Info>
  **Vendor Types** Latchel works with Service Providers across 30 types of home maintenance trades: Elevators, Fencing, Flooring, Carpet Cleaning, Handyman, Electrical, Painting, Plumbing, Roofing, Garage Doors, HVAC, Locksmith, Restoration, Sanitizing Service, Septic, Water, Extraction, Appliance, Cleaning, Concrete/Driveway/Masonry, Fireplace/Chimney, General Contractor, Glass/Windows, Hot Water Heater, Irrigation/Landscaping/Trees, Junk Removal/Hauling, Pests/Extermination, Pool, Snow Removal, Awning/Sunshades, Welding.
</Info>

### **Section 2 - Subscription Packages**

<Note>
  **Product packages** are custom names created by the Latchel user during account setup and are not standardized. As a result, **it is not recommended to rely on the Product Package name** to understand the services included. Instead, you should:

  1. **Review the ‘subscriptions’** to identify the package the customer is subscribed to.
  2. **Reference the “_base product_”** to determine how the account should be serviced accurately. 

  To learn more about subscription packages and how to check them on a PM’s profile, please watch the [video](https://www.loom.com/share/0553236d1e614557bbf8349d20f6cfbb).

  For any misalignment between subscriptions and product packages, please submit a Product Support (PS) ticket. Refer to the guide here: [How to Escalate to Product Support](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1485373477/How+to+Escalate+to+Product+Support).
</Note>

1. These are the products we **_currently_**offer to customers:
   1. 24/7 Subscription
   2. After-Hours Subscription
   3. Home Assistant **(Can be sold alone or paired with maintenance)**
      1. **Assisted Ledgering**
      2. **Optional Benefits**
   4. Autopilot (formerly known as SaaS)
   5. On-Call & 24/7 Emergency
   6. Basic (can be paired with Home Assistant)
2. Technology Solutions
   1. **Latchel Chatbot (All)-** All residents can send their maintenance requests in through text message, we see these as Text Message input.
      1. [↔ How to: Use Text Message and Webchat Functions (in Latchel Console)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/766509545/How+to+Use+Text+Message+SMS+Webchat+Functions+in+Latchel+Console)
3. Maintenance Coordination
   1. **Latchel Contractor Network (All)** - All Property Managers, excluding those on the On-Call product, can Opt-in or Opt-out of our Latchel Contractor Network. View the "Property Manager" section to check.
   2. 24 x 7 x 365 Emergencies\*\*(24/7 Subscription) -\*\*Only 24/7 Subscription packages benefit from Emergency Services everyday, all the time (24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year for, including all weekends and holidays). This 24/7 subscription can be bundled with a Premium or Autopilot subscription for non emergencies.
      1. If you handle a non-emergency work order, please ensure it is forwarded to the Property Manager. If you do not have access to forward the work order, escalate to Operations Support Specialists to move the work order to waiting for PM.
   3. 5pm-9am Monday through Friday Emergencies\*\*(After Hours)\*\*After Hours subscriptions benefit from Emergency Services after business hours (5pm to 9am from Monday through Friday). During normal business hours, Latchel does not complete Emergency Services for the After Hours customers (forward the message to the Property Manager, instead)
      1. For Property Managers with an After-Hours subscription, emergency handling is determined by the **time the work order is received**. If a request is submitted at **8:50 AM** (within the after-hours window) and the after-hours period ends while the team is preparing for dispatch, the request should **continue to be handled end-to-end**, including dispatching an available technician.
      2. **Acceptable appointment times for After-Hours emergency work orders:**
         <Note>
           Note: Emergency rates and higher emergency budgets are intended for same-day or within 24 hours appointments. If an appointment is scheduled more than 24 hours out, treat it as standard (non-emergency) for rates/budget expectations; see [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332893/How+to+Handle+Emergency+Work+Orders) and [How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2600337430/How+to+Set+expectations+with+Service+Providers+when+dispatching) for guidance. 
         </Note>
         1. Property-damaging/extreme habitability: same-day appointment.
         2. Other emergencies: same-day or within 24 hours appointment.
      3. **Follow-ups and PM requests received during business hours**
         1. **Identify the Work Order Status**
            1. Review the work order details to confirm it requires follow-up.
            2. Check the Property Manager’s subscription type to verify they have **After-Hours** coverage but not **24/7 emergency** coverage.
            3. If a resident provides new information that escalates the situation from a regular emergency to one involving property damage or extreme habitability concerns
               1. Escalate to the Property Manager. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2537652248/Job+Aid+PM+Outbound+Communication+for+Emergency+WOs)
            4. If the issue is not property damaging or extreme habitability or if it was already classified as property damaging or extreme habitability and the Property Manager has already been notified
               1. _Hi \[Resident’s/SP's Name\], We will notify the Property Manager of this follow-up. They are responsible for handling the next steps on this work order. You may also reach out to them directly for any updates. Let us know if you need anything else!_
               2. Create a task for follow-up and assign it to the Property Manager
                  1. Limit to **one task per day** to ensure the PM is notified without being overwhelmed.
         2. Ownership and next steps once after-hours ends:
            1. If Latchel secures a confirmed appointment during after-hours, set/confirm the appointment and document it on the work order (date/time, SP, scope).
            2. After-hours maintenance coordination responsibility depends on when any follow-up is received:
               1. Follow-ups or PM requests received during business hours: follow the guidance under 'Follow-ups and PM requests received during business hours' in this section (create a PM task, limit to one per day, use the provided message scripts, and escalate only if PD/EH or the situation escalates).
               2. During business hours, Latchel does not continue end-to-end emergency dispatching/coordination for After-Hours-only customers beyond securing a confirmed appointment. Once an appointment is secured, any follow-ups or next steps received during business hours are owned by the Property Manager. If the appointment falls through, Latchel will only re-engage in end-to-end coordination when the follow-up is received during the after-hours window (per the 8:50 AM example above).
   4. Non-emergencies\*\*(Premium and Home Assistant only) -\*\* We handle non-emergency maintenance requests from Premium customers.
   5. Project Coordination\*\*(Premium and Home Assistant only) -\*\*We handle job coordination and scheduling of the Service Provider and resident for Premium customers
   6. Autopilot:
      1. For non-emergencies, **automatically generated tasks** **will now be assigned to the Property Manager**.
      2. For non-emergencies, **automatic emails generated by Latchel** **will come from the Property Manager’s email address**
      3. Property Managers have full use of Latchel software to run their maintenance tickets.
      4. Automation still runs and the Latchel team acts as intake for new work order requests. Any follow-up communication (e.g. texts) get routed to the PM via the Latchel console.
   7. Basic **only** - Under this subscription, Latchel performs **intake only**. These requests must **not**be handled directly by Latchel and should be forwarded to the Property Manager for action. For any follow-up requests received, create a task for the Property Manager. Even if the Property Manager requests an upgrade to an emergency, Latchel should not dispatch, coordinate, or take further action on the issue.
      1. A bug report is only required if the issue falls outside the expected behavior of the Basic subscription—specifically if automated tasks were incorrectly assigned to Latchel, please post them in the **#bug-report**channel.
   8. **Basic with Home Assistant -** Property Managers handle maintenance requests independently (outside of Latchel) but continue to use Latchel for concierge requests under Home Assistant to provide residents with added perks. As a result, Latchel only manages work orders that are marked as Resident Responsible, as directed by the system. For all other non-resident-responsible maintenance requests, residents should be advised to contact their property manager directly.
4. **Basic Maintenance Intake (Legacy) -** These are non-autopilot emergency customers. We handle all emergency intake and dispatching, 24/7 (unless specified otherwise by critical notes or pre-dispatch instructions). Non-emergency requests are automatically forwarded to the Property Manager.
5. **Non-maintenance requests (Premium and Home Assistant only) -** We will take non-maintenance related requests 24/7 and pass those on to the property manager
6. Home Assistant will also include additional benefits focused on the resident
   1. See this SOP for more details on all features offered for each program/subscription - [Job Aid: Latchel Product Features, Pricing and KPIs](/Job-Aid-Latchel-Product-Features-Pricing-and-KPIs)
