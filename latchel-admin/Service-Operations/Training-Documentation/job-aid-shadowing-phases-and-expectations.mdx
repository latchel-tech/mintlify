<Info>
  **Purpose of this document**

  This is a guide to assist seasoned Ops Associates and Troubleshooters in delivering effective shadowing sessions and ensure we are providing an equal shadowing experience to all New Hires while dividing shadowing into 3 different phases.
</Info>

## **Coordination**

The Ops Trainer will arrange a weekly plan for New Hires and Shadowing Partners once they begin shadowing. Supervisors will arrange a shadowing plan for cross-training associates following the guidelines in the “How to: Perform Cross-training Programs “ SOP. If the Shadow Partner cannot attend a session for some unexpected reason, The Organizer or Operations Support Specialists can look for a different Shadow Partner as long as they meet the shadow partner requirements.

- Ops Associates: Shadow partners should be Ops Associates who have been with Latchel for at least 90 days and met our productivity & CX Experience goals in the previous month. (Meet or exceed the team average in the task they are assigned to, See in [Monthly Productivity  2023](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JUQNLLv1neclupF5tazwa_Zen-ypmR3mZOx90-u6Z-A/edit#gid=2088971207) \+ Meet or exceed the CX goal of 95%, See in [Customer Experience Review](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11g_qx-XbGTujAK04kS-rI8qtHLz6ZqrsFbWLis3knAE/edit#gid=1881221863))
- Ops Troubleshooters: Shadow partners should be Ops Troubleshooters (or cross-trained) who have been with Latchel for at least 90 days and met our productivity & CX Experience goals in the previous month. (Meet or exceed the team average in the task they are assigned to, See in [Monthly Productivity  2023](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JUQNLLv1neclupF5tazwa_Zen-ypmR3mZOx90-u6Z-A/edit#gid=2088971207) \+ Meet or exceed the TS CX goal of 90%, See in [Troubleshooting Ratings](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hMT5oOMmZjl0ennUWmW1AJ9lrzU811qX0R1z9MQ2C7g/edit#gid=1454868643))

<Warning>
  **Attention:** Shadow partners should not have any ongoing PIPs. If you cannot find anyone that meets the needed requirements, move on to the next closest person and contact the Training team lead if you have any questions.
</Warning>

## **Phase I: Shadowing**

The Shadow Partner will perform their normal activities while the New Hire observes and asks simple questions along the way while taking notes on general topics that they can discuss more thoroughly with Ops Trainers in New Hire debriefs. This phase will be primarily covered via self-service Coassemble training.

## **Phase II: Reverse Shadowing**

This is a transition phase where the New Hire will go from observing to taking action. The Shadow Partner will divide the session between teaching and observing the New Hire so they can begin performing the activities they have been learning **_using a “I do a task, you do a task” approach_**. The Shadow Partner will closely guide the New Hire through the process since they may feel insecure and have a lot of questions. The New Hire will gradually perform activities more autonomously and the Shadow Partner will be responsible for ensuring the process being followed is the correct and most efficient.

**Shadow Partner responsibilities:**

- Share the screen and audio of the computer while conducting the shadowing sessions following the [Using Slack to Shadow Calls](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2337767441) guide.
- Update your console status to “Training” when you are doing reverse shadowing.
- Reverse shadowing session should start at 50% shadowing and 50% reverse shadowing. Go back and forth using a **_“I do a task, you do a task” approach_** and progressivley allow the tranee to practice more until they end up practicing with minimal assistance.
- Encourage the New Hire to take action while they are being observed.
- Intervene during reverse shadowing when there is an improvement opportunity such as guiding the New Hire to the proper path of a process, pointing out recommendations that the New Hire can use to improve, correcting incorrect information the New Hire may have, etc.
- Respond to any questions the New Hire may have regarding the process that is being shadowed and escalate to Operations Support Specialists or Ops Trainers if unsure of the answer.
- Point out any training opportunities about specific topics where the New Hire may need to improve to the Ops Trainers or Supervisor, if applicable.

**New Hire responsibilities:**

- Share the screen and audio of the computer while conducting the shadowing sessions following the [Using Slack to Shadow Calls](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2337767441) guide.
- Update your console status to “Training” when you are reverse shadowing.
- Pay very close attention to the shadowing sessions and take note of anything important that the Shadow Partner may use while working on their own.
- Receive and follow the instructions and recommendations provided by the Shadow Partner when reverse shadowing is being conducted.
- Commit to trying on your own before asking for help from the Shadow Partner during reverse shadowing. The Shadow Partner is there to guide and support, but the New Hire should push themselves to do as much as possible on their own to maximize their learning.
- Ask questions to the Shadow Partner while conducting the reverse shadowing if you have any doubts regarding the next steps to take.
- Take notes about the shadowing sessions and highlight any process that is not clear and share this with the Ops Trainer or Supervisor in your daily update so it can be explained in more detail in a debrief session.

## **Phase III: Practice with Minimal Supervision**

At this point, the New Hire will have a buddy partner assigned to them. They will work on their own but they will have this buddy to reach out to in case they come across a difficult situation or need any assistance. This phase will also include reverse shadow sessions with the Ops Trainer for New hires who will be able to explain and provide more context around any questions the New Hire still has.
