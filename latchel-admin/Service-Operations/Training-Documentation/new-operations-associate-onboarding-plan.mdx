## 🧠 Purpose

<Tip>
The purpose of this SOP is to provide a structured, consistent, and efficient process for onboarding new employees. This SOP ensures that new hires are introduced to the organization’s culture, policies, tools, and workflows, enabling them to become productive, engaged, and compliant members of the team as quickly as possible.
</Tip>

## Checklist for the Training Coordinator

- **Create Onboarding Plan**

- Prepare a detailed onboarding schedule for the new hire, including training modules, shadowing sessions, SOP readings, and tasks.

- **Schedule Shadowing / Reverse Shadowing Session Invites**

- Confirm schedule of shadowing partners with supervisors (PTOs, etc)

- Set calendar invites for all shadowing sessions with assigned shadowing partners.

- Send **welcome message** to the new hire in Slack.

- Share the **onboarding plan** with the new hire.

- Share **reminders and daily checklists** for their first week.

- **Slack Channel Setup** – Add New Hire to Channels:

- #nesting-questions

- #shadowing_sessions_2025

- #service-operations

- #status_updates

- #ops-troubleshooter

- #svc-ops-requests

- #ops_info_center

- #ops_assistance_hub

- #core-values

- #core-team

- #ps-escalation

- #feature-requests

- #general

- #bug-report

- #work-order-payments

- #ask-answer

- **Send All Hands Invite**

- **Slack Posts for Onboarding** - Create a post introducing the new hire in:

- operations-supervisors

- operations_support

- shadowing_sessions_2025

- **Post Training Feedback**:

- Gather feedback from the **new hire** about their overall training experience.

- Include any **special mentions or shoutouts** to their shadowing partners.

- Gather feedback from the **shadowing partners** regarding the new hire’s performance and engagement.
