title: "How to: Check for updates on training materials"
---

This document aims to describe the step-by-step process to follow every time a Department Newsletter is issued.

##  Instructions

### To see updates in the weekly newsletter:

1. Every Friday, the Service Operations Trainer needs to go to the  [#service-operations](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C2R3MUKGT) channel on Slack to find the most recent post announcing the weekly newsletter.
2. Once there, they will open the newsletter on Confluence and read through the contents for any updates or announcements.
3. After reading the newsletter, they will acknowledge it by filling in the corresponding Google Form.
4. Finally, they will confirm if the newly added content needs to be added or updated in our training materials. To do that, the Service Operations Trainer will follow the process listed next.

### Check for updates in an SOP or Job Aid:

1. The Service Operations Trainer goes to the SOP or Job Aids listed in the newsletter.
2. Then, they will identify the update and if it impacts any existing training content.
3. If there is any impacted training content, they will go to the [Weekly Update on Training](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OJUjkMx7lNI8KxO8heGjVuOE3XuP034b0F9dH57W8M4/edit#gid=0) tracker to assess if we need to update any training materials and how to proceed in that case. In order to do that, the Service Operations Trainer will log the newsletter of the week in the **_Newsletters Screened_** tab.

<Tip>
  #### To log the newsletter of the current week in the **_Newsletters Screened_** tab:

  1. Paste the **URL** in the corresponding column.
  2. Update the **Newsletter Date** column matching the newsletter’s title.
  3. Register the location of the update by matching the **Folder**, **Course**, and **Module** columns with the location of the contact on Coassemble.
  4. In **Type of Update**, describe how impactful the update is and categorize it.
  5. In **specifics**, write a sentence describing the update made.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Types of Updates:**

  - **_Minor Update:_** Any change that can be made under 10 minutes. See examples below. 
  - **_Major Update:_** Any change that takes longer than 10 minutes and/or impacts more than one single course.  See the examples below.

  Minor Update Examples:

  1. A change in a course title - _Latchel’s Operations Essentials_ to _Latchel’s Service Operations Essentials._
  2. A change of name for a slack channel. - #ops-request to _#svc-ops-request_
  3. A change of name of a Job Aid or SOP listed in a credits screen.
  4. A minor naming update - _Operations to Service Operations._

  Major Update Examples:

  1. A new Organizational Chart was published.
  2. A new SOP or Job Aid published.
  3. An update for a process listed in a Job Aid or SOP.
  4. A new video is required.
  5. A video news to be updated/refreshed.
</Tip>

The **_Courses by Module_** tab describes the structure and contents of the courses and will help identify any updates to make. This tab is only used to list the location of the contents and the update dates and it includes the following columns:

- _Folder:_ Shows the title of the course’s folder on coassemble.
- _Course:_ The name of the course. See _Guidelines for Naming Training Materials_
- _Module:_ The name of the modules inside the courses. See _Guidelines for Naming Training Materials._
- _Last Revised on:_ The date when the materials were last updated.

<Info>
  If it's a major update, the Service Operations Trainer will notify the Q&T Team Lead should they add it to the Training Roadmap.
</Info>
