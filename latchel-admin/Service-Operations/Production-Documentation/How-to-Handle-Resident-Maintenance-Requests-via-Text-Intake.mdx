## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  This protocol ensures associates have a clear, step-by-step path for handling text-based maintenance requests from intake through WO creation, including emergency escalation.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 - **Identify the Resident & Offer Their Portal Link**

1. Search for the resident's profile using their phone number.
2. Navigate to People
3. Choose "Residents"

<Frame>
  ![image-20260604-211304.png](/images/SVC-556.png)
</Frame>

3. Search for the resident.
4. **Note**: Ensure you always tick **Show Disabled Accounts** so existing disabled resident profiles will appear in the search results. Their resident dashboard still works, so you can still provide them with the **Resident Dashboard URL** when needed.

<Frame>
  ![image-20260609-185219.png](/images/SVC-557.png)
</Frame>

4. If found (includes disabled profiles): Navigate to the resident's profile → scroll to Miscellaneous → copy their unique Dashboard URL (no login required).

<Frame>
  ![image-20260604-212137.png](/images/SVC-558.png)
</Frame>

5. If not found:
6. See Step 1b — Unrecognized Resident, below.

#### **Step 1a — Recognized Resident**

Send their unique submission link first. This is always the preferred first offer because it creates a complete, accurate work order with the least friction.

\*\*Say something like:_💬"Hi \[Name\]! You can submit your maintenance request through your personal portal — no login required. Here's the link: \[Dashboard Link\]. Just tap and follow the prompts. Let me know if you run into any trouble!"_

#### **Step 1b — Unrecognized Resident**

First, confirm the Property Manager they are associated with. Once confirmed, provide the Resident Work Order Submission URL so they can submit their maintenance request directly.

\*\*Say something like:\*\*💬_Hi! I wasn’t able to find an account associated with this phone number, so I’m unable to pull up your personal submission link right now. No worries — I can still help. Can you please confirm the Property Management Company you are associated with? Once confirmed, I can provide the Resident Work Order Submission URL so you can submit your maintenance request directly._

Be transparent that we are unable to locate their resident profile using the phone number provided, but still offer the available option to help them move forward with submitting their request.

Once the resident provided their Property Manager’s details, proceed with the below steps:

1. Navigate to the Property Management Company’s profile. Then go to **Details** tab.

<Frame>
  ![image-20260608-235944.png](/images/SVC-559.png)
</Frame>

2. Under the \*\*Portal Settings, \*\*look for **Resident Work Order Submission URL**

<Frame>
  ![image-20260609-000120.png](/images/SVC-560.png)
</Frame>

3. Provide the resident with the link:
4. \*\*Say something like:_💬"Hi! Thank you for providing the information. Here's the link: \[Resident Submission URL\]. Just tap and follow the prompts. Let me know if you run into any trouble!"_

### Step 2 -\*\* If Resident Pushes Back on the Link or Can't Use It\*\*

<Note>
  Do not only send the resident a portal link or phone number without offering further help. If they cannot or do not want to use the link, offer to continue intake by SMS or outbound call.
</Note>

Some residents will decline the link ("I don't want to click links," "I'm not near a computer," "just help me here"). That is fine — do not insist on the portal.

Offer the two alternatives:

1. **Option A** — Intake via SMS: Take the work order through the text conversation by asking the required intake questions one at a time.
2. **Option B** — Outbound call: Offer to call them and take the request over the phone.

\*\*Say something like:\*\*💬 _"No problem at all! I can either take your request right here over text, or I can give you a quick call — whichever is easier for you. What works better?"_

**SMS Intake — Required Questions**

If the resident chooses text intake, collect the following in order. Ask one at a time to keep the conversation manageable:

**Core Questions (required to submit a work order):**

1. Property address (if not already known from their profile)
2. What is the issue? Please describe what you're seeing or experiencing.
3. Are you available for a service provider to come in? If so, what days/times work best?

**Follow-Up Questions (recommended for triage & severity classification):**

4. When did it start? (e.g., it just happened vs. ongoing for a week)
5. Is there any active leak, no heat/AC, or safety concern?

Once you have enough information, create the work order using the standard incognito mode process. See: [How to Create a New Work Order from Incognito Mode (All Submissions)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions).

### Step 3 - **Create the Work Order via Incognito Mode**

<Note>
  📌 **General Rule (applies to all intake methods):**

  - If the resident stops responding, disengages, or cannot complete intake, only proceed to create a work order if you have **at minimum: a property address and a general description of the issue**. If both are present, continue with the information available — do not delay. If either is missing, do **not** create a work order. Instead, close the conversation with notes and document on the resident's profile.
  - If the situation is clearly an emergency based on what the resident has already described, enter **"Resident did not provide info"** on remaining Harmonia prompts to move through submission quickly. Speed of submission is the priority.
  - ⏳ After submitting with skipped fields, Harmonia may take longer to process. Go to the property's **Intake Sessions** and monitor — it may show **"Summary Unavailable"** at first. Allow **2–3 minutes** for it to refresh, then verify the WO outcome is correct before proceeding. Refer to FAQ section for more details.
</Note>

**Option A - SMS Intake**

Once you have the minimum intake information from Step 2 and the resident is still engaged, open Incognito Mode and begin creating the work order.

- Use the information already provided in the text conversation to answer as many Harmonia prompts as possible. Do not ask the resident to repeat details they already shared.
- If Harmonia asks for information that has not been covered yet and the resident is still actively responding, ask follow-up questions through text one at a time.

Once the available information has been completed in Harmonia, submit the work order and proceed to Step 4.

**Option B - Outbound Call Intake**

<Note>
  Before calling the resident, post in `#ops_info_center` that you are completing SMS work order intake via an outbound call. Include the WO/contact context as appropriate, but avoid unnecessary personal details.
</Note>

If the resident prefers to complete the intake over the phone, follow the steps below.

- Call the resident back using the standard outbound call process.
  - Refer to the guidelines outlined in[Job Aid: Call Flow for Outbound Calls](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-Call-Flow-for-Outbound-Calls).
- Once connected, let the resident know you are calling to continue gathering the information needed for their maintenance request.**Suggested blurb:** _“Hello \[Resident Name\], this is \[Your Name\] from your dedicated maintenance line on behalf of \[Property Management Company\] on a recorded call. I’m calling to help complete the intake for the maintenance request you started through text. I’ll just need to ask a few quick questions so we can submit the work order correctly.”_
- Complete the intake using the [standard phone intake process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions). Use any information the resident already provided through SMS so they do not need to repeat details unnecessarily.
- If the resident does not answer or puts you on hold mid-intake, follow the standard missed outbound call process and document the callback attempt.
  - Refer to [Job Aid: Call Flow for Outbound Calls](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-Call-Flow-for-Outbound-Calls).
  - Before closing out, review all information gathered (from SMS and any partial call). If anything suggests property damage, extreme habitability, or an emergency — follow Scenario C.
- Once the intake is complete, create the work order through the standard Incognito Mode process.
  - Refer to [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions).
- After the work order is submitted, confirm that it moved to the expected outcome based on the intake result and the property’s configuration. Proceed to Step 4.

### Step 4 - Confirm the Work Order Submission Outcome

After submitting the work order, confirm whether the submission was completed successfully and review the outcome shown in the Console.

- Confirm that the work order was created successfully.
- Review the work order status, severity, and any workflow prompts or next-step instructions shown after submission.
  - Refer to Step  [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions)
- Confirm that the work order moved to the expected outcome based on the intake result and the property’s configuration. For example:
  - An Emergency work order should generate the appropriate troubleshooting task for troubleshooter task group.
    - **If the WO is flagged as Emergency with a TS task** → Leave the task to the TS team. Do **not** take over the TS task. Note in the WO summary that the issue is a potential PD/EH based on assessment and that an EEC call is required.
    - **If confirmed Non-PD/EH** → Proceed to follow Step 2b of Scenario C (digital notification only — no phone call). Leave any TS tasks to the TS team.
  - A non-emergency work order should move forward based on the Property Manager’s subscription, intake settings, and standard workflow.
    - **If the WO comes out as non-emergency per intake settings but there are PD/EH indicators** → Create a TS task and let TS handle the EEC call following the [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs).
  - If the work order does not move to the expected status or workflow, review the submitted intake information and update the work order only if the correct action is clear. If the correct outcome is unclear, notify the Operations Support Specialists for guidance.
- Document any important SMS intake details that were not captured during submission, and note in the work order summary that the intake was completed through SMS for transcript review if needed.

Once the submission outcome has been confirmed, proceed based on the result:

- If the work order was created successfully and no additional action is needed, proceed with closing out the SMS conversation according to the standard workflow.
  - **Say something like:💬\*Hi \[resident name**\], thank you for contacting us. I’ve submitted your request and the appropriate team will review it shortly. You’ll receive an SMS with the next steps and any important updates. Please let us know if there’s anything else we can assist you with.\*
- If the resident stopped responding, refused to answer intake questions, or the submission could not be completed, proceed to the applicable edge-case scenario below.

### Step 5 - **Handle Edge Cases During Intake**

The following covers the edge cases that need explicit guidance to ensure consistent handling across the team.

#### Scenario A — Resident Stops Responding During SMS Intake

If the resident goes silent mid-conversation:

1. Wait 3 minutes after your last message.
2. Send a soft follow-up:
3. \*\*Say something like:_💬"Hey, just checking in! No rush at all — if you need to step away, feel free to reach back out or call us at \[hotline number\] whenever you're ready. We're here to help."_
4. Wait another 3 minutes. If still no response, proceed to close the conversation.
5. Before closing: Assess what you already know. If the resident described anything that sounds like property damage, water intrusion, no heat/AC in extreme weather, or a safety issue — do NOT simply close out. Notify the PM before ending the conversation (see Scenario C).
6. **Do you have an address AND an issue description?**

- ✅ **Yes** → Create the work order with the available information, note it as incomplete in the WO summary, then close the conversation.
- ❌ **No (missing one or both)** → Do **not** create a work order. Close the conversation and document the interaction in the resident's profile notes. If any part of what was described sounds like an emergency, property damage, or habitability concern, follow Scenario C before closing.

5. Close the conversation using the standard "[End Conversation](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Use-Text-Message-SMS/Webchat-Functions-in-Latchel-Console)" workflow and document your notes on the work order or resident profile.

#### **Scenario B — Resident Refuses to Answer Intake Questions**

Some residents will resist the intake process ("why do you need all this?", "I already told you the drain is clogged", "just send someone"). Handle this in two stages:

- **Stage 1 — Explain Why It Matters**
  - \*\*Say something like:_💬"I completely understand — I just need a couple of quick details so we can get the right person out to you and make sure your request doesn't fall through the cracks. It'll take less than a minute!"_
- **Stage 2 — Work With What You Have**
  - If the resident still won't engage, use what you do know:
  - If you have address and a general issue description, create the work order with what you have and note it as incomplete in the work order summary.
    - For Emergency work orders, Troubleshooters should escalate to the Property Manager only if the intake information is incomplete and the work order cannot move forward without Property Manager input. Refer to [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)for the proper emergency handling process.
      - If the resident provides incomplete details but there is still enough information to assess the situation and determine the correct next step, continue handling the work order based on the emergency diagnosis guidelines. Refer to [How to: Properly Diagnose Emergency Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Properly-Diagnose-Emergency-Situations).
    - For non-emergencies, put it in Pending and assign a follow-up task to the Property Manager.
  - If you don't have enough to create a WO at all (no address, no issue): let the resident know what you need as a minimum and try once more.
    - \*\*Say something like:_💬"Totally understand you're busy. The only things I absolutely need are: your property address and a quick description of what's wrong (even just a few words is fine). Everything else I can follow up on. Can you share those?"_
    - If the resident still does not provide an address and issue description after the follow-up attempt, **do not create a work order**. Document the interaction in the resident's profile and close the conversation. If anything shared prior suggests an emergency, PD, or EH — escalate per Scenario C regardless.

If the resident completely disengages or refuses any further communication:

- Document the interaction in the resident's profile notes.
- If any part of what they described sounds like an emergency, property damage, or habitability issue: escalate to PM immediately (see Scenario C).
- If it's clearly non-emergency: close the conversation and note that the resident was unresponsive to intake.

#### **Scenario C — Emergency, Property Damage, or Habitability Identified via SMS**

<Warning>
  ⚠️ Important: An incomplete text intake does not reduce your obligation to act on a known emergency. The governing policy is the [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs). The rules below are derived directly from that policy — do not deviate without guidance from Ops Support Specialists.
</Warning>

<Note>
  📋 Governing Policy: Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs ([Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)) — Troubleshooters may ONLY call the PM (via EEC) in the specific scenarios defined there. When in doubt, contact Ops Support Specialists before calling.
</Note>

**Step 1 — Classify the Issue: PD/EH or Non-PD/EH?**

The most important determination is whether the issue is Property-Damaging (PD) or Extreme Habitability (EH). This single distinction governs whether you call or go digital.

Always refer to [**How to Identify Property Damage and Extreme Habitability Situations**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations) when assessing whether the issue meets Property-Damaging or Extreme Habitability criteria.

**Step 2a — If PD/EH: Create WO and Call Down EEC List**

1. Create a work order immediately with whatever information you have, even if intake is incomplete. Severity: Emergency.
2. Do not wait for complete intake — act on what you know.
3. **Troubleshooters** assigned to action the Emergency work order must follow the standard emergency handling process, including the required steps for Emergency Escalation Contact outreach when applicable.
4. Refer to [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders) and [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
5. After calls: send an SMS to all EEC contacts who have SMS enabled.
6. _SMS to EEC (PD/EH, resident unresponsive or incomplete intake via text):_

**Step 2a — Exception: EEC Notes Say No After-Hours Contact**

If the work order falls under the scenario of EEC escalations and is being handled during after hours and the EEC notes or critical notes explicitly state that no after-hours contact is available, follow the after-hours no-contact guidelines outlined in the [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)

**Step 2b — If Non-PD/EH Emergency: Digital Notification Only (No Phone Call)**

For emergency situations that do not involve property damage or extreme habitability, the default action is digital notification only — you do not call the EEC list unless pre-dispatch or EEC instructions state otherwise. Use the digital workflow:

1. Create a work order with what you have.
2. **For Troubleshooters** follow the standard process to escalate this to PM.
3. Refer to the steps outlined in Step 1.d.4 [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders).

**Step 2c — After-Hours Rule for Non-PD/EH**

If you're handling a non-PD/EH emergency after hours (5:00 PM – 9:00 AM) and need to follow pre-dispatch instructions or call contacts, refer to the guidelines outlined in Step 1-2.c.ii.5 [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders).

### Scenario Quick Reference

Use this table to quickly confirm the correct action for common situations. Always consult the full scenario sections above for complete guidance.

| **Scenario** | **Associate Action** | **Escalate to PM?** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Resident stops responding — non-emergency** | Wait 6 min total, send follow-up. **If address \+ issue known → create WO with notes, then close. If missing either → do not create WO, close with notes only.** | No escalation |
| **Resident stops responding — PD/EH emergency** | Create WO immediately. Follow guided workflow. Call down EEC list. | **Yes — call EEC via guided workflow** |
| **Resident stops responding — non-PD/EH emergency** | Create WO. Use guided workflow digital notification (no call). **If pre-dispatch/EEC notes instruct otherwise, follow those instructions.** | No phone call — digital notification only (unless pre-dispatch/EEC notes say otherwise) |
| **Resident refuses intake — non-emergency** | Work with minimum info (address \+ issue type). Put WO in Pending with follow-up task. | No escalation |
| **Resident refuses intake — PD/EH indicators** | Create WO immediately with partial info. Follow guided workflow → call EEC. | **Yes — call EEC immediately** |
| **Resident refuses intake — non-PD/EH emergency** | Create WO with partial info. Use digital notification workflow (no call). **If pre-dispatch/EEC notes instruct otherwise, follow those instructions.** | No phone call — digital workflow (unless pre-dispatch/EEC notes say otherwise) |
| **EEC notes say no after-hours contact (any PD/EH)** | Do NOT call. Move WO to hold status. Submit a PS ticket. Document that calls were not made. | No — hold WO, submit a PS ticket, document, await PM |
| **Full intake via SMS — all info collected** | Create WO normally via incognito mode. Severity per Harmonia/intake settings. | Only if severity triggers standard escalation |
| **Resident prefers outbound call** | Place outbound call and take request over phone using standard phone intake process. | Per normal phone intake SOP |

## ❓Frequently Asked Questions:

<Accordion title="What if the submitted WO turns into a non-emergency but still has PD/EH triggers?">
  If the work order is ultimately submitted or classified as a \*\*non-emergency \*\*based on the PM Intake settings, but the resident’s text intake includes possible **property damage (PD)** or **extreme habitability (EH)** triggers, Ops associates must not ignore those triggers.

  Create a task for the **TS team** so they can review and determine whether any additional action is needed, including a potential call to the Emergency Escalation Contacts (EEC) if the situation qualifies under the PM outbound communication policy.

  **Required action:**

  1. Create the work order with the available intake details.
  2. Document the PD/EH trigger clearly in the WO notes, including what the resident said via SMS.
  3. Create a task for the TS team to review the WO for possible PD/EH handling.
  4. Include enough context in the task so TS can determine whether EEC outreach is required.

  **Example task note:** Resident submitted this request via SMS. WO is currently non-emergency, but intake included a possible PD/EH trigger: \[briefly describe trigger, e.g., active leak, water intrusion, safety concern, no heat in extreme cold\]. Please review and determine whether EEC escalation or additional troubleshooting is needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when I submit a work order using 'Resident did not provide info' on Harmonia prompts?">
  When you skip Harmonia prompts by entering **"Resident did not provide info,"** the work order may take longer than usual to process. This is expected behavior — Harmonia needs additional time to generate the summary when fields are left incomplete.

  **What to do after submitting:**

  1. Navigate to the property's **Intake Sessions** to monitor the submission.
  2. The intake session may initially display as **"Summary Unavailable"** — this is normal. Do not resubmit or assume the work order failed.

  <Frame>
    ![image-20260605-015513.png](/images/SVC-561.png)
  </Frame>

  3. Allow **2–3 minutes** for the session to refresh and fully process.
  4. If the intake session has not updated after **10 minutes**, do not resubmit through Harmonia. Instead, manually correct the work order using the **Session Outcome** option:

  <Frame>
    ![image-20260605-015703.png](/images/SVC-562.png)
  </Frame>

  <Frame>
    ![image-20260605-015724.png](/images/SVC-563.png)

  3. Refer to Q11 of this guide: [Follow Up Guidelines for On-Call Customers (app.latchel.com)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Follow-Up-Guidelines-for-On-Call-Customers-applatchelcom).
  4. Once the summary populates, review the **WO outcome** (severity, category, workflow) to confirm it is correct before proceeding to Step 4.

  ⚠️ **Important:** Submitting with limited information increases the chance that Harmonia may miscategorized the work order. Always verify the outcome before moving forward — especially for emergency submissions where incorrect severity could delay the response.
