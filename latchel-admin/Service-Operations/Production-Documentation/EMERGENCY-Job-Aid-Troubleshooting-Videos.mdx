## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent delays, misdiagnosis, and unnecessary service calls during emergencies by equipping troubleshooters with a curated library of troubleshooting videos covering the top maintenance issues residents report. The goal is to help team members quickly understand how common home systems work, accurately diagnose problems, and know when it’s safe and appropriate to guide residents through troubleshooting—or when to escalate to a professional.
</Note>

<Note>
  This document contains a collection of YouTube videos with information about the top 90% maintenance issues residents call us for help. The videos provide information about how certain house components work, instructions on how to diagnose the issue, and how to repair it.

  This resource can be used in two ways:

  1. Onboarding of new team members
  2. Research an issue before calling a resident to troubleshoot
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Process Overview

BEFORE using the videos below on actual work orders, READ the guidelines below:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1" defaultOpen>
    Some of these videos are listed to provide context and knowledge about different maintenance issues
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2" defaultOpen>
    There will be some issues that will require special tools that a resident may not have at home. If that happens, you shouldn't ask the resident to execute those steps. This information is here to help you to understand how the issue is fixed and to identify how far you can go, i.e. when a specialized service provider should take over. This will also help you to understand what they will do and what type of service provider is required
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3" defaultOpen>
    There will be some repairs that could be dangerous for a resident to do on their own, so you will NEVER ask them to do that. Pay special attention to repairs that require the person to interact with electricity, like AC repairs. We have videos with those repairs in the list mostly for your knowledge only
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4" defaultOpen>
    Some repairs may require high effort or doing things the resident may feel uncomfortable doing. Always ask the resident if she/he is ok with attempting doing that
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Video Collection

This list is organized to start with the most frequent maintenance issues we have so far encountered at Latchel.  It groups the videos based on categories. For each category, you will see common topics, links to videos, the video duration in minutes, our recommendation to watch, and notes including either a summary of the content or other tips related to that topic.

If you are onboarding to Latchel, follow the recommendation to watch the videos that are listed as required. The total time to watch the required videos is about 7 hours. The supplemental videos can be watched whenever you have some “free” time to learn more about that issue category.
