## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to prevent delays, confusion, and poor customer experiences when Spanish-speaking customers reach out via SMS, email, or phone and no bilingual associate is immediately available. The goal is to provide a clear, step-by-step process for ensuring these customers receive timely, respectful, and accurate assistance—either by quickly connecting them with a Spanish-speaking team member or, if none are available, by using approved translation tools and scripts.
</Note>

<Tip>
Before proceeding with the below steps, make sure that you have already completed the Coassemble Course on [How to Use Google Translate](https://latchel.coassemble.com/#/course/157296/overview).
</Tip>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Calls

<Note>
**What happened prior to this step?**

You are either starting your assignment on handling inbound calls or have ended a call and is receiving a new one.
</Note>

#### Process Overview

After accepting the call, you will be informed that the call is a Spanish call. Currently, we only have one phone line supporting Spanish calls, and the Spanish-speaking customer can be calling about any inquiry, including a new work order request (emergency or not emergency), follow-up on an existing work order, ask about our resident benefit program, etc.

### SMS/Email

At times, a Spanish-speaking customer may reach out via SMS or email, and ***no bilingual team members*** are available to assist immediately. In these situations, it's essential that we don't leave the customer without a response. Ensuring they receive timely assistance helps maintain a positive customer experience and prevents unnecessary delays in resolving their issue.

1. When an associate receives an SMS or email in Spanish, they must respond to the customer with:
   1. **SMS** 🗨️ Hola \[customer's name\], gracias por comunicarse con nosotros. Por favor, denos un momento mientras buscamos un asociado que hable español para asistirle.\
      (**English** *Hi \[customer's name\], thank you for reaching out. Please give us a moment while we look for an available Spanish-speaking associate to assist you.*
      1. Proceed to Step 2.
   2. **Email**
      1. Proceed to Step 2.
2. Post it in the `ops_assistance_hub` channel to check if a Spanish-speaking associate is available.
   1. Refer to [How to Escalate Between Teams](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776044545/How+to+Escalate+Between+Teams) for escalation procedures.
3. Operations Support Specialists will check for an available Spanish-speaking associate and tag them to handle the task.
   1. If no one is available for the entire day, Operations Support Specialists will instruct the associate to assist the customer using Google Translate.
4. The associate will handle the conversation using Google Translate, starting with the following disclosure:

   **"Hola \[Customer's Name\], estamos utilizando una herramienta de traducción para comunicarnos con usted. Pedimos disculpas por cualquier error y agradecemos su paciencia. ¿En qué podemos ayudarle?"**

   *(English: "Hello \[Customer's Name\], we are using a translation tool to communicate with you. We apologize for any errors and appreciate your patience. How can we assist you?")*

5. Once the conversation is complete, the associate must update the thread to confirm that communication with the customer was successful.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q: Can I use a different translation tool for these scenarios?">
**A:** Google Translate is the recommended tool for handling these situations to ensure consistency and accuracy in translations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: How should I handle a troubleshooting task or a task labeled for a Spanish-speaking customer if no bilingual team member is available?">
1. **If the issue involves property damage or extreme habitability concerns**, proceed with dispatching assistance immediately.
2. **If the issue does not pose a risk to property or habitability**:
   1. Keep the current work order status and defer the troubleshooting task to the next day when a Spanish-speaking team member is available.
   2. Send the customer an SMS:
      1. **"Hola \[Customer's Name\], gracias por comunicarse con nosotros. Enviaremos a un asociado que hable español para llamarle y ayudarle con su consulta mañana. Apreciamos su paciencia."**

         (**English:** *"Hi \[Customer's Name\], thank you for reaching out. We will have a Spanish-speaking associate call you tomorrow to assist with your concern. We appreciate your patience."*)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What should be done if a Spanish-speaking customer is mistakenly routed to the non-bilingual queue?">
**A:** Respond to the customer with the following message:

🗨️ **English:** *Hi \[customer's name\], thank you for reaching out. We will have a Spanish-speaking representative call you back to assist you.*

**Next Steps:**

1. Ensure you obtain the customer's callback number.
2. Post the request in the **ops_assistance_hub** channel to check for an available Spanish-speaking associate.
   1. Refer to [**How to Escalate Between Teams**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776044545/How+to+Escalate+Between+Teams) for proper escalation procedures.
3. Operations Support Specialists will:
   - Check for an available Spanish-speaking associate and tag them to call the customer.
   - If no one is available, hand off the request to the **next day's support team** for follow-up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if the resident is not an English or Spanish speaker?">
1. **Attempt TS Call** – Ask if there's anyone available who can speak English.
   1. If the resident cannot understand, **send an SMS in their language** (using Google Translate) asking if there's someone available who speaks English.
   2. If they respond with a contact, **call that person**.
   3. If still no response, **attempt to contact all other available residents** listed.
2. **Check other listed tenants first** – If available, try to connect with them to see if they can help communicate in English.
   1. **If no English-speaking tenant is available** – Send an SMS (or email) using Google Translate.
      1. Example message (to be translated):
      2. "Hello \[Customer's Name\]! We apologize for any errors. Is there someone available who can speak English so we can assist you"
3. **If still no response/clarity, proceed to forward the WO to PM following PM Input needed.**
</Accordion>

## 🎯 **When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
