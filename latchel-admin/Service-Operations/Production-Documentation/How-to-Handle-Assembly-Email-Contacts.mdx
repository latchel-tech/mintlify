## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that all customer and partner emails received in Assembly are handled promptly, accurately, and with clear documentation. The goal of this SOP is to provide a standardized, step-by-step process for reviewing, responding to, and documenting email requests—whether related to maintenance, feedback, or other inquiries. This process prevents missed or delayed responses, miscommunication, and unresolved issues—helping Latchel deliver consistent, high-quality support and maintain strong relationships with residents, property managers, and service providers
</Note>

<Note>
  🎓**Training Content**

  The contents of this page have been included in the \*\*Call Center Cross-Training \*\*Coassemble course.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
  For guidance on how to navigate your inbox in Assembly, refer to [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-Assembly-Guide).
</Note>

### 📬Read the Email

1. Read the email and determine what actions need to be taken
   1. If the request is **not related to a maintenance request**, proceed to [How to: Intake Non-Resident Requests (Non-maintenance Requests)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Intake-Non-Resident-Requests-Non-maintenance-Requests)
   2. If the request is related to a **maintenance request:**
      1. Review if there’s an existing WO.
         1. Refer to [Job Aid: Finding an Existing Work Order vs. When to Create a new Work Order](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Finding-an-Existing-Work-Order-vs-When-to-Create-a-new-Work-Order)
         2. If there’s an existing WO, proceed to update the WO accordingly.
         3. If there’s isn’t an exiting WO, proceed to create one
            1. Refer to [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions)

#### 🚫Not related to a Maintenance Request

<Note>
  **Handling Feedback from a Customer**

  **Why are we collecting customer feedback on their experience?** Receiving feedback from all customers is a not only an _important_ facet to Latchel Customer Obsession - it's integral to our growth! When it comes to our service and products, we improve as a team by receiving detailed feedback about what matters most to our customers and the pain points that are consistently arising for them. As Latchel COO Will Gordon reminds us, _"Let's remind ourselves to not only Think Big but to also seek nuance and detail others might miss."_

  Service Providers can now offer feedback on their experience with Latchel via their personal Service Provider Dashboard interface, which connects them to a Google Form Survey.

  1. Access the survey response.
  2. For now, follow these brief instructions.
     1. If the feedback is actionable, the agent should complete that action.
     2. If the feedback is non-actionable (and is a feature request or other), the agent may escalate the request. Refer to [How to: Use the Serv Ops Intake Workflows](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-the-Serv-Ops-Intake-Workflows)
  3. Leave a note in the Assembly ticket about what you did to handle the feedback.
  4. Mark the note as "Solved"
</Note>

1. If the request is **not related to a work order**
   1. Take action in the Latchel Console
      1. See [(Premium) How to: Handle Customer Information Updates](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Customer-Information-Updates)
      2. See [How to: Intake Non-Resident Requests (Non-maintenance Requests)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Intake-Non-Resident-Requests-Non-maintenance-Requests)
2. Respond to the email, thanking the customer for the information and summarize the action you took.
   1. If you have additional questions, include in the message.
   2. Move to '[Complete Task](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Complete-Task)'

#### 🛠️Related to a Maintenance Request

1. If the request is related to a **maintenance request**, check if there is a work order (see: [Job Aid: When to Create a New Work Order (and Find Related Existing Work Order](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-When-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-and-Find-Related-Existing-Work-Order))
   1. If not:
      1. Create a work order. (see: [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions) )
      2. Move to '[Complete Task](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Complete-Task)'
   2. If yes, determine what type of request the customer is making
      1. If they are <u>providing information previously requested</u> or<u> if they are providing other helpful information to progress with the work order:</u>
         1. Update work order with new info (e.g. update regarding status of a work order, upload invoices, upload estimates, etc.)
         2. Review tasks linked to the email request and update as needed. \*(Since those handling non-emergency and emergency emails are trained for emergencies as well.) 
            1. Take action if the email requires it (e.g., contacting a resident, coordinating with a service provider) or resolving a work order. Close tasks if the email resolves the issue, and move the work order. 
            2. If this information resolves the task, close the task and move the work order to completion (if not automated).
         3. If associates come across an instruction from the email that requires reassigning a Service Provider without a specific one stated (possibly needing to contact all preferred listed and all other service providers),
            1. **Note for LVN Service Provider**, refer to [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers).
            2. Associate should create a task and assign it to Assign Service Provider (non-emergency WOs) and Troubleshooters (emergency WOs) team for emergency requests instead.
         4. If the information generates another question, create a task to follow-up on the answer to that question. Clarify in the task to check email responses before contacting someone.
            1. Create a task (See: [Job Aid: What is a task?](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-What-is-a-task))
         5. If there are other actions required
            1. Create a task (See: [Job Aid: What is a task?](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-What-is-a-task))
         6. Respond to the email thanking for the information in the public session and summarize the action you took. If you have additional questions, include in the message.
         7. Move to '[Complete Task](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Complete-Task)'
   3. If they are requesting information about a work order:
      1. Open work order
      2. Reply with the information in the public session and move to '[Complete Task](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Complete-Task)'
         1. If the information will require further research or contacting other people, escalate to the appropriate team.
            1. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams)
            2. Reply to customer email saying we will escalate and follow-up.
            3. Move to '[Complete Task](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Complete-Task)'

### 📩Reply to the Email

1. To respond to the email, follow Steps in [How to: Respond to an Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests).

### ✅Complete Task

1. Wrap-up
   1. Copy Assembly ticket number and paste into a note in the work order with a summary of the update and actions you took [How to: Write Effective Notes](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes). Save note.
   2. Copy the Latchel work order URL and paste into the internal notes session of the Assembly ticket. This will enable to quickly link the call to the work order and facilitate future research.
   3. Change the status of the email in Assembly by marking it “closed”
   4. Close the email.

## **Handling Emails addressed to other teams or individuals**

If you encounter an email addressed to another team or individual, or an email you believe needs to be escalated to another team, you should still send a response to the customer before taking further action.

Please make sure to:

1. Acknowledge that their request was received
2. Advise that we are notifying the appropriate person or team
3. Let them know the matter is being escalated and that follow-up will be provided
   1. Refer to Steps in [How to: Respond to an Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests).

**Say something like:** “_Hi \[Customer Name\], thank you for reaching out. We’ve received your email and are forwarding your request to the appropriate person/team for review. They will follow up with you directly regarding the next steps.”_

4. After responding, proceed with the appropriate internal routing steps.
   1. Refer to the process here: [How to: Respond to an Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests) on how to reassign a ticket to other team.

## ☎️Handling External Call Center Email Follow-Up Requests

External call centers (TMP, Voicelink, or Sunshine) may send emails requesting follow-ups on work orders. When this happens, email associates must follow these steps:

1. **Review** the details in the email.
   <Frame>
     ![image-20250401-203002.png](/images/SVC-525.png)
   </Frame>
2. **Locate** the corresponding work order in Latchel.
3. Determine the **Work Order Status** and Take Action
   1. **If the work order is already "Forwarded to PM":**
      1. Create a task for the follow-up.
      2. Assign it to the PM Group.
   2. If the work order is still active (handled by Latchel):
      1. Review the order for any updates that may already address the request.
      2. Contact the resident if necessary to provide updates.
4. **Close the ticket** in Assembly, noting the action taken.
5. **Document** the request and the action taken in the work order notes.

## **📧Handling Emails from “No-Reply” or Bounced-Back Addresses (e.g., Invoices or Important Communications)**

<Note>
  **When does this apply?**

  - You receive an email from a **noreply** address (e.g., `noreply@vendor.com`)
  - The message contains important information (e.g., an invoice, service update, or request)
  - You need to respond or follow up with the customer, but cannot reply directly to the message
  - **You encounter a bounced email** — for example, when the recipient's email address is no longer in use or cannot be found.
</Note>

1. Confirm that the email contains actionable information (e.g., invoice, request, status update).

- Only the ones listed in the FAQ below are the emails that do not need a response
- Identify who the email is from (e.g., which vendor or service provider).

2. Look up the customer’s contact email on file in our system (e.g., Latchel system or previous communication).

- Refer to [How to: Navigate Service Provider Profiles](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Product-Documentation/How-to-Navigate-Service-Provider-Profiles)

3. **Create a New Email:**

- Refer to [How to: Respond to an Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests).
- Enter the **customer’s actual email address** as the recipient (from your lookup in Step 2).

4. Include a brief note to explain why you're reaching out (e.g., “This is in reference to an invoice sent from a noreply address”).
5. Send the email to the vendor.
6. Leave notes on both Assembly tickets (noreply and new email sent)  referencing the other ticket.

## ❓Frequently Asked Questions

### **What do I do if I’m no longer able to handle the email I grabbed?**

If you’re working on emails as a secondary task and your primary queue picks up and you’re no longer able to handle the email you were working on, proceed to unassign the ticket from yourself to allow another team member to grab it. Refer to [How to: Respond to an Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests).

### **What if the email looks like an automated/spam/no-reply email?**

1. In some cases, you will encounter emails where a public response is not required, such as:
   1. Spam marketing
   2. Phishing attempts
   3. Emails from an email address explicitly indicated as “No Reply”
   4. Need To Verify emails generated by Latchel.

<Note>
  **What kinds of emails do not need a response:**

  **⚠️** **General Rule: All emails listed below do not need a response AND do not need to be forwarded or escalated to any team or individual. Simply mark the ticket as "Closed" in Assembly. Place an internal note that no action is required, no notes on work orders, or follow-up needed.**

  1. Automated emails - like Latchel, most vendors also send out automated emails (i.e, appointment confirmation, survey or service feedback emails, etc.) Be careful in determining whether the email is automated or not.
  2. Direct Communication Between Customers - If a customer (Resident, PM, Owner, SP) is directly contacting another customer and Latchel is simply CC’d, no action is needed unless the email specifically asks for our intervention.
  3. Service Provider directly reaching out to Property Managers (or vice versa) and Latchel was just cc’d to the email.
  4. Service Providers, Property Managers, or Residents that are already talking or texting with another Latchel representative at the time the email was sent.
  5. Unsolicited sales or marketing emails from outside companies **—** If you receive an email from an outside company trying to sell, pitch, or promote their services to Latchel (e.g., data providers, software vendors, staffing agencies, marketing firms, "partnership" proposals):
     1. Do not reply to the email.
     2. DO not forward or escalate to any team or individual
     3. Mark the ticket as "Closed" in Assembly
     4. No notes, work order, or follow-up is needed.<br /><br />**📝 NOTE:** Do not confuse these with actual sales leads (PMs/Owners interested in using Latchel) — those still get escalated to Sales per the existing process.
  6. **Acknowledgement Emails** (e.g., thank-you emails):
     1. For positive review emails, feel free to share the customer’s exact feedback (verbatim) in the **#core-values** channel.
</Note>

### When not to send a CSAT Survey

<Note>
  The CSAT survey is **automatically included in all email replies**. Therefore, the exemptions listed below apply **only to new outbound emails** that are not sent in response to a client’s inquiry.

  **In other words:**

  - If you are replying to a client’s email, the CSAT survey will be automatically sent to customers 30 minutes after your ticket has been closed.
  - If you are sending a new outbound email or any other proactive outreach without prior client interaction, the exemptions below apply and you do **not** need to include a CSAT survey.
</Note>

1. When requesting photos during/following a call/SMS
2. When sending a follow-up/update after a call/SMS
3. Any other outbound communication in which the client didn’t respond or interact - _This exclusion does not apply to situations where the client initially reached out (e.g., via a noreply email address or any other scenario where we’re unable to directly respond in the ticket). In these cases, since the new outbound email is a response to an existing inquiry, you are still required to include a CSAT survey in the new email._

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- **If the request or situation falls outside the scope of the SOP or is not covered by the documented process**, pause and escalate to the appropriate team or supervisor for guidance rather than proceeding on your own.
- **If you are no longer able to handle the email you grabbed** (for example, if your primary queue picks up), unassign the ticket so another team member can take over.
- **If the issue escalates to property damage, extreme habitability, or urgent action is needed and the standard process would cause delays**, escalate immediately as outlined in related emergency SOPs.
