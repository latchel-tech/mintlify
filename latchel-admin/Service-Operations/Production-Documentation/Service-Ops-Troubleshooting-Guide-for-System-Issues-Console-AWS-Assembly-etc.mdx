<Note>
  We want to reduce unnecessary support escalations and improve troubleshooting efficiency for Console, AWS, or Assembly issues. The goal is to guide team members through basic steps to resolve common technical problems on their own before reaching out for help—saving time for both the requester and the support team.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Process:

### Step 1: Clear Cache and Cookies

- Go to your browser settings.
- Clear the cache and cookies.
- Refresh the window or tab where the issue is occurring.
- If this does not resolve the issue, proceed to Step 2.

### Step 2: Notify Support via Slack

- Notify the appropriate support channel on Slack (status\_updates).
- Briefly describe the issue and mention that you've already cleared your cache and cookies. Let them know you're now proceeding with a computer restart since the initial troubleshooting step didn't resolve the problem.
- Proceed to Step 3.

### Step 3: Restart Your Computer

- Restart your PC.
- After restarting, open the application again and check if the issue persists.
- If the issue continues, proceed to Step 4.

### Step 4: Switch to Backup Internet Connection

- Switch to your backup internet service provider.
- Ensure that the backup provider is different from the one currently in use.
- Try accessing the Console, AWS, or Assembly again.
- If the issue still occurs, proceed to Step 5.

### Step 5: Record and Report

- Record a Loom video clearly showing the issue as it happens.
- Provide context on what steps were attempted.
- Post the Loom link along with a brief description of the issue to the appropriate Slack channel.

**Important Notes:**

- Always follow these steps before escalating a technical issue.
- Including a Loom video significantly reduces resolution time by providing visibility into the problem.
- Use clear, professional language when describing the issue.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I don't have a backup internet provider?**

A: Note that in your Slack message, and proceed to Step 4.

**Q: Can I skip directly to posting in Slack?**

A: Only if the issue is preventing you from performing all basic troubleshooting steps (e.g., system crash or full service outage).

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should only deviate from the process in the SOP in specific, documented scenarios:

- **If the situation is not covered by the SOP or you are unsure how to proceed:** Pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance—do not proceed on your own.
