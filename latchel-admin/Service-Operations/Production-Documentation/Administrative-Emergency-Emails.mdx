## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent missed or delayed responses to urgent administrative issues by providing a clear, step-by-step process for locating, assigning, and resolving Administrative Emergency Emails in Assembly. The goal is to ensure that every emergency admin email—such as "Need to Verify" requests, voicemails, or other urgent tickets—is quickly found, properly assigned, and resolved with thorough documentation.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### How to find and assign an emergency administrative ticket to me?

1. Administrative Emails will show up in the "**Emergency Admin**" teamspace.
2. Select the "**Emergency Admin**" teamspace" from the Assembly dashboard.

  src="/images/image-13.png"

/>

3. Grab the oldest unassigned ticket.
   1. Refer to [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3208249355/Service+Ops+Assembly+Guide#Step-3---Navigating-a-ticket.) for details

### What do I do next?

1. Manually change the ticket status to **Open**.
2. Assign the ticket to yourself and **start the timer** before reviewing the ticket content.
3. Leave an **internal note** documenting the action you are performing (e.g., _"Calling the resident to verify..."_).
4. Review the content of the ticket to understand what is being requested and work on it:
   1. For Need to Verify tickets refer to [How to: Handle "Need to Verify" Emails](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/768999793/How+to+Handle+Need+to+Verify+Emails)
   2. For Voicemail tickets refer to [How to: Handle Voicemails (Emergency and Non-Emergency)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Voicemails-Emergency-and-Non-Emergency#how-to-handle-voicemails-emergency-and-non-emergency)
   3. For other tickets, refer to the individual instructions of the ticket.
5. When you have finished working on your ticket:
   1. Leave a "Private" note with a reference to the Work Order link and/or explaining the work you performed.
      1. Refer to Step 3 on [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3208249355/Service+Ops+Assembly+Guide#Step-3---Navigating-a-ticket.) for details.
   2. Close your ticket. Refer to Step 4 on [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3208249355/Service+Ops+Assembly+Guide#Step-3---Navigating-a-ticket.) for more information.
   3. **Unassign yourself** and **stop the timer**.
6. Once your current ticket has been closed, go back to the "Emergency Admin" teamspace and refresh the page to load any new tickets and take a new one..
