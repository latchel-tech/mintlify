## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
The purpose of this document is to provide instructions for Service Ops agents on collecting air filter information from residents.
</Info>

### **Background**

<Info>
Latchel offers air filter delivery through our partner Filter Time as one of our [<u>Marketplace Benefits</u>](https://latchel.com/marketplace-benefits-overview/). Marketplace Benefits are add-on enhancements to [<u>Latchel’s standard benefits offering</u>](https://latchel.com/resident-benefits-overview/) that a PM can choose to offer to their residents. Most PMs provide their properties’ air filter information directly to Latchel. However, in some cases, Latchel must collect this information directly from residents.
</Info>

**Content***

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
See video walk through [here](https://www.loom.com/share/94ae719216434ff5bf164da71a1cfb45?sid=e70b549a-3093-4ddb-8ab0-3221ba9807b9).
</Note>

1. On the 15th of each month (or next business day), the Customer Profitability Team (CPT) posts in #operations_support a list of any properties with missing air filter information.
2. Operations Support Specialists will enter their name in the spreadsheet column “Assignee” and send the following email and text message to all active residents at the properties listed in the file shared by CPT.
  1. **Email**: Use the macro “(Operations) **Collecting Air Filter Information from Residents**” in Assembly to create the email with the subject “*Request for Air Filter Measurements*”
  2. **SMS**: Use the below template when sending SMS to the residents

<Tip>
SMS Template

*Hello! We’re working with your Property Manager to provide air filter shipments directly to your door. This form explains how to submit your filter information:*[*https://latchel.typeform.com/to/ve5O9pds*](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/ve5O9pds)*. Please complete it as soon as possible. Thank you! The Latchel Team*
</Tip>

c. Make a note of the actions taken in the spreadsheet column “Service Ops Notes”.

d. Update the thread to notify CPT that you have completed the actions needed for the list.

1. On the 20th of each month (or the next business day), the CPT posts in #operations_support on which properties still haven’t provided filter information.
2. Operations Support Specialist resends the email and SMS templates from Step 2 to all active residents at the properties that still haven’t provided filter information.
3. On the 25th of each month (or next business day), the CPT posts in #operations_support linking a spreadsheet of properties that still haven’t provided filter information (see example here: [<u>April 2024 Legacy Oak filter measurements</u>](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1WH7NIDV_yR4ABP2ik3q78oHbZsiWvT6_1yczuPTRLfw/edit#gid=0)).
4. Operations Support Specialist assigns a Service Ops agent to
  1. Review[the information in Questions 1 & 2 of this help guide](https://help.latchel.com/s/article/FAQ-Air-Filters-for-Residents) to familiarize themselves with **how to find and measure air filters**
  2. Call each active resident at the properties in the spreadsheet to walk them through locating and measuring their air filters.
    1. Let the resident know they need to fill out the form that was initially sent to them. Update the spreadsheet column “Service Ops Notes” that the resident was given instructions to complete the form.

<Tip>
*Thank you for taking our call. We sent you an email and text message last week with a link to a Typeform survey that explains how to find and measure your filters, and then submit the sizes. To ensure we have all the necessary details, please complete the form. Thank you!*
</Tip>

ii. If the resident advised that they no longer have the link, resend the link via SMS. Update the spreadsheet column “Service Ops Notes” that the resident was given instructions to complete the form.

<Tip>
SMS Template

*Hello! We’re working with your Property Manager to provide air filter shipments directly to your door. This form explains how to submit your filter information:*[https://latchel.typeform.com/to/ve5O9pds](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/ve5O9pds) *. Please complete it as soon as possible. Thank you! The Latchel Team*
</Tip>

iii. If they are unable to locate and/or measure their filters, please explain why in the spreadsheet column “Service Ops Notes”.

iv. If a resident doesn’t answer, leave the following voice message and make a note of it in the spreadsheet column “Service Ops Notes”.

<Tip>
Voice Mail

*Hi [resident name]! We’re working with your Property Manager to provide air filter shipments directly to your door and we need your help measuring the filters in your home. We sent you an email and text message last week with a link to a Typeform survey that explains how to find and measure your filters, and then submit the sizes. Please complete this survey within the next 2 days. Thank you!*
</Tip>

iv. The Service Ops agent updates the assignee to their own name for tracking purposes.

c. Operations Support Specialist shall post in #cpt-updates when you’ve called all residents and made notes in the spreadsheet

## [❓](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332893/How+to+Handle+Emergency+Work+Orders)Associates Responding to Resident Inquiries FAQs

Residents may contact Latchel upon receiving our communication or if they encounter difficulties in locating or measuring their air filters. Therefore, associates are expected to familiarize themselves with the process to ensure appropriate responses when residents reach out.

**The resident responded to the SMS/Email they received regarding the air filter information request.**

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Resident wants to submit their filter information">
Q: What should I do if the resident reaches back with their air filter information?

A: Advise the resident to complete the form sent to them via the link in the SMS/Email. If they no longer have the link, provide them the link via SMS. Refer to Step 6.b.i and 6.b.ii
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Resident needs assistance in locating or determining the size of their air filter">
Q: What should I do and advise the resident if they ask assistance in locating and measuring their air filter?

A: Visit this [help guide](https://help.latchel.com/s/article/FAQ-Air-Filters-for-Residents), and walk the resident through Step 1 and Step 2.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Resident reported that they were unable to locate and provide the size of their air filter">
Q: What if the resident could not locate or measure their air filter

A: Ensure that the resident already tried the steps outlined in the help guide. If still unsuccessful, escalate to Operations Support Specialists by posting the inquiry in the #ops_associate_escalations channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Operations Support Specialist team handling the channel inquiry">
Q: What should I do if an associate posted an update about a resident having an issue locating or obtaining their air filter measures?

A: Operations Support Specialist will review the concern and ensure that the associate has already conducted the walkthrough with the resident. If the walkthrough was unsuccessful, they should collect the resident and property's information. They will then post this update in the #cpt-updates channel for further assistance.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

For residents inquiring about air filter shipment details or reports that they receive a damaged, wrong, or missing filter(s), please refer to the help guide - [FAQ Air Filters for Residents](https://help.latchel.com/s/article/FAQ-Air-Filters-for-Residents)

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
