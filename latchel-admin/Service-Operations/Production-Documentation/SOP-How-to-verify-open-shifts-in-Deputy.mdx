## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
The purpose of this document is to provide instructions on how to use Deputy to see shifts that are open and could be "claimed" to another associate, so we can avoid gaps in coverage.

This could happen when associates "offer" their shift up for someone else to grab, when they know they will be taking time off, or when we decide to proactively publish new shifts to offer overtime at times we know we will be understaffed, so we don't need to rush to find associates to help

Operations Support Specialists is the team responsible for this process, however other roles can provide support and fill in as needed
</Note>

<Note>
**Who should read this document?**

Operations Support Specialists

Supervisors
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
**What happened prior to this step?**

We have posted extra shifts in Deputy, or an associate has offered their current shift for swap, creating "open" shifts
</Note>

### 🗂️ Process Overview

1. Every start of the shift, Operations Support Specialists should verify the current coverage and gaps for their shift and remind associates that we have open spots to be claimed. This will be mostly important when we are proactively offering overtime during periods of time we know we will be busy
2. Operations Support Specialists will log into Deputy, verify open shifts and post a reminder to the teams eligible for that time slot to sign-up for it.
   1. Keep in mind that we will have some associates that have been cross-trained in multiple areas and can also claim those slots. We are starting to track cross-trained associates in [Zoltar](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1S7ybxAhNH_JIMlYyV-zFLacL2bptWb9kWTjHPiWwDik/edit#gid=1851274354), under the "Cross-Trained Associates" Tab.
3. There are 3 ways to check for open shifts:

### 1 - "Me" Tab

1. In the "Me" tab, you will see the Unfilled of Open shifts in the "Needs Attention" Section:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/SVC-590.png" alt="image-20220525-152141.png" />
</Frame>

### 2 - "Schedule" Tab

1. In the "Schedule Tab", you will select the option to "view by area". We organize "areas" by teams, but also will have "areas" dedicated to "extra time slots" posting for certain types of work.
   1. In the example below, we have posted several slots for Ops Troubleshooting. There, you can see all slots and if they have been claimed or not. If they have been claimed by an associate, you will see their name, if not, you will see the tag "open"

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/SVC-591.png" alt="image-20220525-151422.png" />
</Frame>

### 3 - "Reports" Tab

1. In the "Reports" tab, scroll down to the "Report Builder (BETA)" section and click on the "Open Shifts" button.

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/SVC-592.png" alt="image-20220525-152347.png" />
   </Frame>

2. The report will open and you can sort the data but scheduled date or area name. The slots that are still open will be showing as "True" in the Schedule Open column.

If you prefer, you can export the file to to google sheets/ excel and filter the data in a spreadsheet.

</Frame>
