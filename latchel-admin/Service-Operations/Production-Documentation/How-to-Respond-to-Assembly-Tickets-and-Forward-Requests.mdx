## **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to ensure that Assembly tickets—requests or issues submitted by associates or customers—are handled promptly, accurately, and with clear documentation. The goal of this SOP is to provide a standardized, step-by-step process for reviewing, responding to, and closing Assembly tickets. This process prevents missed or duplicate responses, miscommunication, and unresolved issues—helping Latchel deliver consistent support, reduce operational errors, and maintain high service standards for both internal teams and customers
</Note>

2. Before sending the email, update the recipient:

      1. <img

### 📨 How to: Use an email templated response/escalation

Templates help standardize responses and ticket escalations. They allow you to quickly share Work Order details with customers or internal teams handling escalations. Templates also ensure we provide consistent, customer-focused email responses.

1. While composing a new email or replying to an existing one, click the 'bracket' button at the bottom right corner of the editor.
   1. <img
        src="/images/image-110.png"
        alt="Image"
        title="Image"
        style={{ width:"61%" }}
      />
   2. You will be presented with a list of available templates. Search for and select the template you need for the ticket.
      1. <img
           src="/images/image-111.png"
           alt="Image"
           title="Image"
           style={{ width:"56%" }}
         />
      2. Once selected, the template will automatically be applied to the message body.
         1. <img
              src="/images/image-112.png"
              alt="Image"
              title="Image"
              style={{ width:"62%" }}
            />
         2. 📝 NOTE: Assembly allows the use of multiple email templates. To ensure only the required template is used, verify that the correct template is checked.
            1. <img
                 src="/images/image-113.png"
                 alt="Image"
                 title="Image"
                 style={{ width:"60%" }}
               />
2. Fill out the necessary information and make any required edits.
3. Click 'Send' to complete the process.

### 📥 Handling Emails with Subject: "New Latchel Website Form Submission"

Customers may submit a form on the Latchel website, triggering an automated check to see if the provided email matches an existing Latchel user. If a match is found, the submission is forwarded to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) for review.

1. Search for the customer in the Latchel system to verify their details.
   <img
     src="/images/SVC-223.png"

     style={{ width:"62%" }}
   />
2. Since the form does not specify the request, call the customer to confirm what assistance they need.
   1. "We received your form submission via Latchel's website. Do you have a maintenance issue we can help with."
3. Based on the conversation, follow the necessary guidelines to assist the customer.
   1. If the issue requires further action, proceed according to standard procedures.
   2. If you cannot reach the customer by phone, send a follow-up SMS or email to ensure they receive assistance.
4. Record all relevant details in:
   1. The email thread
   2. The customer's profile in the Latchel system

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
