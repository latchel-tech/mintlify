<Note>
  We want to prevent delays, resident frustration, and compliance risks when a property manager's preferred and Latchel Network service providers are unavailable by providing a clear, step-by-step process for sourcing, vetting, and assigning a qualified service provider from outside the network. The goal is to ensure that every maintenance job moves forward quickly and safely—even when we must go beyond our usual provider pool—by guiding associates through researching, contacting, and onboarding external providers, confirming credentials, and securing payment within approved budgets.
</Note>

**What is a Credit Card Sourced Service Provider?**

These Service Providers are outside of the Latchel system; sometimes, these Service Providers may have completed one job with Latchel before or the Service Provider does not want to be in the Latchel Contractor Network. Emergency Services and After Hours services often use these Service Providers. We secure and pay these Service Providers with a credit card.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

     1. Go to the Property Management Company's profile.

### Step 1 - Find a Qualified Nearby Service Provider

1. Find a Service Provider within the general vicinity of the resident's home.
2. Use an online search engine to research Service Providers for the trade category and location
   1. Research (city name)\+(job type)
   2. Research (zip code)\+(job type)
   3. Google Search and Yelp
      1. Positives to look for:
         1. Ratings and Reviews
         2. 4 stars minimum with 49\+ reviews
         3. 24-hour service is a plus as are free estimates
      2. Negatives to look for:
         1. Under 4 stars (not high quality).
         2. Exception- if you cannot source a Service Provider and it's causing property damage.
3. **How many Service Providers do I call?**
   1. _Emergency Services_
      1. Latchel services residents across the United States. Metropolitan cities will have more options for Service Providers to call than rural towns.
         1. The maintenance request will expect the agent to call up to 5 contacts.
      2. If you cannot source a Service Provider, Forward the Work Order to Property Manager.
   2. As prompted by the Latchel Console, escalate the situation by calling the property manager.
4. Proceed to Step 2

### Step 2 - Secure a Service Provider for the Job

1. Agent introduces himself or herself.
2. Calling from the property management company.
   1. Agents can avoid introducing themselves as being from "Latchel" because Service Providers are often spammed by 3rd party companies.
      1. To distinguish ourselves as different than 3rd party spammers, we tell the Service Provider that we represent the resident's property management company.
         > Say something like: "Hi, my name is (agent name) \[Preferred: on behalf of (PM Company); LCN/LVN: a maintenance coordinator on behalf of (PM Company)\]"
      2. If the Service Provider asks if we are a 3rd party,
         > Say something like: "We work directly with the Property Manager"
         1. This adds extra weight to our bargaining position.
      3. If the Service Provider asks who will handle payments, refer to [How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Set-expectations-with-Service-Providers-when-dispatching)
      4. Ask the Service Provider if they have insurance.
         > Say something like: "Do you have insurance?"
   2. Tell the Service Provider where the resident lives (the city)
   3. Tell the Service Provider the resident's maintenance request.
   4. EMERGENCY - Tell the Service Provider what you've done to troubleshoot the issue.
3. Ask if the Service Provider can go out to complete the job.
   1. If no, thank them for their time and hang up.
      1. Proceed to the next Service Provider on your list from Step 1, and return to the beginning of Step 2.
   2. If yes, ask the Service Provider for his or her soonest time available to service the property.
      1. Tell the Service Provider the resident's address.
      2. Details about payment
         <Warning>
           ‼️ IMPORTANT: Mention the Approved Budget (NTE= Not To Exceed refers to the "Approved Budget" in the Existing Work Order)
         </Warning>
         1. Ask if the Service Provider charges a Trip Charge
            <Note>
              **What's a Trip Charge?** Trip Charge - (n.) Minimum fee when the Service Provider is dispatched, even if they do not perform any work.
            </Note>
         2. Ask if the Service Provider charges a Diagnostic Fee
            <Note>
              **What's the Diagnostic Fee?** Diagnostic Fee - (n.) Fee the Service Provider charges to assess the issue. They may charge this fee even though they can't complete the work.
            </Note>
         3. Ask if the Service Provider can invoice
            1. If yes, ask the Service Provider to email [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) with the invoice.
            2. If no, proceed with "Issue a Card" procedures. See: [(TECH 101) Job Aid: Service Provider Payment (Issue a Card)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/\(TECH-101\)-Job-Aid-Service-Provider-Payment-\(Issue-a-Card\))
               1. If the Service Provider needs to be paid upfront before doing the job, you may Issue a Card up to the Approved Budget for the trip charge or diagnostic fee. We will need the service provider to submit an itemized invoice for the charge.
                  <Warning>
                    ‼️ IMPORTANT: The Service Provider must call back to reissue an additional card if the job will cost more.
                  </Warning>
               2. Prompt the Service Provider to leave a voicemail and that someone will return the call right away.
         4. Remember to NOT exceed the Hard Budget when adding the total sum of (TRIP CHARGE) \+ (DIAGNOSTIC FEE) \+ (COST FOR REPAIR)
      3. Ask the Service Provider to take before and after pictures of the job (if applicable)
         - Ask the Service Provider to upload the pictures through the Latchel platform, email to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) or send through SMS text.
      4. Ask for the Service Provider's email address OR phone number to SMS to send them the Work Order.

**Latchel Blurb**

> Say something like: Hi, my name is (agent name) \[Preferred: on behalf of (PM Company); LCN/LVN: a maintenance coordinator on behalf of (PM Company)\]. I have a resident in (resident's city) that has an issue with (maintenance request issue). We tried to (steps you took for Troubleshooting the Work Order), but it hasn't worked.. would you have a technician that can go out? When is the soonest you can go? (Give the address to the Service Provider).
>
> The "Not to Exceed budget is \$XX". If you go over this amount, please call us back at \[Please refer to [Informational: Latchel Communication (Phone Numbers)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Latchel-Communication-\(Phone-Numbers\)) for the appropriate phone number.\].
>
> Do you charge a trip charge? Do you have a diagnosis fee?
>
> Can you please take pictures, before and after? What's your email address or phone number that can receive SMS to receive the work order?
>
> Would you be willing to invoice us? ("Yes, we can invoice")--\> Great. Please send the invoice to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) ("No, we don't invoice/We need a card number upfront")--\> No problem, I have a Credit Card. Is it OK if we pay you after you've completed the work?

4. If the Service Provider ✅ agrees to take the Work Order,
   1. Proceed to Step 3
5. If the Service Provider ❌ refuses, Say,
   <Tip>
     Latchel Blurb "Thank you for your time. (hang up call)"
   </Tip>
   1. If you can still source for more Service Providers, return to the beginning of Step 2 to try sourcing another available Service Provider.
   2. If you have cannot source for any more Service Providers,
      1. EMERGENCY - Forward the Work Order to the Property Manager.
         1. Proceed to Step 5 - Write Effective Notes.
      2. Non-Emergency - Continue to call Service Providers until you can schedule any appointment with one.
         1. If you need assistance, contact your shift leader or manager.
         2. Proceed to Step 5.- Write Effective Notes

### Step 3 - Service Provider Agrees - Add a New Service Provider

1. Add a new service provider following [(TECH 101) How To: Add a New Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/\(TECH-101\)-How-To-Add-a-New-Service-Provider)
   1. Proceed to Step 4

### Step 4 - Assign the (Newly Created) Service Provider

1. Review whether the Property Manager has "Opted-in" or "Opted-out" of the Latchel Service Providers Network (see instructions at the top of this page)
2. Depending on the Work Order status, navigate to the screen to assign Service Providers.
   1. You can choose Take Action and select "Find Service Provider" or "Set a new confirmed work date"
      - To learn about changing Service Providers depending on the Work Order status (including Scheduling with Resident and Pending Statuses), see this SOP [(TECH 101) How to: Reassign a Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/\(TECH-101\)-How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider)
   2. Click "Search for Latchel Network Service Provider" to Bypass the Preferred Service Providers screen
   3. Click "Cannot Find Nearby Service Provider"
   4. A screen will appear that asks you to "Search for a Service Provider to assign this work order to"
      1. Type-in the company name (as entered in Step 3) and Click on it when you see the correct name appear in the list
         1. Details and contact information about the specific Service Provider will appear.
            1. Click "Submit" to assign the new sourced Service Provider.

               </Frame>
         2. You will also need to select the appointment date that you have discussed with the Resident and Service Provider

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You may deviate from the process only in specific, documented scenarios. Here are the main situations when deviation is allowed:

- If a Property Manager, Owner, or company leadership gives direct instructions that override the SOP (e.g., assigning a specific provider, skipping steps, or handling a job differently), follow their direction and document the exception.
- If the PM's pre-dispatch notes or account instructions require a different approach (such as not using auto-assign, contacting only certain providers, or following a unique escalation path), you must follow those instructions—even if it means not using the standard workflow.
- If you have followed the sourcing process (e.g., called all required providers, made the required number of attempts) and still cannot assign a provider, escalate to the PM or their escalation contacts as instructed. For property-damaging or extreme habitability issues, follow the escalation pathway; for other cases, create a task for the PM.
- If you encounter a situation not addressed by the SOP, or you are unsure how to proceed, pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance. Do not break the SOP on your own.
