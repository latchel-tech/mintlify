## Purpose

Show Service Operations team members what are the hardware and software requirements to perform their job duties.

## Technology Guide

### Operations Associates

Operations Team members

### Step 1 - Set up Hardware

Recommended minimum hardware requirements:

- Dual monitors with computer/laptop.
- 8GB RAM is the most important recommendation. Standard HDD is acceptable. Agents will have multiple programs/windows open at any given time, they will need sufficient RAM to handle the load.
- Enterprise-grade wired headset.
  - Wideband audio, Digital Signal Processing (DPS), noise-canceling boom mic.
  - Brands: Logitech H570e, Plantronics Blackwire series or similar.
- Webcam (built-in or external)
- Optional: Mouse (wired or wireless)

Recommended minimum internet requirements:

- **Hardwired broadband.** — What's this?
  - "Broadband" is generally defined to mean a minimum of 25 Mbps download speeds.
  - Hardwired means the PC should have an Ethernet connection to the agent's home network. Although WiFi can be robust enough for the work we are doing, there are too many variables to make it a standard practice. Typically remote workers in a production environment are required to have a hardwired connection. Additionally, hardwired connections are more secure.
- **25 Mbps download**
  - Check your download speed: [http://www.dslreports.com/speedtest](http://www.dslreports.com/speedtest)
  - Or, Agents should check their speed with their ISP provided speed test. For example: [http://speedtest.xfinity.com/](http://speedtest.xfinity.com/)

<Note>
Other speed test sites are NOT recommended as many of them are Flash based and are not robust enough to be reliable.
</Note>

- **2 Mbps upload**

### Step 3 - Set up Desktop Environment

Multiple monitors are highly recommended by veteran Operations Associates. The workload is easier to manage when you can have more desktop space by extending your view to two screens.

Agents should have an appropriate office environment. Working at a desk looking at monitors for an entire shift can take a physical toll. Ergonomics are important for the agent's well-being. A comfortable and supportive chair, properly adjusted keyboard, monitors should be at the correct height, etc.

### Step 4 - Set up Remote Work Environment

**Webcam Background:** When the Operations agent is on video camera during Latchel meetings, the background environment should be controlled (i.e. there aren't people walking behind you, non-distracting background).

**MUTE YOUR MICROPHONE, PLEASE!** Noise in your environment should be controlled; when the agent is on the phone or in a meeting with a Latchel team member, background noise should be controlled.

Agents should have their own workspace that is free of distractions. Preferably a home office with a door for privacy.
