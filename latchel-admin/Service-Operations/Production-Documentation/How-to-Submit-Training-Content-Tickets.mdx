<Note>
**Purpose of the SOP:** Provide a guide for associates to submit training content tickets for course issues, outdated content, course improvement suggestions, and any other opportunity for improvement in training content.
</Note>

## Step-by-step guide

If you encounter any issues while going through a Coassemble Training course — such as a course module not working, outdated content, or if you have a suggestion to make the course better — use the Slack Workflow **"Coassemble Course"** in the **#svc-ops-requests** channel.

1. Go to **#svc-ops-requests** channel. Click on **"Workflows"** and select **"Coassemble Course"**.

<Frame>
  ![Coassemble Course Workflow in Slack](/images/TC-6.png)
</Frame>

2. Fill out the fields:

   - **What Coassemble course are you reporting?**
     Copy and paste the course name.

   - **What is the issue/suggestion you're reporting?**
     - *Course module is not working* — The course section or unit is malfunctioning or inaccessible.
     - *Outdated content* — Information in the course is no longer current or relevant.
     - *Course improvement suggestion* — A recommendation for enhancing the course's quality or effectiveness.
     - *Other* — Any issue or feedback not covered by the above categories.

   - **What's the urgency of your request?**
     - High (I am unable to complete the course)
     - Medium (Important information is outdated)
     - Low (The course can be improved)

   - **Describe the situation you're reporting**
     Specify the issue identified in the content.

3. Click **Submit**.

This will automatically create a new request for the Training team who will look into your request and resolve it. Once your ticket has been addressed, or if more information is needed, you will receive a Slack message from the training team with an update about your ticket submission.
