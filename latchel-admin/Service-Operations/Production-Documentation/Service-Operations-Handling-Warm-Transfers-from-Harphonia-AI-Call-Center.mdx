## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure a seamless and frustration-free experience for residents and associates when calls are transferred from Harphonia (AI Call Center) to the Latchel call center team. The goal is to prevent confusion, missed information, and repeated explanations by standardizing how warm transfers are handled.
</Note>

<Note>
  **What is Harphonia?**

  Harphonia is Latchel’s AI-powered call center platform designed to handle routine maintenance intake calls from residents. It can collect caller information, understand and document maintenance issues, and answer common questions. When a situation requires human attention—such as complex requests, caller identity verification, or when a resident asks to speak with a person—Harphonia seamlessly transfers the call to a live Latchel associate, ensuring residents always have access to personalized support when needed.

  For **Harphonia intake calls**, the call recording is now visible directly in the WO in [**app.latchel.com**](http://app.latchel.com/), via a “Call Recording” button in the **Intake** tab.

  **Note:** For Spanish intake sessions, a **Translate** button is now available on the intake conversation (in both the Intake Session and Work Order views). Click it to translate the conversation to English. Click **Show Original** to return to the Spanish version.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
  This SOP applies to all Latchel call center associates who handle calls that are warm-transferred from Harphonia (AI Call Center) for our four beta customers, beginning December 22, 2025. As part of this beta launch, associates should follow these guidelines whenever a call is routed from Harphonia to ensure a smooth and consistent experience for both residents and property managers.
</Note>

### **Step 1 - Receiving a Warm Transfer**

1. When a call is transferred from Harphonia, you will receive:
   1. **Caller’s Name** (spoken and spelled)
   2. **Caller’s Phone Number** (spoken twice)
   3. **Brief Description of the Maintenance Issue**
   4. **Reason for Transfer** (e.g., caller requested a human, couldn’t identify caller, etc.)
2. Calls will come from the number: \+1(458)302-2187

### **Step 2 - Greeting and Verification**

1. Greet the caller.
2. **Say something like:** _"Hi, this is \[Your Name\], from the maintenance intake team for \[PM Company Name\]. Thank you for your patience. You’ve been transferred to a live associate so we can assist you further. I have the details you provided, but let’s quickly confirm a few things to make sure I understand your request and can help you as efficiently as possible."_

### **Step 3 - Confirm Details**

<Note>
  **Always check the transfer reason before proceeding, as this will determine whether you have caller information to confirm.**
</Note>

1. If you have the caller’s information (name, phone, issue), repeat it back and ask for clarification.
   1. **Say something like:** _“Just to confirm, I have your name as \[Name\], your phone number as \[Phone Number\], and you’re calling about \[Brief Description of Maintenance Issue\]. Is that correct, or is there anything else you’d like to add or clarify about your request?”_
2. If you do **not** have the caller’s information (i.e., transfer reason is “couldn’t identify caller”), proceed directly to Step 4.2.

### **Step 4 - Address the Reason for Transfer**

1. **If the transfer reason was “caller requested a human,”** thank them for their patience and proceed.

- **Say something like:** _"Thank you for your patience. I understand you wanted to speak with a live agent, and I’m here to help you now."_

2. **If the transfer reason was “couldn’t identify caller,”** politely verify their identity and update records as needed.

- **Say something like:** _"For security, may I please confirm your name, phone number, and the property address associated with your request? This helps us make sure we’re assisting the right person."_
- **📝 NOTE:** If you believe the caller is providing new or updated information compared to what is currently in the system, proceed to update the necessary details according to your access level.
  - Refer to the [How to Handle Customer Information Updates](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Customer-Information-Updates) SOP for guidance.

3. **If the transfer reason is “other”** (general requests, follow-up, or unclear reason), use a general response.

- **Say something like:** _"Thanks for your patience. I see your call was transferred for additional assistance. Could you please tell me a bit more about your request so I can make sure I have all the right information to help you?"_

### **Step 5 - Proceed with Standard Intake or Escalation**

1. Follow the standard intake process for maintenance requests.
   1. Refer to [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions) for standard residents
2. Follow other corresponding SOPs depending on the customer’s concern.

### **Step 6 - Documentation**

1. Place in the work order notes/call logs:
   1. That the call was a warm transfer from Harphonia
   2. All details received from the AI
   3. Any additional information gathered during the call
   4. The reason for the transfer
   5. Actions taken and outcome of your call

### **Step 7 - Closing the Call**

1. Confirm next steps with the resident and set proper expectations.

## ❓Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q: Can residents request to speak with a live agent during their call with Harphonia?">
  **A:** Yes. Residents can select the option to speak with an agent at any time during their call with Harphonia. This immediately triggers a warm transfer to a Latchel associate.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What happens if Harphonia cannot identify the resident?">
  **A:** If Harphonia cannot identify the resident, it will offer to search again or transfer the call to a live agent. In these cases, the call is warm-transferred to a Latchel associate for further assistance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Do I need to perform an immediate callback when a call that has been warm transferred from Harphonia gets disconnected?">
  **A:** No, an immediate callback is not required. Transferred calls that disconnect after reaching an agent will automatically appear in the Harphonia audits. Our CC associates should still report the disconnected call as usual in the #task-force-graham so it can be verified, but no additional immediate action is necessary.
</Accordion>
