## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent missed, delayed, or mishandled inquiries that come through the Latchel public “Contact Us” form. The goal is to ensure every Hubspot Form email—whether from existing customers, job seekers, sales prospects, or service providers—is promptly reviewed, accurately categorized, and routed to the right team for action.
</Note>

**Content**\*

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### 🗂️Overview

The Latchel public homepage has a [“Contact us” form](https://latchel.com/contact/) that allows anyone to get in contact with Latchel. As a result, we can get messages about any topic, but there are four main themes have been identified so far:

1. Existing customers requesting assistance
2. People interested in working at Latchel
3. New prospects interested in Latchel / Sales-related questions
4. Service Providers wanting to get onboarded to Latchel
5. 🚫 Unsolicited sales or marketing from outside companies (close without responding — see section below)

The email will have the following appearance:

**Subject**: It’ll state it comes from the HubSpot Form “Contact Us Form - 2023”.

**Body:** The body will contain the contact details of the person who reached out and their message.

<Note>
  All emails received through this form should be actioned unless they are clearly spam, in other words, don’t just close an email if it’s not clearly spam.
</Note>

Before taking action, use the contact details and message to investigate if it might be coming from an existing customer, meaning, a Resident, Service Provider, Property Manager, or Owner already on file on Latchel.

If the message comes from an existing customer, proceed to the section “Existing customers requesting assistance”.

### 🚫 Unsolicited Sales or Marketing from Outside Companies

Occasionally, outside companies will use the Contact Us form to pitch their services **to** Latchel (e.g., data providers, software vendors, staffing agencies, marketing firms, "partnership" proposals). These are **not** sales leads interested in becoming Latchel customers — they are companies trying to sell their products or services to us. We are not accepting those types of offers at this time.

1. Do **not** reply to the email.
2. Do **not** escalate to Sales or any other team.
3. Mark the ticket as **"Closed"** in Assembly.
4. No notes or follow-up needed.

**⚠️ Do not confuse this with a sales lead.** A sales lead is a Property Manager or Owner interested in **using** Latchel — those still get escalated to Sales per the "New Property Manager Leads / Sales-related questions" section above. An unsolicited solicitation is a company trying to **sell to** Latchel.

| ✅ Sales lead — escalate to Sales | 🚫 Unsolicited marketing — close immediately |
| --- | --- |
| _"I manage 200 rental units and I'm interested in Latchel's services."_ | _"Our company provides property data services and we'd love to discuss a partnership with your leadership team."_ |
| _"I'm a landlord with 10 properties — can someone walk me through pricing?"_ | _"We help property management companies with AI-powered marketing — can I speak with someone in charge?"_ |

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
