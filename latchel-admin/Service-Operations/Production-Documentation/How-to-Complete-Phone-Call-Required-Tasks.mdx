<Note>
  We want to prevent missed follow-ups, duplicate efforts, and stalled work orders when automated communication fails. The goal is to ensure every "Phone Call Required" task is handled promptly and thoroughly—by researching the work order, making the necessary call, and documenting the outcome—so that no customer request is left unresolved and Latchel delivers consistent, proactive service even when automation can't reach the customer
</Note>

**What is a task with "Phone Call Required"?**

Latchel relies on automated messaging to communicate with all customers. "Phone Call Required" is a special task that is created when a person cannot be reached through automated means. This typically means there is no text-able number or email address on file. This can be generated from either the Resident or the Service Provider.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

1. Research the work order status to gain an understanding of what is happening within the work order and what action is pending. This will also determine if you need to call the Resident or the Service Provider.
   1. Due to the method of generating these tasks, the work order can be in any status that requires outside input to move forward. Look at the status to see what action needs to be taken.
   2. Typically the system needs simple information from the resident or service provider or it is sending the resident a schedule update. Once this information is given, it can move into the next status. You will likely be the first contact with the resident or provider at this point.
2. Read the task and see what is needed to move forward. Find and follow the respective SOP.
3. Call the Resident or Service Provider to take action according to the SOP that addresses the action we need from them. If they don't answer the phone, leave a voicemail and follow the SOP specific to this issue to guide you on the need to create a task to make additional follow-ups

<Warning>
  ‼️ IMPORTANT: Latchel has a standard for starting a phone calls due to regulation requirements of recording the call. For information on how to make an outbound call, refer to: [↔ How to: Make Outbound Calls Within the Latchel Console](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/\(TECH-101\)-How-to-Make-Outbound-Calls-Within-the-Latchel-Console)
</Warning>

4. Update the work order with the outcome of that call and new information that you gathered from the customer.
   1. For more information on how to update customer information, see: [(PREMIUM) How to: Handle Customer Information Updates](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/TECH-101-How-to-Make-Outbound-Calls-Within-the-Latchel-Console)
5. Take effective notes following [↔ How to: Write Effective Notes](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes)

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
