<Note>
  We want to prevent missed or delayed responses to customer voicemails that are missing or initially inaccessible in Assembly. The goal is to provide a clear, step-by-step process for retrieving these voicemails—ensuring that no important communication from residents, property managers, or service providers is overlooked due to technical issues or inaccessible recordings.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

1. Open the email in Assembly that contains the voicemail.
2. Highlight and copy the voicemail URL/link.

<Frame>
  ![Voicemail URL/link in the email](/images/SVC-160.png)
</Frame>

3. Open the URL in a new browser tab.

<Frame>
  ![Voicemail URL opened in a new tab](/images/SVC-161.png)
</Frame>

4. Copy the SID reference number, which usually begins with "RE".
5. Open the voicemail tracking spreadsheet and search for the SID reference number.
6. Once found, copy the corresponding Call SID from Column C.

<Frame>
  ![Call SID in Column C](/images/SVC-162.png)
</Frame>

7. Go to the Twilio Drive to search for the Call SID. Navigate through the folders:
   1. Click on the year the call was made.
   <Frame>
     ![Selecting the year folder](/images/SVC-163.png)
   </Frame>
   2. Select the month.
   <Frame>
     ![Selecting the month folder](/images/SVC-164.png)
   </Frame>
   3. Then choose the date.
   <Frame>
     ![Selecting the date folder](/images/SVC-165.png)
   </Frame>
8. Locate the recording using the Call SID.

<Frame>
  ![Locating the recording using the Call SID](/images/SVC-166.png)
</Frame>

9. Double-click the file to play the voicemail.
