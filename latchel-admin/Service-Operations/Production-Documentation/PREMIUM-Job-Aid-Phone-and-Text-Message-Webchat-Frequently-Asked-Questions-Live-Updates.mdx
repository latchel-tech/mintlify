## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent inconsistent, incomplete, or incorrect responses when associates handle common questions via phone, text message, or webchat. The goal is to provide a comprehensive, regularly updated FAQ resource with clear scripts and guidance—so associates can quickly identify scenarios, give accurate answers, and know when to escalate or reference other SOPs.
</Note>

<Note>
  **🎓Training Content**

  The contents of this page have been included in the \*\*Call Center Cross-Training \*\*Coassemble course.
</Note>

<Note>
  **What is happening prior to this process?**

  The Associate has received a correspondence from a customer through a text message, Webchat, or phone call. The Associate is responsible for determining whether or not a simple answer or action can be taken or if it needs to be escalated.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **Advance the Work Order**

  Anytime you indicate that you are taking an action for a customer and/or Work Order, it is imperative to follow up by completing that action and writing effective notes about your actions.

  For more information on what you should or should not communicate to customers, review [↔ A Letter from the CEO: Customer Obsession](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/a-letter-from-the-ceo-customer-obsession).
</Danger>

<Note>
  **Operation Hours for Text Message/Webchat**

  Review guidelines for how to handle text messages/Webchat messages outside of Operations Hours. [How to: Handle Text Message/Webchat Contacts (in Latchel Console)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-handle-sms-webchat-contacts-new-support-framework)

## **<u>Resident FAQ's</u>**

### **Q. The Work Order is incomprehensible; the resident has submitted a "gibberish" Work Order**

Check the Work Order Notes to see if there is any other Associate history on the Work Order.

1. If another Associate has already sent messages to try to contact the resident regarding the Work Order, and it has already passed 24-hours (and the resident has not responded to messages from Latchel),
   1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). We haven't heard back from you in regards to the work order you recently submitted. Please call your hotline if you need to create a new request._
2. If this is the first time that a Non-Emergency Associate comes across the Work Order that is incomprehensible, then send a text to the resident:
   1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). You recently sent in a maintenance request, but we need more detail from you to move forward. You said (insert jibberish description from customer).Can you please explain what is going on and include pictures for me? We need this before moving your request forward._
   2. Move work order into "Pending" with a note: "Requested a better description from the tenant of the issue"
      1. Click "Put this job on hold"
         1. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)
         2. "Please summarize why this action was taken"
            1. Write✍: "Requested a better description from the tenant of the issue"
         3. "Select the contact to assign the follow-up task"
            1. Select the Resident as the contact
         4. "Please set a date and time for the follow-up task"
            1. Set the follow up task to 24 hours if the tenant has not responded.
         5. "Please summarize the next steps to be taken"
            1. Write✍: "Transferring to the property manager if the resident is unresponsive after 24 hours"
               1. For more information see: [(Premium) How to: Transfer the Work Order to a Tier 2 \[T2\], Tier 3 ](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-escalate-between-teams)[T3](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=LTG&title=T3)[ or Transfer to Property Management \[ TPM \]](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-escalate-between-teams)

### **Q: "Can I get the phone number to contact the Service Provider please?"**

1. **Say something like**: _No problem! I'll get that for you. (Relay phone number to the Resident)_
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Navigate to the Existing Work Order Interface
      2. Locate the Service Provider's phone number and give it to the Resident.
         1. [How to: Navigate the User Work Summary Page Interface](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-navigate-the-user-work-summary-page-interface)

### **Q: If a resident replies to an automatic text message stating the work has been completed.**

1. **Say something like**: Thank you for letting us know. I will update that this work order has been completed.
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Update the work order and place it into the "Completed" status. Click into "Action" and select" Complete"
         1. For more information, see: [How to: Confirm Completion (Emergency and Non-Emergency)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-confirm-completion-emergency-and-non-emergency)

### **Q: If a resident wants to submit a work order with you through the text message...**

**Step 1 — Look up the resident and send their personal submission link:**

- **Say something like**: _"Hi \[Name\]! You can submit your maintenance request through your personal portal — no login required. Here's your link: \[Dashboard Link from Miscellaneous in resident profile\]. Just tap and follow the prompts!"_

**Step 2 — If resident pushes back or can't use the link:**

- **Say something like**: _"No problem at all! I can take your request right here over text, or I can give you a quick call — whatever's easier for you. Which do you prefer?"_

If via **SMS**: Ask required intake questions one at a time (address, issue description, urgency, safety concerns, availability) and create the WO via incognito mode.

If via **call**: Place an outbound call and take the request using the standard phone intake process.

If the resident asks how to submit the work order in their portal, refer the resident to this page: [/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wrGAA/How-can-a-Resident-Submit-a-Maintenance-Request](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wrGAA/How-can-a-Resident-Submit-a-Maintenance-Request)

### **Q: If you are setting availability with a resident**

1. Request for the resident's availability using the below Blurb, which is targeted to making collecting availability easier.
   1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). In order for us to schedule appointment with the service provider, kindly provide 3 dates of your availability between Monday through Friday, for the time frame of 8am-12pm or 12pm-4pm._
      1. The Associate will then:
         1. Update the resident availability via "Select Custom Time Slot"
         2. Educate the resident about the convenience of using their Latchel Interface for future scheduling needs and how to update availability there.

### **Q: "The tech did not show up to my appointment!"**

1. **Say something like**: _We apologize for the inconvenience.  We will reach out to the service provider for an update and reach out to you shortly_.
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Follow up with service provider via SMS/phone.
         1. See: [Job Aid: Solving Appointment or Issue Resolution Disputes Between Resident and Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/job-aid-solving-appointment-or-issue-resolution-disputes-between-resident-and-service-provider)

### **Q**: "**I wanted to follow up on the status of my (issues) being repaired?"**

1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). Absolutely! Let me check on the status of that for you!_
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Search in the Latchel Console for the work order
      2. Verify the status of the work order
      3. Determine if there is any action that could be taken
      4. Update the requester with the status
   2. **<u>Related:</u>**<u> </u>The Property Manager has already 🔐closed the resident's Work Order when the resident checks on its status.
      1. **Say something like**: _Hi (name if applicable). Please reach out to your property manager for an update on (issue) repair as they have closed out work order as complete._

### **Q**: **"Can tech come today to repair my (issue)?"**

1. **Say something like**: _Unfortunately we cannot promise same day service, however we will notify the service provider of your availability today.  If our service provider cannot go today, can we get some additional times you may be available?_
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Update the Resident’s availability.
      2. No need to contact the Service Provider as the system will automatically notify the provider as the job will move into "Scheduling with Service Provider"

### **Q**: **"I’ve been waiting for over 3 weeks for someone to come out and handle this (issue)."**

1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). I apologize for the delay. We will reach out to (property manager/service provider) to get this (issue) resolved for you soon._
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Search for the work order in the Latchel console
      2. Identify the reason the work order is blocked
      3. Take the appropriate next steps to resolve the issue or escalate

### **Q**: **"Do the technicians work on weekends?"**

1. **Say something like**: _Unfortunately, most of our service providers work Monday to Friday between 8am and 5pm. We can note your availability or preferences, but we cannot guarantee weekend service._

### **Q**: **"Can tech call before they arrive?"**

1. **Say something like**: _We will contact and instruct the service provider to call before arrival. Please be advised, we cannot guarantee they will call. Please let us know if the service provider meets your expectations once the work is complete._
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Write a Note on the account with the request
      2. Send SMS to provider with the request

### **Q: "I no longer live at that Property Address"**

1. **Say something like**: _Hi (customer first name). Thank you for letting us know. We will confirm with the property manager and remove your information from our system._
   1. The Associate will then:
      1. Disable the resident
         1. [How to: Handle Customer Information Updates](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-handle-customer-information-updates).

### **Q: The Resident has questions about the Home Assistant Program**

1. Please check this SOP: [How to: Handle Home Assistant and Benefits Inquiries](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/how-to-handle-home-assistant-and-benefits-inquiries)
