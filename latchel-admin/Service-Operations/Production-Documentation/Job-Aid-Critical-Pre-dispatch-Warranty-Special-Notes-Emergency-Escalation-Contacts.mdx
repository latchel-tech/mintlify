## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent missed or incorrect actions by ensuring associates always review and follow critical notes, pre-dispatch instructions, and special notes on work orders. The goal is to catch essential details, avoid conflicting directions, and escalate unclear or conflicting information—so that every work order is handled according to the property manager’s or client’s specific requirements.
</Note>

### **Pre-Dispatch Instructions**

<Warning>
  For PMs with On-Call rotations schedule on their pre-dispatch or EEC list, refer to the guidelines in [Service Ops: How to Use Contact Rotations (EEC + Pre‑Dispatch)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-How-to-Use-Contact-Rotations-EEC-Pre-Dispatch).
</Warning>

<Warning>
  📌 **After-Hours Note:** For non-PD/EH emergencies during after-hours (5:00 PM – 9:00 AM), **before contacting anyone listed in pre-dispatch or EEC instructions**, you must first ask the resident whether the issue can wait. If the resident agrees to wait, defer the pre-dispatch contact calls to business hours. **This after-hours exception applies only to contacting people — other pre-dispatch instructions (e.g., handling steps, troubleshooting guidance) should still be followed immediately regardless of time of day.** This does not apply to true PD or EH emergencies
</Warning>

**✅ How to use Pre‑Dispatch Instructions**

- **Review pre-dispatch instructions as part of the work order review process, especially before troubleshooting with the resident.**
- Pre-dispatch instructions are **not limited to dispatching or only specific exemptions/scenarios**. Always check them, and if a pre-dispatch note applies to the current situation or action being taken, follow it as written.
- If a pre-dispatch note applies to the current work order situation, follow it even if the action is not related to dispatching.

### Emergency Escalation Contacts

<Warning>
  For PMs with On-Call rotations schedule on their pre-dispatch or EEC list, refer to the guidelines in [Service Ops: How to Use Contact Rotations (EEC + Pre‑Dispatch)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-How-to-Use-Contact-Rotations-EEC-Pre-Dispatch).
</Warning>

The EEC list are the specific people listed under the “Emergency Escalation Contacts List” section for the work order’s Property, the Property’s Portfolio, or the Property Management company. They are designated by the Property Management company to be called **_in order_**, during emergencies, when escalation is required. Associates must review EEC notes as part of the work order review process, especially before troubleshooting with the resident. EEC instructions are **not limited to only previously listed exemptions or PD/EH situations**; if an EEC note applies to the current emergency or work order situation, follow it as written.

> 🧩 **How to use Emergency Escalation Contacts (EEC)**
>
> - Use the EEC list to determine **who** to call, and in **what order**, when escalation is required.
>   - Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
> - The "**Who Ya Gonna Call**" tool now provides an AI suggestion for the correct contact and call order based on time of day and scenario. Associates should review the AI recommendation first, while also verifying the information against the original escalation instructions displayed below.
>   - If the AI recommendation conflicts with the original escalation instructions, please submit a bug report so the discrepancy can be reviewed.
> - Always check for any **EEC‑specific notes** that change _when_ or _why_ you call EEC.
> - In those cases, the EEC‑related instruction can **override the default escalation SOP**.

### Warranty Information

<Note>
  Property Manager help guide: [https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Add-Warranty-Information](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/How-to-Add-Warranty-Information).
</Note>

#### General Rules for Warranty

- All non-emergency work orders for the property will be sent to the Property Manager for approval before dispatching begins.
- This setting **does not** apply to **emergency situations**—Latchel will continue to dispatch emergencies as needed, even if the warranty note includes owner preferences such as contacting the owner first or using the owner’s contractor. Additionally, Latchel **does not** **coordinate** with **Home Warranty companies**.
  - In the case of an emergency at a property covered by a Home Warranty, Latchel will attempt to resolve the issue directly.
    - Refer to [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders).
- If it can be de-escalated, the system will automatically place the WO in Pending with a task to PM to review warranty. Ops should not move the WO forward or override this status. For full steps on handling de-escalated emergency WOs with warranty mismatches, see [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders).

## [❓](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q1. What to Do if There Are Conflicting Details Between Critical Notes and Pre-Dispatch Notes:">
  **Answer.** Property Managers typically have greater visibility into pre-dispatch notes, making these notes more likely to be up-to-date compared to critical notes. **When pre-dispatch instructions and critical notes conflict, pre-dispatch instructions take precedence whenever they are applicable to your current action** — this is not limited to dispatch-related actions.

  1. **If a pre-dispatch note applies to your current situation or action, follow it as written** — this is not limited to dispatching.
  2. **If a pre-dispatch note applies to your current situation or action, follow it as written** — this is not limited to dispatching.
  3. Refer to: [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)
  4. If a pre-dispatch note references EEC actions (e.g., "call EEC for all emergencies" or "if resident is unresponsive, call EEC"), follow the instruction as written.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q2. What to Do if Information is Vague or if an Update/Request to Add or Modify Critical or Pre-Dispatch Notes is Received:">
  **Answer**. Operations associates no longer have access to add critical notes or pre-dispatch notes to the work order. If you receive a request or notice that a critical note or pre-dispatch note is vague, escalate the issue to Product Support.

  1. For detailed instructions on how to escalate, refer to the guidelines here: [How to Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q3. Can I provide the Property Manager’s phone number or information if the critical notes/pre-dispatch instructions state to share it with other customers?">
  **Answer. **Yes, you may provide this information as long as it is explicitly stated in the critical notes or pre-dispatch instructions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q4. For PD/EH situations, if the critical notes/pre-dispatch instructions state not to call the escalation contact, should I follow this or our policy?">
  **Answer**. You must follow the critical notes/pre-dispatch instructions. If they state **not** to call the escalation contact, do not call them, even for PD/EH situations.

  For additional action, send the PM an SMS about the situation since we have not called them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q5. How should we handle critical notes or pre-dispatch instructions when they conflict with standard procedures, such as recall work order guidelines?">
  **Answer:**

  **_Always check the critical notes and pre-dispatch instructions before taking any action on a work order._**<br />Critical notes always take precedence over general SOPs and Job Aids, including the recall Work Order guidelines. Pre-dispatch instructions take precedence over critical notes.

  If a work order—recall or otherwise—includes specific instructions from the Property Manager or client, such as a direction to call the Emergency Escalation Contacts before dispatch or other instructions, you must follow those instructions exactly as written. This applies even if those instructions differ from the typical steps outlined in recall handling procedures.

  **In short:**<br />➡️ Follow critical/pre-dispatch notes first, **when applicable to your task**.

  Example: If you are working on dispatching a Service Provider for a recall work order and there is a critical note that says “Call the PM before dispatching,” you must follow that instruction—even if there are no pre-dispatch instructions—because it directly applies to the action you're taking.

  This ensures we're honoring PM/client directions and delivering service that aligns with their expectations.

  **After-Hours Exception:** For non-PD/EH emergencies handled after hours (5:00 PM – 9:00 AM), the timing of when you follow pre-dispatch or EEC instructions may be adjusted. Before acting on those instructions, you must first ask the resident whether the issue can wait. If the resident agrees to wait, defer the pre-dispatch/EEC contact calls to business hours and select "Work order does not need immediate dispatch." The instructions themselves still apply — only the timing changes. This exception does not apply to true PD or EH emergencies, which always follow immediate dispatch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q7: What should I do if an emergency WO has a warranty note saying to contact the owner for all maintenance or that the owner may use their own contractor?">
  **Answer.** Follow the standard emergency process and proceed with dispatch per the existing emergency SOP. Emergency work orders are handled directly by Latchel regardless of owner or contractor preference notes in the warranty information.

  1. **Do not stop or delay emergency dispatch** because of the warranty note.
  2. **Submit a Product Support (PS) ticket** to verify whether the PM/Owner intends the preference to apply to emergencies as well, so the note can be moved to pre-dispatch notes if needed, or whether it should only apply to non-emergency maintenance.
  3. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q8:  What is the correct status for a Work Order when a service provider needs to verify warranty details with the PM/owner?">
  - If a service provider needs to confirm warranty details with the property manager/owner, create a task following the template for **“[Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates)”** notification. This is sufficient and **does not require updating the Work Order (WO) status to “Waiting for PM.”**
  - If the PM/owner confirms that they will **handle the repair through their warranty provider**, the WO may then be placed in **“Waiting for PM”** status. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)  for guidance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q9: What is the 'Who Ya Gonna Call' button?">
  A: This is an AI-powered tool that analyzes the EEC instructions, current time/day, and scenario to suggest the correct contact order. It **auto-populates** in guided TS workflow for PD/EH; **manually accessed** via Action Menu, PM Company, or Property page for all other scenarios
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should only deviate from the process outlined in the SOP in the following situations:

- **If you encounter a scenario not covered by the SOP or the notes, or you are unsure how to proceed:** Escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance—do not proceed on your own.
