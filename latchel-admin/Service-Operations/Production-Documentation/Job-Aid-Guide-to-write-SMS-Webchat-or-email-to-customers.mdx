## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent miscommunication, unclear messaging, and customer frustration caused by poorly written SMS, webchat, or email communications. The goal is to ensure every message sent to customers is clear, professional, and accurate—so customers receive the right information the first time, trust our service, and experience smooth, effective interactions with Latchel.
</Note>

### \*\*How email communication should be different than SMS/Webchat? \*\*

#### **<u>SMS/Webchat</u>**

Refer to [How to: Handle SMS/Webchat Contacts (New Support Framework)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-SMS/Webchat-Contacts-New-Support-Framework)  for additional information on SMS/Webchats.

**SMS/webchat** are usually used when customers are looking for fast communication, so we should not only be quick to reply, but also be \*\*concise \*\*and focus on the **key information** or data points needed to resolve the situation.  In SMS/webchat conversations, we will be direct to the point but still be mindful that we are being **polite**, so always say “**please**” when you are asking for something and “**thank you”** when the customer provide an information, answer your question, or take an action.

If you are reaching out to a customer via SMS (outbound contact), instead of answering a question from them (inbound contact), be mindful to provide more information on what work order you are asking about and why you are reaching out to them. Keep in mind they may not even know that work order exists, so provide the appropriate context before jumping into asking questions.

Also, if a customer reached out with a question, after you answered it, **ask if there is anything else you can help with**.

**_For example:_**

“ Hello! In order to schedule your maintenance appointment for (_ISSUE_), please let us know your top 3 available dates and times from Monday to Friday between 8 AM to 12 PM and 12 PM to 4 PM. Don’t worry, if your schedule changes last minute, you can always update your availability via our online portal (_LINK)_. Please let us know if you have any questions. Thank you!”

“ Hello! Please submit all maintenance requests through your resident portal (_LINK_), or contact the maintenance hotline provided by your property manager (Phone number). This way we can collect more details on your maintenance issue and dispatch the right service provider.  Is there anything else I can assist you with today?”

**Note**: If the person you’re trying to text displays a warning icon next to the latest message sent, you need to contact the person via phone call instead. Messages to this contact won’t be delivered. If they don’t answer and have no option to leave a message, **send them an email**. Refer to [Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules)

#### **<u>Email</u>**

In \*\*email \*\*communications, customers may not be expecting a fast response and may also need some type of reminder on the context of the situation. Therefore, while writing emails, assess if you should provide a summary of the situation as you answer the question, and if more details should be provided to help the customer understand what is going on. Those details could be, for example:

- Name of the resident, service provider, or PM that was involved
- Property Address
- Date the resident contacted us, or other key events happened
- Issue - what happened, when, where, severity / damage
- What we have done so far, what is going on now, what is happening next
- Cost of the repair, breakdown to show why it is adding up to that number (itemized cost)

**_For example:_**

_“Hello (PM Name)_

_The resident (name) from property (address) have contacted us on (date) requesting (issue). We have dispatched (service provider name) on (date) who assessed the situation and provided an estimate cost to repair of (_$$ value). The cost is ($$ _value) above your budget, so I am reaching out for approval. See below the cost details provided:_

_(1) remove toilet and unclog plumbing - \$ value_

_(2) replace with new toilet - \$value_

_(3) dispose of old toilet and clean - \$value_

\*Please reply to this message with your decision or questions. \*

_Thank you!_

Latchel Maintenance Team  / Latchel Customer Success Team,

Visit our page for other helpful instructions

[https://help.latchel.com/s/](https://help.latchel.com/s/)

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
