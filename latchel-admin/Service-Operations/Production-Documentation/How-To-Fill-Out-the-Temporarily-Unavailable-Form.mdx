## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  This process makes sure the Temporarily Unavailable Form is filled out correctly so the data is reliable. The information is used by leadership to track attendance, identify if attendance impacts team metrics, and determine if an associate may need to be placed on a PIP. Accurate entries help ensure fair evaluation, better planning, and clear visibility into team performance.
</Info>

<Info>
  ## **Important Notes**

  - **Make sure** to check your available **Sick Leave credits** before filling in the minutes.
  - You can **combine** Sick Leave, PTO, and Unpaid Leave in the form if needed.
  - This form must be completed whenever you are unavailable for your shift.
  - Don’t forget to file it in Deputy as well.
  - Follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

1. Go to the **#status_updates** channel.
2. At the top, click the **Workflows** tab.

<Frame>
  ![image-20250825-205145.png](/images/SVC-72.png)
</Frame>

3. Find _Temporarily Unavailable_ and click the **play button** next to it.

<Frame>
  ![image-20250825-205500.png](/images/SVC-73.png)
</Frame>

4. From the drop-down, select **“What is causing you to miss work?”**
   1. If you choose **“Not Feeling Well”** or **“Other”**, make sure to provide detailed information in the next field.

5. Enter the **date** you were or will be away.
6. Complete the remaining section by following the detailed instructions below:

### **Form Sections & Instructions**

#### **Sick Leave (SL) Minutes**

- **Specify the exact number of minutes** you want to apply from your **Sick Leave** credits.
- **Important**: If you are feeling unwell or need to care for a sick relative, **and you have Sick Leave credits**, then **Sick Leave credits** should be used. Only fill in the minutes if you have **enough Sick Leave credits** at that very moment.

**_Note:_** If you don't have enough Sick Leave credits, proceed to fill out the PTO or Unpaid Leave fields accordingly.

#### **PTO Minutes**

- If you don't have sufficient Sick Leave credits, **but** you have/will provide a **doctor's note** or any verification to your supervisor, you may apply your **PTO minutes** as well.
- **Specify the number of PTO minutes** you'd like to apply.

#### **Unpaid Leave Minutes**

- If you don't have enough **Sick Leave credits** and cannot provide a doctor's note, any hours that cannot be covered by Sick Leave will need to be tagged as **Unpaid Leave**.
- Whenever you're unavailable for work due to **Power Outages, Internet Connectivity Issues, or Device/System Failures**, please note that these events will be **tagged as Unpaid Leave**.
- **Specify the number of Unpaid Leave minutes** in this section.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- **Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to your supervisor or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
