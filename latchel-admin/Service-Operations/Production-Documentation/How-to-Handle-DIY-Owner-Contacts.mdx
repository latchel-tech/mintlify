## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that contacts from DIY property owners are handled accurately, securely, and in line with both Latchel’s and our partners’ requirements. The goal of this SOP is to provide clear, step-by-step guidance for verifying ownership, documenting requests, and escalating issues when needed—especially for properties managed through platforms like NOMAD and RentRedi.
</Note>

## 📝Handling Tasks and Follow-Up for Rentredi and NOMAD Related Work Orders

<Warning>
  **Effective May 07, 2025, we no longer send automated emails to Owners when a work order is moved to 'Pending' status. If input from the Owner is genuinely needed due to this status change, please reach out to them directly. If they are unresponsive, follow up with an SMS or email that clearly explains why their input is required.**
</Warning>

Owners, residents, and service providers using Rentredi and NOMAD often reach out for assistance with their work orders. When handling these requests or tasks, it’s crucial for our associates to ensure proper follow-through. Below is a step-by-step procedure for managing these requests.

<Warning>
  For Property Damaging and Extreme Habitability Situations

  Always make **3** call attempts on a **_single follow-up_** with the residents/service providers.
</Warning>

<Note>
  - Non-emergency tasks will be deferred to the next business day, while emergency tasks will be deferred to the next day.
  - All work order updates and the outcomes of SP/Resident communications must be communicated to the owner.
</Note>

Below actions are applicable for Emergency (Non-Property Damaging and Non-Extreme Habitability Situations and Non-emergencies)

### **Scenario 1. Straight-forward requests from the owner (e.g., updating owner approval, canceling a work order, marking a work order as completed, etc.), including property owner instructions documented in critical notes, work order notes, or pre-dispatch instructions.**

1. _Hello \[Owner’s name\]! Thanks for reaching out._ _I have \[state the action you performed to address the owner's request\]. If you need further assistance, please feel free to text us at 206-717-8374. Thank you and have a great day._
2. Update and move the work order forward

<Warning>
  **Note – When owner instructions are vague or left via voicemail/message**:
</Warning>

- If the owner leaves a **message** with **vague or incomplete instructions** (e.g., “please move this sooner,” “please prioritize this,” “please handle this urgently”) and it’s not fully clear what they want you to do:
  - **Call the owner to verify**
    - Clarify exactly **what they want changed** (timing, budget, severity, vendor, etc.).
      - Once clarified, **proceed according to Scenario 1**:
        - Take the action, update and move the work order forward,
        - Document their instruction clearly in the WO.
    - **If the owner is unresponsive**
      - Leave a voicemail and/or send SMS/email summarizing:
        - What they requested in their original message (as best as you understand it), and
        - What clarification you need to proceed.
        - Then follow the applicable existing steps in this SOP based on what you were trying to do:
          - If you were trying to **update / move the WO forward** (Scenario 1),
            - Document that you attempted to reach the owner and are **waiting on their confirmation**.
          - if their input is required to proceed, **keep the WO in Pending** and set a follow‑up task per the “Handling Tasks and Follow‑Up” section.
          - If you were trying to **follow up with a resident/SP on the owner’s behalf** (Scenario 2),
      - Follow the same “unsuccessful owner contact” pattern already described in Scenario 2 (send SMS/email, defer task, then complete once follow‑ups are done).

  We may provide an estimate with the property owner, but we do not track, collect, or enforce estimates for Preferred Service Providers. Any received estimate should be forwarded to the property owner for approval.

#### **🔔NOMAD Owners - Approval Process Reminder**

NOMAD owners typically receive an email notification when an estimate is submitted. In most cases, they will review and either approve or decline the request. When they approve through their NOMAD portal, an automated note will appear on the work order stating that the owner has approved the estimate.

<Warning>
  Please keep in mind:<br />The **budget approval must be processed _after_ the budget increase request**, as this ensures the approval corresponds specifically to the submitted estimate.
</Warning>

Here’s how to proceed:

1. **If there is a note indicating that the owner has already approved the estimate via the NOMAD portal _and_ the approved budget has been updated**, you may proceed with scheduling.
2. **If there is a note showing the owner’s approval, but the approved budget has _not_ been updated:**
3. Check the **Files** section or **Notes** on the work order to review the submitted estimates.
4. **If the approved amount is certain** (for example, there is only one estimate that clearly aligns with the approval), update the approved budget accordingly and proceed with scheduling.
5. **If the approved amount is unclear** (for example, if there are multiple estimates submitted or if there was a previous approval that may cause confusion), reach out to the owner for clarification before proceeding.

## 📝Handling Work Orders When a Property Becomes Disabled Mid-Process

There may be instances where the Ops team is actively managing a work order, and the associated property becomes disabled partway through. This raises questions about how to proceed with the task and ensure proper handling. The guidance below aims to clarify the appropriate next steps in such cases. See:  [https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-DIY-Owner-Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-DIY-Owner-Contacts)

<Note>
  Some properties managed by NOMAD may **no longer be active with them or may be in poor standing,** which makes verification necessary.

  A property may also become **disabled when no resident is listed in the system**. In such cases, associates must first **verify the property status and determine whether the property is vacant or occupied** before proceeding with any further actions.
</Note>

1. Determine the \*\*property status:  \*\*
2. Vacant Property – \*\*Owner \*\*Reached Out
3. Point the owner back to NOMAD.
4. Create a PS ticket.
5. \*\*Resident \*\*Reached out for Vacant Property
6. Call the owner to relay the potential tenant’s concern and verify the property status.
7. Route the owner back to NOMAD.
8. Create a PS ticket.
9. If the property became disabled with \*\*listed residents: \*\*
10. **If the issue _is_ property-damaging or an extreme habitability concern:**
11. Escalate immediately through the Ops Assistance Hub
12. Support Specialists will validate with NOMAD before reactivation.
13. Once NOMAD confirms, Support may proceed with enabling/reactivating the resident or property.
14. Ops can continue with the work order handling.
15. **If the issue is NOT property-damaging or an extreme habitability concern**
16. Ops submits a **Product Support (PS) ticket** to relay the caller’s concern (disabled property) and the PS team to validate with NOMAD.
17. Escalate the work order to the Operations Support Specialists to move it to **Waiting for PM**.
18. Be sure to leave **clear and accurate notes** explaining why the status was changed.
19. Refer to this guide:[How to Write Effective Notes](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1058211367)

## NOMAD Custom Action Sets:

\*This is simply a reference to understand that additional automation is in place.

- [NOMAD - Owner Model](https://app.latchel.com/admin/action-set-group/91)
  - Resident Missing Contact Information - Sends a notification when the resident of the WO is missing contact information. 
  - Resident Scheduling Delay Notification - If we've been waiting on a resident for 48 hours, then notification is sent to the owner that we're stuck because of the resident.
  - Waiting for an Invoice or Completion - If a job sits in completion or invoice for 48 hours then a notification is sent to the owner that we're pending confirmation from the vendor.
  - Escalation to Owner - In any type of Pending state due to troubleshooting outcome, process delay, etc. (waiting, forwarded, pending) the job will fire an email and SMS to the owner letting them know we need their help to move something forward. We want them to text or call our ops team to put things back on track or so we can tell them to take over.
  - Vendor Scheduling Delay Notification - If a job has been waiting on a vendor, then we send notification to the owner - 48 hours for urgent WOs and 72 hours for normal WOs.
  - Service Provider Assigned Email Notification - Sends notification to the Owner when a SP gets assigned to the WO. 
  - Appointment Confirmed Email Confirmation - Sends notification to the owner when an appointment is scheduled with the date/time information. 
- [Auto Fill SP Instructions with Owner Info](https://app.latchel.com/admin/action-set-group/110)
  - SP Instructions with Owner Contact Info - Inserts the Assigned Owner of the Property contact information in the SP Instructions of the WO.   
- [Needs PM Approval \> Needs Owner Approval](https://app.latchel.com/admin/action-set-group/85)
  - Move Needs PM Approval \> Needs Owner Approval - Must have Default State 5 (Needs Owner Approval) modified to exclude the PM company. All nonemergency severity settings are set to Needs Pre Approval for this action set to work and move those WOs from PM Approval to Owner Approval. 
- [Lockouts: Always Resident's Responsibility Notification](https://app.latchel.com/admin/action-set-group/117)
  - Notification to Resident for Lockouts - Send SMS/Email notification to the resident when they submit a lockout WO. This notification lets the resident know they are responsible for finding, calling, scheduling and paying for their own locksmith.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You can break the SOP in the following situations:

- **If strictly following the SOP would cause confusion or delay that could negatively impact the resident or client, especially in urgent scenarios.** Use your discretion, but always document the reason for the deviation and notify the appropriate support specialist.
- **If you receive direct instructions from a Property Manager, owner, or company leadership that override the SOP,** follow their direction and document the exception.
- **If the request or situation falls outside the scope of the SOP or is not covered by the documented process,** pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance—do not proceed on your own.
