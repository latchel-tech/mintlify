# **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to make sure that agents are always productively engaged, even during non-busy hours, while maintaining sufficient coverage for emergency tasks, admin emails, and calls. By scheduling training, meetings, or coaching only when workload and staffing allow, we reduce idle time, prevent missed SLAs, and ensure our customers continue receiving timely service.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Who should read this document?**

  - Operations Support Specialist
  - Ops Supervisors
  - Ops Managers
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Process Overview

Although the expectation is for all Operations to be busy with a work order, there would be times when the workload is less than the agents currently logged in. During these non-busy hours, the Operations Support Specialist can schedule activities for agents to take in to lessen agents idle time.

### Verify if the non-emergency task queues are low.

- How to check non-emergency task queues:
  - Go to Emergencies Real time Dashboard 2.0, then choose Tasks queue under the Real-Time dropdown.

### Verify if the Call Center team has enough staff to cover the incoming calls.

For us to check how many call center agents we have, log in to [Amazon Connect](https://latchelphone.my.connect.aws/real-time-metrics). Here we are able to see how many agents we have on the phones and their current status, for example: On contact, Available, Wrap up, and Offline. We can also see how many calls we have had so far (Handled In), and this will help us know if it has been slow and if we can remove agents from calls.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  This SOP should generally be followed to schedule activities during non-busy hours, but exceptions may apply in unique operational situations:

  - **Unexpected spikes in workload:** If emergency tasks, non-emergency queues, or call volume suddenly increase, pause all scheduled activities and reassign agents to handle live work.
  - **Staffing shortages:** If multiple agents call in or go on unplanned leave, prioritize coverage over scheduled activities.
  - **Critical operational priorities:** During urgent issues or high-priority escalations, such as prolonged outages or major resident emergencies, scheduled meetings or trainings should be delayed until the situation stabilizes.
  - **Real-time tool limitations:** If dashboards (Emergencies Real Time, Assembly, Amazon Connect, etc.) are temporarily unavailable, delay scheduling activities until coverage can be confirmed.

  This ensures that training, coaching, and meetings enhance productivity without compromising service quality or SLA compliance.
</Note>
