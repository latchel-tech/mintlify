## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

The purpose of the AI Summarize feature is to provide Operations (Ops) staff with a quick and efficient way to review work order histories. By generating concise summaries of complex or lengthy activity logs, the tool helps Ops understand the status and key details of work orders faster, improving efficiency and supporting informed decision-making.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

The AI Summarize feature is now available for Operations (Ops) staff and provides an efficient way to generate summaries of work order histories. This tool is designed to streamline the review of work orders, particularly those with lengthy or complex activity logs.

**Guidance for Use:**

**Best Use Cases:** The feature is particularly useful for work orders with long histories or multiple activities, offering a quick overview that saves time compared to manual review.

**Encouraged Usage:** Ops are encouraged to leverage the Summarize feature when opening work orders with extensive or complex histories.

**Use Judgment:** For simple tasks or work orders that do not require detailed investigation, the feature is not required. Ops should exercise judgment in determining when to use it.

**Feedback:** Feedback on the AI Summarize feature is encouraged to support continuous improvement. A feedback option will be available in the tool upon official launch.
