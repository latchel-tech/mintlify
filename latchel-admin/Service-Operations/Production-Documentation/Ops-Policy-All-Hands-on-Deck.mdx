# 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  _We want to ensure that Latchel can respond effectively to extreme operational pressure, such as unexpectedly high queue volumes or aging work orders, by quickly reallocating resources and prioritizing critical tasks. This process helps prevent missed SLAs, delays in emergency responses, and poor customer experiences while keeping the team coordinated and focused during high-pressure periods. By having a clear “All Hands On Deck” protocol, we reduce confusion, ensure accountability, and maintain service quality for both residents and property managers._
</Info>

# Content

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Info>
  **What happened prior to this step?**

  Operations Support Specialist is monitoring the work queues according to [(Operations Support Specialist) Job-Aid: Work Queues & Labor Management](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-Job-Aid-Work-Queues-Labor-Management#operations-support-specialist-job-aid-work-queues-and-labor-management) and is struggling to bring the queues size and age down to acceptable levels while having to keep associates working on all queues.
</Info>

### Introduction

“All Hands On Deck” is an expression of a naval origin. It means that in a storm or other emergency, the captain might yell **all hands on deck**. This is a command for **all** the sailors to **come** to the **deck** of the boat to help navigate the storm.

In Latchel, it means that the we will call the attention of all operations team and pause all non-essential, or non-urgent, work to focus on working our backlogs and bringing our operation back to a “healthy” state.

### Step 1 - When to apply this policy & How it will be communicated

If Operations Support Specialists or Operation Supervisors believe we don’t have enough resources to control unexpected volume spikes or service levels, an Operations Supervisor or Operations Manager should apply the All Hands on Deck policy. If the Operations Support Specialist has attempted to contact the next level but has not had a response in 15 minutes, they should make the call as well.

If the ops supervisor agrees “All Hands On Deck” is necessary, they will post the “All Hands On Deck” call in the  #operations channel and in the #operations\_support channel, they will also post direct instructions to each team with a link to this SOP.

### Step 2- What happens when “All Hands On Deck” is called?

<Info>
  The table in Step 3 will give a more detailed guideline of the actions that are expected.
</Info>

1. Ops supervisor will cancel, or request to cancel, all meetings that are pulling operations resources away from the operations, unless a meeting is deemed necessary to coordinate the execution of the “All Hands On Deck” policy
2. Training/Cross-training will be paused for every associate beyond their first 21 days.
3. The team will be re-allocated by operations leadership (Supervisors or Managers) or Operations Support Specialists to meet operational needs.

### Step 3 - Team re-allocation

We have set our priorities on how to manage our queues on-going [(Operations Support Specialist) Job-Aid: Work Queues & Labor Management](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-Job-Aid-Work-Queues-Labor-Management#operations-support-specialist-job-aid-work-queues-and-labor-management) with the goal of providing a great customer experience while being cost effective. When the “water level” of our queues goes much greater than our acceptable level, we will shift our priorities to be 100% focused on customer experience and cost will be deprioritized.

Latchel has 2 core responsibilities:

- Coordinate Emergency Maintenance and Resident Responsible work orders
- Assist property managers with using the Latchel platform to coordinate non-emergency maintenance.

Each action below will be designed to lead to efficiency of these 2 responsibilities.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  This SOP should be followed whenever queue volume or task aging reaches critical levels, but exceptions may apply based on context or operational constraints:
</Note>

- **Temporary system limitations:** If dashboards, queue monitoring tools, or internal communications channels are unavailable, document the attempt and implement the All Hands adjustments as soon as the tools are restored.
- **Partial staffing availability:** If some associates or managers are unavailable due to pre-approved PTO, emergencies, or unavoidable absences, leadership should adjust responsibilities and priorities accordingly while still maintaining focus on high-impact queues.
