# **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to ensure that all emergency admin emails are addressed promptly, even when sync delays occur between Gmail and Assembly. The goal is to prevent missed tickets, maintain timely responses to emergency work orders, and keep Latchel's operations reliable and consistent.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Process Overview

These type of tickets are handled exclusively by our emergency admin team. The only back up that we have will be the Operations Support Specialist team who has access to Assembly. Handling these tickets quickly will ensure emergency work orders are promptly troubleshot, followed up on, and updated. These includes Need to Verify emails, Voicemails, and other tickets. Note that all emergency work should be handled immediately.

1. Refer to [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Service-Ops-Assembly-Guide#service-ops-assembly-guide) to know how to access Assembly views.
   1. **"Emergency Admin" Teamspace:**
      1. This teamspace will show tickets for the emergency admin troubleshooters.
      2. ![Image 20250226 225945](/images/image-20250226-225945.png "Image 20250226 225945")
   2. **"Operations Email Team" Teamspace:**
      1. This teamspace will show tickets for all email associates.
      2. ![Image 20250226 230009](/images/image-20250226-230009.png "Image 20250226 230009")
2. As an Operations Support Specialist, you should review the number of present emergency admin troubleshooters and whether they have active emails being worked on.

<Note>
  NOTE: Spikes occur on Assembly, and it may take a few moments for the troubleshooters to notice it since they might be working on other tasks when the emergency ticket queue is clear. As an Operations Support Specialist, it is your responsibility to notify the troubleshooters about any pending emergency emails that need attention by posting them in the ops-troubleshooter channel on Slack.
</Note>

### Assembly Sync Verification and Escalation Steps

This process ensures that Admin tickets are not missed due to sync delays between Gmail and Assembly. By checking both the ticket queue and the Gmail inbox, we can quickly catch and escalate any sync issues, avoid delays in response, and maintain reliable support coverage.

1. Check the admin ticket queue in Assembly every hour as part of your regular cadence. Refer to [Operations Support Specialist Cadence](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-Cadence#operations-support-specialist-cadence)
   1. Make sure it's being monitored consistently.
2. If no new tickets come in within the hour,
   1. Check the Gmail inbox (using the access provided) to double-check if emails were received but didn't sync.
3. If you find tickets that didn't sync,
   1. Immediately report it to the Assembly team in the [#assembly-x-latchel](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C07NXANFKL4) channel
   2. Keep us in the loop. If there is parity between Assembly & Gmail, there is no need to post for management.
   3. Post a message and tag the management team in the #operations\_support channel about the issue and share any response you get from the Assembly.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

This SOP should be followed as a guide for regular monitoring, but support members can adjust based on real-time circumstances. For example, if the emergency admin team is already handling a surge in tickets, if Assembly or Gmail is temporarily unavailable, or if leadership provides an alternative escalation method, Operations Support Specialists may modify the timing or steps. The key is to ensure emergency emails are caught and addressed without delaying customer support or follow-ups.
