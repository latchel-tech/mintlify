## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent inconsistent handling and delays when managing customer communications and tickets across multiple channels. The goal of this SOP is to ensure Service Ops uses the Assembly Application in a standardized, efficient way—so every customer request is tracked, responded to, and resolved promptly, reducing errors and improving the overall experience for both customers and Latchel.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

Assembly is an AI-native all-in one customer experience management platform that connects to Slack, Email, Teams, SMS, and more. To know more about Assembly, visit their official website: [https://www.askassembly.app/](https://www.askassembly.app/)

To know more about its features and functions, refer to [https://docs.askassembly.app/usage/platform/teams](https://docs.askassembly.app/usage/platform/teams).

<Note>

### Step 1 - Accessing your dashboard

Once you've completed the onboarding process and signed up with Assembly, you'll arrive at your Dashboard.

### Step 2 - Going through your Inbox

#### **My Inbox**

Your individual Inbox is exclusively yours. It displays tickets assigned to you and is not accessible to other team members.

#### **Teamspaces**

In this space, inboxes are general, and everyone has access to them.

<Note>
  Every Inbox will have a sub-inbox labeled “Interactions,” where you can view the tickets within that Inbox.
</Note>

| **Team Space** | **Members** |
| --- | --- |
| Operations Email Team | All email associates including Emergency Admin team |
| Emergency Admin | Emergency Admin team |
| Slack Support Tickets | Operations Support Specialists |

#### **Statuses**

Tickets have 3 statuses: **_needs response_**, **_open_**, and **_closed_**.

- **Breaching** occurs when the Service Level Agreement (SLA) is not met within the defined timeframe. It indicates that the response or resolution time for the ticket has exceeded the agreed-upon limit, signaling a failure to meet the expected service standards.
- **Needs response** refers to a ticket that has either been newly raised by a customer or an existing “open” ticket that has been updated by the customer.
- **Open** tickets are those that are waiting for the customer to respond or take action. Typically this could be after your first response or after you have resolved the issue and are waiting for confirmation from the customer.
- Once issues are resolved or are stale, you can move the ticket into the “**closed**” state.

- **Timer** - The "Timer" tracks the amount of time spent on a ticket, helping to monitor how long an issue has been open or under review. Please remember to start the timer when you begin working on a ticket, and stop it once you’ve completed the task.
- **Status** - The "Status" indicates the current stage of the ticket. It can be set to "Needs Response," "Open," or "Closed," reflecting the ticket's progress and actions required.
- **Owner** - The "Owner" refers to the team member assigned to handle the ticket. This person is responsible for managing the issue and ensuring it gets resolved.
- **Team Owner** - The "Team Owner" property shows which team or department is responsible for the ticket, ensuring that the appropriate group is addressing the issue.
- **Labels** - "Labels" are tags you can assign to tickets to categorize them by topic, priority, or any other specific criteria, helping to organize and filter tickets more efficiently.

### Escalation Procedure for Application Issues

For any issues encountered in the application, associates must escalate the matter by creating a post in the **_#assembly\_ops\_war\_room_** channel using the workflow “Bug and Feature Request\_SVC OPS”. It is essential to provide a video recording of the issue to ensure accurate documentation and troubleshooting.

If you are unsure if it is a bug or feature request, escalate to Operations Support Specialists. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-escalate-between-teams).

If the troubleshooting guide in [Service Ops: Troubleshooting Guide for System Issues (Console, AWS, Assembly, etc.)](https://help.latchel.com/service-ops-troubleshooting-guide-for-system-issues-console-aws-assembly-etc) has already been completed and the issue is system-related, affecting the majority of active users and significantly impacting operations (e.g., leaving no viable workaround), Ops Support Specialists may escalate the report directly to the **_#assembly-x-latchel_** channel using the corresponding workflow.

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
