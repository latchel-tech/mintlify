## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that LCD support requests from property owners, residents, and service providers are handled efficiently, accurately, and with clear communication. The goal of this SOP is to provide step-by-step guidance for Email Associates and Operations Support Specialists on managing these requests—ensuring timely responses, proper documentation, and smooth coordination between all parties.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Important:** If you are **not an email associate** with access to view **LCD-related work orders**, **escalate it to the Operations Support Specialist** so you can be provided with instructions on how to proceed. Please familiarize the “Key Reminders” section of this page.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Key Reminders**:

  - The primary role of the team is to act as intermediaries between the **Service Providers and** **Owners**, facilitating resolution and ensuring high-quality service delivery.
  - **Email** is the primary communication method. If an email address is unavailable, we can use alternative methods as needed.
  - **LCD-related work orders** start with **“d-”** in the work order number.
  - In LCD, the user who creates the work order is automatically listed as the **Property Manager (PM)**. If you need to notify the **Owner** and no owner is listed, you may contact the **PM user assigned to the work order** instead.
    - If an email address is available, send an email.
    - If no email is listed, reach out by phone.
  - After you take action on a work order (such as changing the status, adding notes, etc.), **remember to refresh the page** to see your updates reflected. Some changes may not appear until the page reloads.
  - **LCD Partners Include:**
    - RentRedi
    - Innago
    - Baselane
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
  Service providers, owners, or residents may contact Latchel for support regarding their work orders. When this occurs, we should ensure their inquiries are routed to the correct party to maintain a smooth flow of communication. Unlike regular Latchel work orders, **_vendors have full access to the landlord’s and resident’s contact information_**. Therefore, **_Latchel should not make calls or take actions that service providers can handle themselves_**. Instead, our role is to guide service providers on how to locate information and complete tasks within Latchel, ensuring a smooth flow of communication.
</Note>

Below are the steps the Operations team should follow to ensure inquiries are properly routed and communication remains smooth.

### **🔍Locating a Work Order**

1. Search for the job in the **Console** using the available work order information.
   1. You can search by:
      1. **Work Order Number**: Always starts with `d-`.
      2. **Slug**: A simplified URL code that directs you to the specific work order.
         1. Example: In the URL `https://lvn.latchel.com/admin/work-order/Ui6MS`, `Ui6MS` is the slug.

### 🗨️Communication Instructions

- **Zapier Requests** - These support requests are generated through the integration and **do not originate from direct emails** sent by Service Providers (SPs) or Owners.
  - To respond:
    - **Do not reply to the request directly.**
    - Instead, send a **new email** to the appropriate recipient (either the SP or Owner), depending on who the message pertains to.
- **Direct Email Requests** - These are emails sent **directly** by the SP or Owner from their email address.
  - To respond:
    - You can **reply directly** to these emails as they come from a valid contact.
      - Refer to [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests)

### **🗣️Owner/Resident requests**

If an owner/resident follows up about a work order:

1. **Requests for general information update about the work order**
   1. **Review Recent Updates**
      1. Open the work order and review the latest notes, actions, and status updates.
   2. **Provide the Owner/Resident With an Update**
      1. Reply/Send the owner/resident an **email** with the current status or any relevant information.
         1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner/Resident's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, it is currently \[status update—e.g., scheduled for \[date\], awaiting a response, pending service provider update, etc.\]. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
   3. **Document the Follow-Up**
      1. Leave **complete notes** on the work order detailing your follow-up, including the **Assembly ticket number** for reference.
      2. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
         1. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests)
2. **Follow-up requests to the service provider (missed appointments, etc)**
   1. **Check for Updates on the Work Order**
      1. Review the work order in **Console** to see if there are any recent updates from the service provider (SP).
         1. **If an Update Is Available:**
            1. Reply/Send the owner/resident an **email** providing the latest updates.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner/Resident's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, it is currently \[status update—e.g., scheduled for \[date\], awaiting a response, pending service provider update, etc.\]. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
            2. Leave **complete notes** on the work order detailing the follow-up, including the **Assembly ticket number**.
            3. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
               1. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests)
         2. **If No Update Is Available:**
            1. Reply/Send the owner/resident an **email** acknowledging their request. Let them know you’ve forwarded the follow-up request to the service provider.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner/Resident's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, there are no new updates at this time. We’ve forwarded your follow-up request to the service provider and they will reach out to you for an update. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
            2. Follow up with the **service provider via email**, requesting a status update.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Service Provider's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel following up on work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. The property owner/resident has requested an update on the status of this job. Please reach out to them and provide the latest update at your earliest convenience._
            3. Leave **complete notes** on the work order about the follow-up, including the **Assembly ticket number**.
               1. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
               2. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests)

### **👷‍♂️Service Provider requests**

If an SP follows up about a work order:

1. **General information about the work order**
   1. **Review Recent Updates**
      1. Check the latest notes, actions, and status updates on the work order.
   2. **Provide the Service Provider With an Update**
      1. Reply/Send the SP an **email** with the current status or relevant information. Encourage the SP to review work order updates directly through their **Service Provider Portal**.
         1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Service Provider's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, it is currently \[status update—e.g., scheduled for \[date\], awaiting resident response, pending owner update, etc.\]. You can always monitor and update the work order through your dashboard \[insert the work order link for the SP\]. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
   3. **Document the Follow-Up**
      1. Leave **complete notes** on the work order, detailing your follow-up and including the **Assembly ticket number** for reference.
      2. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
         1. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-respond-to-assembly-tickets-and-forward-requests)
2. **Follow-up requests to the resident or owner**
   1. **Check for Updates on the Work Order**
      1. Review the work order in the **Console** to determine if new updates are available from the owner.
         1. **If an Update Is Available:**
            1. Reply/Send the SP an **email** with the most recent update.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Service Provider's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, it is currently \[status update—e.g., scheduled for \[date\], awaiting resident response, pending owner update, etc.\]. You can always monitor and update the work order through your dashboard \[insert the work order link for the SP\]. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
            2. Leave **complete notes** on the work order, including the **Assembly ticket number**.
            3. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
               1. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-respond-to-assembly-tickets-and-forward-requests)
         2. **If No Update Is Available:**
            1. Reply/Send the SP an **email** acknowledging their follow-up. Let them know you have forwarded their request to the landlord/resident.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Service Provider's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. We received your request regarding work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Upon reviewing the work order, there are no new updates at this time. We’ve forwarded your follow-up request to the owner and they will reach out to you for an update. You can always monitor and update the work order through your dashboard \[insert the work order link for the SP\]. Please let us know if you have any questions!_
            2. Follow up with the **owner via email**, requesting the necessary information or approval.
               1. **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel following up on work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. The service provider has requested an update on the \[approval, etc\]. Please reach out to them and provide the latest update at your earliest convenience._
            3. Leave **complete notes** on the work order about the follow-up, including the **Assembly ticket number**.
            4. Close the Assembly Ticket with proper notes about the action taken.
               1. [How to Respond to Assembly Tickets and Forward Requests](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-respond-to-assembly-tickets-and-forward-requests)

**Task Creation in LCD Work Orders**

We **DO NOT** create tasks for LCD Work Orders, as LCD is a limited coordination service with generally straightforward transactions with customers.

## Invoice Statuses Guidelines

Operations associates must verify whether payment has been initiated by reviewing the invoice status under the **"Invoices"** tab in the corresponding work order. Below are the common invoice statuses typically observed.

**“Paid”** - This invoice status indicates that the payment has been successfully processed.

- Click the **_invoice number_** to view additional details.
  - **Note:** For _paid_ invoices, a confirmation note will indicate that the payment was successfully processed, along with the date it was completed.
- Inform the service provider that the payment has been successfully processed.
  - **Say something like**: “_Hi \[Service Provider Name\]. Thank you for waiting. As per the review, the payment was successfully processed on \[date stated in the invoice\]. Please let us know if you need assistance with anything else._”
- If the service provider or owner requests a copy of the receipt or reports that the payment hasn’t been reflected yet, please escalate the case to the Payments Team by following the steps outlined in FAQ No. 6.
- If the status is **unpaid** or **pending**, please don’t share any info with the service provider. Post a request in the **_work-order-payments_** channel for assistance, utilizing the 'Payment Inquiry' workflow. Always include the WO link on the request.
  - Escalate the ticket/email to the payment team following the steps outlined in FAQ No. 6.

## **[❓](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-handle-emergency-work-orders)Frequently Asked Questions:**

Q1: It says our primary communication method is email, what if there is no available email address from the request and in our system for the user profile?

**A:** Create a user account in [<u>app.latchel.com</u>](http://app.latchel.com/) so you can send the number an SMS.

The new user account must be created under this property address [https://app.latchel.com/admin/property/617248](https://app.latchel.com/admin/property/617248) under “Residents” (for residents) and in the “Owner” tab for owners. No need to disable them afterwards.

**B:** If the number added is not textable, resort to calling the owner directly using the profile created in [<u>app.latchel.com</u>](http://app.latchel.com/).

Q2: If a call is specifically requested in the request ticket, can I call?

**A:** Yes. If a call is explicitly requested in the ticket (supervisor call, urgent assistance, etc), you may proceed with contacting the customer by phone. Be sure to document the call details in the work order notes.

Q3: If an owner requests to cancel a work order, can I cancel it?

**A:** Yes. You can cancel the work order upon the owner's request. However, kindly remind the owner that they also have the option to cancel the work order directly through their portal.

**Remember**! Cancellations made within four hours of the appointment start time may incur a service fee.

**Cancellation Request Outside of 4 Hours**

- **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. Per your request, we have canceled work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. You can also cancel work orders directly through your portal for future requests. Please let us know if you need any further assistance._

**Cancellation Request Within 4 Hours**

- **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. Per your request, we have canceled work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\]. Please note that since this cancellation was made within four hours of the scheduled appointment time, a service fee may apply. Let us know if you have any questions or need further assistance._

Q4: Can a service provider cancel a work order?

**A**: Yes. Service providers can cancel a work order by removing themselves from it. When this happens, the work order is automatically canceled, and a notification is sent to the owner to keep them informed.

Q5: Can I update the status of a work order?

A: Yes, you can update the status of a work order (cancel, reschedule) as long as the new status allows customers to continue managing the work order on their end. However you have to remember that sometimes it may be a little tricky.

For example: An **owner reports that they accidentally canceled a work order.** Follow these steps to reinstate it and ensure it progresses correctly:

1. **Reschedule the Work Order**
2. **Handling Resident Availability**
   - If the resident does not have availability listed, set it to **"All day"** to ensure the work order moves forward.
   - **Do not follow-up on availability**—the service provider is responsible for scheduling directly with the resident.
3. **Notify the Service Provider**
   - Once the work order is in **"Scheduling with Service Provider,"** send a notification to the service provider, instructing them to contact the resident for scheduling.
   - **Say something like**: _Hi \[Service Provider's Name\], This is \[Your Name\] with Latchel. Work order \[d-XXXXX\] at \[Property Address\] was accidentally canceled by the owner and has now been reinstated. Please reach out to the resident directly to confirm their availability and proceed with scheduling. Let us know if you have any issues or require further assistance._

Q7: If either the owner or the service provider has a complaint, what should we do?

**Step 1: Investigate the Complaint** Acknowledge the customer’s concern politely. Begin gathering key details.

Step 2: **Ask Clarifying Questions (if needed)**

Collect the What, When, and Who: What – What exactly is the issue or concern? (e.g., poor service, scheduling error, communication issue, billing concern) When – When did the issue occur? (e.g., appointment date, time of incident, communication timeline) Who – Who was involved? (e.g., service provider name, Owner, tenant)

Also: **_Review the ticket details, communication logs, and related history._**

**Step 3: Categorize the Complaint**

Check if the complaint falls under any of the defined categories in the Job Aid/FAQ (e.g., billing dispute, reassignment of service provider, earlier appointment request).

✅ If the complaint matches a category/FAQ item: Follow the resolution steps/information outlined in the Job Aid/FAQ.

❌ If the complaint does not match any category: Proceed to Step 4.

**Step 4: Determine Urgency**

Assign an urgency level to guide response timelines:

**High Urgency – Initial contact/investigation within the same day** Safety concerns (injury, hazard) Legal/financial risk (threats of legal action) Service provider conduct issues Missed/incorrect appointment Repeated customer dissatisfaction

**Standard Urgency – Initial contact/investigation within 24–48 hours** General feedback Minor communication issues Non-urgent process concerns

**Step 5: Escalate** Post in the **#ops-lcd-support-requests** channel.

Tag **Cezcille**. Include a summary of your investigation: What, When, Who Urgency level Actions already taken Reason for escalation

➡️ **If Cezcille is unavailable:** The available Support will take over the initial investigation for High Urgency complaints (same-day response/investigation required). For Standard Urgency complaints, document and escalate in the channel as normal, to be handled when available.

**Step 6: Documentation** Update the work order/ticket with: A note of the investigation and/or escalation. The Assembly link if related to an email.

Q8: What should we do if an owner expresses doubt or anxiety about the approved budget and no estimate has been provided yet?

Service Providers must obtain an estimate and receive approval in Latchel to charge more than the approved budget, which helps reassure the Property Manager that their budget is being protected.

In cases where the approved budget is already a point of concern, it's best to temporarily lower the budget to a nominal amount (e.g., \$1). This ensures that the Service Provider is required to submit an estimate for approval before proceeding with any charges, providing an added layer of oversight and comfort to the PM.

Q9: What should we do if an LCD owner requests help with a specific service, but we don’t have any available vendors for that trade in their area?

Sample Inquiry: _An owner wanted to explore contractor options in the area and has specifically requested assistance with tree removal and bathtub re-glazing services. As per the owner, the only available contractors in his area are plumbers._

**Note:** We do **not** actively source contractors on behalf of LCD users.

**A:** If there are no vendors for the requested trade in the owner’s area, we can respond with something like:

**Say something like**: _“Thanks for reaching out! At this time, we don’t have any \[requested trade\] contractors in your area. We’re always working to expand our network, so feel free to check back soon.”_

Q10: What should we tell the owner if they want to get a list of technician types available in their area?

**A**:  **Say something like**: “_We understand that knowing which technician types are available in your area is important when evaluating maintenance support options. At this time, we’re unable to provide a detailed list of available trades by zip code or location. However, improving visibility into our service coverage is something we’re actively working on. In the meantime, if a needed trade isn’t available through the Latchel integration, we recommend handling that request outside of Latchel and marking it accordingly in your property management platform. Thank you”_

Q11: How can we tell if a general inquiry is about LCD versus a standard maintenance work order issue?

Latchel supports the LCD Partners such as Rentredi, Innago and Baseline.

Example for Rentredi: There are two types of Latchel products that RentRedi users can buy. There is a "subscription" service and an "on-demand" service (LCD). You can identify which service the customer is using by the work order number. LCD work orders start with "d-

**If the inquiry is general and not tied to a specific work order:**

- **Search for their name, phone number, or email** in [app.latchel.com](https://app.latchel.com).
  - If they **don’t appear as an active owner** in a RentRedi account, they are likely an LCD user.
- **Ask them directly** whether they use Latchel’s **monthly subscription** or **on-demand “contractor dispatch”** services.
  - **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner’s name\], To better assist you, could you confirm whether you're using Latchel’s monthly subscription service or the on-demand “contractor dispatch” service? This helps us route your request appropriately and ensure you get the right support._

Q12:If an owner requests to reassign the service provider or asks for an earlier appointment, is this possible?

**A.** Reassignment or searching for an earlier appointment is not available at this time. Kindly inform the owner of this limitation.

- **Say something like**: _Hi \[Owner’s name\], We currently don't support (re-assignment/ rescheduling and searching for earlier appointments) on this free service. We do support that on our managed service or with a direct subscription. If the current appointment does not work for you, you can cancel the appointment at no cost.  Please let us know if you have any questions!_

Q13: What if the Owner reaches out and asks why their credit card information was taken when the LCD work order was submitted if this is a free service?

**Say something like:** “_Thanks for reaching out! While we do collect card details when a work order is submitted, no charge is made upfront. Your card is only billed after a job or appointment is completed. Using the Latchel app to connect with a service provider is free.”_

Q14: If an Owner/PM requests to release a cancelled work order on our end so they can take over and mark it as Completed (because they already had someone perform the work outside of Latchel), is that possible?

- **Say something like**: “_Hi \[Owner/PM\], Thank you for your request. We’ll take care of this for you. We’ll mark the work order as Completed and document that the issue was resolved outside of Latchel per your instruction. Please don’t hesitate to let us know if you need assistance with anything else.”_

Proceed with the following steps:

1. Remove the assigned Service Provider (SP) from the work order.
2. Mark the work order as _Completed_ and select **“Resident Resolved the Issue.”**
3. Add complete and detailed notes on the work order to document the action taken, as requested by the Owner or Property Manager.

Q15: What if the Owner asks why a work order was cancelled?

Check the work order details. In most cases, the work order was canceled because no service providers accepted or were available to take on the job. You can verify this by reviewing the cancellation reason listed in the work order, as shown below.

**Say something like**: “_Hi \[Owner/PM\], thank you for reaching out. Upon review, the work order was canceled because no service providers accepted or were available to take on the job._

_We understand this may be disappointing and sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this situation may have caused. Thank you for your understanding, and please feel free to reach out if you have any additional questions._

Q16:What if the Owner asks to reopen the cancelled work order because no one accepted it?

We do not reopen cancelled work orders. We also do not recommend submitting a new request for the same issue, as it will most likely result in the same outcome since provider availability remains the same.

You may respond to the Owner using the following message:

**Say something like**: “_Hi \[Owner/PM\], thank you for reaching out. Upon review, the work order was canceled because no service providers accepted or were available to take on the job._

_At this time, the work order cannot be reopened, and submitting a new request for the same issue would likely result in the same outcome._

_We understand this may be disappointing and sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused. Thank you for your understanding, and please let us know if you have any other questions.”_

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- **If strictly following the SOP would cause confusion, delay, or negatively impact the customer, resident, or service provider—especially in urgent or emergency scenarios.** Post your concern in the **_ops-lcd-support-requests_** channel.
