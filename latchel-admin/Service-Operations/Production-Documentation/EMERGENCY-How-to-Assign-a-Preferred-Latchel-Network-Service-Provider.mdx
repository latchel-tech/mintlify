## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to ensure emergency maintenance issues are dispatched quickly and to the right service provider, minimizing delays and confusion. The goal of this SOP is to provide clear, step-by-step instructions for assigning either a Property Manager’s preferred vendor or a Latchel Network Service Provider during emergencies. This process prevents missed appointments, repeated outreach, and leaving emergency work orders unresolved.
</Note>

## 🗂️Definition Matrix

<Warning>
### About Emergency Services - Dispatching a Service Provider

The term "dispatch" is used to describe assigning any Service Provider (whether they are (1) Preferred or (2) Latchel Service Providers). This is a term used in emergency situations, similar to term "dispatch" used during 911 emergency services. We use it at Latchel to remember that we act on the urgency that Emergency Services require. For more information, see:  [↔ How to: Handle 911-Severity Type Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-911-Severity-Type-Work-Orders)

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **(EMERGENCY) Rules for Calling the Service Provider **

1. Refer to https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2600337430.
2. **General rule**: 📞Call each Service Provider 3 times, or until the Service Provider confirms.
  1. **‼️IMPORTANT: If a Service Provider contact has multiple phone numbers on file, you must attempt ALL available numbers, with up to 3 dial attempts per number, before counting that contact as unreachable.**
    1. The “3 attempts” rule applies per number, not per Service Provider. An SP with 2 numbers = up to 6 total dial attempts; an SP with 3 numbers = up to 9 total dial attempts.
    2. Start with the primary number and complete all 3 attempts.
    3. Repeat the same 3-attempt process for each additional number on file, such as secondary, tertiary, etc.
    4. Only consider the Service Provider unreachable once every number on file has been dialed 3 times with no confirmation.
    5. Do not move on to the next Service Provider or escalate as unreachable until all numbers have been fully exhausted.
    6. This applies to all Service Provider types, including Preferred, LCN, and Sourced SPs, across all steps of this SOP.
  2. If the Service Provider ✅ picks up and gives verbal confirmation of the job, follow the instructions on the screen. Say,

  3. If the Service Provider❌ does not pick up on the third call, leave a message. In the voicemail you can say,

3. 📞Call the 2nd, 3rd, etc. Service Provider listed 3 times each until one confirms, or you run out of contacts.
  1. If the Service Provider does not pick up on the third call, leave a message.
4. **Additional Rule: Standardized Outreach Attempts to Preferred Service Providers:**
  1. Ops must complete 2 outreach attempts to the Preferred Service Provider before escalating away from the Preferred SP, defined as:
    1. **First outreach attempt** to each preferred SP = up to **3 dials** in a row (following the above steps 1-3).
      1. Call the Preferred SPs up to 3 times in a row (following our standard spacing between dials) or until the preferred SP answers
      2. Move through the preferred list (2nd, 3rd, etc. SP) the same way: **3 dials per preferred SP** on this first pass.
    2. **Second outreach attempt – 1 dial only per preferred SP **(second pass through the list)
      1. If **no preferred SP has been secured** after the first attempt pass (3x dial per Preferred SP), make **one additional dial** to **each preferred SP** on the list.
      2. This second attempt happens **after you have completed the first attempt for all listed preferred SPs**, not immediately after the 3rd dial to a single SP.
    3. If, after completing the second attempt pass (1 dial per preferred SP), **no preferred SP can be secured** (no answer/declines), stop preferred‑SP outreach and proceed per this SOP to:
      1. the next eligible SP type (e.g., LCN) **or**
      2. escalation to **PM / Waiting for PM**, depending on opt‑in/out status and current workflow step.

### **Step 1: Troubleshooting Dispatch Instructions**

<Warning>
You should only assign a Service Provider to an emergency WO as long as the Service Provider was able to confirm an appointment. You should never leave an emergency WO in “Scheduling Service Provider” status.

**EMERGENCY appointment window and pricing expectations**

- When confirming the appointment, note whether the Service Provider can go **same‑day / within 24 hours** or only **later than 24 hours out**.
- Emergency pricing and higher emergency soft budgets are intended for **same‑day or within 24 hours** appointments.  
- If the Service Provider **accepts the job but can only schedule more than 24 hours out** (for example, dispatch deferred to a later date), the visit should be treated as a **standard (non‑emergency)** appointment for pricing and soft budget, even if the work order remains emergency severity.
- When this occurs, follow the guidelines outlined in [**How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2600337430)  to communicate clearly to the Service Provider that **non‑emergency rates and budgets apply** for that appointment time.

If the SP insists on getting the job without an appointment, inform the SP that they can contact us back once they can confirm an appointment, but they won’t be assigned to the job until then.

**📝 NOTE: If the SP says they will call the resident directly, or says "I'll get back to you" / "I need to check my schedule" — this is not an acceptance. Do not assign. Redirect them:**

- Say something like: *"We appreciate that! We'll take care of coordinating with the resident on our end. What we need from you is a date and time that you can commit to for this job — are you able to give us that right now?"*
  - If they still cannot commit:
    - Say something like: *"No worries! Just so you're aware, since we weren't able to confirm an appointment, you have not been assigned to this work order and we will be continuing to source other available providers. You're welcome to text or call us back when you have a more definitive schedule, but we can't guarantee the work order will still be open at that point."*
- ⚠️ Assigning without a confirmed appointment while continuing to source creates a **double dispatch** risk. Continue sourcing the next SP.

Note that the PM may request us to assign a certain service provider. Contact the specified service provider once (calling x3 on a row) and in the event that they have been unresponsive to confirm an appointment, escalate the WO back to the PM. 

- Contact the escalation contacts, refer to [Job Aid: Defining Property Damage and Extreme Habitability](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations)
- For Non-Property Damage and Extreme Habitability, proceed to creating a task for the PM with the task subject “**Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed**”
</Warning>

### **Step 2: Assign a Preferred Service Provider**

<Note>
### About Preferred Vendors

***What's a Preferred Service Provider?*** Preferred Service Providers are Service Providers that the Property Management company identified when they first joined in as customers with Latchel. They're contractors that often have an existing trust relationship with the Property Management company and the Property Manager would like them to be the priority over other vendors. The Property Management Company **typically** pays these Service Providers directly. However, if the PM has the 'Preferred Service Provider Payments' toggle **ON** in their Company Profile → Payments tab, Latchel can process payments for Preferred SPs **via Direct Deposit only**. Refer to https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3256221699 for details.

### ** Step 4: How to: Handle Latchel Service Provider Sourcing**

<Note>
### About Vendor Sourcing

These Service Providers are outside of the Latchel system; sometimes, these Service Providers may have completed one job with Latchel before or the Service Provider does not want to be in the Latchel Network. Emergency Services oftentimes uses these Service Providers. This is because we secure and pay these Service Providers with a credit card. If we pay the service provider via credit card we still require them to submit an itemized invoice.
</Note>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Based on the SOP and related guidance, you can deviate from the process in these cases:

- **Pre-dispatch instructions require a different approach:**  
If the property manager’s pre-dispatch notes specify handling the emergency differently (e.g., do not use auto-assign, contact only certain service providers, or follow a unique escalation path), you must follow those instructions—even if it means not using the standard workflow.
- **No service provider can confirm an appointment:**  
If you have called all required service providers (3 attempts each) and none can confirm, escalate to the property manager or their escalation contacts as instructed. For property-damaging or extreme habitability issues, follow the escalation pathway; for other cases, create a task for the PM.
- **Explicit instructions from leadership or PM:**  
If a property manager, owner, or company leadership gives direct instructions that override the SOP (such as assigning a specific provider or skipping steps), follow their direction and document the exception.
