## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  _We want to ensure residents and property managers receive clear, accurate, and consistent support when inquiring about Home Assistant and resident benefits. The goal is to streamline responses, reduce confusion about available services, and empower residents to access their benefits easily—while also supporting property managers in creating new revenue streams. This process matters because it enhances the resident experience, prevents miscommunication, and helps Latchel and our partners deliver a seamless, hospitality-focused service._
</Note>

## **ℹ️**Background

Since the end of 2019, Latchel began pivoting away from being just a maintenance coordination package to becoming a **_resident experience platform_**. This is important because the Property Management industry has shifted towards a hospitality mindset where **_resident experience is now the most important thing for PMs_**, even more than growth. We are the **_only_** company in the industry that offers a resident-focused amenities platform!

## **🏠**What is Home Assistant?

Home Assistant has two main components:

1. The **Resident Benefit Hub**
2. The ability to schedule **Resident Responsible Work Orders**. You’ll hear these referred to as **_concierge requests_** and these include things like house cleaning, dog walking, furniture assembly, etc.
   1. Starting in November 2024, a new benefit has been rolled out, introducing Amenify to our current Home Assistant customers. For more information about this service and guidance on responding to common customer inquiries, please refer to the following link: [How to Respond to Amenify Inquiries](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-To-Respond-to-Amenify-inquiries).
   2. During intake, or when handling a call from a resident requesting this type of submission, please refer to the guidelines here: Step 4: Resident Caller Request [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Create-a-New-Work-Order-from-Incognito-Mode-All-Submissions)

## **👉**Why do PMs love Home Assistant?

The whole purpose of Home Assistant is to create new revenue streams for PMs. Apart from monthly rent, **_residents pay PMs a monthly fee for Home Assistant_**. A portion of this fee comes back to Latchel, and the rest stays with the PM. **_This is why it’s super important that we make the Home Assistant experience as smooth as possible for their residents!_**

<Accordion title="Free Rent Reporting -- FAQs">
  **Q: What is the new free rent reporting benefit?** A: Latchel now offers free rent reporting for residents through our partner Self. Residents who register will have their rent payments reported to all three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion). This helps residents build their credit at no cost.

  **Q: How is this different from the paid Pinata rent reporting?** A: The free version through Self requires residents to register and does not include rent rewards. Pinata is a paid add-on that includes rent rewards and other features.

  **Q: How can residents sign up for free rent reporting?** A: Residents can register directly through the instructions provided in their resident dashboard or benefit communications. If they need help, they should contact their property manager or visit [http://latchel.com/residents](http://latchel.com/residents).

  **Q: Can residents upgrade to include utility payments in credit reporting?** A: Yes! Residents can upgrade for a small monthly fee to have their utility payments reported as well, giving them more ways to build credit.

  **Q: How can a Property Manager switch to the free version?** A: Submit a PS Ticket. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)

  **Response Script:** “Latchel now offers free rent reporting for residents through our partner Self. Residents who register will have their rent payments reported to all three major credit bureaus. If you’d like more details or want to switch to the free version, I can open a Product Support ticket on your behalf. Residents can learn more or register by visiting their dashboard or [_http://latchel.com/residents._](http://latchel.com/residents.%E2%80%9Dhttp://latchel.com/residents.%E2%80%9D)

  **Q: What happens if a PM cancels the Rent Reporting (Self) program? How does this impact residents who are already enrolled?**

  A: This scenario requires escalation — **submit a PS Ticket on the PM's behalf**. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support).

  Ops does not currently have confirmed details on the specific impact to enrolled residents' credit reporting or credit history when a PM cancels the program. Until Product Support provides official guidance, use the response script below and escalate.

  **Key points to communicate to the PM:**

  - Their request to cancel has been received and will be reviewed by our Product Support team.
  - Product Support will clarify any impact on residents who are currently enrolled, including whether previously reported rent payments remain on residents' credit reports and whether active reporting stops immediately or after a transition period.

  **Response Script:**

  _"Thank you for reaching out about canceling the Rent Reporting program. I want to make sure we handle this carefully, especially regarding residents who are already enrolled. I'm going to open a Product Support ticket on your behalf so our team can walk you through the cancellation process and clarify any impact on your residents' credit reporting. You can expect a follow-up within 24 hours. Is there anything else I can help with in the meantime?"_
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if a resident submits a work order requesting a drain pack (or other benefit item)?">
  Drain packs (and other benefit items like Pest Control Kits) are fulfilled through the **Resident Benefits Hub**, not through work orders. If a resident submits a work order specifically requesting a drain pack or similar benefit item, follow these steps:

  1. **Cancel the work order.** Benefit-related items are not processed as work orders. Cancel the work order and note the reason (e.g., "Drain pack requests are fulfilled through the Resident Benefits Hub").
  2. **Send the resident an SMS** with instructions on how to complete the request through their Benefits Hub. Include the relevant help guide link.Suggested blurb:_"Hi \[resident name\]! Thanks for reaching out. Drain packs are actually fulfilled through your Resident Benefits Hub, not through a work order. You can request your drain pack by visiting your resident dashboard at \[insert dashboard link\] and clicking on the "Benefits" tab. Here's a step-by-step guide to help: [https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wrGAA/Resident-Benefit-Drain-Pack-Instructions](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wrGAA/Resident-Benefit-Drain-Pack-Instructions). Please let us know if you need any further assistance!"_
  3. **Direct the resident to their Benefits dashboard** to submit the request properly. You can find the resident's dashboard link on the bottom left of their resident profile page (see the Step-by-Step Process section above for details).

  **⚠️ Important:** This same process applies to any benefit item that comes in as a work order (e.g., Pest Control Kits). Always cancel the work order and redirect the resident to their Benefits Hub with the appropriate help guide link.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Benefit Cancelation Request">
  When a resident requests to cancel their Latchel benefits, the correct process depends on which type of customer they belong to:

  ### **A. Property Manager (PM) Customers**

  Ops should **instruct the resident to contact their property manager directly** to request cancellation. PMs control enrollment and opt-out permissions. Some allow cancellation and some do not.

  1. Verify the resident belongs to a PM customer.
  2. Search the resident’s information and confirm they **DO NOT** have “Home Assistant” Only subscription.
  3. Provide the script directing them to contact their PM.
  4. **Say something like**: _“Thank you for reaching out! To cancel your benefits, please contact your Property Manager directly. They handle all benefit cancellations and will be able to assist you with the opt-out process.”_

  ### **B. Home Assistant Residents**

  Ops should **send the cancellation request to Ethan** ([ethan@latchel.com](mailto:ethan@latchel.com)). Home Assistant is a partner, not a PM, so cancellations must be processed manually through our partnership channel.

  5. Gather:
  6. Resident’s name
  7. Address
  8. Contact info
  9. Email Ethan ([ethan@latchel.com](mailto:ethan@latchel.com)) with subject: **“Home Assistant – Cancel Benefits Request”**.
  10. Inform the resident their request is being handled.
  11. **Say something like:** _“Thank you for your request. Your cancellation is being reviewed and handled by our team. If any further information is required, we’ll be in touch with you promptly.”_
</Accordion>
