## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
*We want to ensure that all Need To Verify (NTV) Tickets and voicemails are accurately reviewed, escalated, and resolved in a timely manner. By clearly identifying, documenting, and collaborating on these items, we reduce the risk of missed or duplicated work, maintain operational efficiency, and provide a consistent, high-quality experience for our customers and property managers.*
</Note>

## **Procedure:**

<Note>
**Transition from Emergency Admin Associates:**
</Note>

a. When all Emergency Admin Associates have completed their shifts for the day, Operations Support Specialists will take over handling Emergency Admin Tickets and Voicemails.

<Note>
**Identification of Need To Verify Tickets (NTV):**
</Note>

a. While handling Emergency Admin Tickets and Voicemails, if Operations Support Specialists encounter a Need To Verify Tickets, they should carefully review the details to confirm that there is no corresponding property in our console or Work Order (WO) system already created.

<Note>
**Posting NTV in Ops-Troubleshooter Channel:**
</Note>

a. Upon confirming the need for further investigation, Operations Support Specialists will post the details of the NTV in the Ops-Troubleshooter channel.

b. Operations Support Specialists will tag a troubleshooter in the post to ensure prompt attention and resolution.

<Note>
**Handling Voicemails Requiring Calls:**
</Note>

a. If Operations Support Specialists encounter a voicemail that requires a call instead of a simple update to the Work Order, they should post the details in the Ops-Troubleshooter channel.

b. Operations Support Specialists will tag a troubleshooter in the post to assign the task of making the necessary call and handling further action.

<Note>
**Communication and Collaboration:**
</Note>

a. Operations Support Specialists will provide clear and concise details of the NTV or voicemail in the Ops-Troubleshooter channel post to facilitate the action on how to move forward.

b. Operations Support Specialists and the troubleshooter will collaborate to resolve the issue efficiently, ensuring accurate handling of the NTV or voicemail.

<Note>
**Follow-Up:**
</Note>

Once the NTV or voicemail has been resolved, Operations Support Specialists will update the Assembly ticket internal note accordingly and ensure proper documentation.

*Note: This procedure applies specifically to Operations Support Specialists handling Need To Verify Tickets and Voicemails.*

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Tip>
This SOP should generally be followed whenever handling NTV Tickets and voicemails, but exceptions may apply in specific scenarios:
</Tip>

- **Staffing limitations:** If Operations Support Specialists are temporarily unavailable or over-assigned, escalate the NTV or voicemail directly to a troubleshooter rather than delaying action.
- **System outages:** If the Work Order console or Assembly is inaccessible, document the attempt and follow up as soon as access is restored, ensuring no ticket is overlooked.

This approach ensures that NTV Tickets and voicemails are managed efficiently while allowing flexibility to respond to urgent operational needs.
