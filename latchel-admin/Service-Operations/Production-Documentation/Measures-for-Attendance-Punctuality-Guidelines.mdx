## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  Consistent attendance and punctuality are critical for team efficiency, customer satisfaction, and overall success at Latchel. This SOP provides clear, fair guidelines to set expectations, encourage accountability, and outline consequences when attendance issues arise.

  This process matters because:

  - Missed hours impact team capacity and service levels.
  - Repeated lateness or absences disrupt workflows.
  - A culture of accountability helps every associate thrive and deliver excellent support.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 - Attendance Thresholds

- **Missed Work Hours (Unpaid Leave):**
  - Associates who accumulate **10 hours** of unpaid leave within a calendar month will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- **Consistent Lateness:**
  - Associates arriving late for a total of **4 hours per week** over **two consecutive weeks** will also be placed on a PIP.
- **Excessive Missed Hours:**
  - Accumulating **40 hours or more** of unpaid leave in a calendar month will result in termination.

### Step 2 - Absenteeism & Job Abandonment

- **Excessive Absenteeism:**
  - 2+ unexcused absences in 30 days → disciplinary action.
  - 3 unexcused absences in 12 months → grounds for termination.
- **Job Abandonment:**
  - Failing to report to work for **3 consecutive days** without notifying a Supervisor will be treated as voluntary resignation (job abandonment).

### Step 3 - Sick Leave & Emergencies

- **Sick Leave:** Refer to the [Sick (Personal/Carer's) Leave Policy](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Sick-\(Personal-Carer's\)-Leave-Policy---Operations-Production-Team).
- **Emergency PTO Requests:**
  - Must be approved by the Supervisor.
  - Examples of valid emergencies include:
    - Severe weather conditions threatening safety/property.
    - Severe illness requiring hospitalization.
    - Accidents or injuries requiring immediate medical attention.

### Step 4 - Recording Attendance

- Associates must submit a bolt in the `status_updates` channel to log sudden lateness or absence in Deputy.
- Supervisors must verify work hours by cross-referencing Deputy and confirming via the bolt in the `pay-reconciliation` channel.
- Equipment issues (internet/power outage) will also be tagged as Unpaid Leave.

### Step 5 - Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- A structured program to address attendance or punctuality concerns.
- **Duration:** minimum of 4 weeks.
- Includes clear goals, timelines, and Supervisor support to help associates improve.

### Step 6 - Termination Process

- If attendance issues persist despite corrective actions, termination may follow.
- All terminations will follow Latchel's official policies and procedures.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

This SOP covers most attendance and punctuality situations. However, escalate or deviate when:

- You believe an associate's safety or wellbeing is at risk.
- A situation falls outside the scope of the outlined policies.
- Following the SOP exactly would cause significant harm to the team, customer, or Latchel operations.
