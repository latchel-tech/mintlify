# 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  We want to make sure all inbound customer calls are answered promptly and accurately by monitoring real-time call metrics in Amazon Connect. The goal is to prevent missed calls, quickly identify performance or connectivity issues, and maintain a seamless customer experience while supporting associates in managing call volume effectively.
</Info>

# Content

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Info>
  ### Process Overview

  The following metrics are available in our real-time call metrics reports in Amazon Connect. These should be monitored at least every 30 minutes to ensure calls are not being missed and agents are set to the appropriate status for taking calls.
</Info>

### Step 1

1. Inside [<u>Amazon Connect </u>](https://latchelphone.my.connect.aws/real-time-metrics?reportArn=arn:aws:connect:us-west-2:356760302025:instance/5f8c8154-2858-4a38-975a-8bba18568fba/report/a26f6fea-1324-4847-b1b6-82826ab9379a)you can find the “Analytics and optimization” section from the left-hand side menu.

<Frame>
  ![Amazon-Connect-Real-time-metrics (1).png](/images/SVC-106.png)
</Frame>

### Step 2

1. From this section, you can find “Real-Time Metrics:
2. This queue will show performance metrics from the past 2 hours and 24 hours. There are 2 separate tables labeled per timeframe.

<Frame>
  ![Amazon-Connect-Real-time-metrics (2).png](/images/SVC-107.png)
</Frame>

2. All Amazon dashboard metrics definitions can be found **[here](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/connect/latest/adminguide/real-time-metrics-definitions.html).**

### Step 3

1. As an Operations Support Specialist, you should review these performance metrics at least every 30 minutes throughout your shift to ensure customer calls are being answered and the team is able to handle to volume in the queue.

<Info>
  NOTE: There might be instances of missed calls. As an\_Operations Support Specialist\*, you hold the responsibility to inform the associate about these missed calls to uncover the root cause. Any identified connectivity issues must be escalated to management for a thorough examination.\*
</Info>

# 🎯 When to Break This SOP

This SOP should be followed as a guideline, but support members can adjust their approach based on unique scenarios. For example, if there is a sudden spike in call volume that requires reassigning staff, if Amazon Connect metrics are temporarily unavailable, or if leadership directs a temporary change in call handling, Operations Support Specialists may modify monitoring frequency or escalation steps. The key is to ensure calls are addressed without delaying response times or impacting customer service quality.
