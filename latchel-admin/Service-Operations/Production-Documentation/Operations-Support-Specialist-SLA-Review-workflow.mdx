## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

We want to ensure that all SLA-related performance data is accurately captured, documented, and submitted after every shift. This helps us identify missed SLA intervals, understand the reasons behind them (like staffing gaps, high volume, or system issues), and implement improvements to maintain consistent service levels. By completing the SS SLA Review workflow thoroughly, we protect customer experience, keep internal teams accountable, and provide Latchel with actionable insights to continuously optimize operations.

**Who should read this document?**

- Operations Support Specialist
- Ops Supervisors
- Ops Managers

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Process Overview

SLA (Service Level Agreement) Reviews play a vital role in monitoring and managing service performance to ensure the fulfillment of agreed-upon service levels. These reviews involve a comprehensive evaluation of the performance metrics, goals, and targets stated in the SLA, with the aim of assessing their achievement and identifying opportunities for improvement. See: [Service Level Agreement (SLAs) for our Products](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/794526591/Job+Aid+Latchel+Product+Features+Pricing+and+KPIs#JobAid:LatchelProductFeatures,PricingandKPIs-JobAid%3ALatchelProductFeatures%2CPricingandKPIs-1.ServiceLevelAgreement(SLAs)forourProducts)

Operations Support Specialists must submit the SS SLA Review workflow within 48 hours following their shift, or sooner if the data is available.

**Missed Admin Tickets Interval**

Go to the [Emergency Admin Missed Interval](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qFEk0ycGutpxOPTjIkNA5OEEc8iQNcSOCl2NZPnU4VM/edit?gid=696011515#gid=696011515) file and open the Heat Map_P90 P75 P50 tab.

Make sure you are referencing the correct week. To validate, check Column B or M, depending on the week being reported. The report uses a week starting on Sunday (1) through Saturday (7) as shown in Row 4. The numbers in Column C and N represent the hourly intervals. If certain hours do not appear in these columns, it means that no tickets were missed during those intervals. Review the data to identify any missed intervals.

If an interval is missed, please create a detailed note explaining what happened during that hour. Consider the following:

- How many people were assigned to the Admin queue?
- How many tickets were breached?
- Were you short-staffed at that time?
- Did a sudden volume of tickets come in while team members were on lunch, break, meeting, or training?
- Was there a system issue that contributed to the ticket influx?
- What assistance was provided by Support during the interval?

Even though the workflow is submitted up to 2 days after the shift, it is highly recommended to document shift events on the same day to ensure accuracy, instead of relying on memory to recall details from the previous day.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

While this workflow is designed for standard SLA reporting, there are situations where you may need to deviate slightly:

- **Data is delayed or missing:** If a dashboard hasn't updated yet or shows "No data available," submit the workflow with that note and explain in the comments what steps were taken to retrieve the data.
- **High-volume or urgent incidents:** If a sudden spike in calls, SMS, webchats, or emergency tasks occurs, prioritize monitoring and addressing real-time SLAs over completing the workflow immediately. The workflow should still be submitted as soon as possible once the urgent situation is under control.
- **New tools or dashboards introduced:** If a new tool is created to monitor SLA for a specific queue, incorporate the data from this tool into your workflow, even if it changes the usual sequence of reporting.
- **System outages or technical issues:** If a system is down or reporting tools aren't accessible, document the situation and escalate appropriately, then complete the workflow once data is available.

The key is to ensure accurate reporting while adapting to circumstances that could impact SLA monitoring or customer experience.
