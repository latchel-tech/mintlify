## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure associates can quickly and accurately access all relevant information about service providers to make informed decisions. The goal of the "How to: Navigate Service Provider Profiles" SOP is to prevent errors like assigning the wrong vendor, overlooking critical notes (such as "DO NOT USE"), or missing compliance and payment details.
</Note>

## 🗂️Introduction

Service Provider Profiles are a centralized hub for managing service provider information, skills, and interactions. These profiles provide essential details about services, skills, work orders assigned, vendor communication, and payment history. Knowing how to navigate these profiles effectively can help smoothen our operations, ensure the right vendor is chosen for a work order, and maintain a clear record of all their work and the communication we have with them.

This guide will walk you through the key sections of a service provider profile, highlighting how to interpret and utilize the information within each area. Whether you're updating vendor details, reviewing skills, or adding new notes, mastering navigating this profile will help optimize vendor management and ensure smooth workflows.

### **Step 3: Explore the Tabs**

The service provider profile contains several tabs, each offering detailed information.

![Image 20260610 201638](/images/image-20260610-201638.png "Image 20260610 201638")

**Details Tab**

<Frame>
  ![image-20260610-201818.png](/images/SVC-352.png)
</Frame>

- The **Skills** section outlines the vendor’s specific capabilities, allowing associates to confirm whether they can handle the requirements of a Work Order. Examples of listed skills might include HVAC services like "Repair A/C," "Replace Freon Charge," or "Thermostat Repair," as well as broader services such as "General carpentry work" or "Install new flooring." Associates must ensure these skills align with the job's needs before proceeding.
- The **Service Provider Status** section provides details about the vendor’s current standing, including whether they are part of the **Latchel Service Provider Network, Verified**, or **Blacklisted**, and the type of **Payment System** they use.
- The **Pricing** section breaks down the vendor’s charges, including their **Minimum Charge**, **Regular Rate**, **Evening Rate**, **Weekend Rate**, and **Emergency Rate**. Reviewing these details helps associates estimate costs and determine if the vendor's pricing fits within budget constraints for the job.
- **Verification Information** section is essential for ensuring that the vendor meets compliance and legal requirements before assigning them to a job. Details such as the vendor’s **Insurance Provider Name**, **Insurance Policy Number**, and **License Number**, are stored here.
- The vendor’s **Address** is listed in this section and is crucial for verifying that the vendor can service jobs near or within their ZIP code. Assigning jobs outside of their service area should be avoided to prevent logistical issues.
- Lastly is the \*\*Miscellaneous \*\*section which includes additional information, such as the vendor's **Website URL**, if available. This can provide further insights into their services and professional background.

### **Step 4: Add or Review Notes**

1. Navigate to the **New Note** section on the right-hand side of the Details tab.

   - Example: “The vendor called about issues with his password, account information was sent”

1. After making changes or reviewing notes, always save updates by clicking the **Save Note** button or updating the relevant fields.
2. Ensure your updates are clear, concise, and accurately reflect the situation to maintain an organized profile.

What should be done if a service provider has multiple profiles with conflicting or outdated information?

- **Profile alignment is required when a service provider has multiple profiles (such as LCN and preferred vendor profiles for different Property Managers) that contain conflicting, outdated, or incomplete information.** This includes—but is not limited to—discrepancies in contact details, identified skill gaps, or updates to profile information. Aligning profiles ensures accurate communication, correct job assignment, and consistent service delivery across all Property Managers.
  - Submit a PS ticket. refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support#how-to-escalate-to-product-support)
