## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  _We want to ensure all team members follow consistent, clear guidelines for basic service operations. The goal is to minimize errors, streamline communication, and maintain high standards by providing straightforward rules everyone can reference, saving time and reducing confusion across the team._
</Note>

<Note>
  ### What if the message was sent before or after Operations Hours?

  - If you're responding to a message early before 8am or from the night before, you can always apologize for missing their message (if appropriate) and reminding them of our operations hours. 
  - Communications outbound (when responding to after operations hours inbound communications) should stick to the hours outlined in this SOP.
</Note>

## 🔒Private Property Manager/Owner: Handling Private Information

<Danger>
  ❌ Never disclose the Property Manager/Owner's **private information (e.g. User’s Name (first or full), user and company phone number & email addresses)** **UNLESS** the information is explicitly authorized within the provided documentation—such as critical notes, pre-dispatch instructions, work order details, special notes, warranty information, or emergency escalation contacts—or the Property Manager/Owner has clearly instructed you to share it. Doing so is grounds for **termination.**

  Property Managers/Owner use and trust Latchel to take care of communication with their residents for any issues pertaining to maintenance coordination. Latchel gives Property Managers/Owners peace of mind by taking resident communication off of their plate, allowing them to focus on other business activities. **Under no circumstances should Latchel associates provide customers with Property Managers/Owners' phone numbers.** If a resident requests contact information for their Property Manager, please refer them to the phone number listed in their lease agreement.

  Please note that, in most cases, the Property Manager and the Property Owner are not the same person. For PM-managed properties, any resident requests to speak with the owner should be directed to the Property Manager. For DIY owners, the owner serves as their own Property Manager → refer to [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-DIY-Owner-Contacts).
</Danger>

## 🔒 **Service Provider Contact Information**

<Danger>
  ❌ Never give out a service provider’s personal or company contact information (e.g., phone number, email, or address) **unless the requester is registered in the system.** Sharing information with unverified individuals is a breach of policy.

  Service providers partner with us to coordinate work through our system. To maintain accountability and accurate tracking, details should only be shared with verified Residents, Property Managers, DIY Owners, or Owners listed in the platform.

  👉 If the requester is registered, you may provide the approved contact details. If they are not, politely explain that direct details cannot be shared and reassure them we will coordinate with the provider on their behalf.
</Danger>

## 🔒 **Sharing Work Order Information with Registered Users**

<Warning>
  Property Managers, Residents, and Service Providers each have access to a dashboard that determines what information they are authorized to view.

  ✅ **If the requester is a registered user with dashboard access:**

  - Before sharing any work order details, verify what information is visible to them in their dashboard.
  - You may provide or clarify **only the information that is already available in their dashboard view**.
  - If the information appears in their dashboard, it is considered approved to share.

  🚫 **If the requested information is not visible in their dashboard:**

  - Do not provide the information, even if they explicitly request it.
  - Politely explain that we’re unable to share details beyond what’s available in their dashboard.
  - Reassure them that Latchel will continue coordinating internally as needed to the registered customers in the system.
</Warning>

## **🕒**Operations Hours

Acceptable operations hours are defined as **Monday through Sunday from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm property time**. Please note that **holidays** are considered outside acceptable operations hours.

  <Accordion title="**📩**Follow-Up Procedures">
    <Note>
      The following process applies to follow-up tasks for all customer types. Please note that critical notes and pre-dispatch instructions (along with any other relevant notes) will take precedence over the procedures outlined here. Follow up tasks may be for scheduling, estimates, materials and unresolved issues.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      **For Multiple Follow Up Tasks:** When handling multiple follow-up tasks, if there is at least one work order related to Property Damaging or Extreme Habitability, you must adhere to the **triple dial procedure** for follow-ups. This ensures that urgent issues are addressed promptly.

      Always review all open tasks before proceeding. If a call has already been made for a specific task, you may skip that work order during your follow-up.
    </Warning>

    <Accordion title="Emergencies">
      - **Property Damaging/Extreme Habitability Issues**
        - Make **3** call attempts on a single follow-up when trying to reach customers (residents and service providers). These situations require immediate action to prevent further damage or ensure the safety of residents.
          - If the resident/service provider is reached:
            - If they request to be contacted the same day again, defer the task to the requested time.
            - If they do not agree to a follow-up and the issue remains Property-Damaging or Extreme Habitability, proceed with escalation by contacting the appropriate escalation contacts[.](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
          - If unsuccessful after the first attempt, send an SMS (if enabled), then call the escalation contacts.
            - If unsuccessful in calling the escalation contacts, refer to the next steps outlined in [this SOP](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs).
          - Send SMS to the resident or service provider about the outcome of your contact with the Property Manager
      - **Non-Property Damaging/Non-Extreme Habitability Issues**
        - Make **1** call attempt on a single follow-up to reach customers (residents and service providers). While these cases require timely communication, they do not pose an immediate threat to property or safety.
          - If the resident/service provider is reached and requests to be contacted again the same day, defer the task to the advised time.
          - If unsuccessful after the first attempt, send an SMS (if enabled) then defer the task to the next day for another follow-up.
            - If still unsuccessful after the second follow-up, complete the task and escalate to the Property Manager by creating a new task and assigning it to them.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Non-Emergencies">
      Make **1** call attempt on a single follow-up to reach customers (residents and service providers). This ensures communication is maintained without overwhelming customers with multiple calls for less urgent matters.

      - If the resident/service provider is reached and requests to be contacted again the same day, defer the task to the advised time.
      - If unsuccessful after the first attempt, send an SMS (if enabled) then defer the task to the next business day for another follow-up.
        - If still unsuccessful after the second follow-up, ensure the work order is on **Pending** status with a task assigned to the Property Manager.
    </Accordion>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🖇️ Cross-Training & Queue Scope">
    Cross-trained associates are expected to **work within the scope of their assigned queue(s) at all times**. Being cross-trained does not authorize associates to take on functions outside their current assignment. If an associate is assigned to Flex, they may be handling both SMS and TS, or CC and TS, during the same shift or block.

    **Queue Assignment Types:**

    - **Single-queue assignment:** The associate is assigned to one queue, such as SMS, TS, or CC. They handle that queue's work only.
    - **Flex assignment (SMS + TS):** The associate is explicitly assigned to handle both SMS and TS. They may work SMS while also being available for TS actions, but the proper task creation and handoff process still applies.
    - **Flex assignment (CC + TS):** The associate is explicitly assigned to handle both CC and TS. They may work CC while also being available for TS actions, but the proper task creation and handoff process still applies — with one added step CC requires that SMS does not (see below).

    **Key Rules:**

    - If you are assigned **SMS only**, you handle SMS work only — even if you are also trained for Troubleshooting (TS) or Call Center (CC).
    - If you are assigned **CC**, you handle CC work only — even if you are also trained for SMS or TS.
    - If you are assigned **Flex (SMS + TS)**, you are authorized to work both SMS and TS within that assignment.
      - If you capture a **TS action** on a work order while handling SMS, **create the TS task first** so the action is visible, trackable, and available for the TS team.
      - After creating a TS task, you may pick it up yourself if it is still available after finishing your SMS work.
      - ❌ Do not skip task creation just because you are trained for TS or intend to handle it yourself.
    - If you are assigned **Flex (CC + TS)**, you are authorized to work both CC and TS within that assignment.
      - **🛑 Diagnostic questions are not troubleshooting.** The diagnostic and simple clarifying questions needed to correctly identify the issue and create the WO are always in scope during the inbound call — this is intake, not troubleshooting.
      - ❌ Do not proceed with actual troubleshooting of the issue while still on the inbound call, even though you are cross-trained or Flex-assigned to TS.
      - ✅ Once the WO is created, **finish and end the current inbound call first**, then create the TS task so the action is visible, trackable, and available for the TS team.
      - ✅ After creating a TS task, you may pick it up yourself **by placing a new outbound call from the WO** — do not continue troubleshooting within the same inbound call. This matters for two reasons: (1) the troubleshooting call log must attach to the WO, which only happens on a call placed from the WO itself, and (2) the Emergency WO breach timer only stops once that first call from the WO is made — continuing on the original inbound call does not stop it.
      - ❌ Do not skip task creation just because you are trained for TS or intend to handle it yourself.
    - TS work belongs to the TS team. SMS work belongs to the SMS team. CC work belongs to the CC team. Flex assignments are the exception only when the associate is explicitly assigned to SMS + TS or CC + TS.
    - The expectation is to **act when a misclassification is apparent from what is already in front of you** (e.g., flagging, escalating, or creating the appropriate task), not to take over another queue's responsibilities or go looking for them outside your assignment.

    **‼️IMPORTANT:** If you encounter a situation where a task clearly belongs to another queue and you are not assigned to that queue or Flex, follow the established escalation or handoff process (e.g., posting in #ops_assistance_hub, tagging @support, or creating a task for the appropriate team). Do not self-serve.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="**⏳📞**Unresponsive Contacts and Invalid Phone Numbers">
    <Note>
      **Always leave a voice message** when there is an active voicemail and you cannot reach the person you are trying to contact.
    </Note>

    - If you attempt to contact a customer by phone and they are unresponsive or the number is invalid:
      - Attempt to reach the customer via **text message.**
      - If SMS is not possible or unsuccessful, send an **email**.
    - If the customer does **not** have an active phone number and **no** registered email on file:
      - **Document the failed contact** due to unavailable communication methods.
      - Proceed with the **applicable escalation path.**
        - If property damaging/extreme habitability condition, refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
          - If unresponsive, refer to **Step 3.e.ii.8** of [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold#how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)
        - If non property damaging/extreme habitability condition,
          - Keep the work order **status unchanged**.
          - Create a task Title as **_“Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed”_**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="**🚫📩**Undelivered Messages/Phone Number is Not Textable">
    - If you see a **warning icon** next to your latest message or the phone number is not textable
      - You should **attempt** reaching the contact **via phone call**
        - If they don’t answer and have no option to leave a message, **send them an email**
    - If you have called the phone number and identified it to be non-textable (e.g., SMS is disabled or the number is connected to a phone tree)
      - Leave a voicemail.
      - Send an email with the necessary details.
    - If you have committed to send an SMS follow-up or notify a service provider via text about work order details (e.g., dispatch information), follow these steps:
      - **Verify SMS Capability** **_during the call_**
        - Ask if the phone number on file can receive text messages.
        - Proceed Based on their response:
          - If the number is textable, send the message as needed.
          - If the number is not textable, ask for an alternative textable number.
            - If they provide one, send the message to that number.
            - If no alternative number is available, send the details via email instead.

    ### Active Communication Scenarios

    - **If the Customer is Responsive**:
      - If you are actively communicating with the customer and they acknowledge your last message, it means messages are successfully being sent.
      - **No Action Required**: If the customer has acknowledged the latest message, no further action is needed regarding message delivery. The message is considered delivered.

    ### Outbound Communication Without Immediate Response Required

    - **Sending Follow-Up Task Updates**:
      - If you are sending an outbound communication that does not require an immediate response (e.g., sending an SMS about a work order update):
        - **Wait 2 Minutes**: Allow 2 minutes to ensure there is no error and the message is delivered.
        - **Verify Delivery**: After the 2-minute wait, check for any delivery issues or errors. Then, end the conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="**📞❌If a Customer Hangs Up or the Call Gets Disconnected**">
    <Warning>
      For associates handling inbound calls, please notify the Operations Support Specialists before logging out to perform an immediate callback.

    If a customer hangs up or the call drops unexpectedly, here’s how to respond based on the tone and context of the interaction:

    2. **Leave a Voicemail (if available)**
       - Explain why you're calling and ask them to call back. Include your name, callback number, and any reference info.
    3. **Send a Follow-Up SMS/Email**
       - If they don’t answer, send a short, professional text to let them know the call was disconnected and ask for a callback.
       - **Note:** If SMS is disabled, proceed with email
         - _Hi [customer name]. It seems the call ended unexpectedly. We're following up to assist with your work order. We just need [briefly state the info needed] to move forward—please feel free to call us at  [PM Latchel-Assigned Phone Number for Residents/Service Providers/Unknown] [206-900-9466 for PMs]. Thank you!_
    4. **Leave a Note**
       - See [How to Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes#how-to-write-effective-notes).

    **😡 If the customer was irate and hung up:**

    Ask yourself: **Do we need information from the customer to move forward?**

    - **Yes:**
      - Follow the steps 1-4 above.
      - Then, document the attempt on the work order.
    - **No:**
      - Do **not** follow up. Simply document the interaction on the work order.
  </Accordion>

## NOMAD and RentRedi

NOMAD and RentRedi accounts have a very unique setup different from most other Property Managers in Latchel. NOMAD and RentRedi are third-party platforms that cater to homeowners and enable them to manage their rental properties through these platforms, which in turn use Latchel to coordinate the maintenance. As a result, properties under these accounts don’t have a traditional Property Manager since the Owners themselves act as Property Managers of their individual properties, therefore, for all intents and purposes, always treat the Owners as the Property Managers for these accounts.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should break this SOP only in rare situations where following the standard guidelines would cause harm, violate company policy, or prevent urgent resolution. For example:

- If following the SOP would delay response to an emergency or create a safety risk, escalate immediately and follow emergency protocols.
- If you encounter a scenario not covered by these guidelines, or you are unsure how to proceed, pause and escalate to your supervisor or Operations Support Specialists for guidance.
- If you make a mistake or cannot complete a required action after multiple attempts, immediately offer to transfer the case to a specialist for support.

_When in doubt, do not break the SOP on your own—always escalate for guidance. The goal is to maintain safety,_
