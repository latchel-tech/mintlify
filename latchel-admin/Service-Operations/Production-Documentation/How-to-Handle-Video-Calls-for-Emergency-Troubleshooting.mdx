## **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to prevent misdiagnosis, unnecessary service calls, and delays when troubleshooting emergency maintenance issues that can't be fully assessed over the phone. The goal is to ensure associates use video calls to visually confirm and diagnose problems in real time—improving accuracy, empowering residents to show the issue directly, and enabling better decisions about dispatching or resolving emergencies.
</Note>

For information on the Video Call technology, refer to this SOP: [How to: Use Video Call for Troubleshooting](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-Video-Call-for-Troubleshooting)

<Tip>
  Training Content

  The contents of this page have been included in the Video Troubleshooting and Troubleshooter Onboarding Coassemble courses.
</Tip>

### Process Overview

**What is happening prior to this process?**

The Latchel Troubleshooter cannot sufficiently assess or troubleshoot a maintenance request via phone call; the use of a video call would make troubleshooting easier or give the troubleshooter, or potential service provider, valuable information.

<Warning>
  ‼️ VERY IMPORTANT: Review information on the Video Call technology in this SOP: [How to: Use Video Call for Troubleshooting](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-Video-Call-for-Troubleshooting)
</Warning>

### Step 1 - Get the Resident Permission to Start the Video all and End Phone Call with the Resident

**Ask to begin a Video call**

You are on a phone call with the Resident and you find that visually seeing the maintenance issue would give higher probability to either: (1) Solve the entire issue with the resident, or (2) Be better informed to choose the correct type of Service Provider, or (3) Empower the Service Provider to have pertinent details to complete the job with more efficiency when they arrive.

- Ask the resident if the phone number you called is a smart phone.
  - If they mention that ✅ "Yes, it is a smart phone."

> Say something like, "Excellent! We have the capability to use video call to further troubleshoot this issue. It's straight forward to use and will not require you to download any application-- just click on a link in a text I will send you and the video call will start in your phone's web browser. Does this work for you?"

- If the resident says, ✅ "Yes, this works."

> Say something like, "Thank you! I'll send you the text now, did you get it?" (wait for confirmation)

<Note>
  📝 NOTE: Click "Create Video Chat Room" and ask the resident if to check that they received the text message before you hang up the phone!
</Note>

> You can also say something like, "Just a warning that as soon as you click on the link in the text message, the Video Call will automatically open your camera facing in front of you. See you there!" (then hang up and enter video chat)

- If the resident says, ❌ "No, I do not agree."

> Say something like, "Ok, we will take care of your maintenance request with the information we can get over the phone."

- If they mention ❌ "No, it is not a smartphone." -OR- ❌ "I don't know if my phone can handle video calls".

> Say something like, "Ok. I was going to offer the use of our video call system to further troubleshoot this issue. But no worries; we will take care of your maintenance request with the information we can get over the phone."

**Prepare for the Call**

The Latchel user will review information in the Technology SOP for Video Calls to begin the phone call: [How to: Use Video Call for Troubleshooting](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-Video-Call-for-Troubleshooting)

The call will automatically begin 🔴 recording as soon as the new browser window opens.

Latchel video guidelines include:

- Environment Set Up: If you turn the camera on, the background environment should be controlled (i.e. there aren't people walking behind you, non-distracting and noisy background).

<Note>
  📝 NOTE: The video 🔴 recording will show your video image in a small circle. However, be aware that the 🔴 recording captures the rectangular width of your camera space.
</Note>

- MUTE YOUR MICROPHONE! When the resident is speaking, noise in your environment should be controlled.

### Step 2- Begin the Video Call and Troubleshooting the Issue

**To learn more about Beginning the Video Call:**

Review information in the Technology SOP for Video Calls: [How to: Use Video Call for Troubleshooting](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-Video-Call-for-Troubleshooting)

<Warning>
  ‼️ IMPORTANT: Beware of expending too much time on the Video Call if it's not working; focus on taking care of the Emergency Work Order! Not all customers will have the capability to complete Video troubleshooting, or you might encounter issues with specific customers.
</Warning>

- Greet the customer by (1) introducing yourself again, (2) verifying that the customer can both see and hear you, (3) mentioning that the video call is being recorded.

<Warning>
  ‼️ IMPORTANT - You will need to provide verbal notification to the customers receiving the video call that calls are being recorded. This is a legal requirement and we need to comply with it.
</Warning>

**Latchel Blurb**

> Hello again, my name is (agent name). Can you hear me? Can you see me? To inform you, this video call is being recorded for quality and training purposes.

- Begin troubleshooting the maintenance request via Video Call.
  - Recap what you have already learned from the phone call.
  - Recap why it will be beneficial to use this video call function to further investigate into the issue.
  - Be specific about what you would like to see next and why.

> Say something like, "Okay, so I want to see \_\_\_\_ to see if it could be an issue with \_\_\_\_\_."

### Step 3 - End the Video Call

**To learn more about Ending the Video Call:**

- Tell the Resident how you will handle the Work Order now, given the information that you learned from the video call and troubleshooting discussions.
- Tell the Resident that they will be receiving a message that will allow them to rate you (the agent) on your troubleshooting skills. You can ask them and thank them for taking the time to review your work.

> Say something like, "After this call you will receive a very brief survey that helps Latchel to provide a better experience for our customers. The survey will ask you to rate my troubleshooting with you today. I would really appreciate if you took the time to complete it! Thanks!"

- Write Effective Notes into the Work Order Notes.

<Note>
  📝 NOTE: There is no option to create notes while in the Video Call browser window with the resident.

  You may enter the Work Order and use the Notes section of the Work Order (or another writing application, such as Google Docs) as a Notepad while you're on the Video Call.
</Note>

- Review information in the Technology SOP for Video Calls and how to End the Video Call and how to view 🔴 recording video and call log: [How to: Use Video Call for Troubleshooting](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Use-Video-Call-for-Troubleshooting)
