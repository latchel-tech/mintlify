## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
Associates need clear, fair, and supportive guidelines for taking time off when sick or caring for family members. Without structure, sick leave can be misused or misunderstood, creating gaps in coverage and stress for both associates and the team.

This process ensures:

- Associates can rest and recover without fear of losing their job.
- Supervisors have consistent rules for approval and documentation.
- Operations continue smoothly, even when absences occur.
- Mental health is valued alongside physical health.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **Step 1 - Sick Leave Entitlement & Accrual**

- Associates are entitled to **7 days of Sick Leave per year**.
- Sick Leave accrues **bi-weekly at 2.1536 hours per 80 hours worked**.
- If fewer than 80 hours are worked, accrual is adjusted.
- **Sick leave** must be used within the **calendar year** it is earned. Any **unused sick leave** will **expire at the end of the year** and will **not carry over** to the following year.
- Team members are not paid for unused sick leave upon termination of employment or contract.

---

### **Step 2 - Sick Leave Usage**

- Sick Leave may be used for:
  - **Personal illness.**
  - **Caring for a sick family member (Carer's Leave).**
  - **Mental Health or Rest Days** (with **5 days' advance request**).
- Management encourages associates to save Sick Leave for genuine health needs.

---

### **Step 3 - Extended Sick Leave (2+ Days)**

- For absences **longer than 2 days**:
  1. Use accrued Sick Leave first.
  2. If Sick Leave is insufficient, Supervisors *may* approve **PTO** to cover the balance. However, this is subject to the ***Supervisor's discretion***.
  3. Supervisors may reasonably require a **doctor's note** or medical verification.

---

### **Step 4 - Exhausted Sick Leave Credits**

- If all Sick Leave is used and no documentation is provided:
  - The absence will be recorded as **Unpaid Leave**.
- See [[**Measures for Attendance & Punctuality Guidelines SOP**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Measures-for-Attendance-&-Punctuality-Guidelines)] for more details on unpaid hours.

---

### **Step 5 - Supervisor Responsibilities**

- Approve or deny Sick Leave and emergency PTO requests fairly.
- Request verification (e.g., doctor's note) for absences of **2+ days**.
- Encourage associates to use Mental Health Days proactively to avoid burnout.

---

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Tip>
Escalate or deviate when:
</Tip>

- A medical emergency requires immediate hospitalization.
- An associate's situation is sensitive and requires discretion (e.g., mental health).
- Operations coverage is at risk and needs Ops Manager involvement.
