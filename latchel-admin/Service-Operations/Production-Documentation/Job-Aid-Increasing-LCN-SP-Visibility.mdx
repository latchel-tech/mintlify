## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

We want to prevent LCN service providers from being overlooked or underutilized in specific geographic areas due to system limitations on territory visibility. This process should only be used for ZIP codes, properties, or cities that are active properties or cities managed by Latchel. The goal is to provide Operations Support Specialists with a clear workaround process to artificially expand an LCN SP's coverage area—by creating dummy work orders only in Latchel-managed target locations—so these providers appear as "nearby" and are more likely to be matched with jobs.

Completing a dummy work order makes the SP "nearby" within a 25-50 mile radius, significantly increasing their visibility.

The system takes into account both the current address and addresses of previously completed work orders of the SP.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

**What happened prior to this step?**

An LCN service provider contacted us to request the addition of territories in their service area. A Latchel representative completed the [Request to Increase LCN SP Visibility](https://slack.com/shortcuts/Ft08LWKZ82HM/b447618f1f619f75f9892edda035eae9) workflow in the ops_assistance_hub channel to initiate the process.

**Blurb for SPs**

*Hi [SP Name]! We've received your request to add territories to your service area. Our team will review your current coverage and assess the feasibility of expanding into the requested territories. While we're in this process, if you've received notifications for test accounts, please disregard them. We'll let you know if any additional information is needed. Thank you for your patience!*

Latchel's system uses the service provider's company address and historical work order data to determine proximity to properties. Because LCN service providers don't have configurable service territories like Preferred or LVN SPs, this workaround allows OPs to artificially expand their perceived coverage area, boosting visibility within the platform.

**Sample Requests:**

*"HomeTech Pro LLC would like to add Chicago, IL, Charlotte, NC, St. Louis, MO to their service territories"*

### Identify the Target Area

In the above request, we've identified the city where increased visibility for the LCN service provider is desired.

**Option 1:** If the service provider provided a city, first confirm the city is currently managed by Latchel or has active Latchel-managed properties by referencing the [Active Properties Page](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/ca2bb475-b6c6-4836-8428-0a89f62d0b37/page/S79lC). Then use Google Search or Latchel property data to identify an appropriate ZIP code for that active Latchel-managed city/property.

### Assign the LCN SP

- Set the SP as a preferred vendor under Gordon Family Properties (Test).
- Manually assign the target LCN SP to the dummy work order.
- Confirm that the work order reflects the correct SP and target location.
- Once completed, remove the SP from Gordon Family Properties (Test) preferred vendor list.

### Complete the Work Order

- Mark the work order as "Completed" once it is created and assigned.
- If you're unable to mark the work order as complete due to an invoice requirement, please use the following workaround: impersonate the Service Provider's dashboard access. Refer to this [video tutorial](https://www.loom.com/share/e2a4d154587943708d580bb6e7e408b4?sid=50c82433-829a-4984-b836-7512a08ddf4d) for guidance.
- Optionally, add a private note (e.g., "Dummy WO to increase SP visibility in [ZIP code/City]") for internal reference.
- Repeat the process for the other target location.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
