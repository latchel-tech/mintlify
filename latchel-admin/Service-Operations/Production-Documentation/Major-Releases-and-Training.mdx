### Checklist for Major Releases Requiring Associate Training

This checklist ensures that all major process changes are clearly communicated, properly documented, and effectively adopted through structured training and post-launch monitoring.

1. **Change Overview & Alignment**
   1. Description of the change and its purpose
   2. Associates and teams impacted
   3. Timeline for rollout, including training start and completion dates
   4. Designated owners responsible for post-announcement monitoring and support
2. **Process Creation**
   1. Creation and finalization of the new or updated process documentation (SOP)
   2. Validation and approval of the process before rollout
3. **Process Announcement (Slack SOP Publication)**
   1. Formal communication of the new process to all impacted stakeholders
   2. Clear messaging on effective date, expectations, and where to find documentation
4. **Training Coordination & Completion:**
   1. Coordinate training schedules with Support (Support schedules training pull-outs since they manage real-time operations and determine optimal timing)
   2. Sample post [here](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/CUMBLQDRU/p1766023394130449).

**Key Points:**

1. Clear articulation of the goal and expected impact of the process change

1. Training start date and completion deadline
2. Duration of training pull-out (including SOP review and comprehension time)
3. List of involved stakeholders (supervisors, support, associates)
4. Training completion tracker ([spreadsheet sample](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1I4I99c7_V5ifwK1DsckkMumyuSFtuHuW9d3oO-gF-ZY/edit?usp=sharing))
