# 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  This process ensures that tasks, emails, calls, and SMS/webchats are properly staffed throughout a shift. It helps Operations Support Specialists determine when it’s appropriate to move team members between tasks or offer voluntary time off (VTO) while maintaining coverage and service levels.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **Schedule Adherence**

#### Step 1 - Deputy Inspection

1. Check Deputy to see if there are any agents marked as late or absent.

</Frame>

#### Step 2 - Operations Channel Review

1. If a post has been made to the #status\_updates Slack channel, Operations Support Specialists should inform the associate's supervisor on the #operations\_support Slack channel.

<Frame>
  ![image-20221029-105226.png](/images/SVC-79.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 3 - Supervisor Notification in Status Thread

1. If a post has been made to the #status\_updates Slack channel, Operations Support Specialists should inform the associate's supervisor on the same thread for reference.

<Frame>
  ![image-20221029-105439.png](/images/SVC-80.png)
</Frame>

#### Step 4 - No Status Update & Associate is Late

1. If no post has been made in the #status\_updates Slack channel and the associate is marked late or absent in Deputy for 5 or more minutes, Operations Support Specialists must submit a feedback ticket. Please refer to [**How to: Submit Associate Feedback Tickets**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Submit-Associate-Feedback-Tickets#how-to-submit-associate-feedback-tickets).

### **Staff Availability**

#### Step 2 - Task Switching and VTO

1. If the work order volume is low on the Troubleshooting team, Call Center team, and Ops Associate team, the Operations Support Specialists on duty should take the following action:
   1. **Troubleshooting team:**
   2. Check the available cross-trained individuals and move them to take incoming phone calls in Amazon Connect.
   3. If there are no available cross-trained individuals, then proceed to inform any person working OT that they are no longer needed for the day.
   4. If there are still low volumes in the queues, then proceed to offer VTO to the team that has rendered at least 4 working hours within the shift. See [(Operations Support Specialist) When to offer voluntary time-off (VTO)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-When-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-VTO#operations-support-specialist-when-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-vto)
2. **Operations Associates team:**
   1. If the task backlogs shown in the Dashboard are manageable by the number of ops associates present, inform any person working OT that they are no longer needed for the day.
   2. Proceed to offer VTO to the team that has rendered at least 4 working hours within the shift. See [(Operations Support Specialist) When to offer voluntary time-off (VTO)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-When-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-VTO#operations-support-specialist-when-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-vto)
   3. If the task backlogs shown on the Dashboard are NOT manageable by the number of ops associates present, move people from the least number of tasks to the highest number of backlogs.
3. **Call Center team:**
   1. Calls should be answered in less than 1 min to avoid calls rolling over to the call center.
   2. This depends on the number of associates assigned to phones. We should have enough associates to handle calls within 1 minute. If we constantly have more calls in the queue than associates available, we need to move labor to balance it or offer VTO. See [(Operations Support Specialist) When to offer voluntary time-off (VTO)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Operations-Support-Specialist-When-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-VTO#operations-support-specialist-when-to-offer-voluntary-time-off-vto)

# 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  Follow these steps in most situations, but exceptions can be made if:
</Note>

- Leadership directs a different staffing approach.
- Unexpected spikes in workload require immediate reallocation of staff.
- System tools (Dashboard, Deputy, or Slack) are unavailable or malfunctioning.
- Temporary policies or experiments are in place to optimize coverage or efficiency.
