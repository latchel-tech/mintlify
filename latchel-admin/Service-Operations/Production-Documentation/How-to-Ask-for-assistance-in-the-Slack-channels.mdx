## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
We want to ensure associates ask for help in Slack in an organized and detailed way. The goal of this SOP is to prevent confusion, delays, and incomplete information when seeking support.
</Info>

## 📋General Guide

- Review the issue whether it’s for an Emergency or non-emergency work order, email, SMS, etc.
- After the issue has been confirmed or identified, proceed to post the question, concern, or issue in the appropriate Slack channel.

1. **Prior to posting, ensure that all available resources have been thoroughly reviewed and utilized, including SOPs, previous Slack questions/posts, etc.**
2. Always include the links associated with the question, concern, or issue at the beginning of the post.
3. Provide a brief history relevant to the question, concern, or issue, and include the actions you have taken if there are any.
4. Ask for the specific help you need. If you have a suggestion for the next steps, please include a brief explanation of your reasoning. This will help Operations Support Specialists to quickly assess whether the suggestion aligns with the situation without needing to fully review the work order.
