## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

This SOP ensures that Operations Support Specialists can manage workflows, monitor queues, and adjust staffing in real time to keep operations running smoothly. It addresses risks like task bottlenecks, SLA breaches, or unbalanced workloads. By following this process, Latchel maintains high-quality customer service while optimizing associate resources and preventing delays or errors in work order handling.

## Process Overview


<Note>
The role of Operations Support Specialist is crucial in a team or company, as it plays a key part in ensuring seamless transitions between different shifts. This helps to keep tasks and operations running without interruption, along with monitoring SLAs all over the board.
</Note>


### Operations Support Specialist Reporting

The primary objective of the Operations Support Specialist role is to ensure a consistent workflow and maintain high-quality operations throughout various shifts. On certain days, there might be two Operations Support Specialists working during the same shift. To ensure their tasks don't overlap and to guarantee the smooth running of their shift, we allocate their responsibilities accordingly: one would be the _Primary Shift Owner, _and the other would be the _Shift Owner Support_. Here's how a day with two logged-in Operations Support Specialist members operates:


| **Primary Shift Owner:** | **Shift Owner Support: ** |
| --- | --- |
| Real-time monitoring of SLAs across the board.Real-time monitoring of associates' tasks and assignment queues.Reallocate resources (associates) based on aged and assignment volumes. Monitor schedule adherence and staff availability.Agent Assist Duty | Support the Shift Owner to make a successful shift.Handle work order hand-offs submitted by support/ overnight associates.Review and resolve orphaned work orders.Address work orders in bad status.Review and resolve Shift Support tasks on the console.Break Coverage |

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

This SOP is a guide for the daily cadence of support tasks. Support members can adjust the order or timing of tasks if real-time situations require it — for example, handling a sudden spike in emergency tickets, reassigning tasks based on associate availability, or when the **All Hands on Deck policy** is activated. The key is to maintain coverage, meet SLAs, and keep operations running smoothly.
