## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to ensure that requests from **non-residents**—such as property owners, utilities, service providers, or other third parties—who are reaching out for **non-maintenance requests** are handled consistently and accurately. The goal is to capture and route their messages to the appropriate recipient, while clearly communicating any limitations (such as not guaranteeing delivery or action).
</Note>

<Note>
🎓**Training Content**

The contents of this page have been included in the **Call Center Cross-Training **Coassemble course.
</Note>

**Content***

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
Although this SOP is written for inbound calls, the same guidelines apply when we receive **non‑resident, non‑maintenance requests** via **email or SMS**. Follow these steps to verify the sender, identify the property, capture the message, and route it appropriately.
</Note>

<Note>
This option can also be used if the caller is **NOT a resident of a Latchel-managed PM/Owner**. Follow “**Option 2: "Caller is Someone Else**“ to handle requests from these types of customers.
</Note>

## [**❓**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)**Frequently Asked Questions**

<Accordion title="What should Ops do if a non-resident caller contacts us regarding a non-maintenance request for a property that is not listed in the system, but the Property Manager is active in Latchel?">
Ops should first advise the caller to contact the Property Manager directly regarding their inquiry.

If the caller is unable to reach the Property Manager and it is still necessary to relay the information on their behalf, follow the steps below.

1. Send an email to the Property Manager’s notification group:
  1. Refer to[How to: Compose a New Email (Assembly)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Compose-a-New-Email-Assembly) for details.
    1. If you don’t have access to Assembly, relay the email request to Operations Support Specialist team.
    2. Refer to[How to: Ask for assistance in the Slack channels](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Ask-for-assistance-in-the-Slack-channels) for details.
    3. Refer to[How to: Locate the "Default Notification User Group" contact(s)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Locate-the-Default-Notification-User-Group-contacts) for details.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
