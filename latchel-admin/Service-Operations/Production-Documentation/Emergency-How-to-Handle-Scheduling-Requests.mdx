## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that urgent scheduling requests for emergency work orders are managed quickly, consistently, and with clear communication. The goal of this SOP is to provide a standardized, step-by-step process for securing appointments with service providers and residents when emergencies arise. This process prevents delays, miscommunication, and missed appointments—reducing risk to residents and property, and ensuring that emergencies are resolved as fast as possible.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The following process applies when a task is created for Troubleshooters to schedule a work order. Scheduling during dispatch must adhere to the guidelines outlined in [this document](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders), ensuring that a service provider is assigned to a work order only after they confirm an appointment.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  For LCN service providers exhibiting observable behaviors—such as missed appointments or unreasonable excuses—that result in scheduling delays, ensure to update their critical note to reflect the missed appointment or scenario encountered. Follow the format below, as it will be used in the future to determine whether we can continue offering jobs to the service provider.

  **Critical Note Format:**

  📌 _Date | WO# | Missed Appointment/Unreasonable Behavior_

  If they miss **three consecutive WO appointments or unacceptable behaviors**, their profile can be disabled. Associates are expected to check recent work orders to identify any recently completed WOs, which may indicate a successful appointment. Refer to the 3rd step in [https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3265232903](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3265232903) to disable a service provider’s account. Ensure to leave notes as well why they were disabled.
</Warning>

<Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

Emergency work orders typically do not have the status of "Scheduling with Service Provider," as these tasks are manually created based on the specific needs of the work order. Scheduling tasks usually arise from recent changes, such as a service provider being assigned by the Property Manager, an appointment being rescheduled, budget approval, or when maintenance is rescheduled after materials or parts are received. To ensure these scheduling requests are being addressed, a task is created and assigned to the troubleshooting team for action. Below are the steps the team can follow when scheduling with residents and service providers.

### Step 1. **Calling the Service Provider and Resident**

1. **Check Resident Availability**: Verify if the resident has provided their availability in the Resident’s Schedule tab.
2. Contact the Resident (if needed):
3. If no availability is listed, or the “**Permission for Service Provider to Access Property”** toggle is enabled, but the access instructions are \*\*vague \*\*or \*\*unclear \*\*(e.g., “please knock on the door”), reach out to the resident to gather their preferred scheduling times and access details before proceeding.
4. Call the Service Provider using the Latchel Console ( [↔ How to: Make Outbound Calls Within the Latchel Console](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=OM&title=%28TECH%29%20How%20to%3A%20Make%20Outbound%20Calls%20Within%20the%20Latchel%20Console)) and follow the provided script below, replacing the placeholder fields with specific details.
5. **📝** \*\*NOTE: \*\*Refer to [https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules) on our policy for follow up attempts frequency.
6. **Preferred**:
7. **Say something like**: _Hello! This is (insert Associate Name), I'm calling on behalf of (insert PM Company Name). I am reaching out to you today because I see that we have a job assigned to you, but we still need to confirm an appointment with you. The resident has availability at (Date and Time from Resident availability list) Do any of those times work for you?_
8. **LCN/LVN**:
9. **Say something like**: _Hello! This is (insert Associate Name), I'm a maintenance coordinator calling on behalf of (insert PM Company Name). ## 💰Scheduling after Budget Approval

In many cases, Service Providers may require budget approval to proceed with additional work on a job. Once the budget is approved, a work order must be scheduled. Below are the guidelines to follow when scheduling after budget approval. These steps also apply if there is a note on the work order indicating that the Property Manager has already approved the estimate.

1. From the Existing Work Order Interface, change the numbers in the "Approved Budget" to reflect the requested amount

<Frame>
  ![](/images/SVC-942.png)
</Frame>

2. If the issue **is** property damaging or extreme habitability, an appointment should be scheduled for that **same day**.

- In case the resident or the service provider doesn’t have availability within this timeframe and permission to enter was not granted, proceed to contact the emergency escalation contacts.
  - Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
- If no response from the escalation contacts, escalate to Operations Support Specialists (refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams) ) and request them to **move the WO to Waiting for PM status**.

3. If the issue \*\*is not \*\*property damaging or extreme habitability, an appointment should be scheduled for that **same day or the next day**.

- In case the resident or the service provider doesn’t have availability within this timeframe and permission to enter was not granted, create a task for the Property Manager
  - Subject: **Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed**\*.\*
  - Who is this task about? _Select the PM_
  - Select a Date and Time: _Leave as is_
  - Assign to a User Group: _PM user group_
  - Assign to a User: _Leave as is_
  - Priority:\* Leave as is\*
  - _Complete task on any job state change._
  - Description: _We’ve created this task for the Property Manager to \[insert specific information or instructions needed\]. The current status of the work order is \[insert status\]. Kindly provide the requested information or guidance at your earliest convenience to help ensure a smooth continuation of the process. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Thank you!_

## 📅➡️📅Handling a multi-day work order

1. If a job is expected to take multiple days and the service provider has confirmed the start and completion date, create a single appointment window that covers the entire duration, up to a maximum of 7 days.
2. **📝** \*\*NOTE: \*\*If the service provider is unable to provide an exact time, negotiate an estimated duration and set a placeholder appointment accordingly.

<Frame>
  ![image-20250409-231204.png](/images/SVC-943.png)
</Frame>

2. Let the Service Provider know they are expected to coordinate with the resident on a daily basis.
3. **Say something like**: _“Hey \[SP Name\], we've set a multi-day appointment window to give you flexibility. Please reach out to the resident directly each day to confirm your arrival time. Let us know if anything changes so we can keep the job notes up to date!"_
4. Add a \*\*special \*\*note to the work order to indicate it’s a multi-day job, so any follow-up agents are aware. If the SP shares an update (delay, schedule change, etc.), log it in the notes so future agents are in the loop.

<Accordion title="Reassessing Work Order Priority During Scheduling">
  In some cases, the **severity of a work order** may change during the scheduling process. This can occur in scenarios such as:

<Accordion title="PM directs us to assign a preferred vendor and instructs us to let the SP schedule directly with the resident (applies to ER and PD/EH issues)">
  This applies when, during PM outreach for an ER or PD/EH work order (e.g., a non-responsive resident situation), the PM:

  - Instructs us to assign the work order to one of their preferred vendors, **and**
  - Explicitly advises that the SP should coordinate the appointment directly with the resident (i.e., we are not expected to follow up on scheduling), **and**
  - Does **not** request that the WO be forwarded to PM.

  **Important:** Do **not** de-escalate the WO. The issue remains an emergency (ER/PD/EH) and must retain its emergency status for the following reasons:

  - The underlying property risk has not changed — only the coordination responsibility has shifted.
  - De-escalation during 24/7 / after-hours coverage will cause the WO to be auto-forwarded to the PM, which is not the intended outcome.
  - The PM has not asked us to hand the WO back to them; they have only asked us to step back from active scheduling coordination.

  **Steps to follow:**

  1. **Assign the WO to the PM's specified preferred vendor.**

  - Update the assignment in the system and ensure the SP is properly notified of the dispatch.
  - Add a clear note documenting the PM's instruction, including: the PM's name, date/time of the instruction, the named vendor, and the explicit direction that the SP will schedule directly with the resident.

  2. **Leave the WO in its current emergency status** (Scheduling, Pending, or whichever active status applies).
  3. **Create a task on the WO and assign it to the PM** to transfer scheduling accountability. Make sure the PM is aware.

  - **Task title:** "PM-directed: SP scheduling directly with resident — confirm appointment"
  - **Task body should include:**
    - Confirmation that the WO was assigned to \[Vendor Name\] per the PM's instruction on \[date/time\].
    - A note that, per the PM's direction, the SP is coordinating the appointment directly with the resident.
    - A request that the PM confirm the scheduled appointment back to us once known, or notify us if intervention is needed.
    - A flag that this is an ER / PD/EH situation and the WO remains in emergency status.
  - Assign the task to the PM contact.

  4. **Confirm dispatch with the SP (one-time call).**

  - Call the SP once to confirm they received the dispatch and acknowledge they will schedule directly with the resident.
  - If the SP is unresponsive to the initial dispatch confirmation, follow [https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules) for the dispatch attempt only.

  5. **No ongoing scheduling follow-up by Troubleshooters** once the PM task is created and the SP has acknowledged dispatch.

  - Re-engage only if: (a) the resident reports a concern, (b) the SP contacts us for help, (c) the PM responds to the task, or (d) the WO ages without an appointment being confirmed.

  6. **Proceed to [https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Emergency-How-to-Handle-Scheduling-Requests).**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if a PM-assigned service provider's earliest availability is outside the 24-hour emergency timeframe (e.g., 2–3+ days out)?">
  This applies when the PM has assigned a service provider to an emergency work order, and during the scheduling call the SP confirms their earliest availability is **more than 24 hours out** — exceeding the emergency timeframe.

  1. **Confirm and document the SP's earliest availability.**

  - Note the SP's stated soonest date/time in the work order.

  2. **Attempt to reconcile availability.**

  - If the resident has provided availability, check whether any of the resident's windows fall within the 24-hour emergency timeframe. If the SP cannot accommodate any of them, proceed to Step 3.
  - If the resident has not provided availability (or was unresponsive), note this and proceed to Step 3.

  3. **Escalate to the Property Manager based on severity:**

  - **For property-damaging or extreme habitability (PD/EH) situations:**
    1. Call the emergency escalation contacts immediately.     2. Inform the PM that the assigned SP's soonest availability is outside the 24-hour emergency window and request instructions — e.g., whether to wait for the SP, reassign to another provider, or take alternative action.

    4. If escalation contacts are unresponsive, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and request them to move the WO to **Waiting for PM** status following approved scenario **8**.
