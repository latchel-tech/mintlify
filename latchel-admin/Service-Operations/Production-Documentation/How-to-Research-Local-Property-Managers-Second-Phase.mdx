<Note>
  We want to prevent missed sales opportunities, wasted effort, and inconsistent or incomplete data by providing a clear, step-by-step process for enriching and verifying information on local property managers identified in the first research phase. The goal is to ensure associates gather and document accurate, comprehensive company and key personnel details—such as contact info, property types managed, software used, and reviews—so Latchel's sales and partnerships teams can efficiently identify, contact, and convert promising prospects.
</Note>

This is the second phase of the initial task on [How to: Research Local Property Managers](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers).

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

A video walkthrough is available [here](https://www.loom.com/share/d58dee9589d7412abe2a521de475888f) at time stamp 3:48.

<Tip>
  If the information is unavailable, leave the cell blank and proceed to the next required item.
</Tip>

1. Open this [spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1l_b6h8a1sfz4f1kisH7aBmrj5JCnhdxkg7c51JMavIk/edit) and navigate to the "Enriched Companies" tab.
2. Fill in the following columns with the information from the " Sourced Companies" sheet.
   - Existing Customer
   - Sourced Companies
   - Types of Properties Managed
   - Number of Vacancies
   - Software Used
   - Google Review Count
   - Google Rating
   - Key Personnel First Name
3. In this section, we need to ensure the following fields are completed:
   1. **Company's Information**
      1. Company Phone Number - This can typically be found on the company's website, either on the homepage or under the "Contact Us" page.
         <Frame>
           ![Company phone number on homepage](/images/SVC-153.png)
         </Frame>
         <Frame>
           ![Company phone number on Contact Us page](/images/SVC-154.png)
         </Frame>
      2. Company Website - Copy the actual url found in Column B - "Sourced Company Name" to this column as the website may already be linked to the PM company name.
         - If the website isn't listed, you can also visit the Property Manager's official website by manually searching on Google or another search engine.
      3. Company's Address - This is usually located on the company's "About Us", "Contact Us" page or on the footer of the home page. Break down the full address into the following fields:
         - Company Street
         - Company City
         - Company State
         - Company Zip Code
      4. Company LinkedIn - On the company's website, this information is sometimes accessible through a LinkedIn icon, which links directly to their LinkedIn page. You can also try searching for this manually using Google or another search engine.
         <Frame>
           ![Company LinkedIn icon](/images/SVC-155.png)
         </Frame>
      5. Company # of employees
         <Tip>
           Helpful Tip: You can check the company's LinkedIn page to find the number of employees listed.
         </Tip>
         <Frame>
           ![Number of employees on LinkedIn](/images/SVC-156.png)
         </Frame>
         1. You may also find this information on the company's website, typically under the "About Us" or "Our Team" page.
      6. Contacts - The information gathered during the first round of research on local PMs is entered under "Key Personnel First Name". This needs to be reviewed and broken down into the following required fields:
         <Frame>
           ![Key Personnel First Name field](/images/SVC-157.png)
         </Frame>
         1. If the information isn't already provided, you'll need to conduct a manual search using resources such as the company's website, LinkedIn (Contact Info section), Google, social media platforms, and other relevant sources to find the necessary details.
            <Frame>
              ![Manual search for contact details](/images/SVC-158.png)
            </Frame>
            <Frame>
              ![Manual search for contact details](/images/SVC-159.png)
            </Frame>
            <Note>
              Reminder: We are specifically looking for personal contact information of key personnel, not the company's general contact details. Spend 3–5 minutes searching for this information before moving on to the next PM company.
            </Note>
         2. If you find multiple key personnel contacts, simply add another row to enter their details accordingly.
            1. Key Personnel First Name
            2. Key Personnel Last Name
            3. Key Personnel Job Title
            4. Key Personnel Email - This is one of the most important fields, as it enables our Sales team to send marketing emails directly to the PM company and provides a primary means of contact with the Property Manager.
            5. Address
            6. Key Personnel Phone Number - If you can't find a direct number for the individual and it happens to be the same as the company's main phone number, that's perfectly fine. Just enter the company's number in this field.
            7. Key Personnel LinkedIn
      7. Associate Name - enter your name in this field.
      8. Date of Entry (MM/DD/YY) - enter the date of sourcing.
