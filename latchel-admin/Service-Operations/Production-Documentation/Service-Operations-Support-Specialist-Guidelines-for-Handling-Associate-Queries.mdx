## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  This process makes sure associate queries are answered clearly, promptly, and consistently.

  - Associates review applicable SOPs first before escalating.
  - Support builds on that review to give faster, more accurate guidance.
  - Together, this prevents errors, saves time, and keeps our service quality high.
</Info>

## What is happening prior to this process?

<Info>
  When associates need guidance or face access limitations, they first:

  1. Check applicable SOPs in the Service Operations space.
  2. Review available resources.
  3. Confirm if Support intervention is needed.

  If the issue remains unresolved, the query is submitted through escalation channels and tracked in Assembly. Support then responds systematically—ensuring queries are visible, assigned, and resolved consistently.

  For more information on how agents and other users escalate issues to Operations Support Specialists, refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams#how-to-escalate-between-teams)
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

**Access the Ticket**

1. Go to Assembly → Slack Support Ticket teamspace.
2. Check the Needs Response folder and any unassigned tickets.
3. For handling details, "[How to Respond to an Assembly Ticket](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Respond-to-Assembly-Tickets-and-Forward-Requests#how-to-respond-to-assembly-tickets-and-forward-requests)".

**Review the Ticket**

1. Read the description carefully.
2. Check attached files, screenshots, or links for full context.

<Note>
  **Goal:** Understand what the associate needs before taking action.
</Note>

**Clarify Missing Information**

- If details are incomplete or unclear, reach out quickly.
- Ask focused questions to avoid back-and-forth delays.

**Respond Clearly and Professionally**

- Keep answers clear, concise, and respectful, fostering a positive interaction.
- Provide step-by-step guidance that's easy to follow.

<Tip>
  Include relevant SOPs or job aids to empower the associate to handle similar situations independently in the future.
</Tip>

**Closing the Ticket**

1. Once you've resolved the issue or provided the necessary assistance, mark the ticket as closed.
2. Update ticket labels, including tags and categories, to reflect the nature of the query and resolution.
3. Ensure that all necessary documentation is included for future reference.

**Handling Reopened Tickets**

- Watch for follow-ups—tickets may reopen automatically.
- Always check who it's assigned to before responding to avoid duplication.
- Continue assisting until the issue is fully resolved.

<Note>
  **Why this matters:** Following these steps ensures queries are handled efficiently, professionally, and with care, while also leaving clear documentation to guide future problem-solving.
</Note>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

This SOP is your guide for most queries, but we trust you to use judgment when exceptions come up. Break from the standard process if:

**High-priority or urgent issues**

If it's critical (SLA breach, major customer impact, emergency work order), handle it right away—even if it means pausing other, less urgent tickets.

**System or access limitations**

If Assembly, Slack, or related dashboards are down, note your attempt and resume as soon as access is restored.

👉 The goal is always the same: keep operations moving and protect service quality.
