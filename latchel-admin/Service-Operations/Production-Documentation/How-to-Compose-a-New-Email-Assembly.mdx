## **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to prevent missed, delayed, or inconsistent email communications with customers and partners by providing a clear, step-by-step process for composing and sending emails through Assembly. The goal is to ensure every email is created, tracked, and sent correctly—using the right templates, sender address, and documentation steps—so that responses are timely, professional, and easy to follow up on.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Creating an Issue (This is not the actual message that is sent to the customer)

1. Navigate to the "Operations Email Team" Teamspace and click on Interactions.
   1. <img
        src="/images/image-77.png"
        alt="Image"
        title="Image"
        style={{ width:"65%" }}
      />
2. In the top-right corner, click Create Issue, then select Create Single Issue.
   1. ![Image](/images/image-78.png "Image")
3. Enter the required details:
   1. **Issue Title** – The subject of your email.
   2. **Starting Message** – This could be a summary of what the email is about. It is recommended to include the purpose of the email upfront to avoid the need for an additional internal note later.
   3. **Status** – Leave this as Open when creating the ticket.
   4. **Assignee** – Select your name.
   5. **Teamspace** – Ensure that the team owner is "Operations Email Team"
   6. Click Create Issue.
      1. ![Image](/images/image-79.png "Image")
4. Once created, the ticket will appear in your Inbox (if assigned to you) or in the designated Teamspace.
   1. <Tip>
        💡 Tip: You can locate the ticket later by searching for unique details in the search bar at the top of the page.
      </Tip>
   2. <Tip>
        💡 Tip: If you don't see your recently created issue, try navigating to the "General" teamspace and searching using unique keywords from your submission.
      </Tip>
   3. ![Image](/images/image-80.png "Image")

### Sending the Email

1. Open the newly created ticket. Ensure both the Owner and Team Group fields are set correctly.
2. Start the timer.
3. In the Email Body textbox, type your message.
   1. <img
        src="/images/image-81.png"
        alt="Image"
        title="Image"
        style={{ width:"91%" }}
      />
4. Signatures & templates have moved to the bottom-right corner of the editor.
   1. <img
        src="/images/image-83.png"
        alt="Image"
        title="Image"
        style={{ width:"77%" }}
      />
   2. 📝 NOTE: Remember to always click the "Signature" button when composing emails or responses to add your signature.
      1. ![Image](/images/image-85.png "Image")
   3. If you haven't set your signature, you can add them here: [https://www.dashboard.askassembly.app/preferences/profile](https://www.dashboard.askassembly.app/preferences/profile).
5. Click the arrow-down button next to Send and select Send New Email.
   1. A message box will appear—fill in the required details:
      1. To – Enter the recipient's email.
      2. From
         1. [emergency@latchel.com](mailto:emergency@latchel.com) should not be used. We are set up to receive specific emails through the Emergency Admin teamspace only.
         2. Use [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com) for all outbound emails.
            - ![Image](/images/image-84.png "Image")
      3. Subject – Remove the "Re:" to make it appear as a new email.
         1. ![Image](/images/image-86.png "Image")
6. Click Submit.
   1. A confirmation message will appear at the bottom of the page, indicating that the email was successfully sent.
      1. ![Image](/images/image-87.png "Image")
   2. Additionally:
      1. The original ticket will be updated with an automated note confirming the email was sent.
         1. ![Image](/images/image-88.png "Image")
      2. A new ticket will be created with the sent message.
         1. ![Image](/images/image-89.png "Image")
7. **Ticket assignment**
   1. **Original ticket** - Unassign the ticket to yourself.
   2. **Newly created ticket (email sent to recipient)** - Ensure the assignee is set to 'Unassigned' once you've completed your action. This ensures that any customer response will return to the queue, allowing it to be picked up by the next available email specialist.
   3. In the internal note section, indicate the WO link that the email is associated with
   4. On the corresponding WO, add the details of the Assembly ticket related to the email sent (if applicable). Please refer to [How to: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes#how-to-write-effective-notes)
8. Change both ticket's status to 'Closed' once you've completed your notes.
9. Stop the timer.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
