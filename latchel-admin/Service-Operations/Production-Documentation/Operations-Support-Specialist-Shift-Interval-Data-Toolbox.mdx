## Process Overview

In the process of meeting [Service Level Agreements (SLAs)](https://help.latchel.com/\(Operations-Support-Specialist\)-SLA-Review-workflow#sla-metrics), one of the critical challenges organizations face is addressing "missed SLA intervals." A missed SLA interval occurs when the agreed-upon service levels are not met within the specified time frame, leading to potential repercussions on customer satisfaction and overall business performance. To effectively combat and prevent missed SLA intervals, the use of powerful information-gathering tools becomes paramount. These tools act as the compass that guides Operations Support Specialists toward understanding the root causes behind the lapses, analyzing patterns, and making informed decisions to rectify the situation promptly.

In the table that follows, we will dive into the features and functionalities of these tools, exploring how they aid in capturing essential data points, tracking performance metrics, and diagnosing the factors contributing to missed SLA intervals. Our objective is to empower Operations Support Specialists and teams with the insights needed to respond promptly and effectively, ensuring that exceptional service delivery remains at the core of our operations.

## When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  This SOP is designed for standard SLA monitoring, but exceptions may require adjusting the process:

  - **System or reporting delays / new monitoring tools:** If dashboards or reporting tools are unavailable or delayed, rely on alternative sources (e.g., Latchel console, direct agent feedback). Likewise, if a new tool has been introduced to monitor SLA for specific queues, adapt your monitoring to include this tool, even if it temporarily interrupts the regular cadence.

  The guiding principle is to maintain SLA integrity and customer service quality, even if it means temporarily deviating from the routine monitoring cadence.
</Note>
