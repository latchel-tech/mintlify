## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent delays, confusion, and service disruptions when handling work orders assigned to Latchel Vendor Network (LVN) service providers. The goal is to ensure that all interactions with LVN vendors—who operate under special agreements and may use subcontractors—are managed consistently, with clear escalation paths for unresponsiveness, requests for reassignment, or special situations like recalls and cancellations.
</Note>

## What is the Latchel Vendor Network (LVN)?

<Note>
  **Internal Note:** Latchel Vendor Network (LVN) partners hold exclusive agreements with Latchel. It’s important to note that these agreements involve large parent companies **that hire subcontractor technicians to perform job tasks.**

  Learn more about Latchel’s Vendor Network [here](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Latchel-Vendor-Network-FAQs).
</Note>

The Latchel Vendor Network describes a group of service provider (i.e. vendor) companies that Latchel has a special arrangement with. In return for being offered real work orders, LVN vendors agree to:

- use Latchel technology to interact with all work orders
- get paid exclusively via Latchel’s direct deposit payout technology
- guarantee all technicians are vetted, licensed, bonded and insured
- offer free recalls to Latchel customers

## How can I tell if a vendor is part of the LVN?

When viewed on a work order page, an LVN service provider will have a “Latchel Vendor Network” tag next to their name.

## **Unresponsive LVN Service Providers (Active Work Orders)**

<Note>
  If the complaint from the PM or Owner warrants escalation to Product Support, please submit a PS escalation ticket. **NO NEED**to post in the #lvn-escalations channel. You can refer to the list of escalation types that should be sent to Product Support here: 🔗 [How to Escalate to Product Support – Examples of types of escalations](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)
</Note>

For both active and completed work orders, associates may occasionally deal with unresponsive LVN service providers during follow-ups. The following guidelines outline how to handle these situations:

1. **Active Work Orders**
   1. The LVN company is unresponsive to a follow-up call.
      1. If an appointment has occurred, meaning, the LVN SP has already visited the property:
         1. **Note: Keep the current status of the work order.** 
         2. **Escalate to Operations Support Specialists. Refer to**[How to: Escalate Between Teams](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams) 
            1. **Operations Support Specialists**will review the post and if necessary, they will escalate to the LVN SP’s (Puls or Keepe) slack channel for further action. Refer to [https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3203203103#Operations-Support-Escalation-to-LVN-SP%E2%80%99s-channel](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/https//latchelatlassiannet/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3203203103Operations-Support-Escalation-to-LVN-SPE28099s-channel) for guidance.
      2. If an appointment has not occurred, meaning, the LVN SP has NOT visited the property:
         1. Emergency Work Orders
            1. **“_Apologies, but the appointment needs to be scheduled today. Otherwise, we'll have to reassign the job, and the service fee will not be paid. Please let us know how you'd like to proceed.”_**
            2. If the LVN has not scheduled a new appointment within the day
               1. Reassign the WO. Refer to [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider) for details.
         2. Non-emergency Work Orders 13. “_Apologies, but the appointment needs to be scheduled by tomorrow. Otherwise, we'll have to reassign the job, and the service fee will not be paid. Please let us know how you'd like to proceed.”_
            1. If the LVN SP was able to confirm or schedule a new appointment:
               1. Take action and update the WO with the new appointment.
            2. If the LVN still has not been scheduled the next day
               1. Reassign the WO. Refer to [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider) for details.
2. **Completed Work Orders**
   1. For **recall** requests, please refer to [How to: Identify and Handle a Recall WO](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-and-Handle-a-Recall-WO)

## **PM/Owner requests to remove the LVN company from the job**

1. **Note: Always keep the current status of the work order for the below scenarios.**
2. Proceed to inform the PM about the benefits of keeping the LVN company:
   1. 💠 Suggested Scripting: **“_We understand your concerns about the current service provider. It looks like this job has been assigned to a Latchel vendor company. We can reach out to our partner company to ensure the specific technician involved is never assigned to your property again, while still keeping Latchel’s Vendor Network active in your account. Would you like us to reassign the work order to a different technician within the same company, thereby maintaining the warranty and guarantee?”_**
      1. The PM agrees to keep the same LVN company but wants Latchel to send out a different technician:
      2. 💠 Suggested Scripting: **“_I understand your concerns. As the next step, we'll request the company to reassign the work order to a different service provider._**
         1. To get a different technician assigned, call the service provider on the phone number indicated in the Service Provider’s profile and request for them to assign a different technician as requested by the Property Manager.
            1. If the service provider has responded and agreed to the request, update the work order notes to document that the request has been confirmed.
            2. If the service provider is unresponsive to the attempts
               1. Escalate to Operations Support Specialists. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams)
            3. **Operations Support Specialists**will review the post and if necessary, they will escalate this to the LVN SP’s (Puls or Keepe) slack channel for further action. Refer to [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers) for guidance.
      3. PM disagrees with keeping the LVN company and wants a different company dispatched for the WO in question (e.g. Keepe is assigned but they want to assign Puls instead) or if the PM wants to dispatch their own service provider or wants Latchel to assign a different service provider:
         1. If the LVN-assigned company has already been to the property.
            1. Suggested Scripting: **“_Hi \[PM’s Name\]! We’ll need to proceed with a new work order to assign a different service provider to ensure a smooth billing process moving forward. Please note that any warranties from the previous provider may no longer apply, and the original provider may bill you for any initial services rendered. The original work order will be marked as cancelled. Let us know if we can assist with anything else. Have a great day.”_**
            2. Cancel the original work order and make sure a notification (system generated is okay) is sent to the LVN service provider. Once that’s done, proceed with creating a new work order to assign the new service provider
         2. If the LVN-assigned company has not been to the property
            1. Suggested Scripting: “_I understand your concerns. As the next step, we'll remove the currently assigned service provider and we will proceed with reassigning the work order to a different service provider. Let us know if we can assist with anything else. Have a great day.”_
            2. Proceed to reassign the work order. Refer to[(TECH 101) How to: Reassign a Service ProviderHow to: Reassign a Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider).
      4. PM instructed to remove the LVN company but did not provide further instructions on the next steps
         1. **Note**: Ops needs to clarify with the PM the next steps they would like to take to move the work order forward.
         2. If the PM insisted on just removing the LVN company
            1. For emergency WOs
               1. Suggested Scripting: **“_I understand your concerns. As the next step, we'll remove the currently assigned service provider and the work order will be placed in 'Pending' status. Please note that this job is classified as an emergency and no further action will be taken by Latchel while we await your further instructions. Please let us know if there's anything else we can assist with. Have a great day!"_**
               2. Move the work order to 'Pending' to enable the option to remove the LVN company.
               3. Remove the assigned service provider and once that step is completed, create a task to the Property Manager
                  - Subject: **Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed**.
                  - Who is this task about? _Select the PM_
                  - Select a Date and Time: _Leave as is_
                  - Assign to a User Group: _PM user group_
                  - Assign to a User: _Leave as is_
                  - Priority: Leave as is
                  - _Complete task only on Completed or Cancelled job States_
                  - Description: _We’ve created this task for the Property Manager to \[insert specific information or instructions needed\]. The current status of the work order is \[insert status\]. Kindly provide the requested information or guidance at your earliest convenience to help ensure a smooth continuation of the process. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Thank you!_
            2. For Non-emergency WOs
               - Acknowledge the request
               - Suggested Scripting: **_“I understand your concerns. As the next step, we'll remove the currently assigned service provider. Please note that the work order will remain in Pending status while we await your further instructions. Please let us know if there's anything else we can assist with. Have a great day!"_**
               - Place the work order on Pending status with a task to PM
               - Remove the assigned service provider.
      5. PM wants to blacklist an LVN company
         1. Suggested Scripting: **“_I understand your concerns. At this time, we do not have the ability to blacklist specific LVN companies however you may choose to opt out or turn this off. Please let us know if you agree to turn this off so we can escalate the request to Customer Success.”._**
         2. If the PM agrees to turn it off, submit a Product Support Ticket
            1. Refer to[How to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)
      6. In scenarios where the PM expresses dissatisfaction, escalate to Product Support
         1. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product SupportHow to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)

## PM doesn’t want to work with an LVN company ever again:

1. 💠 Suggested Scripting: “_Okay, I understand. I’m unable to do that on my end, but I’ll escalate this to our Product Support team. They’ll be able to help you with this type of request.”_
2. Proceed to escalate to Product Support
   1. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)

## Resident requests

If a resident requests to remove an LVN-assigned SP from a work order, Ops must escalate this to the Property Manager.

💠 Suggested Scripting:“_Okay, I understand. While I’m unable to resolve this directly, I’ll need to refer this matter to the appropriate team for further assistance.”_

1. **Note: Always keep the current status of the work order for the below scenarios.**
2. If the issue is **_property damaging or extreme habitability_**:
   1. Call the escalation contacts. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
3. If the issue is **_non-property damaging or non-extreme habitability_** or **_if the Property Manager is unresponsive_** to the above attempts, place the work order on ‘Pending’ status with a task to the Property Manager:
   1. Select a reason (destination status) for putting this work order on hold: _Pending_
   2. Describe why you moved the job to this status: _The resident is requesting to \[indicate the specific request from the resident_
   3. Who is this task about? _Select the resident_
   4. Set a date and time for the follow-up task: _Leave as is_
   5. Task priority: _Select based on the WO severity_
   6. User group: PM user group
   7. Assign to a User: Leave as is
   8. When should this cancel? Complete task on any job state change.
   9. What action or information is required from the property manager?: _The resident reached out for \[question or inquiry from the resident\]. Would you like us to reassign the work order to a different technician within the same company, thereby maintaining the warranty and guarantee? Please let us know how you want to proceed. Thank you!_

## Resident complaint

### **Process for Handling Resident Reports of Harassment by a Service Provider**

Follow these steps when a resident reports harassment by a service provider working on their property:

#### **Step 1: Acknowledge and Document the Report**

- Listen to the resident’s concern and acknowledge their report professionally and empathetically.
  - _"I understand how concerning this situation must be, and I appreciate you bringing it to our attention. Your safety and comfort are a priority, and we take reports like this very seriously. I will ensure this is addressed appropriately."_
- Take note of key details, including the name of the service provider, the nature of the complaint, and any supporting evidence if provided.

#### **Step 2: Determine the Type of Service Provider**

**For Preferred Service Providers (PSPs):**

- Inform the resident that the issue will be escalated.
  - “_Thank you for sharing this with us. We take this matter seriously and will be escalating it to the appropriate team for further review and action.”_
- Submit a **PS ticket** to notify the PM about the reported behavior.
  - Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)

**For LCN Service Providers:**

- Immediately **disable the provider’s account profile** to prevent further assignments.
  - _"We want to assure you that we take your concerns seriously. To prevent any further issues, we will ensure that this service provider is not assigned to your property again."_

**For LVN Service Providers:**

- Escalate the issue to **#lvn-escalations** for further handling.
  - _"We are escalating this matter to our dedicated team for further review and appropriate action. Your concern is important to us, and we will ensure it is handled promptly."_

#### **Step 3: Confirm Next Steps with the Resident**

- Reassure the resident that the appropriate actions are being taken.
- Inform them that the Property Manager (if applicable) will be made aware of the situation.

#### **Step 4: Escalate Any Additional Concerns**

- If the resident expresses safety concerns or requests further action, escalate the issue to a supervisor or manager for additional review.

## LVN Service provider requests to cancel a work order or remove themselves from the job.

If an LVN service provider requests to remove themselves or cancel a job, we need to escalate the request accordingly.

1. Clarify the Intent behind the cancellation request:
   1. Sometimes, Service Providers say “cancel the job” but actually mean:
      1. They want to be removed from the WO (but the job still needs to be done).
      2. There is no longer an issue, and therefore, no further work is required.
      3. _“**We just wanted to clarify whether no work is needed for this work order or if you would like this reassigned to another vendor.**”_
   2. **Scenario 1**. If the service provider informs that work is no longer required because the resident canceled it directly with them, or after discussing with the resident they confirmed that the work is no longer needed
      1. Proceed to call the resident to confirm this information.
         1. If the resident confirms that service is no longer required, proceed to cancel the work order.
         2. If the resident indicates that the issue is not resolved and service is still needed, proceed to schedule the work with the service provider.
         3. If the resident is unresponsive,
            1. **Place the work order in Pending status** with a task assigned to the Property Manager, informing them that we received a notification from the service provider. However, we were unable to confirm the details as the resident was unresponsive.
   3. **Scenario 2.** If the Service Provider requests to be removed from a Work Order
      1. **“_We will update the work order accordingly and assign another service provider to address the issue. We appreciate your time and assistance.”_**
      2. Reassign the WO. Refer to [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Reassign-a-Service-Provider) for details.

## Handling Emails for LVN Service Providers

<Note>
  LVN service providers may contact us through various communication channels, primarily via email, to submit requests and provide work order updates. Handling these requests is similar to our approach with LCN service providers. For guidance on managing email requests, please refer to the following resource: [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Assembly-Email-Contacts).
</Note>

Below are common scenarios you may encounter when managing emails from LVN service providers, along with guidance on how to address them effectively.

**Request to upload invoice for a cancelled work order** - LVN service providers are set up to receive payments through Direct Deposit.

- If they encounter limitations in uploading an invoice due to a canceled state (such as a denied budget or other reasons), Operations should assist by following the same path outlined in **Q4** [How to: Upload an Invoice for a Work Order](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Upload-an-Invoice-for-a-Work-Order)

**Budget Approval Requests/Updates** - LVN service providers may reach out to inquire about updates on their budget approvals.

- If an estimate is submitted, proceed to update the work order. Refer to [How to: Put a Job on Hold](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Put-a-Job-on-Hold)
- If there is already an existing budget approval request and updates are available, promptly inform the service provider and make an effort to schedule the work order.
  - **_Hi \[Service Provider's Name\],I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to provide you with an update regarding your budget approval request. We have received the necessary updates and are now able to proceed. Please let us know your availability so we can schedule the work order at your earliest convenience. Thank you for your patience!_**
- If there are no updates, proceed to follow up with the budget approval request by escalating to the Property Manager/Owner depending on the subscription.

**Work Order Updates**

- LVN service providers may reach out to provide updates on the work orders we’ve followed up on or to inform us about the progress of a job.
  - Follow the appropriate SOP to take action on the service provider’s request/update.
  - Refer to [SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates)if a task needs to be created for another associate to handle.

## **What if the work order could not be searched in Console.**

LVN Service Providers may reach out regarding certain work orders that associates cannot search for in the Console. These work orders can be identified by their prefix “**d-**”.

- Escalate to Operations Support Specialists.
  - Refer to [How to: Handle LCD Support Requests](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-LCD-Support-Requests).
- If you receive the inquiry by other Inbound Communication, please direct the service provider to [lcdsupport@latchel.com](mailto:lcdsupport@latchel.com) for further assistance.

## [❓](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Q: When a PM proactively requests not to have any LVN service providers assigned to a work order, and the work order is currently in Finding SP status, can we proceed with manual sourcing while waiting for PS to disable LVN?">
  **A:** Yes! You can proceed with manual sourcing in the meantime.

  - If the request was received via **inbound call**, create a task and assign it to Operations Associate group.
  - If it was received via **SMS or email**, the associate who received the request should handle the sourcing in real time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: If a PM requests to allow only a specific LVN service provider (e.g., Puls), but still wants another LVN service provider to be included in the sourcing—can we support this?">
  **A:** Unfortunately, no. LVN participation is currently **all or nothing**—we can't selectively allow or block specific LVNs within a work order.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should only deviate from the process in the **Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers** SOP in specific, documented scenarios:

- **If the situation is not covered by the SOP or you are unsure how to proceed:** Pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance—do not proceed on your own.
