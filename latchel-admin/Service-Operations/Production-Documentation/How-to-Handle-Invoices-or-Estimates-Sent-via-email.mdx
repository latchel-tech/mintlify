## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

We want to prevent lost, delayed, or mishandled invoices and estimates sent by service providers via email. The goal is to ensure every invoice or estimate received through email is promptly reviewed, matched to the correct work order, uploaded to the system, and processed according to budget and payment guidelines—so that no billing request is overlooked, approvals are properly managed, and payments are issued accurately and on time.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

When working on new & unsorted tickets, you will come across emails and estimates from service providers. Email is a very common way for service providers to share these documents.

Read the email along with any attached files and determine what actions need to be taken.

### Step 3 - Attach the Invoice/Estimate file to the Work Order

Skim through the files tab inside the work order and corroborate that the Invoice/estimate has not been uploaded yet.\
a) If the file is already inside the work order, move on to step 4\
b) If the file is not in the files tab upload it to the work order.

### Step 4 - Confirm the approved budget

Corroborate that the amount quoted in the invoice/estimate is equal to or less than the approved budget.

<Note>
All estimates must clearly indicate the exact total amount before proceeding with any approval process. Estimates without the full details must be confirmed prior to moving forward.
</Note>

Refer to [(⛑EMERGENCY) How to: Handle Changes to the Approved Budget](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/797245658) or [(Non-Emergency) How to: Handle Over Approved Budget (aka Estimate)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/764313974) for guidelines.

a) If the amount does not exceed the approved budget, move on to step 5.\
b) If the amount is greater than the approved budget, but not greater than the hard budget limit, update the approved budget limit with the amount quoted and move on to step 5.\
c) If the amount quoted is greater than both the approved and hard budget limit, move on to step 6.

### Step 5 - Take appropriate actions

Always confirm that the job has been completed before processing payment. For issued cards SPs, we need either 1) confirmation from the resident or 2) before and after photos from the SP.

If the file being shared is an invoice, proceed with the following instructions:

a) **Preferred vendors:** Respond to the vendor's email letting them know the property manager will be the one issuing the payment. (See [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332919))

b) **Issued cards vendors:** If the amount is below $500 and the work order has been confirmed complete by the resident, you may call the service provider and issue a credit card payment for the quoted amount. Please note that you can also process payments below $500 via a web link if the SP includes one in their email.
   - I) If the amount is $500 or more or you were unable to contact the vendor, respond to the vendor's email letting them know one of our service provider associates will contact them soon to issue payment. (See [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332919)) Create a task for service provider associates under the "payment processing" group to follow up on the payment. (See [↔ How to: Create a Task](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/767492100))

c) **LVN/LCN direct deposit vendors:** Respond to the vendor's email asking them to submit the invoice/estimate using their Latchel dashboard. (See [How to: Handle Assembly Email Contacts](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332919))

If you processed the payment, make sure to leave an internal note in the Assembly email stating why you did not respond: *No response required. Payment was issued over the phone. [WO link]*

### Step 6 - Escalate the exceeding quote

If the invoice/estimate does not exceed the hard budget limit, move on to step 7.

**Estimate for Emergency Work Order:**

- Attached the estimate received.
- Create a task assigned to the Troubleshooter. Indicate on the task the Assembly Ticket No of the estimate received.
- Task notes: *Please review the attached estimate received via ASM-, check if any additional costs have already been charged, and confirm the exact total cost. Reach out to the service provider if follow-up or additional information is needed, and ensure the total cost is submitted for approval before proceeding.*
- Set the task to Never complete on any job state change.
- Respond to the email advising the vendor that we will seek approval and get back to him ASAP.

**Estimate for Non-Emergency Work Order:**

We should place the work order on hold and select the "waiting for owner approval" option.

Create the task for the property manager, and leave proper notes. (See - [(SOP) How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/766378165/(SOP)+How+to%3A+Put+a+Job+on+Hold))

If the work order is already in "Needs Owner Approval" status, simply uploading the file and creating a task for the PM is sufficient.

**Invoice:**

If the invoice total is above the approved budget, reply to the service provider asking to clarify the amount and confirm if they got approval from Latchel, the Property Manager or the Owner for that amount.

If there's a task already created to request the invoice, update the description in the current "Pending invoice" task with the message:

*"The service provider submitted an over-the-budget invoice, kindly check for the Service Provider's reply to email or contact the Service Provider to follow up"*

If there's not a task to collect the invoice, create a task that "never complete on any job state change" and assign it to the Service Provider Followup group with the message *"Service provider submitted an over the budget invoice, kindly check for Service Provider's reply to email or contact the Service Provider to follow up"*.

If the service provider responded to the confirmation:

a) **Preferred vendors:** Respond to the email and advise the service provider that the amount invoiced exceeds the approved budget limit. Let them know the property manager will be reaching out to them to follow up on the matter. Create a task for the property manager including the details of the invoice.

b) **Issued card vendors:** Respond to the email and advise the service provider that the amount invoiced exceeds the approved budget limit. Let them know that one of our service provider specialists will be reaching out to them to follow up on the matter. Create a task for the "payment issues" including the details of the invoice. (See [↔ How to: Create a Task](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/LTG/pages/767492100))

### Step 7 - Update the vendor

Respond to the service provider and advise them we will advise them just as soon as we hear back from the property manager about the estimate. You should use the Assembly response called *(Operations) Estimate from SP*. Refer to [How to: Use an email templated response/escalation (Assembly)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2511273993)

### Step 8 - Wrap up

Copy the Assembly ticket number and paste it into the note session of the work order with a summary of the update and actions you took (↔ How to: Write Effective Notes). Save notes making them visible to others.

Close the email in Assembly. (See [Service Ops: Assembly Guide](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3208249355))
