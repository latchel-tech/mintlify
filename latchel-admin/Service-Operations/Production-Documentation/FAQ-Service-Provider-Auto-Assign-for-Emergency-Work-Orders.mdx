## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if I'm on the phone offering a job and someone accepts it automatically?**

A1: If the vendor on the phone can perform the job sooner than a vendor who accepted it via auto-assign:

- Assign the job to the vendor on the phone and book the earlier appointment.
- Notify the vendor who accepted via auto-assign:
  - Call and explain that the request was being dispatched when another vendor confirmed an earlier appointment.
  - Apologize for any inconvenience.

A2: If the auto-assign appointment is sooner than the vendor on the phone:

- Let the vendor on the phone know that another vendor already accepted the job via public screens and booked an earlier appointment.
- Apologize for the inconvenience.

<Note>
  📝 NOTE: Troubleshooters retain the ability to re-assign jobs as needed to achieve faster scheduling. Always prioritize the vendor who can complete the job sooner, while communicating clearly and professionally with all vendors involved.
</Note>

**Q: What should we do if the vendor who accepted the job via public screens scheduled it beyond the acceptable timeframe for a property-damaging/ extreme habitability situation?**

If no one has accepted the job yet via manual dispatch:

- Call the vendor who accepted the job via public screens.
- Request an earlier appointment than the one confirmed in the system.
- Explain that the work order must be completed the same day due to the severity of the situation.

If the vendor can confirm a same-day appointment:

- Rebook the work order to the earlier schedule.

If the vendor's earliest availability is not the same day:

- Set expectations that we will contact the list of other vendors.
- Inform the vendor that if an earlier appointment is confirmed with another provider, the job may be reassigned.

<Note>
  📝 NOTE: Always prioritize scheduling PD/EH work orders for the same day, and communicate clearly with vendors when adjustments are needed.
</Note>

**Q: What happens if both a phone-confirmed SP and Auto-Assign accept around the same time?**

What if the Service Provider accepts the job over the phone, but Auto-Assign triggers a successful acceptance shortly after the call ends? Who should be prioritized if both Auto-Assign and a manually contacted SP accept the job at (or around) the same time?

A: In both scenarios, the associate should review the appointment times offered by both Service Providers and proceed with the one who can attend the job sooner, ensuring the best outcome for the resident.

If the associate needs to cancel with the SP who confirmed by phone, they can use the following message to maintain a professional and respectful relationship:

Say something like:

> "Thank you so much for your time and for confirming your availability. While we were finalizing the assignment, another provider was able to schedule the appointment slightly sooner, so we've moved forward with them to ensure the quickest possible service for the resident. We sincerely appreciate your support and look forward to working with you again soon."

**Q: Does the WO go automatically to scheduling after the SP accepts it?**

A: Yes, the SP will need to select a schedule from the resident's availability you entered when accepting the job, and the job state will automatically change to Awaiting work date.

**Q: Do we leave scheduling to the SP, or do we have to call to confirm?**

A: Just leave scheduling to the SP. They will need to select a schedule from the resident's availability you entered in order to accept the job.

**Q: Does it force the SPs to enter a time frame for the appointment?**

A: Yes, they have to select from the resident's available time slots you entered.
