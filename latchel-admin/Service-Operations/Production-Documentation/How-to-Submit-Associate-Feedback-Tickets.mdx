## **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Note>
  We want to prevent missed coaching opportunities, lack of accountability, and inconsistent feedback for Operations Associates. The goal is to ensure every instance of feedback—whether for improvement, concerning behavior, or praise—is documented and delivered promptly, so associates receive timely coaching, recognition, and support, leading to continuous improvement and a better experience for both customers and Latchel.
</Note>

<Warning>
  This process applies to all Operations Associates—both new hires and tenured team members. All feedback, regardless of associate tenure, should be submitted and processed using this SOP.
</Warning>

**Content**\*

## **🔄Types of Feedback**

**Improvement Opportunity**

For process-related issues, such as:

- Incorrect handling of a work order (e.g., tagged wrong status, failed to notify the resident).
- Missed steps in an SOP.
- Incomplete documentation that caused confusion for the next handler.

**Goal:** Document coaching opportunities to improve accuracy and prevent repeat mistakes.

---

### **Concerning Behavior**

<Warning>
  Any feedback related to the Time and Attendance Policy should always be categorized as Concerning Behavior, regardless of whether it is a repeated or behavioral issue.
</Warning>

For **repeated or behavioral issues** where an associate has **already been coached, called out, or received a feedback ticket** about the same action - and the associate repeats the behavior **after the date of that coaching/discussion**.

If the issue occurs **before the coaching session or feedback conversation has happened**, it should be logged as an **Improvement Opportunity**, not Concerning Behavior.

**Examples include:**

- Failing to follow escalation protocols after being reminded and coached.
- Repeating a process mistake (e.g., mis-tagging a work order) that was already addressed in a feedback ticket or 1:1.
- Closing tickets without proper follow-through, even after correction and discussion.
- Showing lack of professionalism in communication repeatedly after being coached.
- Demonstrating unkind, dismissive, or uncooperative behavior toward Supervisors or Support staff, even after being reminded to collaborate respectfully.

**Goal:** To highlight **repetitive behaviors** that show a lack of follow-through on coaching and may require escalation, corrective action, or closer monitoring.

---

### **Praise**

For recognition and positive reinforcement, such as:

- Going above and beyond on a work order.
- Outstanding customer communication.
- Proactive problem-solving that saved time or prevented a breach.
- Helping a teammate succeed.

**Goal:** Encourage morale, celebrate wins, and reinforce positive behaviors.

---

## **📋 Step-by-Step Process**

**Step 1 – Submit the Feedback Ticket in Slack**

1. Go to the **svc-ops-feedback channel**.
2. Open the **“Workflows”** drop-down menu.
3. Select **Agent Feedback**.
4. Complete the **Agent Feedback Workflow** form.
   1. Be clear, specific, and factual.
   2. If available, attach **screenshots or FullStory links** in the Slack thread after submission.

---

### **Step 2 – Ticket Creation in Assembly**

- Once submitted, the workflow will automatically generate an **Assembly ticket**.
- Supporting materials should be attached to the Assembly ticket directly.
- If you don’t have direct access, share materials in the Slack thread of the ticket notification.

---

### **Step 3 – Supervisor Review & Delivery of Feedback**

- Supervisors will review Assembly tickets.
- Feedback should be delivered:
  - **Via Google Meet** for complex or sensitive issues.
  - **Via Slack** for straightforward coaching points.
- Supervisors should keep the following SLAs in mind:
  - **Critical (same day):** Severe issues affecting customers, safety, or property damage (e.g., water leaks not handled correctly).
  - **Urgent (within 24 hours):** Misbehavior, repeat opportunities, or errors raised by a property manager.
  - **Normal (within 48+ hours):** Knowledge gaps or policy misunderstandings without immediate customer risk.

---

### **Step 4 – Deliver Feedback to the Associate**

<Note>
  While all feedback tickets are submitted through the same channel, supervisors should always consider the context of the associate (e.g., new hire vs. tenured) when delivering coaching. Tailor your approach to ensure feedback is constructive and appropriate for the associate’s experience level.

  **‼️IMPORTANT**: Feedback must be delivered confidentially—do not disclose the identity of who submitted it.
</Note>

- Feedback should always be:
  - Specific
  - Actionable
  - Respectful and professional
- Use coaching frameworks such as:
  - [**SBI Coaching Method**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SBI-Coaching-Method#sbi-coaching-method) (Situation, Behavior, Impact).
  - [**STAR Feedback Model**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/STAR-Feedback-Model#star-feedback-model) (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

---

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

- If the associate’s behavior poses a **risk to customer safety, property, or Latchel’s reputation**, escalate immediately.
