# 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  This process ensures business operations can continue as smoothly as possible during unpredictable events that may impact associates or customers. By defining clear monitoring, communication, and response procedures, the plan helps minimize service disruptions, maintain SLA compliance, and coordinate resources efficiently during natural disasters, system outages, or other critical events.
</Info>

## Plan details:

<Info>
  **What happened prior to this step?**

  Leadership or Operations Support Specialist team is informed about an event that will impact a large number of customers (i.e., hurricane affecting several states or large regions) or will prevent our staff from working (i.e., Latchel Console downtime, tropical storm cutting internet and power service for several hours or days).
</Info>

### Process Overview

The Operations Support Specialists will monitor different sources of information to determine if a significant event will pressure the Operations team, and how we serve our customers. Once the event is identified, Operations Support Specialists and Ops Leadership will coordinate activities to mitigate the impact on the customer experience.

These are some events that could stress the need to take action and guarantee minimum impact on service level agreements (SLA).

1. Can impact customers: natural disasters (hurricanes, winter storms), social unrest, or holidays that will generate a higher WO generation than usual.
2. Can impact associates: system downtime, natural disasters (earthquakes, typhoons), political events, or similar that will prevent more than 10% of associates from working more than 4 hours a day, or 2 or more consecutive days.

### Alert mechanism - Monitor events in the US and in regions where our associates are located

1. Operations Support Specialists will have one person in charge per week. This person will visit two different sources of information (weather news websites, government websites, etc.) to obtain a list of potential events happening in the next 14 days.
2. Operations Support Specialists will publish a roster weekly so Leadership knows who to contact for information.
3. Once a month, the Operations Support Specialist team will simulate how the continuity plan will be activated. This is to guarantee all members of Operations Support Specialists know where to find the contact information, notify associates and identify

### Contact information database

1. Ops Managers will coordinate with HR once every two months for the proper contact information updates. This way associates can be contacted in the event overtime or schedule changes are required during a significant event.

### System downtime

1. If system downtime is detected, we will follow the outage procedures available. In the case of the Latchel Console, we will follow these instructions.
2. The [All Hands on Deck policy](https://help.latchel.com/Ops-Policy-All-Hands-on-Deck) will be activated if the system issue takes more than 30 mins to be resolved and/or we experience multiple SLA misses in several channels (webchat, emergencies, inbound calls).

### Event identified - continuity plan activated

1. A message will be posted in the operations\_support Slack channel. All relevant parties will be tagged. This includes the COO, Customer Success Management, Ops Managers, and Ops Supervisors. Operations Support Specialists will share a detailed report including event magnitude, event duration, how is the customer impacted, and the number of associates impacted (if applicable).
2. The event happened without notice:
3. The Operations Support Specialist team will access the contact information of associates that may be on their time off and could not be reached in team meetings or public channels. The goal is to offer overtime or ask associates to start their shifts earlier.
4. The Operations Support Specialist team notifies the associates in public channels and team meetings about the need to recruit volunteers to submit overtime or change schedules.
5. The [All Hands on Deck policy](https://help.latchel.com/Ops-Policy-All-Hands-on-Deck) will be activated.
6. The event is detected ahead of time:
7. Ops Managers and Operations Support Specialists will calculate the need for overtime or schedule changes required to guarantee an acceptable service level.
8. The Operations Support Specialist team notifies the associates in public channels and team meetings about the need to recruit volunteers to submit overtime or change schedules.
9. The Operations Support Specialist team will create overtime offers in the payroll system. Ops Supervisors will assist with schedule changes in the payroll system.
10. The contact information will be accessed to contact associates that may be on their time off and could not be reached in team meetings or public channels.
11. The [All Hands on Deck policy](https://help.latchel.com/Ops-Policy-All-Hands-on-Deck) will be activated if the resources available are not sufficient to guarantee emergencies WO first contact SLA, webchat response times SLA, and inbound calls SLA.

# 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
  Follow this plan in most cases, but exceptions can be made if leadership directs a different approach, the event affects fewer associates/customers than expected, new tools or processes are used, or an unexpected situation requires immediate action to protect service levels or associate safety.
</Note>
