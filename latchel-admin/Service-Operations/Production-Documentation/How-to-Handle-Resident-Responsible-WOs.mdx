## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

We want to ensure that resident-responsible work orders are handled consistently, efficiently, and in line with property manager settings.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

Effective January 24, 2024, we will introduce Amenify to residents for all resident-responsible requests. Work orders associated with these requests will be automatically cancelled in Latchel.

<Warning>
⚠️ Exception — Confirmed Recalls

If a work order is auto-classified as Resident Responsible but is associated with a confirmed recall by the Service Provider, do not proceed with this SOP immediately. Instead, advise the PM of the conflict and request that they review their Intake Settings to determine whether the recall should take precedence. Refer to the Recall Exception process in the [How to: Identify and Handle a Recall WO](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2643296257) for full steps.
</Warning>

For these types of WOs, the residents are notified that concierge requests will be at their own expense and must be submitted through our partner, Amenify. The PM should never be contacted or involved in any way for these WOs, except for the specific cases outlined in this document.

For guidelines on how to assist residents for these types of request during intake, please refer to Step 4: Resident Caller Request of this SOP: [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/781647909).

When viewing a Resident Responsible work order, you'll notice the following indicator confirming its classification as Resident Responsible.

Once a Resident Responsible Work Order is created, the resident will receive a notification, and the work order will automatically be cancelled in Latchel. This notification serves to inform the resident that they are responsible for the payment for the job, which will be handled by our trusted concierge partner, Amenify, specializing in resident-responsible requests.

### Managing Work Order Severity Changes

If a customer provides updated information to Latchel that could alter the severity of a resident-responsible work order (as determined by the Property Manager's intake settings):

- **Residents/PMs:** Guide them to submit a new work order through Latchel. This ensures the issue is managed appropriately.
- **Webchats/SMS:** For PMs that refused this action, refer to [How to: Handle SMS/Webchat Contacts (New Support Framework)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759431202)

### Address Resident Questions

If you get questions from the resident about payment:

**Suggested blurb:**\
Hi, [resident name]! Thank you for reaching out about work order [insert WO # here]. Because this job is your responsibility, you will be responsible for payment. However, our trusted concierge partner Amenify handles all resident responsible requests and offers Latchel residents $300 in yearly credits! Please visit this link to book your service through the Amenify app - [https://m.amenify.com/sign-up?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=partner_latchel&utm_medium=marketing](https://m.amenify.com/sign-up?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=partner_latchel&utm_medium=marketing). This Latchel work has been canceled. You can track the status of any Amenify requests via their app. Please let us know if we can help with anything else, and have a great day!

If the resident continues to inquire about the following, proceed to escalate to the PM (refer to the "Escalate to the Property Manager" section):

- The resident doesn't agree that they should be paying for the request.
- The resident doesn't want to pay.
- The resident states the PM is responsible for this per the lease.
- The resident states the PM said the PM would do it.

If the resident continues to inquire about Amenify, see [How To: Respond to Amenify inquiries](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CoDhwQ).

### Escalate to the Property Manager

Only in the instances described in the "Address Resident Questions" section is acceptable to escalate to and involve the PM, do not do it for anything else.

1. Create a task for the Property Manager on the Work Order.
2. We expect these WOs to be cancelled due to system configuration for resident-responsible WOs; therefore, creating a PM task is sufficient to notify them of the resident's concern.

Refer to [Job Aid: What is a task?](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2528083973) to know how to create a task.

Use and update the following Task Description template:

*"The Resident is inquiring about being responsible for the payment of this request. With your current setting of "Resident Responsible" for this type of request [Problem], the resident would be responsible for payment and they can choose to move forward with this request or not. This setting is best used for concierge requests.*

*If you believe this type of request should be a regular maintenance request, visit your intake settings and update your intake rules to be normal, urgent, or emergency. You can then opt to bill the resident back if required.*

*Alternatively, you can keep things as is and use the Latchel portal to notify the resident that if they want this work completed they will be responsible for booking and paying through Latchel's trusted concierge partner, Amenify, who offers Latchel residents $300 in yearly service credits. Visit [https://help.latchel.com/s/article/How-To-Send-SMS-Messages-From-Latchel](https://help.latchel.com/s/article/How-To-Send-SMS-Messages-From-Latchel) to learn how you can send messages to your residents from your Latchel portal.*
*Do not hesitate to contact Latchel if you need assistance."*
