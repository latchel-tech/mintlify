## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  We want to prevent misclassification, delays, and miscommunication when creating or updating work orders by providing clear guidelines for selecting the correct Skill. The goal is to ensure every work order is accurately categorized—so the right intake settings is set, the appropriate service provider is dispatched, and all parties have the information they need for timely resolution.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Info>
  When a Work Order (WO) is in Troubleshooting status and the issue wasn’t resolved during the troubleshooting call, you will be asked to confirm the Skill is correct. The Skill will then determine the type of Service Provider that is required to complete the job.
</Info>

<Note>
  Always notify the Property Manager of any issues that indicate a 911 situation, whether they are ongoing, contained, part of the current work order you are addressing, or related to a different work order. Ensuring timely notification is critical in all such cases to prioritize safety and proper escalation.
</Note>

### Skill

<Note>
  If the description on a Work Order indicates that the resident had an issue going through the diagnostic flow, please carefully review the description. Use your best judgment to determine the appropriate skill, and update the skill accordingly. The skill of the work order must always be updated to reflect the correct task at hand, regardless of whether you are able to contact the resident.
</Note>

The Skill describes the type of trade that is required in order to solve the issue. Skills are distributed among larger categories to be able to find the one that’s most appropriate to complete the job. For example, if the AC leaking unit is Central Unit the skill required would be under “Heating Ventilation & Air Conditioning”:

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

- If you can’t decide between multiple Problems, it is recommended that you err on the side of caution and choose the more severe Problem.
- If you can’t decide between multiple Skills, it is recommended to favor the broader/more generic skill.
- If you still need assistance, proceed to escalate to Operations Support Specialists, please refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams)
