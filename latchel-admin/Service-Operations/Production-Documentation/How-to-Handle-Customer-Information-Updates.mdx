<Note>
We want to prevent errors, delays, and miscommunication when customers request updates to their contact or account information. The goal is to ensure all changes—whether for residents, service providers, or property managers—are handled accurately, securely, and through the correct channels.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### **Request Type 1 - Update Resident Contact Information or Add a Resident to a Property**

1. **Navigate to the Existing Resident or Property Profile**
  1. If the email mentions the name of the resident, use the name of the resident to find the correct Work Order
    1. Click on "People" dropdown from the top bar of the Latchel Console Home Interface.
      1. Click on "Residents"
      2. Search for the Resident name.
  2. If the email mentions the resident property address, Find the Property Profile
    1. Click on Properties from the top bar of the Latchel Console Home interface.
      1. [↔ How to: Navigate the Latchel Console Home Interface](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OM/pages/440074328)

    3. If you find a matching Property Address, Click on it to reveal the Property Profile.
    4. Within the Property Profile, to view a list of Residents living at that Property Address, Click on the Resident tab.

          1. **"Send **automated Email messages."
            1. If the Resident would like their automated email notifications TURNED OFF, ***Click**** the toggle *so that the blue ball moves to the left and reverts to white.

    1. **Handling Short-Term SP Availability Notifications (Temporary Absence)**
If a Service Provider contacts Latchel to inform that they will be **temporarily unavailable for a short period (e.g., a couple of days):**
⚠️ **Important:**
      1. **Acknowledge** the SP's notification.
      2. **Advise** the SP that in case they receive work order notifications while they are away, they can simply ignore them.
      3. **Ask** if they have a point of contact while they are away.
        1. *🗨️ "Hi [SP Name], thank you for letting us know! We've noted that you'll be away for [timeframe]. If you receive any work order notifications during that time, you can simply disregard them. Do you have a point of contact we can reach in case anything urgent comes up while you're out?"*
      4. **If they DO have a point of contact:**
        - Update the phone number on file to the point of contact's number in the meantime.
        - Let the SP know to call us back to update their phone number once they return.
          - *"Hi [SP Name], thank you for letting us know! We'll update the phone number on file to [POC Name]'s number in the meantime so we can reach them if anything urgent comes up. When you're back, just give us a call and we'll switch it back to your number."*
      5. **If they do NOT have a point of contact:**
        - No further action needed. Reiterate they can ignore any notifications while away.
        - *"Hi [SP Name], thank you for letting us know! We've noted that you'll be away for [timeframe]. If you receive any work order notifications during that time, you can simply disregard them."*
      6. **Document** the interaction in the work order or internal notes.
      - This step applies **only** to short-term absences (a few days).
      - If the SP will be away for a **prolonged period**, do **not** use this process. Instead, escalate to Product Support to have the information added to the SP's critical notes. ### **Request Type 3 - Update Resident Property Information**

1. Latchel cannot add or change Resident Properties **without Property Manager confirmation** about the data for the property.
  1. Ops Associates **will ❌not handle requests to add new properties or change resident–property assignments without PM confirmation.**
  2. Ops Associates **may disable/re‑enable a property** once the PM has confirmed they manage/no longer manage the property and wants it re‑enabled/disabled.
    1. **If you cannot edit the property due to integration/permissions**
      1. If the property is locked by integration or you don’t see an enable/reactivate option, **escalate** per:
        1. https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support
    2. **📝 NOTE:** If an Ops Associate encounters an email describing this type of interaction:
      1. For **resident‑initiated requests to add or update property information** (without PM confirmation), associates **should not modify property records directly**.
        1. Instead, associates can submit a **work order** so the PM can review and update their records as needed. Refer to:
          1. https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/781647909

### **Request Type 4 - Update the Property Manager’s (Including Rentredi/NOMAD owners) Account Information**

1. Ops Associates **will❌not be handling any requests to Update the Property Manager’s Account Information**.
  1. **Important:** This includes critical notes, emergency escalation contacts, pre-dispatch instructions, or changing Service Provider Prioritization settings. 
    1. Note: Critical Notes should serve as Contextual Advice/Information (not meant to hold specific operational instructions) as PMs do not have visibility into these notes and cannot make changes. 
      1. **For emergency escalation contacts:** If the PM requests a change, ask if it applies to all properties on the account.
        - **If YES**, refer to https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SR/pages/2977234945 and proceed with the update.
        - **If NO or the request is only for a specific property**, escalate to Product Support.
    2. PMs also do not have permissions to update Pre-Dispatch Instructions. Critical Notes & Pre-Dispatch Instructions will only be updated and managed by CS and PS moving forward
  2. If an Ops Associate encounters a request to update the PM’s account, proceed to escalate to Product Support https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support 
    1. **Note:** If the Property Manager just wants the associate to take a specific action for a given WO, such as: Calling a different person during an escalation or dispatching a specific Service Provider for a given WO, the associate should proceed to fulfill that request, however, if the PM wants to modify their emergency critical notes, escalation contacts, pre-dispatch instructions, Service Provider prioritization, etc, you should escalate to Product Support to complete the account configuration.
  3. Inform the PM that the request has been escalated:
    1. 🗨️ Suggested Blurb: “*Hello, [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. I’ve opened up a ticket for you with the Product Support team so they can take care of your request. They will be reviewing it and will send you a personal response via email (usually within one business day). Thank you for your patience.*”

### **Request Type 5 - Customer Wants to Cancel**

1. If it's an email from another Latchel Associate about a Property Manager or other customer wanting to cancel services, escalate to* Product Support.*
2. If it's an email from the resident about wanting to cancel a Work Order, escalate to the appropriate team (refer to https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams )
3. If it's an email from a Service Provider about wanting to cancel a job, removed them from the WO(refer to https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/786859062 ) , but Service Providers do not have the authority to cancel entire WOs.

## ❓Frequently Asked Questions:

<Accordion title="What if the owner also resides in the property as a resident?">
📝 **Note:** If an owner also lives in the property, adding them as a resident may result in an error when the same contact information is already registered in the system under the owner profile.

1. In this situation, **do not attempt to add or edit the resident record directly**. Instead, **submit a Product Support (PS) ticket** to align the owner and resident records and ensure the necessary details are added correctly.
2. Refer to [**How to Escalate to Product Support**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)[ ](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)for escalation steps.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if a service provider has multiple profiles with conflicting email addresses?">
This occurs when the email address you’re entering on file is already existing.

<Frame>
  ![image-20260610-191632.png](/images/SVC-750.png)
</Frame>

1. If a service provider has multiple profiles and you are unable to update the email address because the correct email is already associated with another vendor profile under the same company, review all associated profiles before taking action.
  1. Compare the profiles to confirm whether they appear to belong to the same company. Check the company name, phone number, address, service provider name, and any other available details.
2. Before updating, reassigning, or using another profile, confirm directly with the service provider that the other profile also belongs to them.
  1. If the service provider confirms that the other profile belongs to them, do not update the current profile. Instead, use the correct profile where the email address is already listed.
3. If you are actively handling a work order, or if the service provider reaches out advising that they are not receiving notifications, reassign the work order to the correct confirmed profile so notifications can be triggered and sent to the service provider. Ensure that the email address is the best email that the service provider uses.
4. Do not reassign the work order to another profile if the service provider has not confirmed that the profile belongs to them, or if the profile details do not appear to match.
5. Document the action taken in the appropriate work order/internal notes, including which profile was used or reassigned to and why.
6. Submit a Product Support (PS) ticket to flag the duplicate vendor profiles and request review/alignment of the profiles and contact information. If both profiles have work orders tied to them, make sure to include this detail in the PS ticket so Product Support can review the impact before making any profile changes.
  1. Refer to [**How to Escalate to Product Support**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)[ ](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support)for the proper escalation steps.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should only deviate from the process in the SOP in the following situations:

- **If you are not given the option to update or add customer information in the system.** Some Property Management companies require that only Product Support handle these updates; escalate accordingly.
- **If the request or situation falls outside the scope of the SOP or is not covered by the documented process.** Do not proceed on your own—pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance.
