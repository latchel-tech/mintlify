## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
  The Support Compliance Dashboard exists to:

  - Monitor adherence to process standards – ensure Support consistently follows required procedures.
  - Identify compliance on SLA Report submissions – confirm timely and accurate reporting.
  - Provide visibility for leadership (Ops Supervisor & Ops Manager) – enable data-driven coaching, resource planning, and decision-making.
  - Highlight risk areas early – flag non-compliance or performance dips before they impact SLAs, customer experience, or internal efficiency.
  - Support accountability and transparency – ensure both team and leadership have a shared source of truth.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Who should read this document?**

  - Operations Support Specialist
  - Ops Supervisors
  - Ops Managers
</Note>

## Summary

The Support Compliance Dashboard serves as the central tool for monitoring Support's overall compliance and performance. It measures compliance on the SLA Report submissions and Dashboard checks.

The dashboard is meant to be checked on a consistent cadence—weekly for Dashboard checks and SLA submissions.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Accessing the Dashboard

- Open the [Support Compliance Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/b5b7d097-1818-4a9c-aec3-8ee2437a8ca6) in Looker Studio.
- Use the filters (date, team, individual) as needed.
- Ensure you're viewing the correct week or reporting period before analyzing results.

### 2. SLA Reports

This is designed to help assess the performance and accountability of Shift Support team members in relation to their SLA reporting responsibilities.

<Frame>
  ![image-20260805-205418.png](/images/SVC-606.png)
</Frame>

**Steps:**

1. Go to the [Support Compliance Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/b5b7d097-1818-4a9c-aec3-8ee2437a8ca6) and select **SLA Reports**.
2. Use the **Support Name** and **Date Range** filters to view individual compliance records.
3. Review the top summary cards:
   1. **Overall Compliance** - Displays the overall submission compliance percentage across the entire Support team.
   2. **Average Compliance %** - Shows the average submission compliance per Support member, along with the average number of days taken to submit from the original shift date.
4. Check the **SLA Report Compliance** table:
   1. **Shift Date** – The date when the Support member was assigned as the shift owner.
   2. **Support** – Name of the assigned Shift Support.
   3. **Submission Date** – The actual date when the SLA report was submitted.
   4. **Report Submitted** – Indicates whether the SLA report was submitted (Yes or No).

This data serves as both a performance indicator and a compliance measure within the Support Operations framework. It enables teams to track both individual and overall compliance trends, ensuring that SLA report submissions are timely and consistently aligned with operational standards.

### 3. Dashboard Health Check

This page tracks the weekly compliance of Supports in checking all assigned dashboards. Each Support is expected to perform a Dashboard Health Check once per week (Monday to Friday) to stay updated with the latest data trends and ensure dashboard accuracy.

<Frame>
  ![image-20260805-205509.png](/images/SVC-607.png)
</Frame>

**Steps:**

1. Go to the **Dashboard Health Check** page on the [Support Compliance Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/b5b7d097-1818-4a9c-aec3-8ee2437a8ca6) in Looker.
   1. This page summarizes who checked each dashboard and their compliance rate.
2. **Use the filters** (e.g., Week Number, Support Name) to focus on the desired week or team member.
3. **Review the top summary card:**
   1. **Overall SLA Reports Dashboard %** – team's compliance for SLA Reports Dashboard.
4. **Review the main table (Dashboard Compliance Summary):**
   1. **Support Name** – the Support performing the check.
   2. **Dashboard Checked** – which dashboard page they verified.
   3. **Week** – ISO week number when the check occurred.
   4. **Compliance %** – shows 1 (checked) or 0 (not checked), then averaged over time to compute weekly and overall compliance.
5. **Perform the weekly check:**
   1. Open each dashboard page (e.g., Audit Dashboard, SLA Reports, etc.).
   2. Click the green **"Dashboard Checked"** button in the upper-right corner of the page.
   3. This logs the check into the Dashboard Log tab and updates the Compliance Summary automatically.
6. **Use insights from the table and scorecards to identify:**
   1. Supports who consistently perform checks (high compliance).
   2. Those who need reminders or coaching to stay compliant (low compliance).
   3. Overall dashboard health trends week by week.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- If the dashboard is inaccessible due to a Looker Studio outage → Switch to manual reports from Assembly.
- If there's a major service disruption (e.g., emergency spike), act immediately and document later.
- If leadership directs an alternative reporting method for specific periods.
