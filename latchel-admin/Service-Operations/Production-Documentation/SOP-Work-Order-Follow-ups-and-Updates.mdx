## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to ensure that all work order follow-ups, updates, and customer requests are managed efficiently and accurately. The goal of this SOP is to provide associates with a clear, step-by-step process for handling ongoing communications, completing promised actions, and resolving issues. This process prevents missed commitments, duplicate efforts, and unresolved tasks—reducing customer complaints and confusion.
</Note>

<Warning>
  **📝** **NOTE:** Any follow-up that requires a call should be handled by associates outside the Call Center. Call Center associates cannot log out to make outbound calls, so they should create a task for another Ops associate to take action (Operations Associate Group).
</Warning>

<Warning>
  ‼️ATTENTION: For agents handling tasks for both emergencies and non-emergencies.

  Before you begin working on any task, ensure you view all open tasks for the customer you are following up on (resident, service provider, owner). This step is crucial to avoid duplicating efforts and customers complaints, and to ensure we always get it right the first time.

  For detailed instructions on how to handle the tasks queue and view all open tasks, please refer to the following link: [How to: Work the Tasks Queue](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Work-the-Tasks-Queue#how-to-work-the-tasks-queue)

  For information on the frequency and number of times you should contact customers, please refer to **[Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Informational-Basic-Service-Ops-Guidelines-and-Rules).**
</Warning>

## 🗂️Process overview

<Warning>
  ### ✅ Task Coverage Reminder (Required)

  When handling follow-ups or updates, ensure the Work Order always has a task that reflects the next needed action.

  **Do not leave a Work Order without an open task unless the Work Order is Completed or Cancelled.**<br />Leaving a WO without a task can result in delays and orphaned Work Orders.

  For Autopilot WOs, refer to [How to: Handle Autopilot WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Autopilot-WOs#how-to-handle-autopilot-wos).
</Warning>

Agents assigned to incoming communications (SMS/Webchat, Inbound Calls, and Emails) will receive communications from customers following up on their work order, update, or requesting information. It is the Operations Associates' responsibility to: understand the request, which Work Order the communication is pertaining to, and update the WO or create the follow-up task.

<Note>
  **Important:** Before taking action on any task, verify that the communication or update **belongs to the work order you are working on.** If the message or note references a different issue or a different WO:

  - Do not act on it in the current WO.
  - Close the task and update the correct WO.
  - Document in your notes (for both work orders) that the task was generated on an incorrect work order, your actions and outcomes.
</Note>

Refer to the following list for situations that will require a task to be created:

- When residents/PMs/SPs request a callback.
- When we need to confirm with the resident about the completion of a job
- Recreating a follow-up task
- When the resident reports the SP did go but the issue was not resolved
- Residents report missed appointments
- When no access instructions are provided and we need to follow up on it.
- When an owner (Not Rent Redi) reaches out with a request/instruction for a work order.
- SP asking for payments, Payment issues/not going through, the SP asking for payments in advance, or Payment inquiries.
- When the resident is reaching back to say they are available for TS.
- When a WO needs to be scheduled or when SPs/residents want to reschedule
- When an SP advised they would like to be removed from the job due to some instances like; no longer working with PM, does not want to work with 3rd parties, and etc.
- When the SP reports they are waiting for materials
- When a PM requests an emergency escalation **_AND_** follow-up from TS or reassessment is needed.
- When residents/PMs/SPs/owners reached out providing a new information that will result in an emergency work order reassessment - including PD/EH situations
- When a resident submits a photo or video of an issue and no troubleshooter is currently assigned to the work order. (If a troubleshooter is already working on the order, **simply tag them to notify** instead of creating a new task.)

<Note>
  Any received communications that are not maintenance-related should be assigned directly to the appropriate property manager. Refer to [How to: Intake Non-Resident Requests (Non-maintenance Requests)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Intake-Non-Resident-Requests-Non-maintenance-Requests#non-resident-caller)
</Note>

### Work Order Task Assignment Guidelines

- **Troubleshooter (TS) Group** - Tasks must be assigned to the TS group when:
  - **All Emergency Work Orders** –  This includes all follow-up activities related to an **emergency** work order, covering the full lifecycle of the incident while it remains classified as an emergency. This may include, but is not limited to, **outbound and inbound communications** with residents, service providers, and property managers; **status checks** and **progress updates;** **coordination** of repairs or mitigation efforts; documentation of actions taken and outcomes; and **troubleshooting** or **reassessment** of the emergency condition.
  - **Emergency Work Orders requiring troubleshooting or reassessment include:**
    - Active emergency conditions
    - Worsening issues
    - Ambiguous situations where intake settings are unclear
    - Issues involving escalation triggers as defined in the [Quick Reference Guide: Emergency and Non-Emergency Triggers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Quick-Reference-Guide-Emergency-and-Non-Emergency-Triggers#quick-reference-guide-emergency-and-non-emergency-triggers)
      - **Action:** Create a task and assign it to TS for reassessment.
      - **Communication:** Inform the tenant that since there are changes in the situation, they will receive a call from the troubleshooting team.
        - **Say something like:** ”Hi [Resident Name]! Thank you for the update regarding your reported issue. Our troubleshooting team has been notified of the new information and will reassess the situation. Someone from our team will reach out to you shortly to discuss the next steps. We appreciate your patience as we work to resolve this as quickly as possible.
  - **Operations Associate Group** - Tasks can be handled directly by Ops Associates when:
    - The work order is **non-emergency,** or the PM intake settings confirm that the issue no longer qualifies as an emergency.
      - Refer to [Quick Reference Guide: Emergency and Non-Emergency Triggers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Quick-Reference-Guide-Emergency-and-Non-Emergency-Triggers#quick-reference-guide-emergency-and-non-emergency-triggers)
      - Refer to the guidelines outlined [About Property Manager's Intake Settings](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/About-Property-Managers-Intake-Settings#about-property-managers-intake-settings) to determine the appropriate severity of the work order.
      - Associates may **de-escalate the WO** and proceed with the next steps if the criteria are clearly met.

**📝** **NOTE**: If the follow-up is about a non-emergency Autopilot WO, please follow the guidelines on [How to: Handle Autopilot WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Autopilot-WOs#how-to-handle-autopilot-wos)

Follow the steps in [**Job Aid: What is a task?**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-What-is-a-task#job-aid-what-is-a-task) to successfully relay the message to the team.

<Warning>
  Ensure to perform a thorough investigation of the WO prior to initiating a new task. Verify whether a task related to the same issue or assigned to the same group already exists.

  Frequently, there is no necessity to initiate an additional task, and instead, you can simply update the description of the existing task. If you notice another team member is already actively working on the WO, you can notify them via Slack, in addition to creating or updating the task on the WO. If any assistance is needed, feel free to check out with Operations Support Specialists: **[How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams).**
</Warning>

## **Instances when OWNERS reach out with instructions.**

<Warning>
  **Check if the owner is listed as an owner on the property profile:**
</Warning>

**If the owner is listed:**<br />Proceed to follow the owner’s instructions as long as it’s within our process whether the work order is an emergency or non-emergency i.e.: to assign a service provider, set an appointment, cancelation, completion, etc., and then create a task for PM to relay the details of why a specific action was taken by our team.

**If the owner is NOT listed/disabled:**<br />You can say:<br />”Thank you for reaching out. As of the moment, we don’t have your information registered on your Property management company’s profile. We’ll get your details verified with _appropriate team_ immediately and take action on your request afterward. Thank you for your patience.”<br />And then create a PM task on the work order or contact the PM’s escalations contacts if needed.

For Rentredi and NOMAD owners, please refer to [How to: Handle DIY Owner Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-DIY-Owner-Contacts).

## Follow Up Procedures

<Warning>
  **For Multiple Follow Up Tasks:** When handling multiple follow-up tasks, if there is at least one work order related to Property Damage or Extreme Habitability, you must adhere to the **triple dial procedure** for follow-ups. This ensures that urgent issues are addressed promptly.

  Always review all open tasks before proceeding. If a call has already been made for a specific task, you may skip that work order during your follow-up.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="If the PM requested to assign and dispatch a specific SP but the service provider was unresponsive or unable to schedule within the expected timeframe.">
  1. If the issue is property damaging or extreme habitability (refer to [How to: Identify Property Damage and Extreme Habitability Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations)):
     1. Call the escalation contacts. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
  2. If the issue is not property damaging or extreme habitability:
     1. Defer the task to the next day for another follow-up.
        1. If still unsuccessful after the second follow-up, complete the task and create a new task for the Property Manager
        2. Refer to the below [task template](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If the Service Provider completed an initial visit, the issue remains unresolved and must confirm materials with a warehouse/supplier before providing an estimate (no confirmed estimate date or limited responsiveness):">
  1. If the issue is property damaging or extreme habitability (refer to [How to: Identify Property Damage and Extreme Habitability Situations](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Identify-Property-Damage-and-Extreme-Habitability-Situations)):
     1. Call the escalation contacts. Refer to [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs)
  2. If the issue is not property damaging or extreme habitability:
     1. Make up to 2 total contact attempts on consecutive days (no more than 24hrs apart) to ask the SP for the estimate.
        1. Create a task for estimate follow up.
        2. If the Service Provider advises that they must first check with a **warehouse or supplier once it opens** before providing an estimate:
           1. Do **not** defer the task at the start of operating hours.
           2. Allow sufficient time for the Service Provider to obtain required materials and pricing details.
           3. Schedule the estimate follow-up for **12:00 PM** (or a reasonable mid-day time) to confirm whether materials, costs, and the estimate are available.
        3. If the Service Provider can’t provide the estimate but can provide a date when the estimate will be sent:
           1. Schedule the estimate follow-up for that date and place the WO in Pending in the meantime.
           2. No additional follow ups are required until that date.
        4. If after a second attempt, the Service Provider is unresponsive, can’t provide the estimate or can’t provide a date when the estimate would be sent, complete the task and create a new task for the Property Manager
           1. Refer to the below [task template](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates).
</Accordion>

           1. Refer to the below [task template](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/SOP-Work-Order-Follow-ups-and-Updates).

<Accordion title="Assigned Service Providers Also Listed as PM or EEC">
  In some cases, the assigned Service Provider on a Work Order may also be listed as the Property Manager or Emergency Escalation Contact. When this occurs **do not assume** that their dual role changes our obligation to verify scheduling, completion, or issue resolution.

  Ops team should:

  - **Continue to treat the follow-up process as business as usual.**
    - Follow all standard communication and update procedures as you would with any regular service provider.
    - Ensure all updates are clearly documented in the Work Order, and create or update related tasks as needed.
    - If there's any confusion about responsibilities or roles, escalate to Product support for correction.

  ‼️**IMPORTANT**: When handling **PD/EH issues**, if you anticipate that you may need to escalate the case **after calling the service provider**, and the **escalation contact is the same person** as the service provider, **address the potential escalation during that initial call**.

  There is **no need to call them a second time as an escalation contact**—make sure to cover both aspects (service provider support and escalation) in your first conversation with them.

  **Say something like:**

  _“Thanks for the update. Since you’re also listed as the escalation contact for this work order, we’ll consider this conversation as addressing both roles. I’ll make sure your response is documented as the official direction moving forward.”_

  _“Just to confirm—we’d typically escalate this to the PM due to the nature of the issue, but as you're also the escalation contact, your response here will serve as the PM’s direction. I’ll make sure that’s captured.”_
</Accordion>

## Ensuring Proper Follow-Through on Customer Requests

<Note>
  Following through means completing all necessary actions and commitments made during a customer interaction, ensuring tasks are carried out as promised, escalating issues to the appropriate teams when necessary, and confirming that customer inquiries are fully resolved. It is essential for maintaining customer satisfaction and trust, as it demonstrates accountability and responsiveness in service delivery.
</Note>

Step-by-Step Guide to Ensure Proper Follow-Through

1. **Completion of Request:**
   1. Follow up according to the expectations set during the call: Ensure that all commitments made during the interaction are addressed.
   2. Complete the TS guided workflow: Follow the process outlined in the [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders) to manage the request efficiently.
   3. Create a follow-up task assigned to the right user: Utilize the above guidelines provided in this document
   4. Make the follow-up call or SMS: Reach out to the resident, service provider, or Property Manager as necessary to confirm resolution and address any remaining concerns.
2. **Tech Issues Verification:**
   1. If dealing with tech issues, post in Slack (in the #ops-requests channel) using the WO link or the caller account
3. **WO Notes Review:**
   1. Add Work Order notes to refer an escalation to CS including Assembly ticket
   2. Link any related calls or conversations to the WO
4. **Resolution Check:**
   1. If the issue was resolved during the call and no further action is needed, document the interaction properly and close the inquiry.

<Accordion title="Q: What should I do if a customer (Resident, Property Manager, Property Owner, or Service Provider) replies or contacts the team requesting a callback or providing instructions after a TS task has already been deferred?">
  A: Update the task with any new information or instructions provided by the customer. Reset the task status back to the current property time so the TS associate can take immediate action.  If the task moves to _Invalid Time_, re-create a new task to ensure the request is handled properly.
</Accordion>

<Expandable title="Q: How should we handle Business Hours resident follow-ups for After-Hours work orders?">
  **A:** When a resident follows up during Business Hours about a work order previously handled After-Hours, first verify that the property manager’s account has an After-Hours subscription. Then follow the applicable ownership handoff process and create or assign the Property Manager task as directed in the [Informational: Latchel Customers & Customer Subscriptions](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Latchel-Library/Informational-Latchel-Customers-Customer-Subscriptions#informational-latchel-customers-and-customer-subscriptions) guidance.
</Expandable>

<Accordion title="What should I do if a resident reaches out and advises that the owner had sent their own vendor and had already resolved the issue?">
  1. Update the work order’s status to “Completed.” With notes as to why the work order was moved to Completed status.
  2. Reach out to the currently assigned service provider to have them disregard the job. Check with them if they will be billing for the job.
     1. 🗨️ **Say something like**: ”_Hi (Service Provider), we would like to inform you that you can disregard any work for this job since this will now be canceled as the owner has submitted their own service provider to resolve the issue. If your team is billing for the cancellation or any dispatch fees, please send your invoice to [team@latchel.com](mailto:team@latchel.com)._
  3. Create a task for property management advising them of what the resident has informed us, and provide them with information on whether the currently assigned service provider is billing or not. A sample task for property management would be:
     1. **Who is this task about?** _PM_
     2. **Set a date and time for the follow-up task:** _[leave blank]_
     3. **Task priority**: _Normal Severity - Medium, Urgent Severity - High, Emergency Severity - Highest_
     4. **_User group_**: _Choose the user group with “PM” in the title_
     5. **Assign to a User:**_ [leave blank]_
     6. When should this cancel? _Cancel on any state change_
     7. What action or information is for the Property Manager? _Hi, the resident had informed our team that the property owner had sent his/her own service provider to resolve the issue. We have now canceled the work order and informed the assigned service provider to disregard the job. (Add additional notes in case we have information on whether the service provider is billing or not.)_
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How should I handle customer inquiries about an open work order for a Property Management (PM) company that is already disabled in Latchel?">
  If the PM company’s subscription has ended and they are disabled in Latchel, we should not continue working on any open work orders for that company.

  **Politely inform the customer** that Latchel is no longer able to assist with their request and that they should reach out directly to their property management company for further help.

  **Say something like**:<br />“_Thank you for reaching out. The property management company for your home is no longer active with Latchel, so we’re unable to assist with this work order. Please contact your property management company directly for further assistance._”

  <Warning>
    **📝** **NOTE:**  If there is an existing open work order, cancel it on our end to prevent it from progressing to a secondary state that could create conflicts in work order handling.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What should we do if a Work Order (WO) was updated by a PM who is not part of the EEC list, but we later need to contact the EEC team (e.g., when the assigned SP cannot schedule a PD/EH WO, or an SP mentions they already spoke with a specific PM)? Should we contact that PM even if they are not on the EEC list?">
  A: We should always follow the official **EEC contact list** when reaching out to the EEC team, regardless of which PM last updated the WO.<br />The only exception is if a PM explicitly instructs us to contact them directly or to reach out to a different PM/EEC.

<Accordion title="Q: How should I handle follow-ups or updates on Bid Request work orders?">
  Since bid requests generate multiple work orders (one per SP), any follow-up or update on a bid request WO requires careful handling. Determine which scenario applies:

  1. **General follow-up or inquiry:**

  If you receive a general follow-up or inquiry related to a bid request WO (e.g., questions about timeline, status, scope, pricing, etc.):

  - If the information is readily available in the work order, provide it directly to the customer.
  - If not, create a task for the Property Manager. The PM owns the bid process and will provide direction on how to proceed.

  2. **Any scenario that may result in double dispatch:**

  If the follow-up or update indicates that there is a risk of more than one SP proceeding with the job — for example:

  - One of the SPs has already completed or is actively working on the job
  - An SP completed work without an approved estimate or before PM approval
  - The PM approved one bid, but the other SPs haven't been notified to stand down
  - An SP is enroute or has scheduled while another SP has already handled the job
  - Any situation where continued action by other assigned SPs is no longer needed

  Follow this process:

  1. **Notify the Property Manager** — Create a task for the PM informing them of the situation and the risk of double dispatch across the bid request WOs.
  2. **Notify the other assigned SP(s) via SMS** — Contact the SPs on the remaining bid request WOs and instruct them to hold off on any action until the PM confirms next steps.
     1. 🗨️ _Say something like:_ "Hi [SP Name], this is [Your Name] from Latchel. We're reaching out regarding work order [WO#]. Please hold off on any action for this job for the time being, as we are confirming details with the property manager. We will follow up with next steps shortly. Thank you!"
     2. ‼️IMPORTANT: If the risk of double dispatch is immediate — such as when the follow-up was received within the appointment window — **call the SP instead of sending an SMS.**
  3. **Document everything** — Add notes to all related bid request WOs explaining the situation and actions taken.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What do I do if someone (resident, fire department, police, or other emergency services) reaches out about a 911 or PD/EH situation, but the PM/property management company is disabled or inactive (no subscription)?">
  Resident safety always comes first — even without an active subscription.

  1. **Determine the severity of the situation:**
     1. **If the issue is PD/EH only (not 911):** Route the customer to their Property Manager.
     2. **If the issue is a 911 emergency:** Route the customer to call 911 immediately.
  2. **Customer Script:** 
     1. **For PD/EH issues (not 911), say something like:** _"I understand you're dealing with an urgent situation. Since your property management company is currently inactive in our system, we're unable to create a work order at this time. I'd recommend reaching out to your Property Manager directly for assistance. In the meantime, we will send a notification to your Property Manager to make them aware of the situation."_
     2. **For 911 emergencies, say something like:** _"Your safety is our top priority. Please call 911 immediately for emergency assistance. Since your property management company is currently inactive in our system, we're unable to create a work order, but we will send a notification to your Property Manager to make them aware of the emergency."_
  3. **Send a courtesy email to the PM** using the **Failed Escalation Contacts** email under the Emergency escalation contacts tab.
     1. Refer to [Job Aid: Critical, Pre-dispatch, Warranty, Special Notes & Emergency Escalation Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Job-Aid-Critical-Pre-dispatch-Warranty-Special-Notes-Emergency-Escalation-Contacts)
  7. **Document the interaction** (call notes, email sent) so there's a record of what was reported and what action was taken.

  **Email Template**

  **Subject:** Urgent: \[911 Emergency / PD/EH Issue\] Reported at \[Property Address\] — Action Required

  **Body:**

  Hi \[Property Management Company\]! This is \[Associate Name\] from Latchel. We received \[a call from the fire department / a report from the resident / a call from emergency services / a report regarding a property damage or environmental hazard issue\] regarding \[brief description of the emergency — e.g., an active fire / a gas leak / a police-related incident / property damage / environmental hazard\] at \[Property Address\].

  \[If 911: We advised \[the resident / emergency services\] to contact 911, and \[emergency services are on-site / have been dispatched / 911 has already responded\].\]

  \[If PD/EH only: We advised the resident to contact you directly regarding this property damage/environmental hazard issue.\]

  Please note that your account is currently inactive in our system, so we are unable to process a work order for this property. However, we wanted to ensure you are aware of the situation so you can take any necessary steps to follow up.

  If you have any questions or would like to reactivate your account with Latchel, please feel free to reach out to us.

  Best regards,<br />\[Associate Name\]<br />Latchel Support Team

  📝 **NOTE:** No subscription = no work order can be created, but resident safety and PM awareness still take priority. For 911 emergencies, do NOT delay routing the resident or assisting emergency services while attempting to reach a disabled PM. For PD/EH issues, ensure the customer is directed to their PM and that a notification is sent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What should I do if a resident says they plan to hire their own vendor and bill the PM if they don't get a timely update?">
  **A:** This is a de-escalation scenario, not a cancellation request. Follow these steps:

  1. Review the WO for the latest activity and provide the resident with a specific, meaningful update — avoid vague responses.
  2. Do **not** agree to or authorize the resident hiring their own vendor and billing the PM. Inform them that this would need to come directly from their Property Manager.
  3. If the resident still wishes to proceed, advise them that the PM may not reimburse them for costs, and that they would need to **cancel their current work order request first** before making their own arrangements.
  4. Take action to move the WO forward (follow up with the SP, escalate if needed per this SOP's standard procedures).
  5. Create a **high-priority task for the PM** informing them of the resident's frustration and the threat to hire their own vendor.

  For full scripting, blurbs, and task template, refer to **item #5** under "Handling a Resident cancellation" in the [How to: Handle a Work Order Cancellation](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-a-Work-Order-Cancellation#how-to-handle-a-work-order-cancellation).
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You should only break or deviate from the SOP in specific, documented scenarios.

- If the issue escalates to property damage or extreme habitability and the standard follow-up process would delay urgent action, escalate immediately to emergency contacts as outlined in the related SOPs.
- If a Property Manager, owner, or company leadership gives direct instructions that override the SOP (such as handling a follow-up in a specific way), follow their direction and document the exception.
