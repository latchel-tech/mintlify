Bundle up team, **Winter Peak is ON!** ❄️🔥 *From Dec 1, 2025 to Jan 4, 2026!*

The weather may be getting colder, but things are about to heat up with all the busy winter workloads - Heaters, No Hot Water, Pipe Bursts… you name it!

To keep the energy high, we're kicking off our Winter Peak Incentive Program! 🎉
Open to **Ops Associates and Troubleshooters**, this program lets you rack up points for **Quality Performance, Perfect Attendance, and Leadership/Behavioral Excellence** and score bonuses at the end of the season.

And yes… all bonuses go straight to your **Deel account**, ready to spend however you like! 💸✨

## **🎯 Program Structure**

Supervisors will track performance weekly and give points in three major categories.
Top performers in each category (Top 25%) will be entered into a raffle for cash bonuses.

The scoreboard will be based on the global quality score that will include Troubleshooting Ratings, CX review scores and WO audits. | Each week, TS who achieve **95%+ global quality score** earn **5 points**.
