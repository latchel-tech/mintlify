Bundle up team, **Winter Peak is ON!** ❄️🔥 _From Dec 1, 2025 to Jan 4, 2026!_

The weather may be getting colder, but things are about to heat up with all the busy winter workloads - Heaters, No Hot Water, Pipe Bursts… you name it!

To keep the energy high, we're kicking off our Winter Peak Incentive Program! 🎉 Open to **Ops Associates and Troubleshooters**, this program lets you rack up points for **Quality Performance, Perfect Attendance, and Leadership/Behavioral Excellence** and score bonuses at the end of the season.

And yes… all bonuses go straight to your **Deel account**, ready to spend however you like! 💸✨

## **🎯 Program Structure**

Supervisors will track performance weekly and give points in three major categories. Top performers in each category (Top 25%) will be entered into a raffle for cash bonuses.

---

## Type of Raffle

1. **Quality Performance**

**Why?**

_Because we are customer-obsessed and we always insist on the highest standards!_

| **Quality Performance** | **Ops Troubleshooters** | **Ops Associates** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility to be included in the raffle | Be in the Top 25% of the team scoreboard at the end of the program The scoreboard will be based on the global quality score that will include Troubleshooting Ratings, CX review scores and WO audits. | Be in the Top 25% of the team scoreboard at the end of the program |
| How to get points? | Each week, TS who achieve 95%\+ global quality score earn 5 points. Weekly and cumulative scores will be published. | <ul><li>Each week, Ops Assoc who achieve **97%\+ CX Review score** earn **5 points**.</li><li>Weekly and cumulative scores will be published.</li></ul> |
| Number of associates who will receive bonuses. | 4 | 4 |
| Bonus amount | \$50 | \$50 |

2. [**Leadership / Behavioral Performance**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1L8v6YO6DojUIWr93cgKcmzvR50U8QU98OJyYknjhSq4/edit "https://docs.google.com/document/d/1L8v6YO6DojUIWr93cgKcmzvR50U8QU98OJyYknjhSq4/edit")

**Why?**

_Because we value Ownership, Bias for Action, and going above and beyond!_

| **Leadership/Behavioral Performance** | **Ops Troubleshooters and Ops Associates** |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility to be included in the raffle | Be in the **Top 25% of the team** scoreboard at the end of the program |
| How to get points? | Each week, Supervisors will receive a “budget” of points to award to associates who demonstrate: <ul><li>Exceptional initiative</li><li>Leadership principles</li><li>Team support</li><li>Creative problem solving</li><li>Helping others or sharing best practices</li><li>Escalating issues to improve processes<br /></li></ul> **Limit:** Max **3 points per associate per week.** |
| Number of associates who will receive bonuses. | 2 |
| Bonus amount | \$50 |

3. **Perfect Attendance**

_Because we are reliable, we work all scheduled hours for the week!_

| **Perfect Attendance** | **Ops Troubleshooters and Ops Associates** |
| :-- | :-- |
| Eligibility to be included in the raffle | **You will NOT qualify if you:** <ul><li>Have **10\+ mins** inactivity without a time-off request</li><li>Have **over 10 mins** of unpaid time off</li><li>Take extended breaks/lunches</li></ul> **You are STILL eligible if you:** <ul><li>Use approved PTO, Supplemental Leave or Sick Leave for lateness/absences (Follow [**<u>Time Off Policy</u>**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=~5d3c7d0870e3c90c952fc308&title=Policy%3A%20Latchel%20Time%20and%20Attendance%20Policies%20%28Operations%20Production%20Team%29 "https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/pages/createpage.action?spaceKey=~5d3c7d0870e3c90c952fc308&title=Policy%3A%20Latchel%20Time%20and%20Attendance%20Policies%20%28Operations%20Production%20Team%29"))</li><li>Swap shifts in Deputy (and work the new shift)</li><li>Take VTO when volunteered (log it properly)</li></ul> |
| How to get points? | <ul><li>If you meet attendance requirements = **1 attendance point per week**</li></ul> |
| Number of associates who will receive bonuses. | 4 |
| Bonus amount | \$50 |
