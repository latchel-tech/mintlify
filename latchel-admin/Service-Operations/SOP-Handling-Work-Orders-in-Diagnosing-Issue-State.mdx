## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
Ensure WOs stuck in Diagnosing Issue progress efficiently with the right next step: complete diagnostics with the resident, skip diagnostics and schedule (if PM/Owner requests), or cancel. This reduces aging, duplicate work, and confusion across teams.
</Note>

## 📘 Definitions

- **Diagnosing Issue:** Interim state indicating required diagnostic intake has not been completed.
- **Diagnostic Flow:** The guided question path used during WO submission that determines details and severity.
- **Emergency-only customers:** Accounts where non-emergency WOs are forwarded to PM, not worked by Ops.

## 🔔 System Behavior and Notifications

- When a WO is created in **Diagnosing Issue**, the system sends the resident a diagnostic intake request up to **3 times** in **24-hour** intervals.
  - **Standard:** Close/cancel the WO.
  - **Emergency-only customers:** Forward to PM for their action instead of closing.

## Prerequisites

- Confirm subscription type and customer model (e.g., emergency-only vs. full-service).
- Verify there is no duplicate or related WO already in progress for the same issue.
- Have resident or PM/Owner contact information ready if manual action is needed.

## High-Level Pathways

- Resident calls to complete diagnostics → Complete Diagnostics path.
- PM/Owner instructs to proceed without diagnostics → Skip Diagnostics and proceed to Scheduling.
- PM/Owner/Resident requests cancellation or no response after reminders → Cancel Work Order (or forward to PM for emergency-only customers).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1 — Open the WO and Confirm Current State

Navigate to the WO and confirm it is in **Diagnosing Issue**.

<Note>
Check for existing tasks, prior notes, and any PM instructions before taking action. Avoid duplicate outreach or conflicting updates.
</Note>

### Step 2 — Open the Action Menu and Select Diagnose Issue

1. Click the **Action** button.
2. Select **Diagnose Issue** from the menu.

  <img src="/images/SVC-36.png" />
</Frame>

### Step 3 — Choose How to Proceed

In the Diagnose Issue modal, choose one of the three options based on the scenario.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/SVC-37.png" />
</Frame>

#### Option A: Complete Diagnostics (Resident needs assistance, Owner/PM requests to diagnose with the resident)

1. Click **Complete Diagnostics**.
2. **Diagnostic Flow**
   - Ask the resident the guided questions; capture details accurately.
   - Confirm severity outcomes and any emergency triggers per standard emergency handling SOPs.
3. **Proceed with WO submission**
   - Update title/description with diagnostic details.
   - Add necessary photos/attachments if provided by resident.
   - Save and advance the WO to the appropriate next state (e.g., Scheduling).
4. Document a brief summary note: "Diagnostics completed with resident on call; key findings: [summary]. Next step: [scheduling/approval/etc.]."

<Tip>
Best practice: Use clear, concise wording from the question prompts in your notes to improve downstream handoffs.
</Tip>

#### Option B: Skip Diagnostics and Proceed to Scheduling (PM/Owner directive)

1. Click **Skip Diagnostics and Proceed to Scheduling**.
2. Confirm you have PM/Owner direction to proceed without diagnostics (email/SMS/call note).
   - Assign skill/category and any vendor preferences per PM settings.
   - Create or update tasks for vendor outreach/resident coordination as needed.
3. Add a note: "Per PM/Owner request, skipping diagnostics and proceeding to scheduling." Include the date/time and channel of the directive.

<Note>
If skipping diagnostics increases risk of misclassification or missed details (e.g., potential emergency), flag to the PM and consider a quick clarifying call with the resident before dispatch.
</Note>

#### Option C: Cancel Work Order

1. Click **Cancel Work Order**.
2. Select the appropriate cancel reason (e.g., no response after reminders, issue resolved, duplicate).
   - "Resident did not complete diagnostics after 3 reminders; canceling per SOP."
   - "Emergency-only customer: forwarding to PM instead of canceling."
3. For **emergency-only customers**, do **not** cancel; instead forward to PM with a task and note to review/act.

## Operational Controls and Quality Checks

- Verify subscription type and customer model before choosing Skip or Cancel.
- Search for duplicates or related WOs to avoid double work or dispatch.
- Use precise, actionable notes that reflect either the diagnostic outcome or the PM instruction.
- If unsure of the appropriate path, escalate to Ops Support.

## [❓](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332893/How+to+Handle+Emergency+Work+Orders) **Frequently Asked Questions**

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="What if diagnostics reveal an emergency?">
Follow emergency handling SOPs immediately, document findings, and proceed with appropriate dispatch/escalation. Ensure severity and EEC notifications align with standard emergency processes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I skip diagnostics without explicit PM/Owner instruction?">
No. Only skip when the PM/Owner directs proceeding without diagnostics, and document the instruction in the WO notes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if the resident begins diagnostics online after I start?">
Continue through to completion with the resident on the call if that's faster. Avoid duplicate entries; ensure only one diagnostic path completes and notes are consolidated.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
