## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent gaps in training and miscommunication during call shadowing sessions. The goal is to ensure new colleagues can clearly hear both sides of customer calls and observe real interactions, using Slack in a way that reliably shares audio and screen.

  During training, your new colleagues will likely shadow you working on phone calls to understand [how Latchel interacts with our different customers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Latchel-Library/Informational-Latchel-Customer-Service-Tenets-High-5-Your-Customers-Today#informational-latchel-customer-service-tenets-high-5-your-customers-today\).
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

1. **Open Slack in a new tab on your _Google Chrome browser_.** If it is your first time using Slack in your Google Chrome browser, please follow the login instructions. Please note that you may not be able to hear both sides of the call in the Slack app, and Google Chrome is the only browser that supports Slack calls.
   <Frame>
     ![](/images/SVC-3.png)
   </Frame>
2. Search for your colleague's name in the Direct Messages section of Slack to start a conversation, then click the phone icon on the top right of the screen to call them.

<Frame caption="Open a new direct message">
  ![](/images/SVC-4.png)
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Find your colleague">
  ![](/images/SVC-5.png)
</Frame>

<Frame caption="Click the phone icon to start the call">
  ![](/images/SVC-6.png)
</Frame>

3. If you are using multiple monitors, choose which screen you want to share. Make sure you **check the box that says "Share audio"** on the bottom left of the screen. This step is essential for your colleague to hear both you and the customer!

<Frame>
  ![](/images/SVC-7.png)
</Frame>

Have a great call!
