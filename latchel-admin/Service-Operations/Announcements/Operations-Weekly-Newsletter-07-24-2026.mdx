### **Core Values in Action**

*We challenge ourselves to grow, even when it's uncomfortable. We work hard to make our company and products better every day. This is a great example of* **Continuous Improvement** *in action!*

@Elliot Alvarez inspired an update to our quality guidelines.

Quality trend: The de-escalation/severity call itself is usually correct, but the note doesn't cite the specific PM rule or Intake Settings criteria behind it. Elliot flagged that requiring associates to paste the exact Intake Rule into the work order notes every time they escalate or de-escalate severity is probably overkill at this point. It made sense when the feature was new, but we're all up to speed now. He was right. A note that states the decision and explains the reasoning behind a severity change is enough, as long as we're landing on the right outcome.
</Warning>

### Major Update

[(Admins Only) ↔ Job Aid: Service Provider Skills and Definitions](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/791119550/Admins+Only+Job+Aid+Service+Provider+Skills+and+Definitions)

**Why it matters:**

- Garbage disposal work orders were being routed to Appliance vendors, causing delays and higher costs when the issue can be handled by a handyman or general contractor.

**What changed;**

- Garbage disposal WOs categorized under Appliance/Plumbing → Now categorized under **Simple Maintenance Skills**

**What you need to do:**

- When selecting or confirming the skill for garbage disposal issues (jammed, making noise, not draining), assign **"Repair/Replace Garbage Disposal"** under Simple Maintenance Skills — not Appliance.

**When it takes effect:**

- Monday, 07/27
</Note>
