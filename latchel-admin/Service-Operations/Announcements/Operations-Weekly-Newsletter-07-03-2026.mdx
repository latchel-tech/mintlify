**SOPs to Refresh for this Week**

- **WYGC (Who Ya Gonna Call)**
  - WYGC is the tool that pulls up the correct "escalation contacts" for a property.
  - Auto-populates during guided TS when the issue is PD or EH
  - Manual access via Action Menu, PM Company page, or Property page for everything else
  - When to use it: Anytime you need to reach out to the escalation contacts — whether it's to escalate a budget approval, flag that you're unable to secure an SP, or clarify an unclear PD/EH situation. Note that the EEC list itself may also instruct escalation contact outside of these scenarios. Always use WYGC to get the right contact before calling.
  - Reference: [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2537652248/Job+Aid+PM+Outbound+Communication+for+Emergency+WOs)
