## Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to provide team members with a clear process for verifying whether a Property Manager is using the AppFolio Smart Bill "workaround" for invoice syncing. This ensures that any troubleshooting or escalation is based on confirmed configuration data between Latchel and AppFolio.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Impersonate the Property Manager in Latchel

_Ensure the customer is receiving invoice notifications. To do this, you must first access their account using the impersonation tool._<br /><br />a. Open the **Latchel Console** and search for the specific Property Manager's profile.<br />b. Navigate to the **Users** tab within their profile.

<br />c. Select any user from the list and click the **Impersonate User** button.<br />

<br />d. Once inside the PM's account, ensure they are opted-in to receive invoice data. In the top navigation menu, go to **Account Settings**.<br />e. Click on the **Notifications** tab.<br />f. Scroll down to the **Notification Preferences** section and locate **Payments & Invoices**.<br />g. Expand the category and verify that **Email, SMS, and In-App** checkboxes are all **selected/checked**.<br />

### Step 2: Check Latchel Console configuration

Identify the email address currently configured in Latchel to send invoices.

a. Open the Latchel Console and navigate to the specific Customer Profile. <br />b. Go to the **Details** tab. <br />c. Scroll down to the **Maint. Communication Settings** section. <br />d. Locate the **Send invoice notifications** box. <br />e. Verify the following:

- The toggle is ON (blue).
- Check the field **Additional, Specific Email Address (optional)**.
- **Action:** Copy the email address shown (it should end in `@invoices.appfolio.com`).

### Step 4: Verify active usage status (optional)

Once the configuration is confirmed, you can check whether the feature is actively being used.

a. On the same **Smart Bill Entry** page in AppFolio, look at the **How it works** section. b. Check the progress bar (for example, "106/200 bills created"). If there is activity here, the PM is successfully using Smart Bill. <br />c. Scroll down to the **Invoices** list to see the history of recently synced files and their status.

## Important notes

- **Email mismatch:** If the email in Latchel does not match the one in AppFolio, the sync will fail. Update the Latchel Console with the correct AppFolio address immediately.
- **One-way sync:** This setup only sends information from Latchel to AppFolio. It is a workaround because a direct, two-way sync is not currently supported.
- **File constraints:** AppFolio Smart Bill primarily processes PDF files. If a PM reports missing invoices, check whether the original files in Latchel were JPEGs or too large (over 20 MB), as these often fail to sync.
- **System sleep mode:** If AppFolio has been idle for an extended period, the Smart Bill page might take a few minutes to load while the system resumes.

## Troubleshooting checklist

- Is the **Send invoice notifications** toggle ON in Latchel?
- Does the email in Latchel end in `@invoices.appfolio.com`?
- Does the email match character-for-character with the AppFolio **Smart Bill Entry** email?
- (Optional) Is there recent activity in the AppFolio **Invoices** list?
