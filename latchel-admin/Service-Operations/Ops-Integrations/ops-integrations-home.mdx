## What Ops Integrations is

Ops Integrations is the team within Service Operations responsible for keeping Latchel's operation cleanly connected to the systems, data, and workflows of our customers (property managers).

In practice, we're the bridge between what happens in a customer's tools and what happens inside Latchel. We make sure work orders, tickets, and their related data flow correctly in both directions, with no gaps and no duplicates, so the operations team can resolve maintenance quickly.

## Why integrations matter

Every integration connects Latchel's service operation to the customer's world. When an integration works well, requests come in complete, get routed to the right provider, and their status flows back with no manual work. When it breaks, it turns into dropped tickets, delays, and customer friction.

That's why the work of Ops Integrations centers on three things:

- Connect the customer's systems and data to Latchel correctly.
- Monitor the health of those connections to catch issues before they hit the service.
- Operate the specialized ticket flows (emergencies, common areas, recurring, churn alerts) that depend on those integrations.
