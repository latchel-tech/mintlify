**Instructions / How-To**

In this Full story review, the coach utilizes a tool to share the video and audio recordings we have when the associate handled the work order. From the time the associate jumps into the work order, until they leave. The screen of all the actions and steps they took will be recorded.  This helps identify any potential mistakes or missed information by the associate and determine knowledge gaps or behavior issues. This is only for the navigation within the console and allows a better understanding of the path the associate took at all times while handling the work order.

**Step 1: Set the Stage**

- Start by defining what you want to achieve with the full story review. Identify the particular behavior, or scenario you want the person to focus on.
- Emphasize that the aim of the feedback session is to assist them in recognizing their strengths and areas that they could benefit from improvement.
- Establish a secure and encouraging atmosphere for the feedback session, ensuring the coachee feels comfortable and supported.

**Step 2: Describe the Scenario**

- Present the situation or scenario that the person was handling.
- Give a short explanation of the context, including any important details or information that could be missed.
- Make sure the full story shows where the associate could’ve made the mistake, or when they missed the important piece of information.

**Potential Scenarios:**

- Confirm previous temperature _(We can confirm the temperature within the property at the time the associate handled the WO)_
- Confirm if it was within business hours _(Depending the property time we have to follow different paths)_
- Review actions taken by associate _(Determine if they missed any important information/actions while reviewing the WO)_

**Step 3: Go through the full story session**

- Begin the recording by sharing your screen so the associate can review it
- Highlight any important details missed
- Pause the recording if there’s anything that requires discussion
- Determine why associate took this path if needed

**Step 4: Take Notes**

- Make detailed notes during the full story session, capturing specific examples of the coachee's strengths and areas for improvement.

**Step 5: Provide Feedback**

- After the full story session ends, offer constructive feedback that acknowledges their successes while also highlighting areas where they can grow and develop.
- With the observed feedback, provide additional guidance or resources to support their continuous development in the specific skill or behavior area.
- Confirm if the associate has any questions left.
- Encourage associates to use our internal resources like SOPs and continue to share the ones that apply to the feedback.
