title: "Service Ops: Pre-Coaching Evaluation Process"
groups: ["latchel"]
---

These streamlined bullet points outline the key steps involved in the pre-coaching evaluation process, ensuring a focused and efficient assessment of the associate's work order opportunities prior to the coaching session..

1. **Verify reported work order error/opportunity from the svc-ops-feedback channel in Slack.**
   - Initiate a review of the reported work order error/opportunity.
2. **Review work order details and compare them with the call-out.**
   - Validate the accuracy and relevance of the call-out.
   - Cross-reference the reported issue with the details in the work order.
3. **Listen to recorded calls for context and communication.**
   - Listen to recorded calls related to the work order to capture the interaction.
   - Pay attention to the associate's communication, tone, and adherence to guidelines.
4. **Examine Full Story *(if needed)* and any related Slack channel communication.**
   - Conduct a comprehensive review of Full Story surrounding the work order and the associate's action.
   - Analyze and look for instances of instructions, guidance, or collaboration within the channels. Consider any support interactions or escalations related to the work order.
5. **Evaluate adherence to our process, SOP or Job Aids.**
   - Evaluate the associate's use of available resources and references.
   - Assess whether the associate followed established protocols and procedures.
6. **Identify contributing factors to the error/opportunity.**
   - Identify potential contributing factors to the error/opportunity, such as system limitations like a bug or internet connection, etc.
7. **Document key findings, including any gaps in understanding or execution.**
   - Prepare related examples and instances to address during the coaching session.
   - Compile a summary of the evaluation outcomes for reference in the coaching session.
