**Objective:**

This project aims to develop comprehensive guidelines that empower team members to deliver impactful coaching sessions aimed at promoting the personal development of associates. Coaching sessions should always be conducted via Google Meet. These guidelines will outline the key elements, techniques, and best practices necessary to create value-packed coaching sessions that inspire growth, enhance skills, and foster a culture of continuous improvement within the organization.

**Introduction and Rapport Building:**

- Begin by establishing a positive and supportive atmosphere.
- Start to read the Coaching Opportunity to the associate.
- Explain the purpose and structure of the coaching session.
- Build rapport by engaging in small talk or discussing recent achievements or challenges.
- Clearly communicate the specific area of improvement or constructive feedback based on observations, assessments, or feedback received.

**Set SMART Goals:**

- Collaborate with the associate to set Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART) goals. [Guidelines in Establishing SMART Goals](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Guidelines-in-Establishing-SMART-Goals)
- Ensure the goals align with the coaching objective and are challenging yet attainable. [Guidelines in Establishing SMART Action Plans](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Coaches-Guidelines-in-Establishing-SMART-Action-Plans)

**Develop Action Steps:**

- Together with the associate, determine specific action steps to achieve the identified goals.
- Break down the larger goals into manageable tasks or milestones.
- Discuss potential strategies, resources, or support needed to accomplish the action steps.

**Role-playing and Skill Building:**

- Engage in role-playing exercises or skill-building activities related to the identified goals.
- Provide guidance, demonstration, or feedback as the associate practices new skills.

**Check for Understanding and Commitment:**

- Confirm the associate's understanding of the discussed goals, action steps, and expectations.
- Ensure their commitment to follow through with the agreed-upon plan.

**Wrap-up and Next Steps:**

- Summarize key points discussed during the coaching session.
- Reiterate the associate commitment and motivation to achieve their goals.
- Agree on any follow-up actions, deadlines, or additional support needed.
- Express appreciation for the associate participation and commitment.

### **Guide to Severity Definitions (Understanding the Impact Levels of Callouts):**

In addition, you may also refer to this SOP: [How to: Submit and Process Feedback Tickets](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3646521894/How+to+Submit+Associate+Feedback+Tickets)

| **SEVERITY** | **DEFINITION** |
| --- | --- |
| **Normal** | Refers to an oversight that has a minor impact on the overall operation or performance. It is typically a non-critical opportunity. <br />Normal severity callouts do not pose an immediate threat to the team or the organization. |
| **Urgent** | Signifies an error or issue that has a moderate impact on the team's performance, productivity, or customer experience. It requires timely attention and correction to prevent further consequences. <br />Urgent severity callouts may involve situations where immediate action is needed to mitigate the impact, resolve customer concerns, or ensure compliance with essential policy and procedures.<br /><br />**Sample Scenarios:** <ul><li>Failed to update the problem/location accordingly causing incorrect WO severity.</li><li>Failure to follow the critical notes or pre-dispatch instructions.</li><li>Incorrect type of SP assigned.</li><li>Failed to update the WO skill causing to send an incorrect SP.</li><li>Failed to escalate to the PM when needed.</li><li>Failed to properly probe causing to miss the real severity of the issue on a WO.</li><li>Call handling issues (does not have an obvious/direct effect on cx experience).</li><li>Failure to set proper expectations for SPs when dispatching that has caused negative experiences for them.</li><li>Failed to act on what was promised to deliver.</li><li>Completing a task/ticket without taking relevant action.</li></ul> |
| **Critical** | <ul><li>Represents a severe opportunity or incident that has a significant impact on operations, Client/PM satisfaction, or the reputation of Latchel. It requires immediate and decisive action to address the situation and prevent further negative impact. Critical severity callouts may involve serious process violations, major opportunity affecting multiple work orders, or impact client/PM relationship (retention risk). <br /><br />**Sample Scenarios:**</li><li>Ignored the necessity of obtaining secondary approval (cost-related).</li><li>Failure in adhering to PM's direct/special instructions (provided via call/SMS/Webchat/Email).</li><li>Call handling issues with the customers (may include being rude or disconnecting the call without a valid reason).</li><li>Incorrect payment processing.</li></ul> |

**1:1 Coaching vs Shared via team level or Discussed via instant message**

Feedback of Normal severity may not directly affect our external customers. In such cases, it may not necessitate individual coaching sessions. Instead, it can be addressed through reminders in the team channel or discussed via instant messaging with the associate. Here are some scenarios where a 1:1 coaching session may not be necessary:

1. Not adhering to task creation guidelines.
2. Incomplete notes left (Agent noted the link of ZD ticket instead of summarizing the conversation).
3. Issues with grammar or spelling.
4. Neglecting to send a CSAT survey or submitting a survey when it is not applicable.
5. Failing to complete a task after taking action.
6. Not updating Work Order title or description.

For those working on the OCT Tickets, the ticket may skip the assessment and follow the usual process stated in [How to: Submit Feedback Tickets](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3646521894/How+to+Submit+Associate+Feedback+Tickets). Please make sure that notes about the feedback review and the action done (**Shared via team level or Discussed via instant message**) is noted on the comments tab.

_Please keep in mind that although these examples may not necessitate a one-on-one session, if a missed opportunity has led to a negative customer experience, a one-on-one session may be necessary. Additionally, any other situation not mentioned here but significantly affecting the customer experience may also warrant a one-on-one coaching session._
