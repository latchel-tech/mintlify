## Step by step guide

1. **Getting Service Operations Involved**
   1. The change could be initiated internally by Service Operations or an external department, for example: Product, CS, Finance, etc.
   2. The Stakeholder driving the change needs to determine if it may impact Service Operations during the planning phase.
      1. If one of the following questions is true, the Service Operations Change Management Program Manager (PM) should be involved:
         1. Will Service Operations need to execute a new workflow as a result of this change?
         2. Will Service Operations need to modify an existing workflow as a result of this change?
         3. Is Service Operations staffing potentially affected as a result of this change?
         4. Could customers reach out to Service Operations to request assistance related to this change?
         5. Will this change require new or updated customer help guides?
         6. Will this change affect account configuration?
         7. Could this change increase the number of escalations to Product Support?
            1. If in doubt, the Stakeholder can reach out to the Service Operations Change Management Program Manager for clarification.
   3. The Stakeholder will contact the Service Operations Change Management PM.
      1. The following information needs to be provided:
         1. What the intended change is
         2. Problem driving said change
         3. Desired timeframe of implementation
   4. The Service Operations Change Management PM will confirm if the issue in fact affects Service Operations.
      1. Review that any of the questions from step 1.b.i are affirmative.
      2. Determine what teams need to be involved.
         1. Training Team:
            1. Does the release involve modifying an existing behavior (outside of a guided workflow)?
            2. Does the release involve adopting an entirely new behavior (outside of a guided workflow)?
         2. Service Operations Manager:
            1. Does the release impact workload distribution?
            2. Does the release impact staff requirements?
            3. Does the release impact role responsibilities?
            4. Do we need any new blurbs?
         3. Service Operations Business Intelligence
            1. Do we need any report to be updated?
            2. Do we need any dashboard to be updated?
         4. Change Management:
            1. Is there existing documentation that needs to be updated?
            2. Does a new document need to be created?
      3. Determine what’s the appropriate timeframe required to support the change.
         1. Consider the following:
            1. Training required
            2. Updates required to documentation
            3. Updates to reporting or dashboards
            4. Labor planning
2. **(Optional) Involve external departments**
   1. If the change is driven by Service Operations:
      1. The Stakeholder driving the change needs to answer the following questions to determine if there’s any impact to external departments.
         1. Customer Success:
            1. Does the change impact the PM’s expectations of the service received by Service Operations?
            2. Could the change increase invoice charges to the PM?
            3. Could the change increase escalations to the PM?
         2. Finance:
            1. Could the change increase Latchel’s expenses?
            2. Does the change impact Finance tasks?
         3. Product:
            1. Can the change break the platform?
            2. Can the change alter the product offering?
      2. Contact the department’s manager to coordinate the change.
3. **Preparing Service Operations Support**
   1. (If needed) Success metrics and rollback thresholds need to be determined by the Stakeholder driving the change.
   2. Track execution of any required tasks via the preferred project management tool.
      1. In this case the [https://latchel.atlassian.net/jira/software/projects/PPB/boards/38](https://latchel.atlassian.net/jira/software/projects/PPB/boards/38) board in Jira.
      2. Obtain any inputs necessary from the external department (Product/CS/Finance).
         1. Product Input Template: [Change Management\] Product Input Template](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PROD/pages/2554200120/Change+Management+Product+Input+Template?xpis=eyJicmlkZ2UiOiJzbWFydExpbmtzIiwiaWQiOiIxNzg4NDAwNDQ0NzAzIiwic291cmNlIjoiY29uZmx1ZW5jZSJ9)
         2. There isn’t a template available from other departments.
      3. Refer to\* Table of responsibilities per stakeholder: Service Operations \*to know who is responsible for executing the different activities.
      4. Determine if Operations Support Specialists need to be informed before making the change to ensure that they can support the team with any questions.
4. **Launching the Change**
   1. Determine if a War Room channel is necessary to monitor the team.
      1. War Room channels should not remain open more than 2 weeks after the implementation.
   2. Communicate the change.
      1. The communication needs to go out before or right after the change takes place, but it shouldn’t be delayed.
         1. It’s best to avoid making changes on Fridays to be able to monitor the impact over the next day.
            1. Exceptions can be made for things that are business critical.
      2. Determine what is going to be the communication medium
         1. Slack channel:
            1. Usually the [https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C2R3MUKGT](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/C2R3MUKGT) channel.
            2. If the issue affects multiple departments, also post in [https://latchel.slack.com/archives/GFK4DKP5Y](https://latchel.slack.com/archives/GFK4DKP5Y).
         2. Ops/Troubleshooter All Hands.
         3. Separate launch meeting.
      3. Publish the change via the Weekly Newsletter
         1. The Program Manager will publish a weekly newsletter with a compilation of the following as part of the change management process:
            1. Service Operations' goals and objectives (this will not change frequently). (From Ops Leadership)
            2. Discussion topics of the Ops All Hands. (Provided by Training and Quality Program Manager)
            3. SOPs and Job-aids updated. (Collected by Change Management Program Manager)
            4. Product releases impacting Service Operations. (Collected by Change Management Program Manager from #product-release Slack channel)
5. **Post Launch Tracking**
   1. Follow up with the Service Operations team after 24hrs and 48hrs.
      1. Is the team having any issues?
         1. **Serv Ops team to Conduct Audit**
            1. Perform a change management audit to verify that all procedures and protocols are correctly followed.
         2. **Store Audit Documentation**
            1. Save all completed audits in the designated Google Drive folder: **“[Change Management Audits for Major Releases](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1NFGd6jvepqXw60WjD-fCG_SBP7cqmQll?usp=sharing)”**.
            2. This ensures proper documentation, traceability, and compliance tracking.
            3. Submit any feedback to svc-ops-feedback channel and follow-up on necessary coaching.
   2. Gather feedback to iterate and communicate the feedback to the Stakeholder that drove the change.
   3. Close war-room channel (if any) after two weeks.
   4. The Stakeholder driving the change will determine if success metrics are met and execute:
      1. If success metrics are not met, determine if rollback is necessary.
         1. Conduct root cause analysis of why the change failed.

## Table 1. Responsibilities per stakeholder: Service Operations

| **Stakeholder Title** | **Responsibilities** |
| --- | --- |
| Change Management Program Manager | <ul><li>Coordinate change management process, including Product Support and Data Support.</li><li>Compile a list of ongoing product developments that affect operations.</li><li>Update or create any SOP (Refer to: [How to: Create a Latchel SOP](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-Latchel-SOP)).</li><li>Own the feature request intake process.</li><li>Own the library request intake process.</li><li>Provide feedback to any department post launch.</li></ul> |
| Training and Quality Program Manager | <ul><li>Training development to support change (if needed).</li><li>Training planning and coordination with Service Operations Managers, Supervisors & Operations Support Specialists.</li><li>Update or create any job-aid.</li></ul> |
| Service Operations Manager or Supervisor | <ul><li>Associate workload balancing to support change.</li><li>Staff balancing.</li><li>Ops compliance with new processes.</li></ul> |
| Data Support Team Leader | <ul><li>Notify Data Support team members</li></ul> |
| Product Support Manager | <ul><li>Notify Product Support team members</li></ul> |
| Heads of Service Service Operations | <ul><li>Provide Service Operations Goals and Objectives.</li></ul> |
| Business Intelligence | <ul><li>Perform reporting or dashboard updates.</li></ul> |
