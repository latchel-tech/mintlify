<Note>
We want to ensure that Assembly tickets—requests or issues submitted by associates or customers—are handled promptly, accurately, and with clear documentation. The goal of this SOP is to provide a standardized, step-by-step process for reviewing, responding to, and closing Assembly tickets. This process prevents missed or duplicate responses, miscommunication, and unresolved issues—helping Latchel deliver consistent support, reduce operational errors, and maintain high service standards for both internal teams and customers
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### 🧭 Navigating a ticket

When an associate posts in the Slack channel or when an email is received, the application automatically creates a ticket in the inbox. It will appear under "Needs Response" tickets. Click on the ticket to access it and take action.

Refer to [this page](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Operations-Support-Specialist-Guidelines-for-Handling-Associate-Queries) for more information in handling associate queries.

<Note>
📝 NOTE: To quickly view or respond to a ticket, simply click on it. A sleek popup panel will open on the right, letting you reply, update status, escalate, reassign, or add labels and categories — all without losing the context of your inbox.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-196.png" alt="Ticket popup panel" /></Frame>

<Note>
📝 NOTE: Verify that the ticket is unassigned to prevent duplicate efforts. If the ticket you have opened is already assigned, proceed to the next one.
</Note>

<Tip>
💡 Tip: Click the ticket number in the popup panel to open the full ticket in a new window. You can assign the ticket directly from the popup panel or from the full ticket view.
</Tip>

Assign the ticket to yourself by navigating to the right corner, under "Attributes" panel and selecting your name from the "Owner" dropdown list.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-197.png" alt="Owner dropdown list" /></Frame>

Start the timer.

In the comment box, type your response.

The formatting toolbar now appears when you highlight text instead of being fixed in place.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-198.png" alt="Formatting toolbar on text highlight" /></Frame>

Signatures & templates have moved to the bottom-right corner of the editor.

<Note>
📝 NOTE: Remember to always click the "Signature" button when composing emails or responses to add your signature.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-199.png" alt="Signature button location" /></Frame>

If you haven't set your signature, you can add them here: [https://www.dashboard.askassembly.app/preferences/profile](https://www.dashboard.askassembly.app/preferences/profile) .

Click the "Send" button (highlighted below) to submit it.

Assembly gives you the option to send messages either as an internal note or as a new email, depending on your needs:

- Send as Internal: Choose this option if the message is for internal use only and should not be sent to the recipient.
- Send New Email: This option is only used when sending a new outbound email. Refer to [How to: Compose a New Email (Assembly)](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Compose-a-New-Email-(Assembly)).

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-200.png" alt="Send button and message type options" /></Frame>

<Note>
📝 NOTE: Use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Enter to send a message. This allows you to quickly send messages without switching focus. Press Enter only for creating new lines when composing drafts or adding additional information.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-201.png" alt="Ctrl+Enter keyboard shortcut" /></Frame>

To add additional users in CC / BCC, click the three dots in the top-right of the email.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-202.png" alt="Three dots menu for CC/BCC" /></Frame>

On the top right, you can choose either CC or BCC and then enter the email address on the respective field for recipients.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-203.png" alt="CC or BCC field" /></Frame>

Add the internal note (see below sample)

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-204.png" alt="Sample internal note" /></Frame>

Once you completed your response, the ticket will automatically be tagged as "Unassigned" and "Closed."

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-205.png" alt="Ticket tagged as Unassigned and Closed" /></Frame>

Stop the timer.

### How to Forward an email

Follow Steps 1–5 of the above guidelines.

Before sending the email, update the recipient:

- Click the three dots at the top right corner of the email.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-206.png" alt="Three dots at the top right corner of the email" /></Frame>

- Delete all email addresses in the To, CC, and BCC fields.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-207.png" alt="Deleting email addresses in To, CC, and BCC fields" /></Frame>

- Enter the email address of the recipient you are forwarding the email to.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-208.png" alt="Entering the recipient's email address" /></Frame>

- Compose your message and click Send.

After sending:

- You will see a notification at the top-right of your screen confirming the message was successfully submitted.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-209.png" alt="Notification confirming the message was submitted" /></Frame>

- On the same ticket, the activity will be updated to show the forwarded message.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-210.png" alt="Ticket activity showing the forwarded message" /></Frame>

- By clicking the drop-down you can confirm that the message was successfully sent to the correct recipient

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-211.png" alt="Drop-down confirming message sent to correct recipient" /></Frame>

- Add internal notes in the ticket:
  - Indicate that the email was forwarded to the recipient.
  - Include any additional notes or reasons for forwarding the request.

### 🔄 Taking action on a reopened ticket

<Note>
📝 NOTE: if an associate responds to a previously closed issue, the ticket will automatically reopen with a "Needs Response" status.
</Note>

- Open the reopened ticket from your inbox.
  - Reopened tickets assigned to you will appear in your "My Inbox." They can also show up in the general inbox.
  - As support, always check who the ticket owner is before proceeding.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-213.png" alt="Checking the ticket owner" /></Frame>

- Review the ticket details and any previous actions taken.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-214.png" alt="Reviewing ticket details and previous actions" /></Frame>

- Determine the necessary steps to address the issue.
- Document your actions and updates in the comment box. Click "Send" to submit your response.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-215.png" alt="Documenting actions in the comment box" /></Frame>

- Update the ticket status to "Closed".

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-216.png" alt="Updating ticket status to Closed" /></Frame>

### 📨 How to: Use an email templated response/escalation

Templates help standardize responses and ticket escalations. They allow you to quickly share Work Order details with customers or internal teams handling escalations. Templates also ensure we provide consistent, customer-focused email responses.

While composing a new email or replying to an existing one, click the '{ }' button at the bottom right corner of the editor.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-217.png" alt="Template button in the editor" /></Frame>

You will be presented with a list of available templates. Search for and select the template you need for the ticket.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-218.png" alt="List of available templates" /></Frame>

Once selected, the template will automatically be applied to the message body.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-219.png" alt="Template applied to the message body" /></Frame>

<Note>
📝 NOTE: Assembly allows the use of multiple email templates. To ensure only the required template is used, verify that the correct template is checked.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-220.png" alt="Verifying the correct template is checked" /></Frame>

Fill out the necessary information and make any required edits.

Click 'Send' to complete the process.

### 📥 Handling Emails with Subject: "New Latchel Website Form Submission"

Customers may submit a form on the Latchel website, triggering an automated check to see if the provided email matches an existing Latchel user. If a match is found, the submission is forwarded to team@latchel.com for review.

Sample emails:

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-221.png" alt="Sample email 1" /></Frame>
<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-222.png" alt="Sample email 2" /></Frame>

When responding to these emails, follow these steps:

- Search for the customer in the Latchel system to verify their details.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-223.png" alt="Searching for the customer in the Latchel system" /></Frame>

- Since the form does not specify the request, call the customer to confirm what assistance they need.

"We received your form submission via Latchel's website. Do you have a maintenance issue we can help with."

- Based on the conversation, follow the necessary guidelines to assist the customer.
- If the issue requires further action, proceed according to standard procedures.
- If you cannot reach the customer by phone, send a follow-up SMS or email to ensure they receive assistance.
- Record all relevant details in:
  - The email thread
  - The customer's profile in the Latchel system

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
