_**<u>Operations Support Specialist</u>**_

**Scope**

In this role, you will optimize workforce management and provide essential coaching support to enhance associate performance. By ensuring the right number of staff is effectively distributed across our queues, you will monitor real-time queue data and SLAs to align staffing with customer demand, thereby maintaining operational efficiency and service levels. Additionally, you will coach associates by providing guidance on policies and SOPs and addressing escalated inquiries to support their development. Your efforts will contribute to refining processes in collaboration with stakeholders, while also directly assisting customers with complex inquiries as needed.

**Responsibilities**

- <u>Monitor service level agreements:</u> ensure daily service level agreements are met for all operational teams.
- <u>Resource allocation:</u> balance the needs of the external customers and internal customers by allocating headcount where it is needed.
- <u>Deficiencies identification:</u> document and report gaps and opportunities that can prevent operational goals (productivity/efficiency) to be achieved.
- <u>Associate Escalation support:</u> assist associates with product and process-related questions. This way the customer can get the best answer or proper guidance on how soon the issue can be resolved.
- <u>Irate Customer Support:</u>** **address customer inquiries for a minimum of 1 hour per day, using the same standard work as an Operations Associate/Troubleshooter. In addition, talk to customers when they request a supervisor.
- <u>Training support:</u> partner with the Operations Trainer to support activities linked to the training curriculum.
- <u>Real-time Performance Coaching:</u> provide information on how to handle similar situations in the future, in real-time.
- <u>Investigations:</u> use root cause analysis techniques to identify problems and propose solutions to complex customer problems, real-time while a customer is on the line.
- <u>SOP Maintenance:</u> As needed this role will update SOP’s or guidelines to support team

<u>Basic qualifications</u>

- 6+ months experience with Latchel in both Ops Associate & Troubleshooter roles, or 12+ months experience in either role.
- Good performance metrics (quality and productivity).
- English fluency.

Proven examples of:

- Ability to keep pace and thrive in Latchel’s operations environment.
- Strong prioritization and time management skills.
- Research of complex customer inquiries.
- Peer coaching.
- Strong writing skills (i.e., performance feedback).
- Proven examples of data analysis and reports analysis (i.e., spreadsheet functionality, pivot tables, SQL).
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills with the ability to communicate effectively to a wide range of constituencies, in a diverse environment.

<u>Preferred qualifications</u>

- Introductory knowledge of lean management principles.
- Experience leading process improvement initiatives.
- 1+ years of experience in business analytics.
- Proven examples of short-term and long-term plan design to address significant changes, team operation management, and process improvement.

Reports directly to Operations Supervisors with monthly Skip Levels with Operations Manager.
