## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
We want to prevent wasted time, confusion, and inconsistent support by giving associates a single, easy-to-use resource for the most common help guides, scripts, and process exceptions needed to assist Property Managers, Residents, Service Providers, and Owners. The goal is to ensure team members can quickly find and share accurate instructions or blurbs for routine requests—like updating work order status, submitting invoices, or handling special scenarios—without searching through multiple sources or risking outdated information.

**Please note that this is not an all-encompassing list of guides, and the full library can be found at**[http://help.latchel.com](http://help.latchel.com)**.**
</Info>

#### Special Scripts

1. Service providers may reach out to define their communication preferences, such as specifying that they prefer to be contacted only during business hours, requesting not to be called after a certain time, or indicating that they would like to receive follow-ups via text only. It is essential to probe further to understand their needs fully before proceeding with the following actions.
  1. **Scenario 1: Service Provider only wants to be contacted during business hours. OR Service Provider only wants to be contacted by text, call, or email versus all three methods.**
    1. <Frame>
 ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-39.png)
</Frame>
 Suggested Blurb: “*Thank you for contacting us about your communication preferences. At this time, we aren't able to customize communication settings to accommodate this request. However, we can update your primary contact information to your business line, ensuring communication is received during your business hours and through your preferred method. Many providers find this useful for managing contact preferences. If you'd like to add a new primary phone number or need further assistance, please let me know.”*
    2. If the service provider agrees to update their primary contact number
      1. Proceed to update the information. Refer to How to: Navigate Service Provider Profiles
      2. <Frame>
 ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-40.png)
</Frame>
 Suggested Blurb: “*Hi [Service Provider's Name]. I will go ahead and update your primary contact number! Moving forward, all communications will be directed to your new contact number. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please reach out.*”
  2. **Scenario 2: Service Provider states Latchel contacts them too many times for follow ups and is dissatisfied with the current communication preferences**. Provide them with a help guide that will help eliminate follow-up calls in the future.
    1. <Frame>
 ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-41.png)
</Frame>
 Suggested Blurb: “*I’m contacting you today to get an update on [work order / update needed]. Please remember that you can always update work orders through your dashboard: [insert dashboard link] promptly. This can help us avoid unnecessary follow-ups and keep everything running smoothly. Here’s a guide: [provide the link to applicable help guide] that can assist you with this concern moving forward.*”
  3. **Scenario 3: Service Provider states they do not schedule jobs outside of business hours (only weekdays)**
    1. <Frame>
 ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-42.png)
</Frame>
 Suggested Blurb:*“I understand. You can accept and schedule work orders through your dashboard: [insert dashboard link]. You’ll have the ability to schedule the time that works best for your schedule.”*
  4. If the service provider becomes **extremely irate** and **frustrated**
    1. Escalate the issue to Product Support for further assistance. [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1485373477/How+to+Escalate+to+Product+Support)

### Owners - Special Scripts

#### RentRedi Owner wants to cancel Latchel

If a RentRedi Owner mentions they want to cancel Latchel, proceed as follows:

<Tip>
*Hello, [Owner name]! We truly appreciate you reaching out to our team and I’m so sorry we didn’t meet your expectations. Please follow this guide to cancel Latchel through your RentRedi portal:*[https://help.rentredi.com/en/articles/4684908-how-to-pause-switch-latchel-plans](https://help.rentredi.com/en/articles/4684908-how-to-pause-switch-latchel-plans). Additionally, if you would like to take over any of your open maintenance requests, refer to the following guide: [https://help.rentredi.com/en/articles/4745791-take-over-a-maintenance-request-from-latchel](https://help.rentredi.com/en/articles/4745791-take-over-a-maintenance-request-from-latchel). Should you have any follow-up questions, please contact RentRedi directly for assistance.
</Tip>

#### RentRedi Or Nomad Owner wants to request Bids for a job

If a RentRedi or Nomad Owner mentions they want to request multiple estimates or bids for job, we need to inform them that’s not supported:

<Tip>
*Hello, [Owner name]! We truly appreciate you reaching out to our team, unfortunately, our platform doesn’t support requesting multiple bids for a single job for [Rentredi/Nomad] clients yet, but we’ll take note of this for potential platform improvements. Is there anything else I can assist you with today?*
</Tip>

### Exceptions

#### PM - Requesting Multiple Bids for a single WO

We now have a functionality that allows PMs to request multiple bids/estimates for the same job. However, this functionality is only available to PMs themselves, not internal Latchel users, as a result, if the PM reaches out asking us to submit a request to get multiple bids for a job on their behalf provide the following guidance:

<Tip>
*Hello, [PM name]! We truly appreciate you reaching out to our team. At present, I'm unable to submit a bids request on your behalf due to system restrictions aimed at maintaining the integrity of your operations. However, ensuring you have a seamless experience is at the forefront of our efforts. So, let's navigate this together. I would be happy to answer any questions you have, guide you through the bids submission process, or provide any other support you may need.*
</Tip>

Refer to [Bid Requests](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Product-Engineering/bid-requests) which contains the Product documentation to be able to assist and answer questions for the PMs.

#### PM - Removing LCN or LVN Service Provider from a job (not applicable to Preferred SPs)

When a PM inquires about removing an LCN or LVN Service Provider from a job, do NOT attempt to share a guide with them. Property Managers can’t remove this type of SP from a job, and it’ll lead to customer frustration if a guide is shared. In this case, you should take action on the PM’s behalf.

1. **LCN Service Providers**
  1. If an appointment has not occurred, meaning, the LCN SP has NOT visited the property:
    1. Reassign the WO to the desired SP (refer to [(TECH 101) How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/786859062/How+to+Reassign+a+Service+Provider) for details).
  2. If an appointment has occurred, meaning, the LCN has already visited the property:
    1. Move the original WO to Awaiting Invoice (refer to [How to: Confirm Completion of the Work Order with Resident and Service Provider](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/763330650/How+to+Confirm+Completion+of+the+Work+Order+with+Resident+and+Service+Provider) for details).
    2. Create a new WO on the PM’s behalf (refer to [(TECH 101) How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/781647909/How+to+Create+a+New+Work+Order+from+Incognito+Mode+All+Submissions+)), get the Service Provider the PM wants and assign them to the job.
2. **LVN Service Providers**
  1. Refer to [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3203203103/Handling+Work+Orders+Assigned+to+LVN+Service+Providers)
