## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
*We want to make sure that all messages from the #latchel-rentredi Slack channel are accurately captured, routed, and actioned in Assembly by the correct team. This helps prevent missed or delayed responses, reduces confusion about ownership, and ensures RentRedi-related requests and updates are handled efficiently and consistently for a smooth customer and internal experience.*
</Info>

**Content***

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Info>
## **When This Applies**

- Messages are posted in the `#latchel-rentredi` Slack channel and automatically converted into Assembly tickets via Slack–Assembly integration.
- Operations Support Specialists are responsible for reviewing these tickets and taking appropriate next steps based on the message type.
- **The only inquiries that must be reassigned to the PS team in Assembly** can be found in the table below.
</Info>

<Note>
There may be instances where a ticket from RentRedi appears in Assembly outside of the Operations Support Specialists' business hours (4:30 AM – 9:00 PM). In these situations, it is completely fine if no one responds right away. The Operations Support team will take care of any tickets once they are back online during their scheduled hours.
</Note>

### **Check the Assembly Folder**

- The tickets are going to show up in the ***Slack Support Tickets*** teamspace in Assembly.
- Open and review each new ticket.

### **Determine the Type of Request**

Use the chart below to identify which team should take action based on the content of the message:

| Message Type | Action Owner | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WO Instructions or Updates (e.g., cancel WO, approve budget, assign SP, complete WO) | **Operations Support Specialists** | Support takes appropriate action based on the content of the message. |
| WO Disputes, Escalations (not related to a live WO), or Billing Concerns | **Product Support** | Reassign the ticket to **Product Support** in Assembly with a clear context comment. |

### 3. **Take Action or Reassign**

- **If Support owns the action**: Proceed based on applicable SOPs (e.g., cancelling WOs, assigning SPs).
- **If PS owns the action**: Reassign the ticket in Assembly to the “**Product Support**” group.
  - Include a brief internal note: e.g., *“Dispute message from RentRedi – reassigning to PS for review.”*
  - Make sure the status on the ticket is set to “Needs Response”.

---

### FAQs

#### Q. What if the ticket that was created was from a post from a Latchel representative? (e.g., Product Support associate inquiring to Rentredi, Taylor posting some updates… etc)

**A.**No need to take action. Just close the ticket with a note.

#### **Q: What should I do if an inquiry from RentRedi is addressed to Taylor or the Engineering team?**

**A:**Search for the original message in the `#latchel-rentredi` Slack channel. Respond directly in the thread and tag **Jordan** for assistance. Once done, close the Assembly ticket with a note indicating that **Jordan has been tagged in the Slack thread**.

## **Important Notes**

- This process is designed to reduce Slack monitoring and centralize requests through Assembly.
- Any unclear or misrouted messages should be flagged to the Ops Lead or escalated based on judgment.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
This SOP should generally be followed for all RentRedi messages, but exceptions may apply in specific scenarios:
</Note>

- **Unclear or ambiguous messages:** If a ticket’s content does not clearly indicate which team should take action, escalate or flag to the Ops Lead rather than guessing.
- **System or integration issues:** If Assembly or Slack–Assembly integration is down, document the messages manually and process them as soon as the system is restored.

This guidance ensures that tickets are handled efficiently while allowing flexibility to address urgent or unique situations.
