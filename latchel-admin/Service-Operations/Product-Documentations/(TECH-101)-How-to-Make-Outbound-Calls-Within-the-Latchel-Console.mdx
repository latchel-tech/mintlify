<Note>
We want to prevent errors, delays, and miscommunication when making outbound calls to customers, service providers, or property managers. The goal is to ensure associates use the Latchel Console's in-app calling feature correctly—linking calls to the right work order, documenting call outcomes, and following compliance requirements—so every call is tracked, every action is clear, and customers receive timely, accurate service.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Section 1 - Starting the call

**Option 1: Click on the Phone Icon**

If you are calling a Property Manager, a Service Provider or a Resident that we have their phone number in the Latchel Console, you can simply navigate to their information within the work order or customer profile, and click on the phone icon to open the calling window.

For example, if you are calling a Property Manager from within a work order, you will navigate to the Property Manager section and click on the phone icon.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-250.png" alt="Clicking the phone icon in the Property Manager section" /></Frame>

If you are calling a Service Provider with the intention of assigning them to the Work Order, the phone icon appears below their company name after clicking the "Find Service Provider" on the action button.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-251.png" alt="Phone icon below the Service Provider company name" /></Frame>

A new window will open with the call information.

It will show the number that it is being dialed, a list of recently viewed work orders, and a blank space for you to write your call notes before, during or after the call.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-252.png" alt="Call information window" /></Frame>

<Warning>
‼️ IMPORTANT: VERIFY that the appropriate work order has been attached to the call prior to making the call. This way, the relevant Work Order can be linked to it and be visible for research.
</Warning>

- As recommended above, initiating a call from the Work Order will automatically link the two.
- To verify that the exact correct Work Order is linked, click on the three dots from the Latchel Phone Dialer to reveal Recently Viewed Work Orders.
  - The Work Order number and title will appear. The linked Work Order should have a checkmark. If it does not, navigate to the correct Work Order and select.
- If the correct Work Order is not attached, from "Recently Viewed" Work Orders, the user can easily connect the phone call to its most relevant Work Order.
  - While the user is on an active phone call, they may be browsing several Work Orders within the Latchel Console. These Work Orders are likely related to the active phone call; "Recently Viewed" section of the Latchel Phone Dialer will automatically populate the last 10 recently viewed Work Orders,
  - New work orders also appear under the contacts work orders (for example if a new work order is created while you are on the call).
  - If you are not seeing the work order in the list, navigate to the Existing Work Order so the tool will bring that work order to the top of the "Recently Viewed" list.
  - If you are calling a service provider instead of a property manager, the list will show all the recently viewed work orders and all work orders for that service provider.
- The "call log notes" will be logged within that work order you selected. All calls, including the recording and notes will be listed under the "call" menu and will be visible to Property Managers as well.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-253.png" alt="Call log notes within the work order" /></Frame>

**Option 2: Use the phone dialer**

This is the preferred method for calling a number for someone who is NOT a user in the Latchel system. If they are already in the Latchel system, navigate to their profile or the work order you are calling about.

- Click on the phone icon on the top right corner of the Latchel Console. This icon will be visible at any page of the Latchel Console.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-254.png" alt="Phone icon on the top right corner of the Latchel Console" /></Frame>

- Click on the "Dial Pad" to open a new window for the dialer

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-255.png" alt="Dial Pad option" /></Frame>

- A new window will open within the Console where you can type the phone number. Do not include parenthesis, spaces or dashes; you just need to type the numbers

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-256.png" alt="Typing the phone number in the dialer" /></Frame>

- After you typed the number, the window will change to show more details, including the phone number that was dialed and a space to write notes from the call. It will also show the work orders that you recently viewed so you can select the one you are calling about.
- To initiate the call, click on the green phone icon

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-257.png" alt="Green phone icon to initiate the call" /></Frame>

- A confirmation window will open to check if the number you are using to make the call is accurate (i.e. the one you will be using to talk to the customer). This window will only appear the first time you are using a phone number.

### Section 2 - Redialing a Phone Number

**How to Redial a Phone Number**

To Redial a Phone Number (❌without completely ending connection to the call bridge [aka "End Call"]), Click on the orange Redialer tool.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-258.png" alt="Orange Redialer tool" /></Frame>

This is helpful for when an agent reaches a voicemail or busy signal, and they wish to immediately hang up and try calling back again.

Redial will end the child call, and redial the 3rd party.

### Section 3 - Ending and Wrapping up the call

**How to End and Wrap Up the Phone Call**

When you are ready to end the call, you will need to hang up from either the Latchel Phone Dialer or from the phone solution you are using.

- To Hang-up a phone call within the Latchel Console, Click on the red "End Call" button.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-259.png" alt="Red End Call button" /></Frame>

- Hanging up from the Phone Dialer will also terminate the call to the Operations agent's device.
- Make sure you are taking detailed notes of your call within the "Call Log Notes"

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-260.png" alt="Call Log Notes field" /></Frame>

### Section 4 - Researching Prior Calls

**How to Research Prior Calls in Work Order history**

You can see all of the calls made for a work order by going to the "Calls" menu in the work order log – Note: Property Managers call also see this information. There you can open each call log and see details of it, including notes made during the call, who made the call, who was called, duration of the call, and a link to the call recording.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-261.png" alt="Calls menu in the work order log" /></Frame>

If you want more details, you can click on the "Full call details" link at the bottom of the call log (this is only available to internal Latchel users, not property managers)

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-262.png" alt="Full call details link" /></Frame>

You can also see your own and other users' call history by clicking on the phone icon on the top right corner. There you can see the list of calls you have recently made as well as a link to see all of your call history

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-263.png" alt="Call history from the phone icon" /></Frame>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
