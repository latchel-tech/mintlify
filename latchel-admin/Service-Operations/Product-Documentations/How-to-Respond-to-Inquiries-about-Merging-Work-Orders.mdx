## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
We want to prevent confusion, duplicate work, and miscommunication when work orders are merged for property managers (PMs) on the Autopilot subscription. The goal is to ensure that all parties—service providers, residents, and property managers—understand what happened, why the merge occurred, and how to access the correct work order details. This process matters because it saves time, avoids double-dispatching, and ensures everyone is working from the most up-to-date information.
</Info>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
**This feature is only applicable for Property Managers subscribed to Autopilot subscription.**
</Note>

### General FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Q: What is the the new Merge Work Order feature and how does it work?">
*A: The Merge Work Orders feature enables property managers to combine duplicate or related non-emergency work orders at the same property. This helps ensure that multiple issues requiring the same service provider are consolidated into a single dispatch, preventing unnecessary trip fees and streamlining resolution.*

*The steps for merging work orders are detailed in this*[*help guide*](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Merging-Work-Orders)*. Latchel associates can access and use the same view.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: When can a work order be merged with another?">
*A: You or the Property Manager can merge a new work order (child) into an existing one (parent) if both work orders are active at the same property, the work orders are non-emergency, and the child work order does not have a Service Provider assigned.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What happens to the files and history when work orders are merged?">
*A: Files and fields notes such as the following will be added to the parent work order after merge.*

- *Service Provider Instructions*
- *Work Order Description*
- *Notes*
- *Attachments*
- *Special Notes*
- *Access Instructions*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Is there a timeframe limitation for merging work orders, or does the age of the work order matter?">
*A: No, there are no timeframe limitations for merging work orders at this time. Work orders can be merged regardless of how old they are.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title=" Q: If a merge happens by mistake, can Service Ops unmerge or otherwise recover the child work order?">
*A:Currently, there is no direct "unmerge" function. However, you can recover the child work order by unarchiving it and then rescheduling or setting a new confirmed work date as needed. We are working on a new feature that will allow you to duplicate or split a work order, which will make this process even easier in the future.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Are there any status restrictions for merging work orders?">
*A: No, there are no status limitations for merging work orders at this time. Work orders can be merged regardless of their current status.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Why can’t I merge a work order if a service provider is assigned to the child work order? ">
*A: You cannot merge a work order if a service provider is actively assigned to the child work order. This is to prevent confusion and ensure that all assignments are clear and up to date.*

*You must first remove the service provider from the child work order before merging it into the parent work order.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What happens to the work order budget?">
*A: After merging, the budget on the remaining (parent) work order stays the same unless the property manager adjusts it. If you need to update the budget, please do so on the active work order.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: If someone searches by the child work order ID after a merge, what happens?">
*A: If you search for the cancelled and archived child work order, it will still appear in search results. When you click into that work order, you’ll see in the activity log that it was merged with another work order. This helps maintain a clear audit trail and ensures you can always trace what happened to any work order, even after a merge.*

See [help guide](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Merging-Work-Orders) section: “**What Happens After a Merge”**
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### PM FAQs

<Accordion title="Q: How do I merge work orders?  ">
*A: You can merge work orders by following the steps outlined in our*[*https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Merging-Work-Orders*](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Merging-Work-Orders)*. This guide provides a step-by-step process to ensure all relevant information is combined correctly.*

If the PM insists that you take the action on their behalf, follow our standard process as outlined here: [SMS & Webchat Support Framework](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759431202/How+to+Handle+SMS+Webchat+Contacts+New+Support+Framework+) and [Autopilot WOs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2999812097/How+to+Handle+Autopilot+WOs). If they still refuse, you may proceed and take the action for them.

*A: Certainly! Please provide the work order numbers you wish to merge, and I will handle it for you.*
</Accordion>

### SP FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Q: Why did the work order details change or disappear from my dashboard?">
*A: The work order you were assigned to was merged with another open work order: [work order number] for the same property and issue. Please review the updated details in your dashboard under the new work order number. If you have any questions about the merged information or next steps, let us know!*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Which work order should I complete or invoice against?">
*A: Please complete your work and submit your invoice using the active work order: [work order number] shown in your dashboard. The merged work order is now closed, and all updates should be made on the remaining open work order.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: What budget should I follow after work orders are merged?">
*A: The approved budget is the one set on the remaining active work order. Please do not exceed this amount. If you believe additional work will not be covered by the approved budget, please submit an estimate for approval before proceeding with extra work.*
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

### Resident FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Q: I submitted a work order, but now I see a different work order number. What happened?">
*A: Your recent work order was merged with another open work order: [work order number] for the same issue at your property. This helps us avoid duplicate visits and ensures your request is handled efficiently. You can track updates and communication under the new work order number provided in your notification.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q: Will my issue still be addressed?">
*A: Yes! Merging work orders does not affect the status of your request. All details have been combined, and our team is actively working to resolve your issue.*
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
