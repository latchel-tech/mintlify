## **🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve**

<Info>
We want to prevent errors, inconsistencies, and missed details in estimate approvals by ensuring every estimate analysis is peer reviewed for accuracy and completeness before recommendations are finalized. The goal is to catch mistakes, confirm that all pricing and scope details are correct, and ensure that company guidelines are followed—so that property managers and residents receive fair, transparent, and reliable estimates.
</Info>

### **Peer Review Responsibilities**

#### Each associate is responsible for reviewing the work of their peers to verify that estimates have been analyzed thoroughly and aligned with company guidelines. The peer review process consists of the following steps:

1. Selecting an Estimate for Review
  - Choose a completed estimate analysis submitted by another associate.
  - Ensure that the estimate has been fully analyzed before beginning the review.
2. Reviewing the Objectives and Scope
  - Confirm that the estimate addresses the original issue as outlined in the work order.
  - Ensure all requested services and repairs are included in the analysis.
3. Verifying the Pricing Breakdown
  - Check that all charges (labor, materials, fees) are itemized.
  - Compare the prices with local market rates or historical data to determine reasonableness.
  - Ensure that the final total includes all necessary taxes and fees.
4. Auditing the Information for Accuracy
  - Verify that all provider details (name, address, and contact information) are correct.
  - Check that the estimate is dated and includes an expiration date.
  - Ensure that services are described in detail, including what is included and excluded.
  - Confirm that recommendations, risks, or potential follow-up repairs are noted.
5. Providing Feedback and Documentation
  - If errors or missing details are found, document them clearly.
  - Instead of sending feedback directly to the co-associate, escalate it to the Operations Support Specialist for further review.
  - If the estimate passes all review criteria, approve it for the next steps in the process.
6. Escalation Process
  - If a major discrepancy is found that affects the decision-making process, escalate to support who will then assess if a feedback ticket is needed.
  - If pricing inconsistencies are discovered, confirm with the service provider before proceeding.

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
