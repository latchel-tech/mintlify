## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
This process ensures that coaching tickets are documented correctly and consistently in the Coaching Tracker by following a defined set of procedures. It helps prevent incomplete or incorrect entries and supports the accurate generation of coaching and performance data.
</Info>

<Note>
This process applies to all Supervisors who are responsible for reviewing Slack Analysis data and completing Targeted Coaching entries in the Coaching Tracker.
</Note>

## 📝 **Pre-Entry Review**

### **Coaching Feedback Alert**

Supervisors will receive a validation request through the **svc-ops-feedback** channel. This notification indicates that a coaching entry requires review and completion in the Coaching Tracker.

**Each notification includes:**

- **Associate Name** – The agent associated with the coaching ticket.
- **Assembly Ticket** – The ticket ID from the Assembly system that requires review.

### **Responsible Party:**

- **Support / Project Owner –**Responsible for submitting the coaching validation alert through the svc-ops-feedback channel and updating the Coaching Tracker entries as needed.
- **Supervisors –**Responsible for reviewing the notification, validating the coaching entry, and ensuring all fields in the Coaching Tracker are accurately completed.

### **Timeline:**

- **Support / Project Owner –**Must submit the coaching validation tickets no later than Wednesday of each week.
- **Supervisors –**Are encouraged to review validation requests as soon as possible or as their capacity permits.
- All coaching entries should be reviewed and completed within the **same month**. For entries **for the final week of the month**, they should be completed no later than the week following receipt of the notification to ensure timely tracking and reporting.

<Tip>
### **Eligibility for Targeted Coaching**

Based on tracking by the Support team, the **Project Owner** will identify agents who qualify for targeted coaching due to repeat handling issues.

**Focus Agents** are defined as agents who meet any of the following criteria, based on **tickets flagged as PAG (Process Adherence Gap) opportunities** tracked by the Support team:

- Tagged or flagged **three or more times within the same week**
- Tagged or flagged **three or more times over two consecutive weeks**
- Tagged or flagged **more than three times within any four/five-week period in a month**

**Justification for Thresholds:**

- The **three-instance baseline** ensures that coaching focuses on **repeat patterns rather than isolated incidents**, supporting associates where targeted guidance is most beneficial.
- The **“more than three in a four/five-week period”** criterion captures recurring issues over a longer timeframe, allowing timely intervention even when issues are spread out.
- These thresholds are intended to provide **constructive support** and **development opportunities**, not to penalize associates or monitor every individual action.
- Using consistent thresholds ensures fairness and clarity in identifying who qualifies for coaching.
</Tip>

This information is used to determine which agents require a coaching ticket and ensures that coaching is **focused on recurring issues**.

## 🧐 Review Process

1. **Access**the [**Coaching Tracker**](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1q4PqKeUNzN0KPVlOwz5jv6yCqQeP0ix88NHfKnTBkmM/edit?gid=0#gid=0)
2. **Open the ASM Link** (Column D) in the Coaching Tracker to access the Assembly ticket associated with the feedback.
3. **Review the Slack thread** related to the ticket:
  1. This is highly encouraged to ensure the Supervisor sees the full conversation without gaps.
  2. Allows access to any screenshots or additional information exchanged between Support and the Associate regarding the provided resolution
4. Check Relevant SOPs and Remarks from Support
  1. Review the SOP referenced in the tracker and any remarks from Support to fully understand the context of the escalation.
  2. If the information provided does not fully answer the inquiry, **provide a detailed explanation** in the **Remarks from Supervisor’s Validation** column.
  3. If the SOP does not clearly cover the scenario, either the supervisor or associate must **submit an SOP update request**. This ensures requests are made with full context, helping the Support and Training team identify gaps and update the SOP as needed.
5. Fill Out the Coaching Tracker
  1. Update all necessary fields in the tracker based on the review and coaching discussion.
  2. **Refer to the**[**Coaching Tracker Columns Overview**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/3855810583#Coaching-Tracker-Columns-Overview)**section** for detailed descriptions of each column.
6. **Update the Coaching Ticket in the svc-ops-feedback channel.**
  1. Notify the Support / Project Owner that the coaching entry has been reviewed and updated.

## [<u>❓</u>](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332893/How+to+Handle+Emergency+Work+Orders)Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Q1: What if the Support team tagged the ticket as a Job Aid Utilization Opportunity when it should have been a Channel Guidance Opportunity or Self-resolvable Opportunity, or vice versa? How should we tag the validity of the coaching ticket?">
Answer: The coaching ticket should still be tagged as **VALID** as long as there was a real coaching opportunity and the feedback itself is correct. However, the supervisor should note this in the **Supervisor’s Remarks** section and provide a brief explanation as to why.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q2: What if Support referenced an incorrect SOP to support the recommendation or response provided?">
Answer: If the associate’s question is addressed by an existing SOP, the coaching ticket should remain tagged as **VALID**. The Support team should be notified of this opportunity for awareness, and the details may be recorded in the **Supervisor’s Remarks** section.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q3: What if the associate encounters a situation that is outside of their assignment or training?  Example: A CC/SMS agent receives a call from a resident asking a specific question about TS steps.">
Answer: In this case, the post should **not** be tagged as PAG Valid; therefore, the coaching ticket generated should be tagged as **Invalid**.

This rule only applies to situations that need **specialized training**. General or informational guidance—like items in **Informational: Basic Service Ops Guidelines and Rules**, when to create a task for TS reassessment, or how to determine PD/EH situations per the SOP—**doesn’t fall under this rule**. And this isn’t limited to just the examples listed above.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q4: What if the associate did not ask the intended question, and this was only identified or clarified during coaching?">
Answer: In this case, the ticket should still be tagged as **VALID**if the incorrectly asked or unintended question is covered by any existing SOP, since Support may not be able to provide the correct answer or guidance if the intended question is not asked.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Q5: What if the associate knew the answer to their query before Support responded?">
Answer: The coaching ticket should still be tagged as **VALID**, since a Slack ticket was generated when it shouldn’t have. The Supervisor’s tagging should then depend on the RCA to understand why the query was posted or why the associate did not know the answer at that time, even if it is covered in the SOP.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
