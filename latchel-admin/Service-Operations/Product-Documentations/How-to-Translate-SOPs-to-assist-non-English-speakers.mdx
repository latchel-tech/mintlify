<Note>
We want to prevent miscommunication, delays, and confusion when assisting non-English-speaking customers. The goal is to ensure associates have clear tools and steps for translating SOPs and customer communications—using resources like Google Translate and reviewing translations for accuracy—so that all customers receive clear, accurate, and timely information in their preferred language.
</Note>

## ❓ Why would Google Translate be used?

The best use of Google Translate is when you're trying to use blurbs to communicate with customers. By ensuring clear and concise communication, we effectively convey information, promote understanding, and create positive customer experiences.

<Tip>
Pro Tip: While Google Translate is a great tool to help us translate blurbs and other Latchel documentation, it's still important that we review and revise everything before using the translated text to ensure what we're sharing in our non-English interactions is clear and well written.
</Tip>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Activate google translate

Click on the three-dot menu icon located at the top-right corner of the browser window. This will open a drop-down menu.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-237.png" alt="Three-dot menu icon in browser" /></Frame>

In the drop-down menu, click on "Settings" to expand the settings menu.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-238.png" alt="Settings menu" /></Frame>

Scroll down until you find the "Languages" section. It should be located near the bottom of the settings page. Here, you will see your list of preferred languages, make sure you include the language you want to translate to by clicking on the "Add languages" button.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-239.png" alt="Languages section in settings" /></Frame>

Enable the "Use Google Translate" option. Then select the language you want to translate into.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-240.png" alt="Use Google Translate option" /></Frame>

That's it! You have successfully activated the "Translate this page" option in Google Chrome. Now, when you visit a web page in a foreign language, Chrome will offer to translate it for you.

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-241.png" alt="Translate this page option" /></Frame>
