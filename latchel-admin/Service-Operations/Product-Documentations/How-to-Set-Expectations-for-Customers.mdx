## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve


<Note>
We want to prevent misunderstandings, frustration, and repeated follow-ups caused by unclear or unrealistic communication with customers. The goal is to ensure every customer receives accurate, transparent information about what will happen next, who is responsible, and when to expect updates—so customers feel informed, trust the process, and experience smooth, reliable service from Latchel.
</Note>

**Content***


## **📑Definition of Setting Expectations**

Setting expectations involves:

- Clearly communicating the actions that will be taken
- Providing anticipated timelines and outcomes
- Detailing who is responsible for each step
- Explaining how updates or resolutions will be communicated

Effective expectation-setting aligns customer understanding with the provided services, reducing uncertainty and fostering trust.


## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

1. **Clearly Communicate the Next Steps**
  1. Outline the specific actions for the customer’s request or work order.
  2. Break down these steps into simple, manageable parts.
  3. Ensure the customer understands the process to avoid confusion.


1. **Identify Who Will Handle Each Step**
  1. Specify the individual or team responsible for each action using clear and precise language.
  2. Example: "Our internal emergency team," "The appropriate team” → _**when pertaining to the Property Manager**_, or "The Service Provider."


1. **Notify the Resident of Progress**
  1. Explain how the resident will be informed about the status of their request (e.g., SMS, email, phone call).
  2. [Job Aid: Call Flow for Outbound Calls](https://help.latchel.com/job-aid-call-flow-for-outbound-calls)
    1. (Step 8: "Tell the customer what you’re going to do") for guidance on notifying the resident.



1. **Align Expectations with Key Factors**
  1. Base expectations on key factors like the Property Manager’s subscription type, issue severity, critical notes, and pre-dispatch instructions.
  2. Different subscription types, such as 24/7, After Hours, and Autopilot, may affect the response times and services provided to the resident.
  3. Refer to [Job Aid: Call Flow for Outbound Calls](https://help.latchel.com/job-aid-call-flow-for-outbound-calls)
    1. (Step 9) to ensure consistency with service level agreements (SLAs).



1. **Follow Up on Changes**
  1. If there are any changes (e.g., delays, rescheduling), send a follow-up message to the resident.
  2. Maintain transparency to manage customer satisfaction, especially if timelines or responsible parties change.



## 💬Suggested Scripts for Setting Expectations

1. **Resident Reported an Emergency (Team will dispatch within 24 hours)**_“Thank you for letting us know about the issue. Since this has been classified as an emergency and requires immediate dispatch, we will dispatch a service provider within the next 24 hours. We’ll update you on the progress via SMS and confirm once the dispatch is set. Feel free to reach out with any further questions or concerns.”_
2. **Resident Reported a Maintenance Issue (Issue is Not an Emergency)**
  1. **For Premium Subscription**:
_“Thanks for reporting the issue. Our team will dispatch a service provider according to the availability you’ve provided. We’ll keep you updated via SMS. Please contact us if anything changes on your end.”_
  2. **After-Hours + Emergency Subscription Types**:
_“Thank you for reporting this. I’m sending the details to the appropriate team. You’ll receive updates via SMS. We’re here to help if you need further clarification.”_

3. **Autopilot Property Manager Requesting Action Over Non-Emergency**
Refer to:[How to: Handle SMS/Webchat Contacts (New Support Framework)](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-handle-sms-webchat-contacts-new-support-framework)


## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

**Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority.** Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
