## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  This process ensures interviews for Ops Associate candidates are structured and consistent. Using this guide helps interviewers fairly evaluate skills, principles, and Latchel superpowers while still allowing flexibility to explore relevant areas.
</Note>

## Sample Interview Questions

### **Leadership Principles**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customer Obsession">
    How do you assess your customer's needs?

    Tell me about a time that a customer said they needed one thing, but you actually believed they needed something else. What did you do?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bias for Action">
    Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information. How did you make it and what was the outcome?

    Describe a time when you saw some problem and took the initiative to correct it rather than waiting for someone else to do it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Insist on the Highest Standards">
    Tell me about a time that you feel you were not very successful at a task. What did you do? How did you improve?

    Tell me about a time when you were incredibly successful at something. How did you recognize that success and what steps did you take to reproduce that result in the future?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### **Superpowers**

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Service">
    Tell me about a time when you had a difficult customer. How did you resolve their issue?

    Tell me about the best customer service you have ever received. What made it great?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Integrity">
    Describe a time when you didn't know what the right thing to do was. What did you do?

    Tell me about a time where you had to admit a mistake to someone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Charisma">
    Tell me about a time when you needed to convince someone of your method or idea. What did you do?

    Tell me about a time when you had to give bad news to someone. How did they take it?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tenacity">
    Tell me about a time you had an idea to improve a process at work and what was the result.

    Tell me about a time where you were not sure you would meet a deadline or goal, what did you do to accomplish it? Did it work?
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Suasion">
    Describe a time when you were able to convince a skeptical or resistant client to purchase a project or utilize your services.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pathfinding">
    Have you ever ran into a problem you did not expect? How did you handle it?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- This guide is meant to support consistency, not limit the conversation. Interviewers may ask different questions as long as they evaluate the same core principles and Latchel superpowers.
