## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Tip>
This process ensures Supervisors record and review team metrics accurately and consistently. Clear and reliable metrics give leadership visibility into performance trends, help identify issues early, and support data-driven decisions that improve team and customer outcomes.
</Tip>

## Tools & Dashboards

We will use the following tools and dashboards to measure productivity:

- [Supervisors' Productivity](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bC4NWuqf_cRZu0AsbsO4Osfn8QmfWaUDb5Edf1383J4/edit): Use the data in the **Productivity Tab.**

<Info>
In all cases, acceptable productivity levels will be anything that meets or exceeds the team’s average.

To understand each associate’s productivity, it is important to analyze all the queues that the 
associate worked on. This is what we understand as a **“Work Mix”**. Small deviations from the team average should be allowed, as long as there is an acceptable balance between all queues.
</Info>

Below is the Inbound Team metrics dashboard.  We will analyze this dashboard from left to right:

**Average of Hours Worked:** This provides data on the number of hours worked by each associate. Since productivity is measured in hours worked, this element is key to understanding performance.

**Average of Inbound Calls/ Hour Worked: ** This metric calculates a ratio of the volume of calls that an associate took on a given week against the hours worked.

**Average of Inbound Calls** **Total:** This is the total volume of calls handled per week.

**Average of Emails / Hour Worked: **This metric calculates a ratio of the volume of emails that an associate took on a given week against the hours worked.

**Average of Emails** **Total:** This is the total volume of emails handled per week.

**Average of Troubleshooter Tasks/ Hour Worked:**

**Average of Troubleshooter Tasks** **Total:** This is the total volume of Troubleshooter Tasks handled per week.

- [Supervisor CX Experience Review Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/bVr9C): This dashboard is used to monitor Quality Metrics.  In all cases, Supervisors should make sure that associates are not missing the team score goal.

- [Productivity](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/badff70a-ed59-424b-bf41-25dcc9834745/page/p_5x900yoe0c): This dashboard provides efficiency and volume performance per agent. In addition, it provides heatmaps with volume for each queue.

### Operations Associates

---

<Info>
This group includes Inbound Calls, SMS, & Email.  Each group will have as its main metric its assigned queue, matching their role.
</Info>

#### Inbound Team

The inbound team is the team responsible for handling Incoming Calls. Supervisors will make sure that associates are meeting or exceeding the team average for: 
- **Average of Inbound Calls/ Hour Worked**

For quality, Inbound Team members will mostly observe [CX Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_hkpj6byg1c). In most cases, they will be audited in Calls. We should always keep an eye on this score and work with the associates to make sure they are meeting their weekly score goals.

#### SMS Team

The SMS team is responsible for handling SMS & Web Chat. Their secondary task is handling Non-Emergency Tasks. Supervisors will make sure that associates are meeting or exceeding the team average for: 
- **Average of SMS / Hour Worked**

For quality, SMS Team members will observe [CX Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_hkpj6byg1c) and [Autopilot SOP Compliance](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_61vlmcmx1c). In most cases, they will be audited on SMS. We should always keep an eye on these scores and work with the associates to make sure they are meeting their weekly score goals.

#### Email Team

The Email team is responsible for handling emails in the “Operations Email Team and Emergency Admin” teamspace in Assembly. Supervisors will make sure that associates are meeting or exceeding the team average for:

- **Average of Email / Hour Worked**

For quality, Email Team members will observe [CX Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_hkpj6byg1c). In most cases, they will be audited on Emails. We should always keep an eye on these scores and work with the associates to make sure they are meeting their weekly score goals.

### Troubleshooters

---

<Info>
This group includes Troubleshooters handling both Emergency Tasks and Administrative Tasks. Each group’s primary metric is tied to its assigned queue, based on their role. However, Troubleshooters in Administrative Tasks may also assist with the Emergency Tasks Queue as a secondary responsibility, and vice versa.
</Info>

#### Emergency Tasks

The Emergency team is responsible for handling Troubleshooting Tasks. Supervisors will make sure that associates are meeting or exceeding the team average for:

** - Average of Troubleshooter Tasks / Hour Worked**

For quality, Emergency Team members will observe [CX Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_hkpj6byg1c) and [Troubleshooting Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/bVr9C). For CX reviews in most cases, they will be audited on Calls. We should always keep an eye on these scores and work with the associates to make sure they are meeting their weekly score goals.

#### Administrative Tasks

The Admin Team will handle Administrative Triggers. Supervisors will make sure that associates are meeting or exceeding the team average for:

**Average of Admin Tickets / Hours Worked **

For quality, Administrative Team members will observe [CX Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/p_hkpj6byg1c) and often [Troubleshooting Reviews](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/6371b7a5-5d79-4685-a6d7-5b40772c4c49/page/bVr9C). For CX reviews in most cases, they will be audited on Calls. We should always keep an eye on these scores and work with the associates to make sure they are meeting their weekly score goals.

<Warning>
There are cases when associates are requested to focus on a different assignment other than their primary or secondary task. It is a good practice to check the team averages for all queues to understand how this might affect their main metric.
</Warning>

A key part of this is assisting Operations Associates in the Slack channels #ops_assistance_hub and #ops_info_center, where they respond to messages through the Assembly integration.

### Supervisors

Supervisors will use another set of tools, aside from the above-mentioned:

#### [Deputy Hours Report](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/e1b1a0b8-554e-416c-a399-7b77c82febba/page/CGYmD)

This one provides data and insights for Hours Worked, Voluntary Time  Off, Paid Time Off, and  Overtime.  We can find three different tabs in this report, and these have been labeled according to the period: Hours, Weekly, and Monthly. 

In all cases, please make sure to select the date range to show data. In the weekly tab, the pie charts function also as filters. This allows us to have a great level of detail when analyzing data. 

If you are looking to analyze Overtime, you must use the appropriate tab. Since Overtime is understood as any hours above the 40-hour/week threshold, if you are hoping to see monthly scores,  the only tab where the information will be accurate is the monthly tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reporting-13.png" alt="image-20240201-000540.png" />
</Frame>

#### [Console Status Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/u/0/reporting/a3ee031e-663c-4d82-8097-c82c1fbf8d13/page/CBrmD)

This dashboard provides data coming from the Console status updates. Associates are required to update their status on the Console to match the task they are focusing on at the moment. You can learn more about the statuses in the console in [**this SOP**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1657077761/Policy+Latchel+Time+and+Attendance+Policies+Operations+Production+Team).

This is a very useful tool that allows us to understand exactly how much time associates spend on one task. By using this data, we can blend it with productivity and understand how productive they were during that time, and exclude the time spent in other tasks. Keep in mind that the pie charts function as filters.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
This SOP should be followed consistently to ensure accurate and fair reporting of metrics. However, exceptions may apply in specific situations, such as:
</Note>

- When a new tool or system is introduced to measure productivity.

- When a group is assigned a different or temporary metric to focus on.

- During pilot programs or process changes directed by leadership.

- When system issues or outages require using an alternative method.

- When leadership provides direct instruction to adjust or bypass this process.

Any deviations should be documented and communicated to leadership for visibility.
