<Info>
This guide should help break down the concept of these percentiles and how they apply to measuring team performance.
</Info>

Percentile metrics (P90, P75, P50) help us understand how our team is performing across different tasks, and they break down like this:

### **P90 (90th Percentile)**:

- **What it means:** 90% of the performance is better than or equal to this number.

- **Example:** If our P90 response time is **10 minutes**, this means 90% of the time, we respond within 10 minutes or faster. Only 10% of the responses took longer.

- **How to think of it:** This is almost the **slowest** performance we should expect—only the last 10% are slower.

### **P75 (75th Percentile)**:

- **What it means:** 75% of the performance is better than or equal to this number.

- **Example:** If our P75 response time is **7 minutes**, it means that 75% of responses are handled within 7 minutes or faster.

- **How to think of it:** This shows where the **majority** of performance lies—only 25% took longer.

### **P50 (50th Percentile or Median)**:

- **What it means:** Half of the performance is faster than this number, and half is slower.

- **Example:** If our P50 response time is **5 minutes**, this means 50% of responses take 5 minutes or less, and 50% take longer.

- **How to think of it:** This is the **middle point** of performance.

---

### **Why These Percentiles Matter:**

- **P90** shows us the upper boundary, identifying where our slowest responses are.

- **P50** (median) shows the typical response time, giving a sense of the overall pace.

- **P75** gives a solid indicator of how the bulk of the team is performing, sitting between typical and upper-tier response times.

---

### **Quick Example:**

If we have the following response times:
**3 minutes, 4 minutes, 5 minutes, 8 minutes, 10 minutes, 12 minutes**

- **P50** (middle value): 5 minutes

- **P75** (75th percentile): 10 minutes

- **P90** (90th percentile): 12 minutes

So, based on this, 50% of the responses took 5 minutes or less, 75% took 10 minutes or less, and 90% took 12 minutes or less.
