---
title: "Notifying Finance of Variable/Fixed Role Classifications"
---

This outlines the procedure to notify the Finance department when there are alterations in variable/fixed role classifications within the Service Operations team.

## Instructions

1. Open the COGS Headcount spreadsheet for the current year
   - [2022 Headcount Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15YWqiaEKrhOf4C2fX8hbRDS87FaPzeXWbw_xJgS4V5g/edit#gid=1467820396)
   - [2023 Headcount Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1cy48Fe5aUb_dd54My7dylfofQVSCJVQBwBEi2KdvaIY/edit#gid=1536640071)

2. Navigate to the "COGS" tab within the spreadsheet

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/COGS-3.jpg" />
   </Frame>

3. Locate the "Fixed / Variable" column

4. Update this column with the correct classification directly within the sheet

This modification will be reflected in the COGS reporting for the subsequent month (no need to send Finance a direct message about this modification. Their pivot table will show the changes made).
