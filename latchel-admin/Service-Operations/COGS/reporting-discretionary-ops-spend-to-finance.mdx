This how-to guide is to teach ops leadership how to communicate ops discretionary spending to finance for COGS reporting.

## Instructions

1. Open the #cogs-ops slack channel

2. Click the attachments & shortcuts button to see workflows added to the channel

3. Select "ops discretionary spend" workflow

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/COGS-1.jpg" />
   </Frame>

4. Fill out the form by the 10th of each month, reporting the total amount of discretionary spending for the month. This will automatically inform Finance for COGS reporting.
