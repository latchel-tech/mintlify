This process serves to notify the Finance department of modifications within the Service Ops team, enabling Finance to allocate costs appropriately. It is essential to communicate the specific date to them, ensuring costs are adjusted accordingly.

## Instructions

1. Open the #cogs-ops slack channel

2. Click the attachments & shortcuts button to see workflows added to the channel

3. Select "Service Ops Team Changes" workflow

   <Frame>
     <img src="/images/COGS-2.jpg" />
   </Frame>

4. Fill out the form, reporting the details of the changes, including the effective date. This will automatically inform Finance for COGS reporting.

The effective date is the date on which the change takes effect or becomes operative and enforceable. This date may or may not be the same date as the signing date or the date on which the agreement was executed.
