## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to ensure that sourcing new service providers is handled efficiently, consistently, and with clear communication. The goal of this SOP is to provide associates with step-by-step reminders and templates for confirming appointments, documenting outreach, and updating property managers. This process prevents missed appointments, unclear records, and delays in service—helping Latchel deliver timely repairs and keeping property managers informed.
</Note>

**Content***

## 📝Process Cheat Sheet

### Reminder: ALWAYS Get a Confirmed Work Date

For all of these jobs it is **essential** that we get a confirmed work date based on the resident's availability (or anytime, if vacant).

1. If a service provider says they need to call the resident first to schedule, or wants to speak with the Property Manager first, **redirect them.** Let the SP know that we handle coordination with the resident/PM — we need them to confirm a date and time they can commit to right now on this call.
  1. *"We appreciate that! We'll take care of coordinating with the [resident/Property Manager] on our end. What we need from you is a date and time that you can commit to for this job — are you able to give us that right now?"*
2. If, after redirecting, the Service Provider still cannot confirm an appointment — **do not assign the SP to the work order.** An SP saying "I'll call the resident," "I'll get back to you," or "I need to check my schedule" is **not** an acceptance of the job.
  1. Ask them to text or call us back when they have a more definitive schedule. **Set the expectation clearly:** since they cannot confirm, they will **not** be assigned, and **we will continue sourcing other providers immediately.** We do not hold the work order open or pause sourcing while waiting for an SP to call back.
    1. *"No worries! Just so you're aware, since we weren't able to confirm an appointment, you have not been assigned to this work order and we will be continuing to source other available providers. You're welcome to text or call us back when you have a more definitive schedule, but we can't guarantee the work order will still be open at that point."*
  2. If they cannot commit, let them know:
    - They are welcome to reach back out when they have availability
    - However, **we will not wait** — we are continuing to source and cannot guarantee the work order will still be open when they call back
    - They have **not** been assigned to this job
  3. Do **not** disclose the full property address or resident's contact information until the SP has confirmed an appointment.

### 🏷️How to: Send a Message to Property Managers Using Tasks

1. [Create a task](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Create-a-Task) in the job log. Assign it to the Property Manager user group.
2. For the title, use: 
`Service Provider Sourcing Progress Update`
3. For the description use:
`We have contacted (x number) service providers for this job. Unless one of them accept the job beforehand, we will contact these service providers again and up to 5 new ones on the next business day. No action is required on your part unless you want to take over service assignment and scheduling for this job.`
