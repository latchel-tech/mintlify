## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to prevent delays, confusion, and resident frustration when a property manager’s preferred service providers are unavailable or unresponsive. The goal is to provide a clear, step-by-step process for quickly sourcing, contacting, and assigning a qualified service provider—ensuring that every job moves forward without unnecessary waiting or manual guesswork.

When a property manager's preferred network has been exhausted (i.e. none have responded to our auto-assignment contact attempts or none are available), we need to quickly find a confirmed work date.
</Note>

<Note>
**🎓Training Content**

The contents of this page have been included in the **Budget Communication and Approval Process **and **Exception Scenarios for Assigning Service Provider** Coassemble courses.
</Note>

# Content

## Process Cheat Sheet

<Note>
Read the [Service Ops: Sourcing Process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Service-Ops-Sourcing-Process) for a quick reference of templates, reminders, and key steps in the sourcing workflow.
</Note>

<Note>
### High Level Process Overview

1. Contact all Preferred Service Providers from the contact queue.
2. Contact all available Latchel Service Providers.
  1. Keep track of who you called and if they rejected the job using the Summary field.
  2. **Distance & payment priority for LCN providers:**
    - Review **Service Providers within 25 miles of the property** first: contact **DD providers before Issued Cards**.
    - If no 25-mile providers accept, review **Service Providers within 50 miles of the property**: again contact **DD before Issued Cards**.
    - Always prioritize **25-mile providers (DD then Issued Card)** before contacting **50-mile providers**.
  3. Only assign jobs if you can obtain a **confirmed** work date.
    1. ⚠️ See [Service Ops: Sourcing Process](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Service-Ops-Sourcing-Process) for detailed steps on how to handle SPs who cannot confirm an appointment, including blurbs and double dispatch prevention.
3. After Full List Contacted:
  1. **Premium **- **Defer the task to the next day** if no one accepts.
    1. Next Day Contact Attempt
      1. Re-contact **only those providers who haven’t declined** yet.
      2. If still no one accepts, begin **sourcing outside the network**.
      3. If sourcing fails, **escalate to the Property Manager**.
  2. **Autopilot **- **Escalate to Property Manager** if no provider accepts.
4. **If the Property Manager requests continued sourcing**, we **only agree to source one more time**.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Emergency Troubleshooters

<Note>
### Emergency Troubleshooters

If you are an Emergency Troubleshooter and you are working sourcing service providers, you should stop work on a task if an emergency trigger comes in. to do this, make sure you **UNASSIGN** the task from yourself so it becomes available for someone else to pick up.
</Note>

<Note>
1. **📝 NOTE – Preferred Service Providers (Emergencies Only)**
  1. If the work order has **Preferred Service Providers listed**, follow the **Standardized Outreach Attempts to Preferred Service Providers** before moving on to LCN or PM escalation.
  2. Refer to Step 4 under “**(EMERGENCY) Rules for Calling the Service Provider**” of this [SOP](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/EMERGENCY-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Latchel-Network-Service-Provider) to review the guidelines.

### Step 2: Contact Latchel Service Providers

1. Pull up the list of Latchel Service Providers using the Take Action Button.
2. Contact all Latchel Direct Deposit service providers first. Then contact Issued Card service providers.
3. If you contact an Issued Card service provider who will accept a job, ensure you confirm the following:
  1. Are they insured?
  2. Will they accept credit cards?
    1. If they do not accept credit cards, tell them we can do direct deposit with identity verification. If they won't agree to either credit cards or direct deposit with identify verification, then we cannot use them.
  3. They understand the budget limit and will text (206)-900-9466 for any increases before proceeding with work.
  4. Explain to the vendor that Latchel (not the resident) will pay them once they submit their invoice and the resident confirms that the work has been completed. We will also accept before and after photos from the vendor to confirm completion.
  5. Confirm the number you have for them is good to receive text messages.
4. After you contact each service provider, update the Summary field with your sourcing notes

### Step 3a: Assign Service Provider

1. Assign a service provider that accepted the job.
2. After assigning and scheduling the job, clear out the Summary field.

### Step 3b: When No Service Provider Accepts the Job

1. **Non-Emergencies:**
  1. **Premium:** Defer the task to the morning of the next business day.
  2. **Autopilot:** Escalate to the Property Manager.
    1. Create a task for the PM to send them a status update. See [How to: Handle Autopilot WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Autopilot-WOs).
2. **Emergencies:**
  1. If the work order is an *emergency* and there are Preferred Service Providers listed, make sure you have followed the **Standardized Outreach Attempts to Preferred Service Providers** in: [<u>(EMERGENCY) How to: Assign a Preferred + Latchel Network Service Provider</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/EMERGENCY-How-to-Assign-a-Preferred-Latchel-Network-Service-Provider) before concluding that no provider can be secured.
  2. If, after following those rules and contacting available Latchel Service Providers in this job aid, **no one accepts the job**, escalate to the Property Manager immediately. Refer to: [<u>How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders</u>](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Emergency-Work-Orders)

### Step 4: Premium Next day Contact

1. Repeat the steps **from Step 1-3.c** →* Call any service providers who have not rejected the job ***to Step 2**.
2. **If no one accepts the job:**
  1. Proceed to the [**Sourcing Outside Network**](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Source-a-New-Service-Provider-Outside-Network) process.
    1. **If no one accepts even after outside sourcing:**
      1. **Escalate to the Property Manager**
      2. Create a task for the PM and include a status update on your efforts.

## **🎯 When to Break This SOP**

You may **deviate from the process **only in specific, documented scenarios. Here are the main situations when deviation is allowed:

- If a Property Manager, Owner, or company leadership gives direct instructions that override the SOP (e.g., assigning a specific provider, skipping steps, or handling a job differently), follow their direction and document the exception.
- If the PM’s pre-dispatch notes or account instructions require a different approach (such as not using auto-assign, contacting only certain providers, or following a unique escalation path), you must follow those instructions—even if it means not using the standard workflow.
- If you have followed the sourcing process (e.g., called all required providers, made the required number of attempts) and still cannot assign a provider, escalate to the PM or their escalation contacts as instructed. For property-damaging or extreme habitability issues, follow the escalation pathway; for other cases, create a task for the PM.
- If you encounter a situation not addressed by the SOP, or you are unsure how to proceed, **pause and escalate to Operations Support Specialists for guidance**. Do not break the SOP on your own.
