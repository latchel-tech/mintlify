## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve


<Note>
We want to prevent missed tasks, duplicate work, and delays in resolving customer issues. The goal is to ensure every associate efficiently filters, claims, and completes tasks in the queue—so nothing falls through the cracks and customers get timely, accurate service from Latchel
</Note>

Note: You can refer to [Job Aid: What is a task?](https://help.latchel.com/job-aid-what-is-a-task) for a brief overview of what is a task and its components.


## 📋 Step-by-Step Process


<Note>
**⚠️Before you begin**

**Verify You Are on the Correct Work Order**

Before taking any action on a task, confirm that the task is associated with the correct Work Order. Cross-reference the task description and the details of the work order.

Tasks may occasionally be assigned to the wrong Work Order. If you identify a mismatch, ensure to take the action on the correct work order.
</Note>


### How to filter tasks

1. From the main Latchel homepage.
2. Click the tasks icon next to your initials at the top right corner.
3. Open the filters menu.
4. Set up the task filter as follows:
  1. Due: Overdue
  2. For: Work Orders
  3. Sort: Age
  4. User group: see table below
  5. Assignee: Unassigned
  6. Hit “Save Current Filters” and choose a name for your filter, this will ensure that you won’t have to repeat this process each time you work on the tasks queue and the filter will be saved for easy access.

### How to assign a task to myself

1. Open the most recent task (task at the top of the queue) and **before doing any work, assign it to yourself! **This will prevent multiple associates from working on the same task.
  1. Click the “Assign To Me” option.



#### What do you do next?

1. For troubleshooting tasks refer to: [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-handle-emergency-work-orders)
2. For other types of tasks, refer to the individual instructions of the task.
3. When you’re done with the task, and before taking another one, be sure to refresh the task queue by clicking the refresh button:

### Handling the Task


<Note>
The tasks tab will show all pending available tasks that need action. Please note that a property or resident may have multiple maintenance requests. As a result, Residents, Service Providers, and Owners can each have multiple tasks associated with different work orders assigned to them at any given time. This is applicable for both emergency and non-emergency work orders.
</Note>


<Note>
It’s crucial to follow each step in this guideline, especially when handling multiple tasks, to ensure that all open tasks for the customer are properly addressed. Make sure to review and discuss all **pending tasks for the day** for the _**specific contact**_** **that you are calling. This approach helps resolve everything in a single interaction, providing a more efficient and seamless experience for the customer.
</Note>

### **What should be done if an associate needs to release a Work Order due to unforeseen problems? **

This typically occurs when there's a language barrier (e.g. an English-speaking associate encountering a Spanish-speaking Resident), or the associate encounters a system issue that prevents them from completing the assignment.

1. The associate needs to notify Operations Support Specialists by posting the work order, detailing the actions they've taken and the problems they're facing, in the #ops-troubleshooter channel for emergency work orders and in the #ops_associate_escalations channel for non-emergency work orders.
  1. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-escalate-between-teams)

2. Unassign the ticket task from yourself by editing the task as shown above.
  1. The work order returns to the task queue, and anyone available should pick it up.
    1. Note: If there's a language barrier, the Operations Support Specialist team may tag associates who can handle the task.




<Note>
**Actions to Take When Associates are Working on Non-Emergency Tasks:**

Whenever you work on a task, kindly remember the following steps:

1. **Always Call First (**_Unless SOP states otherwise or there's a clear indication in the task that a call may be inappropriate, do not skip this step.**)**_
  - **For Residents:** Call between 8:00 am and 7:00 pm property time is allowed.
  - **For Service Providers:** Call between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm property time. Always check critical notes (e.g., Puls' office hours start at 7 am, so you can call them already).
  - **For Owners (Nomad & RentRedi only):** Call between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm property time.

2. **If You Tried to Contact Them via Phone Call and the Contact Didn’t Answer:**
  - Always leave a voicemail if applicable.
  - Then attempt to reach the contact via text message and email.
  - **For Service Providers:** Outside business hours, send an email only for non-emergency situations.


- **Note:** Only after completing these steps can you defer the task. Note that in some cases, tasks should not be deferred and must continue to follow the corresponding process or SOP.

FOR **EMERGENCY WORK ORDERS** please refer to: [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/HYBCLQ)
</Note>
