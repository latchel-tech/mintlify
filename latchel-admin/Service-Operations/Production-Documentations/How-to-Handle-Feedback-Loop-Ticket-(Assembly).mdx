<Note>
We want to prevent missed or delayed responses, unresolved feedback, and lack of accountability when handling Feedback Loop tickets in Assembly.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Ticket Assignment

A ticket will be assigned to either a supervisor or support to complete it within the defined timeline. Refer to severity definitions to determine the appropriate response time. The assignment will be manually done by Sheriza according to the roster and the specific ticket type.

### Step 2: Accessing Tickets

To access the ticket, open Assembly and navigate to 'My Inbox'.

### Step 3: Acknowledging Tickets

Review the request and acknowledge receipt by responding to the ticket. Your timer starts at this time.

<Note>
Note: Always start the timer when you are actively working on a ticket.
</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-148.png" alt="Acknowledging tickets and starting the timer" /></Frame>

### Step 4: Handling Agent Feedback (For Supervisors)

**Review the Feedback**

- Understand the nature of the feedback (e.g., performance concern, behavior issue, or a positive highlight).
- Identify the context of the feedback by reviewing related interactions, metrics, or notes.

**Assess the Situation**

Gather all relevant data, including:

- Performance metrics related to the agent.
- Any prior feedback or coaching sessions.
- Logs or details of the specific incident (if applicable).

**Validate the Feedback**

- Ensure the feedback is based on accurate and verifiable information.
- Cross-check any supporting evidence (e.g., call recordings, chat logs, or customer comments).

**Provide a Response or Action Plan**

- Respond to the ticket with your assessment of the feedback.
- Include a clear action plan, such as:
  - Coaching for improvement.
  - Recognition of good performance.
  - Escalation for further investigation (if needed).

**Communicate with the Agent**

- Discuss the feedback directly with the agent in a constructive and supportive manner.
- Use this opportunity to clarify any misunderstandings, offer guidance, and set expectations.
(refer to [Service Ops: Guidelines for Value-Packed Coaching Session](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Guidelines-for-Value-Packed-Coaching-Session))

**Make Sure Labels Are Updated Accordingly**

Ensure proper categorization for accurate tracking and reporting. Labels are composed of Tags and interaction types. The interaction types include Investigation, Assistance, and Feedback, which help classify the nature of the ticket. Tags are the list of categories to help classify the ticket refer to [Categories and Directives](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Guidelines-for-Value-Packed-Coaching-Session#categories-and-coaching-directives%3A)

### Step 5: Handling Investigation Assistance Requests (For Supports)

**Review the Request**

Understand the nature of the request, including the issue raised and the scope of the investigation required.

**Complete the Investigation**

Ensure the following elements are included in your investigation:

a. **Summary of the Issue**

Provide a concise explanation of the feedback and the concerns raised.

b. **Findings**

- Utilize all available resources like Slack, Twilio, and Fullstory
- Document clear and objective results of the investigation, highlighting the root cause or contributing factors.

c. **Documentation for Reference**

Ensure all relevant details are properly recorded for accountability and future reference.

**Identify Additional Opportunities**

If, during your investigation, you discover another opportunity that affected the totality of the issue raised, submit a new feedback ticket.

Refer to [the relevant SOP for guidance on submitting feedback tickets](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Submit-Feedback-Tickets#submitting-parent-feedback-tickets).

**Update Labels Accordingly**

Ensure proper categorization for accurate tracking and reporting. Labels are composed of Tags and interaction types. The interaction types include Investigation, Assistance, and Feedback, which help classify the nature of the ticket. Tags are the list of categories to help classify the ticket refer to [Categories and Directives](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Guidelines-for-Value-Packed-Coaching-Session#categories-and-coaching-directives%3A)

**Respond to the Ticket**

- Provide a detailed response summarizing your findings, actions taken, and any recommendations for further steps.
- Log all relevant information, including supporting evidence, in the ticket for proper closure.

**a. Agent Feedback**

Tickets categorized as Agent Feedback should be managed by the supervisors.

### Submitting an Investigation Assistance Request

If a supervisor requires assistance validating a feedback ticket, they must submit an Investigation Assistance Request using the appropriate path in Slack.

**Access the Workflow**

- Go to the #svc-ops-feedback channel in Slack.
- Open the workflow and select Investigation Assistance Request from the available options.

**Fill Out the Required Information**

Provide all necessary details, including:

- The name of the agent involved
- WO link
- Issue Summary and Assistance Needed
- Priority Level (refer to the [Severity Definition](https://help.latchel.com/Service-Ops-Guidelines-for-Value-Packed-Coaching-Session))
- What type of feedback is this?

**Submit the Request**

- Ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
- Submit the request and monitor for acknowledgment or updates from support.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Supervisors if you believe an exception is necessary.
