## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
We want to prevent delays, errors, and miscommunication when uploading invoices for work orders. The goal is to ensure invoices are accurately matched to the correct work order, properly documented, and processed according to the service provider and property manager’s requirements—so payments are not delayed, records stay accurate, and all parties (service providers, property managers, and residents) have clear visibility into job completion and billing.
</Note>

# Content

# Definition Matrix

<Note>
### What is an invoice?

Service Providers send invoices to Latchel to get paid for their work. The Ops agent is responsible for uploading the invoice to help bridge payment communication between the Property Manager and the Service Provider. Sometimes the invoice is submitted to us simply for record keeping.

All Preferred and Latchel Affiliate Service Providers send invoices via email and Ops agents receive them from Assembly; these are the invoices we are working on. Latchel In-Network Service Providers submit invoices through the Latchel console, so Ops agents never interact with these.
</Note>

<Note>
### Who pays who?

Preferred Service Providers are **typically** paid by the property management company directly. Sometimes we take care of their invoices, other times, the SP invoices the PM directly. **However**, if the PM has the 'Preferred Service Provider Payments' toggle **ON** in their Company Profile → Payments tab, Latchel processes payment for Preferred SPs **via Direct Deposit only** (no issued cards). Refer to [Service Ops: How to Handle Payment-Related Inquiries](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Service-Ops-How-to-Handle-Payment-Related-Inquiries) for full details.

Latchel Service Providers are paid by Latchel through direct deposit per invoice that is uploaded through the Latchel console or through a unique credit card number that is created through the "Issue Card" function in the Work Order.
</Note>

<Note>
For invoices which were incorrectly uploaded by a service provider, refer to [https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/759431181#FAQs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/https//latchelatlassiannet/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/759431181FAQs).
</Note>

<Note>
**Invoice Discrepancy Procedure**

If you receive an invoice from a service provider and find that the information (such as property address or work order number) is incorrect, request a revised invoice with the correct information. Do not upload the incorrect invoice to the work order.
</Note>

**Q1: What if the Service provider reached out and advised that they have uploaded an incorrect invoice on a work order?**

For all incorrectly uploaded invoices by the service providers (all types), please do not take further action. Please escalate this to the appropriate team for proper handling.

<Accordion title="Preferred Service Providers">
1. Review the details of the incorrect invoice that was uploaded and take the following information
  1. Invoice Number and amount
  2. Work Order it was incorrectly uploaded to
  3. Work Order it should be uploaded to
2. Escalate to the Property Manager via task
  1. Subject: *Service provider submitted an incorrect invoice*
  2. Who is this task about? - *Choose the service provider*
  3. Select a Date an Time: *Leave as is*
  4. Assign to a User Group: *PM Group*
  5. Assign to a User: *Leave as is*
  6. Priority: *choose based on the WO severity*
  7. Complete task on any job state change.
  8. Description: *The service provider has informed us that they mistakenly uploaded an invoice to work order [incorrect work order number] instead of work order [correct work order number].You may contact the service provider directly to address the payment for the correct work order. Thank you.*
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="LCN/LVN Service Providers">
1. Review the details of the incorrect invoice that was uploaded and take the following information
  1. Invoice Number and amount
  2. Work Order it was incorrectly uploaded to
  3. Work Order it should be uploaded to
2. Escalate to the Payment Issues and Disputes Team via task
  1. Subject: *Service provider submitted an incorrect invoice*
  2. Who is this task about? - *Choose the service provider*
  3. Select a Date an Time: *Leave as is*
  4. Assign to a User Group: *Payment Issues and Disputes *
  5. Assign to a User: *Leave as is*
  6. Priority: *choose based on the WO severity*
  7. Complete task on any job state change.
  8. Description: *The service provider has informed us that they mistakenly uploaded an invoice to work order [incorrect work order number] instead of work order [correct work order number]. Please reach out to the service provider for proper handling. Thank you*
</Accordion>

**Q2: What should be done if an associate incorrectly uploads an invoice and cannot remove or delete it?**

Note: This issue typically arises when an associate uploads the invoice through the "Take Action" button while marking the work order as completed, instead of uploading it to the Files tab.

<Accordion title="For LCN Issued Cards">
1. If there are no succeeding tasks indicating a review from PM is required and if the amount is less than $500
  1. Upload the correct invoice
  2. Proceed to process the payment for the correct invoice
  3. Leave a "Special Note" on the work order indicating that an incorrect invoice was uploaded and should be disregarded. Also, note that the correct invoice has been uploaded and that payment has been processed.
  4. Write Effective Notes
    1. See [How to: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes)
2. If there is a succeeding task or if the amount is more than $500
  1. Upload the correct invoice
  2. Escalate to the Payment Issues and Disputes Team via task to process the payment to the correct invoice
    1. Subject: *Incorrect Invoice was uploaded*
    2. Who is this task about? - *Choose the service provider*
    3. Select a Date an Time: *Leave as is*
    4. Assign to a User Group: *Payment Issues and Disputes *
    5. Assign to a User: *Leave as is*
    6. Priority: *choose based on the WO severity*
    7. Complete task on any job state change.
    8. Description: *An incorrect invoice was uploaded to work order [work order number]. Please assist in processing the payment for the correct invoice [correct invoice file name]. Thank you*
      1. **Note**: Payments team shall handle the documentation moving forward
</Accordion>

Kindly disregard the earlier invoice with the file name [invoice file name]. We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused and appreciate your understanding.*

**Q5: What should we do if a Service Provider reach out informing that they are unable to upload the invoice?**

<Accordion title="If a Service Provider reports difficulty uploading an invoice, follow these steps:">
1. Confirm whether the SP **does not know how to upload the invoice** or is **encountering a technical/system issue** while attempting to upload.
2. If the SP is unfamiliar with the process, share the invoice upload **help guide** and walk them through the steps if needed.
  1. Help Guide: [https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-to-Upload-an-Invoice#uploading-an-invoice-service-providers](https://help.latchel.com/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wpGAA/How-to-Upload-an-Invoice#uploading-an-invoice-service-providers)

3. If the SP confirms they have followed the instructions but are still experiencing issues, **document the specific problem** encountered (error messages, blocked actions, screenshots if available).
  1. **Escalate to Payments**
    1. Create a **task for the Payments team** including the work order number, SP details, and the documented issue.
    2. Payments will review and take the appropriate next steps.
</Accordion>
