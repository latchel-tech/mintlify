## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

_We want to prevent confusion, double-work, and missed responsibilities on work orders where it isn’t clear who is in control. The goal is to help associates quickly identify when a PM is actively managing a work order (including “do not touch” scenarios) so they know when to proceed with the next action versus when to move the WO to Waiting for PM and hold off on further intervention._


## 🔗 Related SOPs

- [SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates](https://help.latchel.com/sop-work-order-follow-ups-and-updates) — Covers follow-up procedures, task creation, and when to move a WO to Waiting for PM vs. keeping current status.
- [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-put-a-job-on-hold) — Covers the approved scenarios and mechanics for placing a WO on hold.


## 📋 PM Active Management Scenarios

_These guidelines apply **to all work orders regardless of status** (including Forwarded, Waiting for PM, Pending, etc.) **any time** we need to decide whether the PM is actively managing the work order._

_The only exception is work orders that were moved to **Needs Approval** due to initial intake rules. Even if these work orders are later escalated, we do **not** consider them to be under active PM management, since **Needs Approval** is the only status they have held as a result of the initial intake process._


<Note>
**General Rule: Recent PM activity (last 48 hours)**

- The PM has updated the work order in any way in the past 48 hours (status change, note, or task update). This indicates they are monitoring or directing the WO.
- If there are no explicit indications in the system that the Property Manager is actively managing the work order, the team should proceed with the next appropriate action.
- Action should only be paused when there is **clear evidence** that the PM is directly managing the matter, **or** when recent activity shows they are still engaged.
  - When there’s clear evidence that the PM is managing the work order, please refer to the [**How to: Put a Job on Hold**](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)** **for the appropriate status to apply to the WO.
</Note>


## ✅ Next Steps After Determining Active PM Management

Once you've confirmed the PM is actively managing the work order, follow the appropriate path below:


### Path A — Move WO to "Waiting for PM"

Use this path when the PM has explicitly requested the WO be forwarded to them, or when the PM's instructions make it clear Latchel should not take further action.

- Move the WO to **Waiting for PM** following the approved scenarios in [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-put-a-job-on-hold).
- Document the reason for the status change in the WO notes.

**Examples:**

- PM says: _"We'll handle this issue directly. Please hold off on further outreach for now."_
- PM says: _"Please do not contact the resident or any vendors until we finish our internal investigation."_
- PD/EH escalation contacts are unresponsive — move to **Waiting for PM** and document per the escalation SOP.


### Path B — Keep WO in Current Status + Create a PM Task

Use this path when the PM is actively managing the matter (e.g., coordinating with their own vendor, waiting on owner approval) but has **not** asked Latchel to stop all action, and no status change is needed.

- Leave the WO in its current status (Pending, Scheduling, etc.).
- Create a task assigned to the **PM User Group** with the title: **"Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed"**
- Set the task to **"Complete task on any job state change"**
- In the task description, summarize the current WO status and note that the PM appears to be actively managing the matter. Request confirmation or next steps from the PM.

**Examples:**

- PM note says: _"We've assigned this to ABC Plumbing. They are scheduled for Thursday 3 PM. We'll update once completed."_ — The PM is managing it but hasn't asked Latchel to stop. Keep current status, create a PM task to confirm.
- PM created a task saying: _"PM to schedule with in-house maintenance"_ — PM is coordinating, no status change needed, create a follow-up task.

**Quick Decision Checklist:**

1. Has the PM explicitly asked us to forward the WO or stop all action? → **Path A**: Move to Waiting for PM per [How to: Put a Job on Hold](https://help.latchel.com/how-to-put-a-job-on-hold)
2. Is the PM managing it but hasn't asked us to stop? → **Path B**: Keep current status, create a PM task for confirmation/next steps
3. Is the issue PD/EH and escalation contacts are unresponsive? → **Path A**: Move to Waiting for PM and document per escalation SOP

For detailed guidance on follow-up procedures, task creation, and escalation contacts, refer to [SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates](https://help.latchel.com/sop-work-order-follow-ups-and-updates).

**Use the guidelines below to identify active PM management (examples may include, but are not limited to):**

1. A call, email, or message indicates that the PM will handle the request or is already addressing the issue. This could come directly from the PM or be reported by the resident or service provider.
  1. **Sample: **Resident tells the associate: _“My property manager called me this morning and said they’re sending their own plumber today.”_
  2. Or PM emails Latchel:_“We’ll handle this issue directly. Please hold off on further outreach for now.”_

2. There is a note in the work order (added by the PM, another team member, or reported by the resident/SP) stating that the PM is taking ownership or has provided a specific plan of action.
  1. **Sample: **PM note inside the WO:_“We’ve assigned this to ABC Plumbing. They are scheduled for Thursday 3 PM. We’ll update once completed.”_

3. The PM is handling the issue outside of Latchel, and a note is added to reflect this (e.g., “PM will coordinate directly with their vendor and update once resolved”). This can also be communicated through the resident or service provider.
  1. **Sample: **PM note:_“We will coordinate directly with our preferred vendor outside of Latchel and update the work order when resolved. No need to dispatch.”_
  2. Or resident says:_“My PM told me they’re working with their own contractor now, not through you.”_
_and the associate adds a note reflecting that._

4. The PM requests that the team refrain from taking further action while they investigate or resolve the issue.
  1. **Sample: **PM message in WO:_“Please do not contact the resident or any vendors until we finish our internal investigation. We’ll follow up once we have direction.”_

5. The PM creates a task assigned to the PM group such as but not limited to asking approval from the owner directly.
  1. **Sample**: PM changes the WO status from **Pending** to **Needs Owner Approval** and adds a note:_“Waiting on owner approval for repair estimate. Will update once approved.”_
  2. PM left a task saying: _“PM to schedule with in-house maintenance”_



### Exceptions to the 48-Hour Rule

Even if there has been **no update in the last 48 hours**, the team should **not resume action** and should treat the work order as **PM‑managed** when:

1. The PM has clearly stated in the WO summary, notes, or pre-dispatch instructions that they will be managing the work order moving forward. _**This includes pre-dispatch instructions that direct Service Ops to forward the WO to the PM instead of continuing outreach or dispatch steps.**_ For example, if the pre-dispatch instruction says that for unreachable tenants during potential Property Damage/Extreme Habitability, the associate should place a note on the WO and forward it to the PM, with no need to call the escalation contact and only email the named PM contact, treat the WO as **PM-managed** and follow **Path A**: document the instruction and move/forward the WO to **Waiting for PM** as applicable.
2. The PM has noted in the WO or created a task indicating they are personally coordinating approvals or next steps (e.g., working directly with the owner, resident, or service provider and will update once there is a decision).
3. PM requested no further Latchel action while they investigate or review options.
4. PM gave standing instructions for this specific WO that we should only act after they update.
5. PM is coordinating directly with a warranty company or third party and will update once they hear back.

In all other situations—especially when there is **no clear ownership note** and **no PM activity in the last 48 hours**—treat the WO as **not actively managed by the PM** and proceed with the next appropriate action per the applicable SOP.
