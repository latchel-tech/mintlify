## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
*We want to prevent miscommunication, missed requests, and inconsistent support quality when clients reach out via SMS or webchat. The goal is to ensure every inquiry is handled efficiently and accurately—guiding clients to self-serve when possible, intervening directly when needed, and escalating appropriately—so that issues are resolved quickly, customer satisfaction remains high, and operational errors are minimized, saving time and frustration for both clients and the support team.*
</Note>

<Note>
This process applies to NOMAD when communicating with a Property Manager who is registered in the PM Users tab.
</Note>

## 📋New 4-Step Support Framework

### 💬Receiving the Initial Client Request.

1. The Associate decides if they can handle the text message/Webchat or if they need to transfer it:
  1. This situation is only applicable when the associate is experiencing technical issues or when a non-English-speaking client reaches an English-speaking agent.
    1. Review [↔ How to: Use Text Message and Webchat Functions (in Latchel Console)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Use-Text-Message-SMS/Webchat-Functions-in-Latchel-Console) to become familiar with how to Assign the Text Message to yourself
    2. If the Associate determines they cannot handle the Text message/Webchat, the Work Order should be handed off to another peer.
      1. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams) for assistance.
2. For On-call clients, always refer to the step-by-step guidelines outlined here [Follow Up Guidelines for On-Call Customers (app.latchel.com)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Follow-Up-Guidelines-for-On-Call-Customers-applatchelcom)
3. For LVN service provider-assigned WOs, always refer to the step-by-step guidelines outlined here [Handling Work Orders Assigned to LVN Service Providers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Handling-Work-Orders-Assigned-to-LVN-Service-Providers). PMs must not be provided help guides with their inquiries for these types scenarios.
4. For other clients, review what the client is requesting and follow the below steps:
  1. 🚫 **STOP — Unsolicited Sales or Marketing Message**If the SMS or webchat is from an outside company trying to sell, pitch, or promote their services to Latchel:🗨️ **Say something like:** *"Thank you for reaching out. Unfortunately, we are not accepting those types of offers at this time. We appreciate your understanding — have a great day!"***Do NOT:****📝 NOTE: No CSAT survey is needed for these interactions**
    1. Send the polite decline message below.
    2. Close the SMS/webchat window.
    - Engage further or ask follow-up questions
    - Forward or escalate to any team or individual
    - Create a ticket, work order, or note
  2. Usually, clients will request assistance in the form of: “*Can you please do X [action] for me?”*
    1. **📝** **NOTE**: If the client hasn’t included their specific issue or request:
      1. 💠 **Say something like**: “*Thanks for contacting Latchel, how can I help you today?”*
  3. Carefully read what the client wrote and note key information (work order, name, address, issues).
  4. <u>If the client has a question that does </u>**<u>not</u>**<u> require </u>**<u>taking</u>**<u> </u>**<u>action</u>**<u> on a specific work order, answer it.</u>
    1. Refer to the following useful resources:
      1. [Job-Aid: Blurbs or Templates to Communicate with Customers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-Blurbs-or-Templates-to-Communicate-with-Customers) for responses to common topics.
      2. [Latchel Processes and Product Wiki](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Latchel-Processes-and-Product-Wiki) for an overview of common topics.
      3. [(PREMIUM) Job Aid: Phone and Text Message/Webchat Frequently Asked Questions (⭕Live Updates)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/PREMIUM-Job-Aid-Phone-and-Text-Message/Webchat-Frequently-Asked-Questions-Live-Updates) for a FAQ.
    2. <u>If it’s regarding a specific WO, you may need to do some research to answer the question:</u>
      1. Search for and match the work order that the client is referring to.
        1. Alternatively, if the client can’t provide a WO number, open the client’s profile (Resident/Service Provider/PM) in Latchel Console to review all open jobs for that person.
          1. Additionally, open the property profile because work orders might be requested by different residents for the same property. Those views will allow you to see all issues/jobs related to that person so you have a more complete picture of what is going on.
            1. If you are unable to find the work order in the profile view, check which filters are being applied and include all that is unchecked, for example, the work order could have been cancelled.
        2. Open the work order and check for WO status, recent notes and pending tasks.
        3. Skim work order for prior email and SMS conversations to see if there were prior interactions for the same issue and if there is anything there that is not in the work order notes.
          1. Refer to [Job Aid: Read the Existing Work Order](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-Read-the-Existing-Work-Order) for details
          2. If there is no information that will help to answer the client’s question or resolve the issue on the WO, check also the email queue for emails related to this work order, resident, property manager, or address
        4. Once you’re able to answer the client's question:
          1. Skip to [Final Assurance and Engagement](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Final-Assurance-and-Engagement) section.
          2. If you need additional help or input from a peer or your supervisor, use the Escalation guidelines here:  [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Escalate-Between-Teams)
  5. <u>If the request is about </u>**<u>updating or taking action</u>**<u> on a Work Order:</u>
    1. **If a Property Manager reaches out regarding an emergency or urgent matter:**
      1. Sample verbatims from PMs: “*This is an emergency*”, “*This is urgent*”, “*Resident safety issue*”, “*Property damage happening now*”, “*Immediate attention required*”
        1. If urgency is not explicitly mentioned:
          1. Review the work order details yourself and check the work order severity to determine if this is related to an emergency work order.
            1. If this is an emergency, proceed with taking action right away. Do not share the help guide
            2. If it is not, proceed with standard handling of the PM’s request → see Step iii.
      2. Prioritize action immediately. Do **NOT provide a help guide**.
      3. Assist the PM directly: update details, process the request, escalate as needed.
      4. Document clearly in the work order notes, including why it was treated as an emergency and the actions taken.
    2. **If a Property Manager says they are blocked or have a limitation on doing the action:**
      1. **📝A “blocker” is any situation where a Property Manager explicitly states they cannot proceed due to an issue on their end—regardless of what you see on your end.**
***Do not question their limitation or try to justify that they have access. Always assist them directly. ***See [list of samples of PM blockers below](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/list-of-samples-of-PM-blockers-below).
      2. Assist the client directly. Take the necessary action on their behalf (e.g., update details, process the request, escalate as needed).
      3. If the blocker is due to a **technical or system limitation** that needs investigation/resolution, escalate to **Product Support →  **[How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support).
        - If the PM is not facing a technical or system limitation, no need to escalate to PS.
      4. Document the action and the reason**. **Use clear, concise language in the work order notes.
    3. **If a Property Manager reaches out without being blocked by any triggers (simply unsure of how to perform an action) AND the request is not tied to an emergency work order,  you can proceed by clarifying the process and guiding them through the applicable steps:**
      1. We’ll first attempt to provide the client with a help article to guide them on taking action themselves.
        1. **📝** **NOTE**: Keep the user type in mind when communicating with customers. Latchel has 3 help centers for users with interface access: (1) Residents, (2) Property Managers, and (3) Service Providers. Owners and landlords are not included since they don’t have dashboard access—so actions may need to be taken on their behalf. To provide the most stellar customer experience for RentRedi and Nomad Owners, refer to [How to: Handle DIY Owner Contacts](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-DIY-Owner-Contacts).
        2. 💠 **Say something like**: *“Absolutely, I can guide you through the steps so you can handle [action] directly anytime you need! We have a detailed help guide that explains how to do this. Here’s the link: [Insert Help Guide URL]. Please take a moment to check it out and let me know if you need any further assistance.”*
          1. The same script structure applies to any help article on [help.latchel.com](https://help.latchel.com). Just update the template with the article’s subject and link. For quick reference to common Help Guide requests, see [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/this-guide).
            1. **If there is no Help Guide relevant or available in***** ***[http://help.latchel.com](http://help.latchel.com):
              1. Take action on behalf of the customer.
              2. Submit a **Public Latchel Help Article Request** in the **#svc-ops-requests** Slack channel (video). Your input is key to growing and improving the Help Center.
          2. **‼️IMPORTANT**: Some actions cannot be performed by users themselves. In these cases, delivering a positive customer experience is critical. Review [this guide](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Help-Center-Reference-Guide) for details on these limitations and how to handle them.
        3. **📝** **NOTE**: If the client asks about multiple items, list them out and adjust your script. For example:
          1. 💠 **Say something like**:  “*To summarize, you need help with: [A, B, C,…]. Let’s start with [A].”*
      2. Wait for the client’s response.

<Note>
If there is a need to place the client on “hold” during the conversation or if we will keep the client waiting while researching or working on the solution, please let the client know you will take a couple of minutes to research.

💠 **Say something like**: *“Is it okay if I take a moment to gather more information?”*

Go back with the client every 2 minutes to thank them for waiting, let them know what you are doing, and that you will need more time.
</Note>

### 📝Assessing The Response and Providing Tailored Support

1. <u>If the client attempts the action and succeeds:</u>
  1. 💠 **Say something like**: *“That’s great to hear you were able to do it! Is there anything else I can assist you with today?”*
    1. **📝** **NOTE**: If the client made several requests at the beginning of the contact, move to the next one.
      1. If nothing else is needed, skip to [Final Assurance and Engagement](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Final-Assurance-and-Engagement) section.
2. <u>If the client prefers not to try it or needs further assistance:</u>
  1. 💠 **Say something like**: *“No problem, I’m here to help! Let’s go through the steps together. Please let me know where you are currently stuck, or what part seems unclear. We can start from there.”*
  2. Proceed to assist the client with any follow-up questions or issues.

#### ⚡Direct Intervention (ONLY IF NECESSARY)

1. If the client prefers not to walk through the steps with you and simply wants you to take action on their behalf:
  1. 💠 **Say something like**: ***“**No problem at all. We're happy to take care of this for you. I'm going to **[recap the action you're going to take]** now.”*
  2. Proceed to execute the task.
    1. **📝** **NOTE**: If the [Product Support Team vs Ops Agents](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Product-Support-Team-vs-Ops-Agents)
    2. In addition to executing the request, proceed to submit a Product Support Escalation.
      1. Refer to [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support) for details.
      2. **📝** **NOTE**: Do not inform the client about this escalation, this is for internal purposes to make sure this customer is given a call by the dedicated Customer Success manager since all of our clients should feel empowered to take action on their end. We’ll want to get to the bottom of why they’re being blocked from doing so.

### 🤝Final Assurance and Engagement

1. After resolving the issue or guiding the client proceed with sending the following messages:
  1. 💠 **Say something like**:*** **“I'm glad I was able to walk you through that. Please let us know if you need any help in the future.*” or "*Glad we could get that resolved for you. Feel free to reach out anytime if you need anything else.”*
  2. Closing statement and sharing CSAT survey:
    1. 💠 **Say something like**: *“Thank you for contacting Latchel. Please take our brief survey to let us know how we did: *[https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP)*”*
    2. **📝** **NOTE**: When should I not send the CSAT survey?
      1. When requesting photos during/following a call
      2. When sending a follow-up/update after a call
      3. Any other outbound communication in which the client didn’t respond or interact
  3. Close the SMS window. (See: [↔ How to: Use Text Message and Webchat Functions (in Latchel Console)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Use-Text-Message-SMS/Webchat-Functions-in-Latchel-Console).

### ❗Handling Unresponsive or Upset Client

2. <u>If at any point during the conversation the client hasn’t responded after 3 minutes of your latest message, you may proceed with ending the conversation.</u>
  1. **📝** **NOTE**: While waiting for a response, you’re expected to take on any other available conversations.
  2. Share the following messages:
    1. 💠 **Say something like**: *“It was my pleasure to message with you today. I will close our conversation now. Please feel free to contact us again in the future ,if you need help with anything else. Take care!”*
    2. 💠 **Say something like**: ***“**Thank you for contacting Latchel. Please take our brief survey to let us know how we did: *[https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP)*”*
    3. If the client is upset, you can use this instead:
      1. 💠 **Say something like**:* “Your feedback is important for improving our services. Please consider visiting this form to share your thoughts on today's interaction. Thank you for helping us enhance your future experiences: *[*https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP*](https://latchel.typeform.com/to/GpWKSIRP)*”*
  3. What if a PM complains that we’re sending them too many CSAT surveys?
    1. [Escalate the issue to the Product Support team](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Escalate-to-Product-Support).
  4. For hostile customers, refer to  [Job Aid: How to Deal with Hostile Customers](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-How-to-Deal-with-Hostile-Customers).

### **📝**Write Effective Notes

1. Wrap-up the message
  1. [How to: Write Effective Notes](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Write-Effective-Notes)
    1. **📝** **NOTE: **It is no longer required to log the conversation manually on the Work Order since the transcript will be logged automatically by the system in the “Transcripts” section, but** **it is important to leave a detailed note on the Work Order if you updated it as a result of SMS/Webchat communication indicating why you took said action.

### **Common Examples of PM-Reported Blockers**

1. **Access Issues:**
  1. “I can’t log in to the portal.”
  2. “I don’t see the option to edit this work order.”
  3. “I’m locked out of my account.”
2. **Technical Errors:**
  1. “The page keeps giving me an error when I try to submit.”
  2. “The system won’t let me upload files.”
3. **Missing Permissions or Features:**
  1. “I don’t have permission to update resident details.”
4. **Other Limitations:**
  1. “I’m unable to update the intake settings.”
  2. “Pages aren’t loading, buttons aren’t working, or I keep getting error messages.”
  3. “I’m stuck in a workflow and can’t proceed.”
  4. “I’m not in front of my computer right now.”

<Accordion title="Q: What if a resident wants to submit a maintenance request via SMS?">
1. **For registered residents:**
Let them know they can submit work orders directly through their dashboard. Provide them with their dashboard link.
  1. 💠 **Say something like**: *"Hi [Resident Name]! You have a personal maintenance portal — no login needed. Here's your link: [Dashboard Link from Miscellaneous in resident profile]. If you'd rather do it here over text or prefer I give you a quick call, I'm happy to help that way too — just let me know!"*
2. **For non-registered residents:**
The call option via the Latchel-Assigned Phone Number can be provided.
  1. 💠 **Say something like**: *"Hi! I wasn't able to find your account by this phone number, so I can't pull up a personal link for you right now. No worries — I can take your request right here over text, or I can give you a quick call to walk through it. Which works better for you?"*

If via **SMS**: Ask required intake questions one at a time (address, issue description, urgency, safety concerns, availability) and create the WO via incognito mode.

If via **call**: Place an outbound call and take the request using the standard phone intake process.
</Accordion>

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

You can "break" or deviate from the SOP for handling SMS/Webchat Contacts (New Support Framework) in the following situations:

1. **Technical Issues or Language Barriers:** If you are experiencing technical issues or if a non-English-speaking client reaches an English-speaking agent, you may escalate to the Operations Support Speclialists to transfer the conversation to another peer who can assist.
2. **On-Call or Special Client Types:** For on-call clients or LVN service provider-assigned work orders, always refer to their specific step-by-step guidelines instead of the general SOP.
3. **Owners and Landlords:** Owners and landlords do not have access to the Latchel interface. For these users, it may be necessary to take action on their behalf rather than guiding them to self-service.
4. **Direct Intervention:** If the client prefers not to try self-service or walk through steps and explicitly requests you to take action for them, you may intervene directly after confirming permission.
5. **No Relevant Help Guide:** If there is no help guide available for the action the client needs, you may take action on their behalf and submit a request to create a new help article.
6. **Emergency Situations:** If a resident describes an emergency, property damage, or extreme habitability issue via SMS: do not simply redirect them. Create a work order immediately with whatever information is available. Troubleshooters will escalate to the PM following: [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Job-Aid-PM-Outbound-Communication-for-Emergency-WOs), and prioritize urgency over intake completeness. An incomplete work order escalated promptly is always better than a complete work order delayed.
7. **If there is a recent interaction (same day) from the Property Manager (PM) about the same inquiry and the PM has already been provided with the relevant help guide, there is no need to provide the help guide again.** Instead, proceed to address their current question or concern directly.
