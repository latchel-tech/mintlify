- [**How to: Use an email templated response/escalation (Assembly)**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3421536275/How+to+Respond+to+Assembly+Tickets+and+Forward+Requests) ([*video tutorial*](https://www.loom.com/share/1ebea18b862d4b06ab392a72902900ae?sid=7ef0234c-9851-4f08-8e60-ed19973d1cff) timestamp 4:57) - Outlines how Operations Associates can use templates in Assembly to efficiently communicate Work Order details to specific Latchel groups and respond to customers promptly.
  - List of available templates in Assembly:
    - **(Operations) Inquiry from Unregistered Owner – Disabled Property** - *Template used to contact NOMAD support when an individual claims to be a property owner but is not listed in the system. Applicable only for non-PD/EH scenarios.*
    - **Operations - Automated CSAT** - *Template for sending an automated Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) message.*
    - **(Operations) Past due payments (Preferred or Latchel vendors)** - *Template used to respond to service providers following up on past due payments.*
    - **(Operations) Need to Verify (PMs with NO Active Integration)** - *Template used to escalate NTV (Need to Verify) emails to property managers (PMs) who do not have an active integration.*
    - **(Operations) Need to Verify (PMs With Chrome Plugin Integration)** - *Template used to escalate NTV emails to PMs with Chrome Plugin integration.*
    - **(Operations) Need to Verify (PMs On Other Integration)** - *Template used to escalate NTV emails to PMs with other types of integration.*
    - **Invoice received and unable to locate the WO/address on our end** - *Template used to respond to service providers when an invoice is received but the related work order or property address cannot be identified.*
    - **(Operations) Invoice from SP** - *Template used to acknowledge or respond to a service provider who has submitted an invoice.*
    - **(Operations) Estimate from SP** - *Template used to acknowledge or respond to a service provider who has submitted an estimate.*
    - **(Operations) Collecting Air Filter Information from Residents** - *Template used to request air filter details from residents.*
    - **(Operations) Budget has been approved** - *Template used to inform the service provider that their budget has been approved.*
  - [Escalation Procedure for Application Issues](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3208249355/Service+Ops+Assembly+Guide)
