<Note>
We want to ensure that associates are only working when there is sufficient volume to justify coverage, while minimizing operational costs and maintaining service quality. By offering Voluntary Time Off (VTO) in a structured way, we reduce unnecessary labor costs during low-volume periods and provide associates with flexibility, without negatively impacting customer experience or Latchel operations.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

**What happened prior to this step?**

The Operations Support Specialist team or Leadership has identified an opportunity to reduce coverage due to extremely low volumes and minimize impact on operational costs. This could happen during holidays (most frequent scenario) or we have forecasted to staff more associates than what real-time data recommends.

### Process Overview

The person monitoring backlogs and incoming work will check every two hours if the associates do not have enough activities to work per hour. Depending on the associate's responsibilities, the Primary Shift Owner will use the criteria below to make a decision:

### Emergency Troubleshooting

Associates do not have enough work to meet a target of 1.5 emergency tasks per hour, based on the data from the last hour. If the emergency tasks per hour are:

- Below 1.5, send 1 associate in VTO
- Below 1, send 2 associates in VTO

Remember to consider before offering VTO:

- Review if we have incoming calls (intake), we can reduce calls waiting first. As a rule, between Monday to Friday (6 a.m. PST - 3 p.m. PST), we need to keep a maximum of 6 agents assigned to inbound calls. Outside those hours, we can aim to have 4 agents (until 7 p.m. PST).
- Offer VTO to any associates on make-up time or overtime, after incoming calls are under control.
- Offer VTO to an associate if they have worked for at least a minimum of 4 hours.
- Attempt to reallocate Flex team members to Admin tasks

### Emergency Admin

Associates do not have enough work to meet a target of 2.5 emergency tasks/tickets (emails and tasks) per hour, based on the data from the last hour. If the emergency tasks/tickets per hour are:

- Below 1.5, send 1 associate in VTO
- Below 1, send 2 associates in VTO.

Remember to consider before offering VTO:

- Review if we have incoming calls (intake), we can reduce calls waiting first. As a rule, between Monday to Friday (6 a.m. PST - 3 p.m. PST), we need to keep a maximum of 6 agents assigned to inbound calls. Outside those hours, we can aim to have 4 agents (until 7 p.m. PST).
- Offer VTO to any associates on make-up time or overtime, after incoming calls are under control.
- Offer VTO to an associate if they have worked for at least a minimum of 4 hours.
- Attempt to reallocate Flex team members to Admin tasks

### Non-Emergency SMS/Webchat

Associates do not have enough work to meet a target of 4 conversations per hour, based on the data from the last hour. If the conversations count per hour is below 4, send 1 associate in VTO. If conversations per hour are below 2.5, send 2 associates in VTO.

Remember to consider before offering VTO:

- Move any task associates temporarily assigned to SMS/Webchat, back to tasks again.
- If data does not fall on a holiday or weekend day, assign SMS/Webchat associates to any critical email or task backlogs.
- Offer VTO to any associates on make-up time or overtime first.
- Offer VTO to an associate if they have worked for at least a minimum of 4 hours.

### Posting of VTO offers

Remember that VTO slots are limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, taking operational requirements into consideration. Once the number of slots and the people eligible based on the criteria are determined, go to the #status_updates channel and announce the open VTO slots and who is available to take them. Ask the team to comment on the thread if they want to take the VTO.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

While this SOP provides a clear framework for offering VTO, there are situations where operational judgment should be applied:

**Unexpected volume spikes**

If work volume suddenly increases while associates are on VTO or about to be offered VTO, pause or retract the VTO offer to maintain adequate coverage.

**Critical coverage requirements**

If SLA thresholds, emergency response times, or inbound call coverage are at risk, defer VTO until coverage is stabilized.

**System or data limitations**

If real-time dashboards or reporting tools are temporarily unavailable, document the observation and make a best-effort decision based on the most recent available data.

**Holiday, weekend, or special events**

While these are frequent scenarios for VTO, if there is a known high-impact event (e.g., major property issue, expected high inbound volume), adjust VTO offers accordingly.

**Associate-specific considerations**

Do not offer VTO to associates who have not met the minimum shift requirement (e.g., 4 hours worked) or whose absence would compromise essential queue coverage.

**Leadership discretion**

If Operations Support Specialists are unavailable, Leadership may override or adjust VTO offers to ensure proper coverage and payroll compliance.
