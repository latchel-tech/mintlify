## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
*We want to prevent delays, miscommunication, and frustration when residents and service providers disagree about appointments or repairs. The goal is to quickly verify the facts, clarify any confusion, and ensure that both parties are aligned—so that work orders are resolved efficiently, accurately, and with minimal disruption for everyone involved. This process helps avoid unnecessary rescheduling, double-dispatching, or missed repairs by making sure all updates are confirmed and documented before taking action*
</Info>

# Content

<Info>
### ⬅️What is happening prior to this process?

A resident has notified Latchel via webchat, phone, or email that something has gone wrong with either the scheduled appointment (Service Provider No-Show) or the actual repair has an issue (Recall Work Order).
</Info>

<Warning>
This process does not apply to On-call customers. For any inquiries from On-call customers, always refer to [Follow Up Guidelines for On-Call Customers (app.latchel.com)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/3132325894/Follow+Up+Guidelines+for+On+Call+Customers+app+latchel+com+) for further instructions. **Non-emergency Autopilot** work orders must follow [How to: Handle Autopilot WOs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2999812097/How+to+Handle+Autopilot+WOs).
</Warning>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

<Note>
For all the scenarios listed in this document, it is crucial to ensure proper follow-through. Associates must adhere to the guidelines outlined in the [Ensuring Proper Follow-Through on Customer Requests](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/edit-v2/782893419#Step-by-Step-Guide).

Failure to follow these guidelines can result in delays, errors, or miscommunication that may impact our service quality. Always review and complete the steps carefully to maintain consistency and accuracy in task management.
</Note>

## **📅🚫**Appointment Issues / Service Provider No-Show

<Note>
**For confirmed Service Provider missed appointments:**

**Emergency timing and automatic budget adjustments**

- For some emergency work orders, internal automations/action sets may **adjust the Approved Budget** based on the **scheduled appointment time**.
- If an emergency work order’s appointment is scheduled **more than 24 hours out**, the system may lower the soft emergency budget to a **non‑emergency soft limit**, even though the severity remains Emergency.
- When reviewing a Service Provider’s budget increase request, always:
  - Verify the **current appointment date and time**.
  - Keep in mind that a lower soft limit on an Emergency WO may reflect that the appointment is no longer within the emergency (same‑day / 24‑hour) window.
  - If the Service Provider is confused about the amount, follow the expectations in [**How to: Set expectations with Service Providers when dispatching**](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2600337430/How+to+Set+expectations+with+Service+Providers+when+dispatching) and then continue the budget approval process as outlined in this SOP.
</Note>

1. Review the Work Order Scheduling Dispute between the Resident and the Service Provider
  1. View the scheduled appointment window via the Latchel Console.
    <Frame>
      ![work date.png](/images/SVC-47.png)
    </Frame>
  2. View the status of the work order.
    1. **<u>Outcome 1:</u> The Resident has provided availability, but the Service Provider did not provide their availability.**
      1. This can happen when the resident believes that by giving us their availability the service provider would come at that time. When this happens, take the time to educate the customer that we collect availability to look for providers that can schedule the appointment at that time, but the appointment is not confirmed until they receive a confirmation from our system.
      2. Status: **"Scheduling with Resident" or "Scheduling with service provider"**
        1. If there is no confirmed appointment it will likely be in the "Scheduling with Resident" or "Scheduling with service provider" status. This occurs when a resident has given availability but it has not been confirmed by the provider.
      3. Apologize to the Resident and explain the situation
        1. **Say something like**,
          <Tip>
          "I apologize for the inconvenience, it appears that time frame was not confirmed with our provider. We will contact you as soon as a time frame has been confirmed!"
          </Tip>
      4. Proceed to ↔ How to: Write Effective Notes and document the call.
    2. **<u>Outcome 2:</u> There is a confirmed appointment, but it was for a different time or day and the resident is confused OR The Service Provider and the Resident have both provided availability, but the Service Provider didn't show up to the appointment.**
      1. Status: **"Awaiting work date" OR "Confirming Completion"**
        1. If there is an appointment but it is not for a current time window, the status will be in "Awaiting work date"
      2. Apologize to the Resident and explain the situation
        1. **Say something like**,
          <Tip>
          "I apologize for the inconvenience, it appears that time frame was not confirmed with our provider. The time confirmed was (insert day and time) does this work for you?"
          </Tip>
      3. If that slot still works, proceed to ↔ How to: Write Effective Notes and document the call
      4. If that slot no longer works, collect availability and set the status to "Scheduling Service Providers" to notify the Service Provider that the window needs to be changed.
      5. If the work order is an Emergency, create a task for the “Troubleshooters” Latchel user group and request to reschedule an appointment. Please keep in mind that the availability and schedule should align with the guidelines provided on dispatching an emergency work order [How to: Handle Emergency Work Orders](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/759332893/How+to+Handle+Emergency+Work+Orders)
        1. The troubleshooter will call the service provider and attempt to reschedule based on the resident’s availability.
        2. If the service provider is unresponsive:
          1. Send them an SMS and defer the task to the next day for another follow-up.
          2. If still unresponsive after the second follow-up:
            1. Reassign the work order to another service provider.
            2. Notify the unresponsive service provider via email or SMS (if available).
            3. Refer to [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/786859062/How+to+Reassign+a+Service+Provider).
        3. If the assigned service provider was recommended by the Property Manager:
          1. PM approval is required before reassigning the work order.
          2. For property-damaging or extreme habitability issues:
            1. Refer to [How to: Identify Property Damage and Extreme Habitability Situations](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/745504771/How+to+Identify+Property+Damage+and+Extreme+Habitability+Situations).
            2. Contact emergency escalation contacts immediately. See [Job Aid: PM Outbound Communication for Emergency WOs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2537652248/Job+Aid+PM+Outbound+Communication+for+Emergency+WOs)
            3. If the escalation contacts are unresponsive, move the work order to Waiting for PM.
          3. For non-property damaging/non-extreme habitability issues:
            1. Send the SP an SMS and defer the task to the next day for a response.
            2. If still unsuccessful after the second follow-up, complete the current task and escalate back to the Property Manager by creating a task:
              1. Subject: **Action Required: PM Instructions or Information Needed***.*
              2. Who is this task about? *Select the PM*
              3. Select a Date and Time: *Leave as is*
              4. Assign to a User Group: *PM user group*
              5. Assign to a User: *Leave as is*
              6. Priority:*Leave as is*
              7. *Complete task only on Completed or Cancelled job States*
              8. Description: *We’ve created this task for the Property Manager to [insert specific information or instructions needed]. The current status of the work order is [insert status]. Kindly provide the requested information or guidance at your earliest convenience to help ensure a smooth continuation of the process. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Thank you!*
    3. **<u>Outcome 3:</u> There is a confirmed appointment, but the resident follows up during the designated timeframe since the Service Provider hasn’t shown up yet.**
      1. Status: **"Awaiting work date"**
        1. If there is an existing appointment and the window time has not passed, the status will be in "Awaiting work date"
      2. Apologize to the Resident and explain the situation
        1. 🗨️ **Say something like**:*“I apologize for the inconvenience, it appears that your appointment is currently set for today [appointment time]. Kindly, await this period.”*
      3. If the resident keeps following up and it is within 30 minutes before the end of the appointment timeframe:
        1. 🗨️ **Say something like**: *“I apologize for the inconvenience, we will reach out to the provider promptly to find out their arrival time and update you accordingly”.*
        2. Contact the Service Provider
          1. If the SP confirms they will arrive within the existing appointment time, proceed to ↔ How to: Write Effective Notes and document the call.
          2. If the SP confirms they won’t be able to arrive within the existing appointment time, collect the Service Provider’s next availability, and reschedule the work order with the Resident based on both parties’ availability.
          3. If the SP doesn’t answer, keep the existing appointment as is, since the SP may still shop up to the appointment.
    4. **<u>Outcome 4:</u> The Resident advised that the Service Provider has rescheduled the repair directly with them**
      1. Status: **“Awaiting work date”, “Pending” or “Confirming completion”**
      2. Service Providers may occasionally reschedule directly with the resident after an initial appointment has been set, without updating the work order. In such cases, the resident may contact us to inform that the service provider has rescheduled the appointment.
      3. Contact the service provider to confirm the new appointment date and time.
        1. If successful, inform the service provider of the information that was provided by the resident.
          1. <Frame>
              ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-48.png)
             </Frame>
**Say something like**: *Hi [Service Provider's Name], I’m reaching out because the resident informed us that the appointment has been rescheduled. Could you please confirm the new date and time of the appointment? This will help us ensure the work order is up-to-date. Once confirmed, we'll make sure everything is accurately documented. Thank you!*
          2. If the service provider confirms, proceed with setting the appointment.
        2. If there's no response, proceed as follows:
          1. Set the appointment based on the resident's information then send an SMS/Email (whichever is applicable) to the service provider.
          2. <Frame>
              ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-49.png)
             </Frame>
**Say something like**: *Hi [Service Provider's Name], we received information from the resident that the appointment has been rescheduled to [date and time provided by the resident]. Since we haven't heard back from you, we’ll proceed by updating the work order based on the resident's details. If there are any changes or updates, please let us know. Thank you!*
          3. Proceed to ↔ How to: Write Effective Notes and document the call. Make sure to note that the appointment was set up as we have been informed by the resident that they rescheduled it directly with them.
          4. Additional action for **emergency work orders**: Escalate to Operations Support Specialists. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776044545/How+to+Escalate+Between+Teams) and notify them that the appointment has been set as informed by the resident.
    5. **Outcome 5. The service provider advised that they have scheduled the appointment directly with the resident.**
      1. Status: **Pending**
      2. Service Providers may occasionally reschedule directly with the resident after an initial appointment has been set, without updating the work order. In such cases, the service provider may contact us to inform that the resident has rescheduled the appointment.
      3. Contact the resident to confirm the new appointment date and time.
        1. If successful, inform the resident of the information that was provided by the service provider.
          1. <Frame>
              ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-50.png)
             </Frame>
**Say something like**: *Hi [resident’s], I’m reaching out because the service provider informed us that the appointment has been rescheduled. Could you please confirm the new date and time of the appointment? This will help us ensure the work order is up-to-date. Once confirmed, we'll make sure everything is accurately documented. Thank you!*
          2. If the resident confirms, proceed with setting the appointment.
        2. If there's no response, proceed as follows:
          1. Set the appointment based on the service provider’s information then send an SMS/Email (whichever is applicable) to the resident.
          2. <Frame>
              ![1f5e8.png](/images/SVC-51.png)
             </Frame>
**Say something like**:*Hi [resident’s name], we received information from the service provider that the appointment has been rescheduled to [date and time provided. Since we haven't heard back from you, we’ll proceed by updating the work order based on the service provider’s details. If there are any changes or updates, please let us know. Thank you!*
          3. Proceed to ↔ How to: Write Effective Notes and document the call. Make sure to note that the appointment was set up as we have been informed by the service provider that they rescheduled it directly with them.
          4. Additional action for **emergency work orders**: Escalate to Operations Support Specialists. Refer to [How to: Escalate Between Teams](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/776044545/How+to+Escalate+Between+Teams) and notify them that the appointment has been set as informed by the service provider.
    6. **Outcome 6: The Property Manager calls back with instructions for an escalated work order**
      - When it applies: WO was previously forwarded/escalated to PM, and PM calls back with dispatch or scheduling instructions
      - If PM gave a confirmed date/time → update the work order
      - If PM only gave assignment instructions → continue normal dispatch/scheduling
      - Document per Write Effective Notes

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

- **The situation escalates to an emergency or poses a risk to resident safety or property.**
If the dispute or delay could result in property damage, extreme habitability issues, or immediate safety concerns, escalate to Operations Support Specialists and follow emergency escalation protocols immediately **1**.
- **You encounter a scenario not covered by this SOP, or you are unsure how to proceed.**
If the dispute involves unusual circumstances, conflicting information, or you’re uncertain about the next steps, pause and escalate to a manager or Operations Support Specialist for guidance **1**.

- **You believe an alternate action would better serve the resident, service provider, or Latchel.**
Use your judgment to escalate if strict adherence to the SOP would cause unnecessary delays, confusion, or dissatisfaction.
