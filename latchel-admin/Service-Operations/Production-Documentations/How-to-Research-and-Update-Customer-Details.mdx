<Note>
We want to prevent errors, outdated records, and miscommunication by providing a clear process for researching and updating customer details. The goal is to ensure that all customer information—such as contact details, property addresses, and account notes—is accurate and up to date, so that service requests and communications are handled smoothly.
</Note>

<Tip>
This process should be executed as a secondary task and only worked on during downtime.

For associates working on this task, ensure to enter your name in the "Associate Name" field.
</Tip>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Identifying Missing Data

- Open the ["Existing Clients"](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1l_b6h8a1sfz4f1kisH7aBmrj5JCnhdxkg7c51JMavIk/edit) export.
- Use filters to identify rows with "NULL" fields in each column

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-224.png" alt="Filtering rows with NULL fields in Google Sheets" /></Frame>

### 2. Prioritize Key Fields

Focus on filling the following fields in this order of priority:

- Address (Address1, City, State, ZIP)
- Address2 (Suite, Floor, etc.)
- Site URL (Website)

### 3. Research Process

For each row with missing information:

#### 3.1 Research Customer Websites

- Search for the company name in Google.
- Visit their official website or verified business directories (e.g., LinkedIn, Yelp, or BBB).
- Record the URL in the Site URL field.

#### 3.2 Verify and Update Address Information

- Look for the contact or "About Us" page on the company's website.
- Record the company's physical address, including suite or floor details if available.

#### 3.3 Use Third-Party Sources

If the official website does not provide sufficient information:

Search for the company on:

- Google Maps: Confirm the address and ZIP Code.
- Property Management Directories (e.g., [http://NARPM.org](http://NARPM.org), [Apartments.com: Apartments and Homes for Rent](http://Apartments.com)).

### 4. Record Updates

- Populate the missing fields directly in the [designated spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1l_b6h8a1sfz4f1kisH7aBmrj5JCnhdxkg7c51JMavIk/edit).
- Use the following format standards:
  - Website URLs: Ensure URLs include "https://" (e.g., [Example Domain](https://example.com)).
  - Address1: Include street address without abbreviations (e.g., "123 Main Street" instead of "123 Main St.").
  - Address2: Use clear identifiers (e.g., "Suite 301" or "2nd Floor").
  - ZIP Codes: Verify against [Welcome | USPS](http://USPS.com) or Google Maps for accuracy.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
