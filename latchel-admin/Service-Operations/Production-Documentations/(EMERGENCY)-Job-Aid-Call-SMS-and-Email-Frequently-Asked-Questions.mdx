# 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
We want to ensure agents provide fast, accurate, and consistent responses to common emergency questions received via call, SMS, or email. The goal is to reduce confusion, prevent miscommunication, and make sure urgent issues are handled efficiently—minimizing delays, errors, and frustration for residents, service providers, and property managers.
</Info>

Content

<Note>
### Advance the Work Order

Anytime you indicate that you are taking an action for a customer and/or Work Order, it is imperative to follow up by completing that action and writing effective notes about your actions.

For more information on what you should or should not communicate to customers, review [↔ A Letter from the CEO: Customer Obsession](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/763265497/+A+Letter+from+the+CEO+Customer+Obsession).
</Note>

<Info>
### How can Customers Submit Work Orders on their own?

1. Property managers use this standard work order submission screen ([app.latchel.com/admin/job/new](http://stage.latchel.com/admin/job/new)) to create emergency and non-Emergency work orders.
2. Residents can submit Work Orders via their Resident Dashboard.
</Info>

# **<u>Resident FAQ's</u>**

### **Q: What if a Service Provider arrives on-site, but the resident did not answer the phone call?**

- First inform the Service Provider that we will attempt to contact the resident

### **Q**: **"Can tech call before they arrive?"**

1. The Resident informs you that they would like the tech to call them when they are on their way to the home
  1. Say,
    <Tip>
    *We will contact and instruct the service provider to call before arrival. Please be advised that we cannot guarantee they will call. Please let us know if the service provider met your expectations once the work is complete.*
    </Tip>
2. Send a text message to the provider

### **Q: The resident has a Non-Emergency Work Order Follow Up**

1. Refer to [SOP: Work Order Follow-ups and Updates](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2511372328/SOP+Work+Order+Follow+ups+and+Updates)

### **Q: The Resident is calling for a follow-up on a work order that is not in the system**

1. Initially, if a work order does not appear to be in the system, the agent is recommended to check under the cancelled column of the property portal. When a work order is forwarded to the PM it appears there.
  1. In the event the work order is there, see: [How to: Handle situations for WOs awaiting PM action](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Handle-situations-for-WOs-awaiting-PM-action)
2. If the work order is not in the system, and not in the Cancelled column,
  1. Create a Work Order.
  2. Refer to [How to: Create a New Work Order from "Incognito Mode" (All Submissions)](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/781647909/How+to+Create+a+New+Work+Order+from+Incognito+Mode+All+Submissions+)

# **<u>Service Provider FAQ's</u>**

### **Q: The Service Provider called and stated that they could not reach the resident.**

1. If the service provider has called Latchel and informed the agent that they are onsite and cannot reach the resident:
  1. Verify if there are any access instructions on the work order page, that would indicate that they may enter.
  2. Contact the resident on file and let them know the service provider is attempting to access.
    1. If they answer, relay the message to the Service Provider
    2. If they do not answer, leave a voicemail and let them know we are trying to confirm they are home for the service provider to enter
      1. Call service provider and let them know you were not successful at contact but will call back if the resident calls us. Ask the technician/service provider if they can attempt to contact again as well.
2. If the service provider confirmed they went out and attempted to contact the resident, but the resident did not answer door/phone:
  1. Contact the resident and inform them that the service provider has been to the property but was not able to access the unit, reschedule the appointment in the system per service provider next available appointment time
3. If the service provider confirmed they attempted to call the resident to confirm the arrival time and the resident did not answer;
  1. Call the resident and let them know the service provider has attempted to contact via phone to provide the arrival time, reschedule the appointment in the system per service provider next available appointment time
  2. If they do not answer, leave a voicemail and let them know we are trying to confirm they are home for the service provider to arrive
    1. Call service provider and let them know you were not successful at contacting the resident but will call back if the resident calls us. Ask the technician/service provider if they can attempt to contact the resident again.

### **Q: How do I Reassign a Service Provider?**

- Follow guidelines here: [How to: Reassign a Service Provider](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/786859062/How+to+Reassign+a+Service+Provider)

### **Q: A Service Provider is calling about a non-Latchel related Work Order**

- Occasionally, service providers will call for information on a work order from the Property Manager, but it was outside of our system. This needs to be referred back to the Property Manager.
  - Ask the Service Provider to contact the Property Manager.  (Latchel is not responsible for non-emergency work orders that are not in our system. We do not want to add that information into our system if the work order does not belong to us.)

### **Q: The Service Provider is experiencing Payment or Invoicing Issues**

- Escalate to Finance by creating a task assigned to that user group.
  - Refer to [Job Aid: What is a task?](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2528083973/Job+Aid+What+is+a+task+)

# **<u>Other Emergency FAQ's</u>**

### **Q. The Property Manager is requesting that an agent change prioritization of their Preferred Service Providers or to Edit Skills for a Service Provider**

<Note>
### Emergency Services

If a Property Manager asks you to change prioritization of their Preferred Service Providers or to edit the skills they want those service providers to execute on their accounts, let them know you will forward the request to our product support team.

Escalate as per instructions in the Emergency Escalation SOP [How to: Escalate to Product Support](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/1485373477/How+to+Escalate+to+Product+Support)
</Note>

### **Q. A Utility Company is calling for Access**

<Note>
Latchel does not get involved with Utility companies. This must be forwarded to the Property Manager.
</Note>

### **Q: If a Property Manager calls back with instructions for an "escalated" work order**

- Property manager calls back and instructs to assign a service provider after you have left a voicemail
  - Work order is already "forwarded to PM"
    - Click on "Take Action"
    - Click on "Set a New Confirmed Work Date"
      <Frame>
        ![image-20230623-185917.png](/images/SVC-44.png)
      </Frame>
    - This will prompt you to "finding service provider" status to take action per property manager instruction
