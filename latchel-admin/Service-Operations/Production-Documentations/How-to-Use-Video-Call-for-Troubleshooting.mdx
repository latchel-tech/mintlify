## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Note>
  We want to prevent misdiagnosis, unnecessary service calls, and delays when troubleshooting maintenance issues that can’t be fully assessed over the phone. The goal is to ensure associates use video calls to visually confirm and diagnose problems in real time—improving accuracy, empowering residents to show the issue directly, and enabling better decisions about dispatching or resolving work orders.

  For information on how to use the Video Call technology, refer to this SOP: [How to: Handle Video Calls for Emergency Troubleshooting](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/How-to-Handle-Video-Calls-for-Emergency-Troubleshooting)
</Note>

<Note>
  **🎓Training Content**

  The contents of this page have been included in the \*\*Video Troubleshooting \*\*Coassemble course.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

### Process Overview

<Note>
  **Technical Specifications for Using Video Call**

  1. Audio
     1. Bluetooth headphones may not function appropriately while using Video Call. Alternatively, use wired headphones or speaker phone during Video Calling.
  2. Video
     1. **‼️IMPORTANT:** If the Latchel user does not have video call functionality on their phone, the Video Call CANNOT be initiated.
     2. If you choose to turn the camera on, prepare the entire rectangular width of your background environment because it will be captured by the video 🔴recording.
  3. **Latchel user** - Device to start the Video Call (from the Work Order)
     1. Video Calling can be initiated via desktop browser or via mobile.
  4. **Customer** - Device to start the Video Call (from the text message link)
     1. Video Calling can be initiated via mobile or tablet
     2. ‼️IMPORTANT: Beware of expending too much time on the Video Call if it’s not working; focus on taking care of the Emergency Work Order! Not all customers will have the capability to complete Video troubleshooting, or you might encounter issues with specific customers.

  Prepare for the Call using instructions outlined in this SOP: [How to: Handle Video Calls for Emergency Troubleshooting](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Video-Calls-for-Emergency-Troubleshooting)
</Note>

<Note>
  For steps on how to initiate video call for on-call residents, refer to the guidelines in [Follow Up Guidelines for On-Call Customers (app.latchel.com)](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/Follow-Up-Guidelines-for-On-Call-Customers-applatchelcom).
</Note>

### Step 1 - Accessing the Video Call

<Accordion title="To learn how to access the video call functionality from an existing work order, click here...">
  1. Enter the Existing Work Order
     1. The Work Order must be in **Troubleshooting Status** for the Emergency (or Non-Emergency) Work Order.
  2. Navigate to the Resident information
     1. Click on the video camera icon.
        <Frame>
          ![SVC-569.png](/images/SVC-569.png)
        </Frame>
        1. 📝 NOTE: Each time that the Latchel agent begins the Video Call by pushing this button, a new Video Call URL link will be created.
     2. Tag the video call to a WO, please refer to  [Tagging Video Calls to a WO](/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentations/Tagging-Video-Calls-to-a-WO) for details.
     3. The video call option will prompt you with the option to begin the Video Chat Room. 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 570](/images/SVC-570.png "SVC 570")
        </Frame>
        1. If you would like to proceed, click “Create Video Chat Room”.
           1. Proceed to Step 2 below.
        2. To exit the option, remove the window by clicking “X”.
  3. You should communicate with the resident on the Video Call procedure, including what to expect next.
     1. **‼️IMPORTANT** Click “Create Video Chat Room” and ask the resident if to check that they received the text message before you hang up the phone!
     2. See the (SOP) for How to: Handle Video Calls: [How to: Handle Video Calls for Emergency Troubleshooting](https://help.latchel.com/latchel-admin/Service-Operations/Production-Documentation/How-to-Handle-Video-Calls-for-Emergency-Troubleshooting)

  <Danger>
    Hang up the Outbound Call from the Latchel Console! After you transition from the phone call with the resident to the video call with the resident, make sure to END the phone call.
  </Danger>
</Accordion>

### Step 2 - Using the Video Chat Room Functions

<Accordion title="To learn more information about the video chat room functionalities, click here...">
  1. Click on “Create Video Chat Room” to open a new browser window to begin the Video Call.
     1. The browser will automatically start 🔴recording the Latchel user live in the video call.
     2. In addition, pop-up will offer the options for the Latchel user to (1) Open your chat window; (2) Copy the Url for the resident; and (3) View Call Log. 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 571](/images/SVC-571.png "SVC 571")
        </Frame>
        1. The option to “Open your chat window” will open a new browser window, which will show a message saying that you are “Connecting to room…”
        2. You can send the Url for the customer via text message in the Latchel Console.
           1. From the resident’s point of view, the resident will receive a text message with a link to meet you in that same video chat room. 
              <Frame>
                ![SVC 572](/images/SVC-572.png "SVC 572")
              </Frame>
              1. After tapping on the URL, the browser may verify that the resident allows the browser window to access their microphone and video. 
                 <Frame>
                   ![SVC 573](/images/SVC-573.png "SVC 573")
                 </Frame>

  2. Video Call Window features
     1. Here is a sample Video Call Window active from the customer’s point of view (via mobile). 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 574](/images/SVC-574.png "SVC 574")
        </Frame>
        <Tip>
          👉PRO TIP: Tap on the user within the circle of the Video Call. This will SWAP🔀 the main and secondary Views!
        </Tip>
     2. ‼️IMPORTANT The video 🔴recording will begin as soon as the agent or the customer enters into the new video chat room. 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 575](/images/SVC-575.png "SVC 575")
        </Frame>
        <Note>
          📝 NOTE:  The video 🔴recording will show your video image in a small circle. However, be aware that the 🔴recording captures the entire rectangular width of your camera space.
        </Note>
        Here's an example: 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 576](/images/SVC-576.png "SVC 576")
        </Frame>
     3. The video camera will automatically start/be open for the customer after entering the Video Chat Room.
        1. Warn the customer about this while you’re on the phone with them.
     4. When you are the only person in the Video Chat Room, the screen will state that you are “Waiting for people to join…”. 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 577](/images/SVC-577.png "SVC 577")
        </Frame>
     5. There are 4 functionalities within the Video Chat Room, which are available for both the Latchel agent and the resident. 
        <Frame>
          ![SVC 578](/images/SVC-578.png "SVC 578")

        1. **(1) End Call**
           1. When the user or customer chooses “End Call”, the Video Call will end.

                ![SVC 579](/images/SVC-579.png "SVC 579")

              1. The user or customer can re-enter into the same Video Call room by using the URL in the Pop-Up that appears after the agent beings the Video Call. 
                 <Frame>
                   ![SVC 580](/images/SVC-580.png "SVC 580")
                 </Frame>
                 <Note>
                   Note: Each time that the Latchel agent begins the Video Call, a new Video Call link will be created.
                 </Note>
        2. **(2) Mute ON/OFF**
           1. Default setting for both users is ON.
        3. **(3) Camera ON/OFF**
           1. Default setting for both users is ON.
        4. **(4) Flip Camera**
           1. Default setting depends on the device being used by the user.

   
</Accordion>

### Step 3 - Using the Video Call Log

<Accordion title="To learn more information about the Video Call Log, click here...">
  1. Review the history section of the existing Work Order to find the Video Call log and 🔴recording hyperlinks. 
     <Frame>
       ![SVC 581](/images/SVC-581.png "SVC 581")
     </Frame>
     1. Click on **“Call Recording”** to enter the URL that will take you directly to the video call 🔴recording.
     2. Click on “**Full call details”** to get detailed information about the video call.
