<Note>
We want to prevent errors, gaps, and outdated information in our HubSpot CRM by providing a clear, step-by-step process for verifying, enriching, and updating contact and company records. The goal is to ensure all customer and prospect data in HubSpot is accurate, complete, and up-to-date—so Sales, Support, and Operations teams can rely on this information for outreach, follow-up, and reporting.
</Note>

## 📋 Step-by-Step Process

To learn more about how to complete the file, please review the required training videos below:

- Process overview (14 mins): [HubSpot Contact Enrichment Training - 30 April 2025](https://www.loom.com/share/ff66ca3a626f444b9dfa92564f808aa7)
- How to find a PMS (5 mins): [How to find PMS Software w. Examples - 3 April 2025](https://www.loom.com/share/716928747dae402ba308ab68147bae81)

Copy/paste the Contact Email Address from [HubSpot Contact Enrichment](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15ACOtqlVNVQhiv0KTPH3yX4LX8pdOrplWaIdb6Y7UE4/edit) in Google

<Frame><img src="/images/SVC-242.png" alt="Searching the Contact Email Address in Google" /></Frame>

Use the Google Results to complete all required spreadsheet fields

- Contact Email Address: Verify and update if needed
  - If the spreadsheet provides a generic email address (ex: [info@rockcapitalok.com](mailto:info@rockcapitalok.com), [admin@rockcapitalok.com](mailto:admin@rockcapitalok.com), [rentals@rockcapitalok.com](mailto:rentals@rockcapitalok.com), etc.), update this with the contact's personal email (ex: [a.deramus@rockcapitalok.com](mailto:a.deramus@rockcapitalok.com))
- Contact First Name: Verify and update if needed
- Contact Last Name: Verify and update if needed
- Contact Job Title: Verify and update if needed
- Contact LinkedIn: Provide the URL to the contact's LinkedIn
  - If they're not on LinkedIn, leave this field blank
- Company Name: Verify and update if needed
- Company Website: Provide the URL to the company website
- Company Phone Number: Provide the company's general phone number as listed on their website, Google page, Facebook page, etc.
  - Do not provide fax numbers
  - Do not use any text values
  - If there is an extension, provide it separately in the next column
- Company Phone Number Extension
  - If there is no extension, leave this field blank
- Contact Phone Number:
  - Goal is to find a direct line, mobile number, etc. (not the general office number)
  - Only complete this field if you find a direct phone number to the contact. Do not repeat the company phone number. If you do not find a direct phone number to the contact, leave this field blank.
  - Do not provide fax numbers
  - Do not use any text values
  - If there is an extension, provide it separately in the next column.
- Contact Phone Number Extension
  - If there is no extension, leave this field blank
- Company Street Address
  - Copy from website or Google page
- Company City
  - Copy from website or Google page
- Company State
  - Copy from website or Google page
- Company Zip Code
  - Copy from website or Google page
- Company LinkedIn
  - If they're not on LinkedIn, leave this field blank
- Company # of employees
  - See guidance in [How to: Research Local Property Managers (Second Phase)](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers-(Second-Phase))
- Property Structure Type
  - Choose from the dropdown
  - If the company manages multiple types of rentals (ex: both single- and multi-family) and one of them includes single family, select Single Family.
  - If the company manages multiple types of rentals (ex: multifamily and commercial) but none of them include single family, select whatever category they seem to have more of.
- Number of Vacancies
  - See guidance in [How to: Research Local Property Managers](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers)
- PMS Software Used
  - See guidance in [How to: Research Local Property Managers](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers) and [How to find PMS Software w. Examples - 3 April 2025](https://www.loom.com/share/716928747dae402ba308ab68147bae81)
  - Select from dropdown
- Google Review Count
  - See guidance in [How to: Research Local Property Managers](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers)
- Google Rating
  - See guidance in [How to: Research Local Property Managers](https://help.latchel.com/How-to-Research-Local-Property-Managers)

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

Do not break or deviate from this SOP on your own authority. Always escalate to an Ops Support specialist or follow explicit instructions if you believe an exception is necessary.
