## 🧠 What This Process Is Trying to Solve

<Info>
We want to prevent delays in handling emergency work orders when troubleshooting tasks are not automatically created due to a service degradation. The goal is to manually create and monitor tasks so that customer issues are addressed promptly, ensuring Latchel operations continue smoothly despite system disruptions.
</Info>

**Content**

<Info>
To learn about what a “Service Degration” is, refer to (SOP) Latchel App Outage
</Info>

## Troubleshooting Tasks are not being created or delayed

In the event that Emergency Troubleshooting tasks are not being created for incoming WOs.

1. Go to the main Latchel page: [https://app.latchel.com/admin/work-orders](https://app.latchel.com/admin/work-orders)
2. Filter by WOs in “Troubleshooting” state:
  <Frame>
    ![image-20230526-230413.png](/images/SVC-95.png)
  </Frame>
3. Create a manual troubleshooting task for each of the WOs displayed.
  1. Refer to [Job Aid: What is a task?](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OPS/pages/2528083973/Job+Aid+What+is+a+task+)
4. Refresh the page as often as possible.
5. Repeat the steps above for any new WOs until the Service Degradation is resolved.

## 🎯 When to Break This SOP

<Note>
This SOP should be followed in most cases, but team members can adjust their approach based on the situation. For example, if the service degradation is resolved temporarily, or if a leadership-directed workaround or tool becomes available, support may skip or modify steps. The goal is to maintain coverage and ensure emergency work orders are addressed without unnecessary delay.
</Note>
