## Opportunity & Impact

You are the reason maintenance requests and work orders keep moving. Every business has a heartbeat–a rhythm to which it operates. Coordination Associates provide that rhythm to Latchel. You help work orders get unstuck. You help residents get their requests taken care of and explain to them what to expect. You provide excellent customer service and enable the rest of the team to be effective in getting maintenance taken care of. This role is not only essential to providing our service to our customers, but it is an excellent launching point to move anywhere else within Latchel.

## Responsibilities

- Receive inbound tenant phone calls.
- Receive overflow vendor and property manager phone calls.
- Receive and process inbound SMS, email, and web chat messages.
- Categorize inbound requests so they are directed to the appropriate team.
- Solve tenant scheduling, troubleshooting, and work order completion issues.
- Enter new maintenance requests and work orders into Latchel system.
- Escalate issues you cannot solve to Process Specialists.

## Qualifications

- Superior knowledge of maintenance coordination best practices, especially in regards to property maintenance and improvement.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

<Note>
Bilingual Spanish-English Coordination Associates are paid an additional $1/hr
</Note>

## Internal Job Information

### How is work routed to a Coordination Associate?

- Inbound phone calls - via Zendesk
- Inbound SMS/Webchat - via Latchel Console
- Inbound emails - via Zendesk
- Tenant scheduling and completion issues - Via Latchel Operations Tasks Sheet (Tenant East, West, Central)
- New work orders - via Zendesk
- Troubleshooting - Via Pagerduty
