## Opportunity & Impact

You are the expert on how to run a maintenance department. You are the first point of escalation for complex issues and difficult situations and are adaptable enough to take them in stride. You solve problems inside of Latchel so our customers can rest easy knowing even their most challenging maintenance problems are taken care of. In this role you will also be primarily responsible for improving the skill set of all Latchel maintenance coordinators through coaching, feedback, and simplifying our processes.

## Responsibilities

- Handle escalations from Operations Associates, Vendor Associates, and Property Manager Specialists.
- Resolve resident/vendor disputes on incomplete work and escalate vendor quality issues to Sr. Vendor Specialist.
- Perform audits of Operations Associates, Vendor Associate, Property Management Associate and other team members' work against Latchel process standards.
- Review problem work orders, take corrective actions, and coach team members on issues that led to the initial problems.
- Provide feedback on process standards and work with Operations Product & Program Management to improve standards.
- Support new hires through training, coaching and feedback.
- Support existing team with learning and following new process standards.
- Develop and execute new processes for new Latchel features and services.
- Update and improve data integrity within Latchel system.

As needed:

- Receive inbound tenant phone calls.
- Receive overflow vendor and property manager phone calls.
- Receive and process inbound SMS, email, and web chat messages.
- Solve tenant scheduling, troubleshooting, and work order completion issues.
- Enter new maintenance requests and work orders into Latchel system.
- Escalate issues you cannot solve to Operations Supervisor.

## Qualifications

- Deep knowledge of maintenance coordination best practices, especially in regards to property maintenance and improvement and tracking and visibility.
- Demonstrated experience in process documentation.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Internal Job Information

### How is work routed to a Process Specialist?

The Process Specialist Work Queue spreadsheet contains

- Resident-reported issues with work order completion
- Escalations from the team (in-system)
- Unknown Tasks (tasks that do not have an assigned contact)
- Orphaned work orders (work orders with no follow-up tasks at high risk of getting "Lost")
