## Opportunity & Impact

You lead the Latchel maintenance coordination team. You build, support, and grow the team responsible for servicing our hundreds of customers across the US. The team looks to you for direction, support, motivation, and encouragement to deliver results for our customers and continuously raise the standards of excellent customer service. Our customers depend on you to set the right tone and have a strong guiding hand in ensuring Latchel delivers on its service promise.

## Responsibilities

- Handle escalations from Process Specialists.
- Lead daily start of shift and end of shift standups with the team.
- Work with your team to drive quality and performance metrics.
- Train, onboard, and develop Process Specialists.
- Hiring and develop all Operations Associates.
- Identify trends, patterns, and problems within Operations and work with own team, Ops Manager, Customer Experience Manager to drive systematic change and process improvement.

## Qualifications

- Strong leadership and people management skills.
- Strong recruiting, coaching and people development skills.
- Demonstrated experience in process documentation, metrics, and reporting.
- Strong data analytics and root cause analysis skills.
- Ability to remain calm and create stability in a chaotic, rapidly changing environment.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
