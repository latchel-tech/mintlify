## Job Overview

We are seeking a dedicated and knowledgeable Product Support Associate to join our team. The ideal candidate will manage customer inquiries, provide expert guidance on our products, and ensure high customer satisfaction through exceptional service. This role requires excellent communication skills, a strong technical aptitude, and a passion for helping customers navigate and resolve their issues. Latchel's product and policies span every corner of property management and change constantly — this role calls for someone who can build real expertise across all of it, not just their own focus area, and who treats accuracy and quality as the standard every answer is measured against.

## **Technical Expertise & Self-Sufficiency**

- Latchel's product, policies, and systems change constantly. This role requires continuously building and maintaining working expertise across every facet of the platform — not just your own team's focus area.
- Independently investigate open questions using every tool and resource available before asking for help.
- Escalating is ownership: when you reach the edge of what you can verify on your own, escalate through the proper channels — and stay accountable for confirming the answer is right before it reaches the customer.
- AI-assisted tools can speed up research, but Latchel policy, verified system behavior, and the facts on the account always take precedence — the associate's own investigation and judgment are what reach the customer.

## Key Responsibilities

- Deliver accurate, high-quality answers on every ticket — accuracy comes first, always, even when it means taking longer or escalating.
- Triage incoming customer inquiries, resolve issues, or escalate them as necessary.
- Investigate billing disputes and collaborate with relevant teams to resolve issues.
- Collaborate with customers to identify the root causes of product issues.
- Ensure complete resolution of customer issues and close the loop on open inquiries. Dive deep into customer complaints and work orders to identify and resolve underlying issues.
- Submit detailed reports on product issues to ensure they are addressed and resolved promptly.
- Address day-to-day customer questions, such as how to add a new service provider.
- Educate clients on self-serve account updates, offering help guides and resources to empower them.
- Maintain a customer-obsessed approach, ensuring clients feel supported throughout the support process.
- Maintain a current understanding of product updates and features, along with a full understanding of software functionality and automation.
- Clearly and effectively convey complex information to customers and internal teams.
- Develop user stories based on customer feedback to guide product development.

## Characteristics

- Holds accuracy and quality to a high standard while still meeting SLA targets — doesn't sacrifice one for the other.
- Highly self-sufficient: digs for the answer before asking for help, and treats escalating — then owning the verification through to the customer — as good judgment, not a shortfall.
- Keeps pace with a fast-moving, ever-changing product and policy landscape without losing rigor.
- Demonstrates exceptional written communication skills and attention to detail.
- Passionate about solving problems and connecting with customers.
- Enjoys working in a fast-paced environment with a combination of routine and ambiguous tasks.
- Exhibits strong investigative and troubleshooting skills to effectively resolve issues.
- Possesses high technical aptitude and business acumen — comfortable becoming an expert across the whole platform, not just one corner of it.

## Ideal Candidate

- Has 3+ years of experience in a customer service-oriented role via phone, text, web chat, or email.
- Is fluent in English and has excellent time management abilities.
- Communicates directly and efficiently, with a clear expression of action steps.
- Thinks quickly on their feet and shows empathy towards customers.
