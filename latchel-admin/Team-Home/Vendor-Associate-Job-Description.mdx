## Opportunity & Impact

Vendors are the face of Latchel and you are Latchel's face to our vendors. You help vendors get the most out of Latchel's system and help them whenever work orders are stuck or require additional assistance. At Latchel, we believe our vendor network has the opportunity to be the single largest revenue driver in our business. You are a key member of the team that is building and maintaining the greatest vendor network in the world.

## Responsibilities

- Receive inbound vendor phone calls.
- Receive and process inbound vendor SMS, email, and web chat messages.
- Solve vendor scheduling, rescheduling, scope change, budget approval, and work order completion issues.
- Assign the best vendor to work orders.
- Assist vendors as needed to build complete estimates, bids, and invoices.
- Review inbound estimates for quality and fair pricing.
- Educate vendors on the best ways to utilize Latchel.
- Escalate issues you cannot solve to Process Specialists.

## Qualifications

- Superior knowledge of home maintenance, construction, and home improvement.
- Demonstrated project management and coordination skills.
- Demonstrated ability to manage 50+ vendors.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

<Note>
Bilingual Spanish-English Vendor Specialists are paid an additional $1/hr
</Note>
