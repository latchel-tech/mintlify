## Opportunity & Impact

You are the first point of contact for our full-service property management clients. You help them understand how their maintenance is progressing, where they need to step in to help out, and where projects need help to get back on track. You act on behalf of our property managers to make sure their maintenance is being handled effectively and support Sr. Property Management Specialists to deep dive issues and problems. You also educate our property management users on how to get even more out of Latchel by taking advantage of more features. When our property management customers think about their relationship with Latchel, they're really thinking about their relationship with you.

## Responsibilities

- Receive inbound property manager phone calls.
- Receive and process inbound property manager SMS, email, and web chat messages.
- Convert property manager feedback and input into actionable information and tasks for the rest of the Latchel team.
- Take proactive action to progress delayed work orders as needed.
- Work in coordination with Sr. PM Specialist on any complex issues and handling Sr. PM Specialist overflow.
- Help draft daily communication updates for all property managers subscribed to Latchel's Premium service in your assigned region.
- Identify work orders where Latchel's operations policies were not followed and work with Customer Experience Managers to improve Latchel's performance.
- Educate property managers on the use of the Latchel platform and encourage them to use more features in Latchel.
- Escalate issues you cannot solve to Process Specialists.

## Qualifications

- Superior knowledge of home maintenance, construction, and home improvement.
- Superb account management and customer relationship management skills.
- Knowledge of property management best practices, especially in regards to property maintenance and improvement.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Internal Job Information

### How is work routed to a PM Associate?

The Property Management Work Queue contains sheet(s) for the following:

- PM Notes/Tasks
- All work orders that require PM input to move forward.
- All work orders that need owner approval.
- All work orders where preferred vendors are unresponsive and no alternatives are available.
- All jobs that are outside of Latchel's SLAs
- All Property Manager invoice disputes
- All property manager payment related issues.
