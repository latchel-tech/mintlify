## Opportunity & Impact

You are the expert on how to improve business operations. You are 100% focused on how to make the Latchel customer experience better for all of Latchel's stakeholders. You look across Latchel's existing processes and operations and identify trends and areas for improvement. You perform deep dives and root cause analysis whenever we do not meet our quality standards. You run process improvement projects to continuously raise our performance bar.

## Responsibilities

- Deep dive critical failures, identify all opportunities for improvement, identify root causes, and develop and implement action plans to eliminate root causes and improve the quality of Latchel's services.
- Perform deep dives, root cause analysis, and run improvement projects to improve the quality of Latchel's services.
- Review problem work orders, take corrective actions, and document issues that led to the initial problems.
- Perform audits of Process Specialist, Sr. Vendor Specialist, Sr. Property Management Specialist and other team members' work against Latchel process standards.
- Identify areas where Latchel lacks a process standard and work with Operations Product & Program Management to build a standard.
- Continuously improve and develop Latchel new hire and new service training program.
- Develop new processes and training as new features and services are created.

## Qualifications

- Deep knowledge of maintenance coordination best practices, especially in regards to property maintenance and improvement and tracking and visibility.
- Excellent root cause analysis and problem solving skills.
- Excellent project management and multi-tasking skills.
- Demonstrated experience in process improvement and process design.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Familiarity with writing queries and building reports in SQL/mySQL/PostgreSQL (Latchel uses PostgreSQL)
