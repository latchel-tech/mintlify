## Opportunity & Impact

You are the main point of contact for our full-service property management clients. You help them understand how their maintenance is progressing, where they need to step in to help out, and where projects need help to get back on track. You act on behalf of our property managers to make sure their maintenance is being handled effectively. You also educate our property management users on how to get even more out of Latchel by taking advantage of more features. When our property management customers think about their relationship with Latchel, they're really thinking about their relationship with you.

## Responsibilities

- Communicate regularly with all property managers subscribed to Latchel's Premium service in your assigned region.
- Receive inbound calls from a select group of clients.
- Handle property manager billing disputes independently and in coordination with Sr. Vendor Specialists.
- Work with property managers to resolve work orders that require their action.
- Escalate scenarios where work orders are delayed to property managers to help get them moving again.
- Educate property managers on the use of the Latchel platform and encourage them to use more features in Latchel.
- Identify work orders where Latchel's operations policies were not followed and work with Customer Experience Managers to improve Latchel's performance.
- Work with property managers to improve their vendor portfolio.
- Deep dive to understand and eliminate the root cause of customer escalations and problems.
- Escalate account configuration issues to Customer Success.
- Travel to meet with property managers locally.

## Qualifications

- Demonstrated track record of superb account management and customer relationship management skills.
- Deep knowledge of property management best practices, especially in regards to property maintenance and improvement.
- Root cause analysis and problem solving skills.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Internal Job Information

### How is work routed to a Sr. Property Management Specialist?

The PM Contacts List contains sheet(s) for the following:

- All work orders that require PM input to move forward.
- All work orders that need owner approval.
- All work orders where preferred vendors are unresponsive and no alternatives are available.
- All jobs that are outside of Latchel's SLAs
- All Property Manager invoice disputes
- All property manager payment related issues.
