## Opportunity & Impact

Service providers are the face of Latchel and you are Latchel's face to our service providers. You will ensure Latchel is offering the best pricing and service to our customers through our service provider network by vetting and qualifying new service providers and ongoing management of existing service providers. At Latchel, we believe our service provider network has the opportunity to be the single largest revenue driver in our business. You will be building our network by sourcing and retaining the highest quality service providers across the country.

## Responsibilities

- Onboard all new service providers and educate them on how to use the Latchel system.
- Source, qualify, and vet new Latchel service providers.
- Perform pricing negotiations with new Latchel service providers.
- Resolve all account, billing, and payment issues with Latchel service providers.
- Assess service provider estimates and invoices for quality, completeness, and pricing.
- Handle escalations for service provider related issues.
- Work with Customer Success to resolve Property Manager/Service Provider related escalations and issues.
- Identify themes and trends across your interactions with service providers and how to improve Latchel's product for service providers.

## Qualifications

- Deep knowledge of home maintenance, construction, and home improvement.
- Strong project management and coordination skills.
- Demonstrated ability to manage 100+ vendors.
- Excellent interpersonal relationship management skills.
- Excellent customer service skills and professionalism.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Internal Job Information

### How is work routed to a Service Provider Specialist?

Latchel has task queues for the following:

- Service Provider payouts & reconciliation (Affiliate + Latchel Service Providers)
- Reactive Service Provider Sourcing

We have queries and work queues for the following:

- LCN Service Provider Onboarding
- Preferred Service Provider Onboarding
- Convert Affiliate Service Providers to LCN
- Proactive Service Provider Sourcing
- "Problematic" Service Providers
- Service Provider Feedback (from service providers)
- Resident Feedback (on service providers)
- Frequent Re-assignments
- Service Provider Tech Support Requests
