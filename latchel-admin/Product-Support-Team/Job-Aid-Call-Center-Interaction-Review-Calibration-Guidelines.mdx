| **Question** | **Did the agent listen to the customer's full problem without interrupting?** |
| **Guidelines** | Yes<br/><br/>Short acknowledgement verbal aids (aja, yes, correct, I see, etc..) are not to be considered interruptions. The agent may intercede if the customer is struggling to find an idea or the correct word. - The agent may direct the customer with questions if the customer has tone down his voice.<br/> |
| **Tips and Remarks** | No<br/><br/><br/> - The agent prevents the customer to explaining a point.<br/>- The agent imprints the sensation of not being willing to help.<br/>- The agent is pushing up the customer to talk faster or stop at all.<br/> * A good practice is to verbally state our will to listen ei. "we're here to help", "please keep going", among others. |
