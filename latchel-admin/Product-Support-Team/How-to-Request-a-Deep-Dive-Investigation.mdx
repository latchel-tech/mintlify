**What is a Deep Dive for S&R?**

An S&R Deep Dive is a thorough investigation conducted by S&R when there is an incident reported by CS or other team members who were **_unable to determine how the flow or process failed_**. Once that reporter has exhausted all options (ex: reviewing calls/messages/emails, work orders, and PM settings) they would then submit a Deep Dive Request (investigation). The S&R escalation team would then research the issue reported to determine what happened, why it happened, and how it could be resolved by providing recommendations to avoid that failure from happening in the future.

**When to submit a deep dive request?**

Any of the below will be eligible for a Deep Dive only\*\* **\_**if the receiver of the complaint is not able to identify the cause of the problem or answer the customer’s question using the resources available\*\* _(WO history, PM settings, audio/video recordings, SMS/Webchat/emails sent, etc.),_\*\* or your investigation takes 5- 10 minutes max\*\*\_. The receiver of the complaint may be able to find the solution to the PM complaint with this preliminary investigation, in which case a Deep Dive request should not be submitted.

- A customer is complaining about how Latchel handled a WO/situation.
- A PM/Resident/vendor is complaining about lack of availability during specific situations/times.
- A PM is requesting a further explanation for Latchel activity, either Console or agent behavior.
- An agent was identified acting against Latchel statutes but there’s reasonable doubt as to the reason why the actions were taken (no customer has complained yet).
- A low score Troubleshooting review was submitted by a resident.

Notes of interest:

- The S&R team requires a **_clear problem description_** and **_the question(s) that need to be answered_**. Please do not submit a Deep Dive Request until you can clearly identify these.
- If the customer is filing a complaint that is unclear or still too subjective, please ask him/her complementary questions to ensure a concise submission and prompt completion.
- Please\_\*\* request specifics from customers \*\*\_including dates, times, contact information, etc. when the object of the complaint is an interaction with Latchel.
- If the preliminary investigation is not successful or turns out to be time demanding (more than 5-10 minutes), the requester should provide the results found so far in their submission. This will avoid the S&R team from duplicating efforts on the Deep Dive.

**Questions to answer before submitting a Deep Dive request depending on the complaint received.**

Note: Please don’t just answer “yes/no” – provide as much detail as possible in your submission based on your 10-minute preliminary research!

- **WO took too long to schedule**\_ “Everyone I spoke to about the situation was unhelpful. No one got back to me after the multiple attempts at contact as the situation got worse and worse.” _\*\* \*\*_(DD-1075)\_
- Was the automatic vendor queue fulfilled correctly?
- Did Latchel assign a vendor according to standard timeframes?
- \*\*WO took too long to be completed **_“Every troubleshooter I've spoken to has either lied to me or pretended to care about my emergency issues simply to appease me”_** \*\*_(DD-463)_
- Was the dispatch delayed by the vendor?
- Was there an unapproved estimate or hold-ups caused by the PM?
- **Invoice is being disputed “**_Latchel took action on the task and called the vendor to let them know a 4k estimate was approved.  Great Jones was still waiting on owner approval” \*\* \*\*(DD-1063)_
- Was there an estimate approved by one of the PM’s?
- \*\*WO was double dispatched **_“This work order had multiple vendor appointments assigned, the PM would like to know how this happened”_** \*\*_(DD-421)_
- Did the PM dispatch a vendor on their own? Did they notify us?
- Was there a duplicate WO dispatched?
- **PM info was disclosed**\_ “The resident reached me to my personal phone number”\_
- Were you able to find the call/message where the Latchel agent provided this information?
- \*\*Latchel Team was unreachable \*\*_“we called and hung up. Also added that the resident hired a plumber because they called Latchel back several times and no one picked up the call” (DD-992)_
- What date did this happen?
- What channel was used to reach us?
- If via phone call, what's the dialer number?
- \*\*Service given beyond subscription \*\*_“The person you have listed as the tenant does not live at this property and rerouted Paul to a property we do not manage” (DD-1157)_
- Did the PM request such services?
- Was there a special approval given on Slack?
- Was the subscription changed after the event?
- \*\*Work Order landed in the wrong state \*\*_“Why was this WO moved to PM responsible?” (DD-1177)_
- Does the problem/location combination have dispatch settings on the Severity/Responsibility tab.
- Were the Severity/Responsibility definitions changed after the event?

\*\*How a deep dive is submitted: \*\*

- The reporter will first click the lightning bolt in the #search-and-rescue-escalations channel to access the workflow. Fill out the form by describing the issue with a detailed summary of the issue and your preliminary findings, answering the suggested questions above. Provide the link and name of the Property Manager for reference. Once filled out, click ‘Submit’.

**S&R Team receives Deep Dive Request**

- The S&R team or Deep Dive Specialist will acknowledge the request in the Slack channel by clicking a checkmark response.
- The S&R escalation team will first review the deep dive request to determine if the investigation is necessary. If an investigation is not necessary, they will inform the requester as to why in the Slack thread. Once S&R has confirmed an investigation is necessary, they will then create a Jira ticket from the post. The team can also communicate their findings through Jira by placing them in the Communication bucket.
- Here are the steps to create a ticket from the deep dive request post in slack:
  - When you hover over the post, click on the 3 dots to bring up this dropdown. Then click on “Create issue from Jira Cloud”
  - The investigator will then fill out the form by first changing Issue Type to “Work Order Deep Dive” by clicking the Latchel plugin to bring up the drop-down menu.
  - Then fill out the summary portion of the form to describe the deep dive request. Then submit. By submitting, the ticket will automatically post on your Jira Board.
  - For quick access to the Jira ticket, click on the thread of the slack channel and click the ticket link.
