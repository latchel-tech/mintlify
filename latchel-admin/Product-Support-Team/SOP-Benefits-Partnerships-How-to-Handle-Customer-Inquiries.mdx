### Goals

1. Understand which teams should answer which types of benefits and partnerships inquiries
2. Provide clear, accurate and customer-obsessed responses to our customers' benefits and partnerships questions

### How do customer inquiries become Assembly Tickets?

1. An email is received at [sucess@latchel.com](mailto:sucess@latchel.com)
   1. This includes tickets escalated from Service Ops [How to: Handle Home Assistant and Benefits Inquiries](https://help.latchel.com/How-to:-Handle-Home-Assistant-and-Benefits-Inquiries)
   2. Case Owners are assigned by Product Support following the table below.
2. A CSM receives a question during a phone call or meeting
   1. After the meeting, the CSM creates an Assembly tickets and assigns a Case Owner based on the table below. The Case should include a summary of the customer’s question and a link to the call or meeting.

<Note>
  When closing out a benefits/partnerships Case in Salesforce, be sure to select **Case Type = Resident Benefits** and the most pertinent **Case Motive** (descriptions [here](https://help.latchel.com/Job-Aid-Interaction-Tags-Explained)). This is super important for tracking and improving this program!
</Note>

### Who handles what?

| Field | Details |

| **Sales** | Requests to add marketplace benefits (Piñata, Air Filters, Cover Pest, Rent Check, insurance, etc.) |
|  | Partner complaints |
| **CSMs** | Benefits usage inquiries. **_Why?_** These usually indicate potential to churn. |
|  | Benefits rollout strategy |
|  | A customer seems frustrated by a response provided by Product Support and/or asks for a phone call. |
| **Program Management** | Any topics for which we don’t yet have documentation |
| **Product Support** | Everything else! See examples and documentation below. |
