<Warning>

Only the case types listed under [Escalate to Nomad Support](#path-2-escalate-to-nomad-support) still require the Nomad Support Escalation Form.

### Which path do I take?

Start every Nomad Owner case by identifying the topic, then follow the matching path.

| Case topic | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Status updates, repair delays, unreturned calls, or other questions about an active work order | Direct to owner, CC Nomad |
| Repair timelines and questions about Latchel's service or process | Direct to owner, CC Nomad |
| Service-quality follow-up after a work order closes (what happened? why?) | Direct to owner, CC Nomad |
| Owner contact-detail corrections | Direct to owner, CC Nomad |
| Invoice dispute where the refund/credit was **approved** | Direct to owner, CC Nomad |
| Contract-related matters — security deposits, move-outs, subscriptions, cancellations | Escalate to Nomad Support |
| Nomad owner portal bugs, access issues, or other platform problems | Escalate to Nomad Support |
| Invoice dispute where the refund was **denied**, the invoice stands, and payment is still owed | Escalate to Nomad Support first (see [Invoice disputes](#invoice-disputes)) |

<Tip>
  When a case contains both types of topics — for example a repair delay question _and_ a subscription cancellation request — handle the service portion directly with the owner and escalate the contract portion to Nomad Support in parallel. Note both actions in the case thread.
</Tip>

### Path 1: Communicate directly with the owner (CC Nomad)

No Escalation Form is needed for these cases.

1. Confirm the topic falls into a direct-communication row in the table above.
2. Reply to the Nomad Owner directly from the case in Assembly, and **CC [support@nomadlease.com](mailto:support@nomadlease.com)** on every message so Nomad has full visibility of the thread.
3. Research the case fully before responding — review the work order, the settings, and the ops/chat history. Do not treat the escalation summary as the source of truth.
4. Own the case through to resolution the same way you would for a Property Manager, keeping the owner updated as the work order progresses.
5. Close the case per [Closing Cases in PS](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/EIBuvw). If the owner goes unresponsive, follow [Handling Unresponsive PMs](https://latchel.atlassian.net/wiki/x/DACqvg).

<Warning>
  Always CC Nomad Support. Nomad stays looped in on every owner-facing message even though they are no longer the one responding — Latchel and Nomad must present a unified front to the owner.
</Warning>

### Path 2: Escalate to Nomad Support

Use this path **only** for contract matters, Nomad portal/platform problems, and invoice disputes where payment is still owed.

1. Escalate via email to [support@nomadlease.com](mailto:support@nomadlease.com) using the **NOMAD SUPPORT ESCALATION FORM** in Assembly.
2. Once this is sent to Nomad Support, the case will automatically go into the **Escalated** status. Close the original email and change the escalated email status to **Open**.
   1. If Nomad Support responds and asks for additional support, continue the conversation by supporting the customer.
   2. If they are unresponsive, continue to Step 3.
3. The escalated case will automatically reopen 24 hours after being in **Open** status. If there is no response from Nomad, the associate can move the case to **Closed** without needing to follow up.

**USE THIS ESCALATION TEMPLATE FOR NOMAD SUPPORT:**

> **Owner Portfolio URL:**
>
> - Copy & paste the Nomad Owner profile link from Latchel.
>
> **Name of the person that reached out (include contact info):**
>
> - Include the owner's name, email and phone number.
>
> **Escalation Summary:**
>
> - Give full context and all details. It is very important **not** to just copy & paste the Ops Escalation (as these are internal facing) → Paint the picture for Nomad Support to understand the escalation and any relevant details.
>
> **Is this related to a specific work order? (If so, please paste the link to the work order):**
>
> - Copy & paste the WO link from Latchel if the escalation is in regards to a specific work order.
>
> **Action Needed from NOMAD:**
>
> - Share next steps for Nomad Support to take regarding the escalation. We can suggest Nomad sets up a call with the Owner to review settings/process/questions. We can also suggest settings updates and anything else relevant to the escalation.
> - If the escalation is simply for awareness, PS can put in this field "No action required - Simply for your records."

### Invoice disputes

Investigate every Nomad Owner invoice dispute per [SOP: Handling Work Order Invoice Disputes](/latchel-admin/Product-Support-Team/SOP-Handling-Work-Order-Invoice-Disputes) first. Where the resolution goes depends on the outcome.

#### Refund or credit approved

- Communicate the resolution **directly to the owner** and CC [support@nomadlease.com](mailto:support@nomadlease.com).
- No Escalation Form is required.

#### Refund denied — invoice stands and payment is still owed

Nomad hears from us before the owner does.

1. Send Nomad Support the **complete supporting package** using the Escalation Form. Do not send a partial package. Include:
   - The invoice in question
   - Job photos
   - The provider breakdown
   - Budget approval history
2. State plainly that the invoice is valid and that **Latchel will not be providing compensation**, so Nomad can decide whether they want to offer the owner a resolution of their own.
3. Wait for Nomad to align with us before any owner-facing communication about the outcome.

<Warning>
  The goal is for Latchel and Nomad to be aligned **before** information reaches the owner. Do not communicate a denied dispute to a Nomad Owner until Nomad Support has had the chance to weigh in.
</Warning>
