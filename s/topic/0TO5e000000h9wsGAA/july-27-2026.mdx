### **New Features**

**Intake Conversation Settings**

We've introduced **Conversation Settings** — a new set of controls that let you shape exactly how Latchel's intake sounds and behaves for your residents. You'll find them under **Account Settings → Intake Settings → Conversation Settings**.

<Frame>
  ![Conversation Settings](/images/Conversation-Settings.png "Conversation Settings")
</Frame>

**Make the intake voice your own**

Every account starts with our default voice, but you can now preview and choose from nine voices to match your brand. Fine-tune it further with:

- Emotion — calm, happy, sympathetic, surprised, or the default
- Voice speed — dial it up or down to fit your style

Your selected voice carries across the whole resident experience — maintenance intake, non-resident intake, and (soon) outbound vendor calls.

<Note>
  Voice customization is currently available for English only. We’ll let you know when Spanish customization is available. 
</Note>

**Set your troubleshooting depth**

You can now decide how much diagnostic guidance Latchel offers residents during intake — and it applies to both voice and web intake:

- **None** — skip troubleshooting and get work orders entered immediately (ideal for luxury or high-touch buildings)
- **Mitigate Damage –** Provide only critical guidance to prevent immediate property damage or safety risks (e.g. water-leak mitigation)
- **Solves Issues** — our recommended default, walking residents through common fixes before a job is created
- **HIGH** — extended troubleshooting paths for the most thorough diagnostics

<Tip>
  Want help dialing in the right setup for your portfolio? Reach out to [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) — we're happy to walk you through it!
</Tip>

### **Resolved Bugs**

**AppFolio Work Order Sync**

<Warning>
  **What was happening:**

  Work orders weren't reliably syncing from Latchel to AppFolio due to Appfolio reporting changes. When a new maintenance request came in, manually triggering the sync didn't push the work order over.
</Warning>

<Check>
  **What's fixed:**

  Manual work order sync to AppFolio is working again — trigger it and new work orders flow into AppFolio immediately, so you can assign and manage requests without waiting on the next scheduled sync.

  If a sync ever looks stuck, reach out to [opsintegrations@latchel.com](mailto:opsintegrations@latchel.com) and we'll jump on it.
</Check>
