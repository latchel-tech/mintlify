### **New Features**

1. **AI Voice for Vendors — Outbound Calls That Chase Status For You**

Following up with vendors is one of the most repetitive parts of a maintenance operation. Now it's one you can hand off.

<Check>
  **What's New!**

  We've extended **AI Voice** to outbound vendor calls. You can trigger a call on demand, or set Latchel to call automatically every day:

  - Manually trigger an outbound AI voice call to a vendor to chase status on one or more open work orders
  - Automate daily calls to a vendor to chase status across their open jobs
  - The AI agent takes the same actions a vendor can self-service in their portal — and generates a task for your team when a request falls outside of what it can handle
  - Vendors can set their own contact preferences, so outreach lands the way they prefer to be reached
</Check>

<Callout icon="hands-heart" iconType="duotone" color="#b19cf8">

  Status chasing stops living on your team's to-do list. Instead of your coordinators working a call list, Latchel works it — and you only get pulled in when something actually needs a human decision.
</Callout>

2. **Clock In / Clock Out and Timesheets for In-House Techs**

Do you run in-house maintenance techs? They now have a one-tap — or hands-free voice — way to mark when they arrive at and finish a job.

- Techs can clock out mid-job to note they're leaving before work is complete, then clock back in when they return
- If a tech forgets to clock out, we default to one hour of logged time — and if they clock out after more than two hours, we add a confirmation step so the record stays accurate
- On work orders where the assigned service provider is an in-house tech, Invoices now appear as **Timesheets**

**From your tech's side:** one tap to arrive, one tap to finish — right from their Latchel dashboard. They can also correct a mistimed entry, attach photos of the work, and log the materials they used.

(4) **Automated Texts Now Come From Your Assigned Number**

We've cleaned up where our automated text messages come from. Every automated text Latchel sends now goes out from your **Assigned Number** — the same maintenance or general intake line your residents already call.

<Callout icon="hands-heart" iconType="duotone" color="#b19cf8">
  **Why This Matters:**

  When an automated text comes from the number residents recognize, their replies land where they can actually be handled — by Latchel's AI and our team — instead of arriving as stray messages your team has to sort out. Fewer one-off texts hitting your inbox, more conversations resolved for you, and work orders that get updated faster.

  Your communication number is now used only for texts a PM sends directly. It's no longer used for any automated message.
</Callout>

<Callout icon="arrow-alt-to-bottom" iconType="duotone" color="#7ed5d5">

  This is groundwork for a much bigger change in how messaging works at Latchel — one that will let the platform handle more of the conversation end to end. Stay tuned: that's our next big feature announcement!
</Callout>

**Let Latchel Manage Your AppFolio Sync!**

Keeping your information synchronized between AppFolio and Latchel no longer needs to be something your team manages.

Latchel can now run the synchronization on your behalf — no more monitoring the plugin, running syncs manually, or restarting the process after being logged out. With Latchel managing the sync, your team can:

- Spend less time monitoring the AppFolio plugin
- Avoid manually initiating syncs
- Reduce interruptions caused by expired sessions or unexpected logouts
- Keep information aligned between AppFolio and Latchel

Our team oversees the sync process, giving you greater confidence that your information stays up to date — while your team stays focused on managing operations and serving residents.
