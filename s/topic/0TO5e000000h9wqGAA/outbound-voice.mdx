### **What is Outbound Voice?**

Latchel has always answered the phone for you. Now it can pick it up on your behalf, too.

**Outbound Voice** is Latchel's newest AI Voice capability, and it flips the maintenance coordination script: instead of your team calling vendors to ask "did you make it out there?" or "when's the invoice coming?", Latchel's AI voice agent makes that call for you — with full context on the work order, the vendor's history, and what still needs to happen.

### **How It Works**

The AI agent takes the same actions a vendor could take themselves in their Latchel portal — confirming or rescheduling an appointment, collecting an invoice, checking on a job's status — and it has full context going in:

- Every active work order assigned to that vendor
- Any prior calls or texts about those jobs, so it doesn't ask the vendor to repeat themselves
- Whether a call is even worth making that day — if there's nothing new to discuss, no call goes out

If the vendor asks for something the AI can't resolve, it doesn't guess — it creates a task for your team with the details, so a human picks it up from there.

<Check>
  **Vendors stay in control too.** A vendor can tell the AI they'd prefer to be called at a specific time of day, and Latchel will call them at that time going forward.
</Check>

### Work Order States and Ages That Trigger Calls

#### States

1. Accept **open** (unaccepted) requests
2. **Scheduling Service Provider** - schedule accepted work orders
3. **Confirming Completion** - Confirm completion of scheduled work
4. **Awaiting Invoice** - Upload/submit invoice for completed work

#### Age Thresholds

- **Under 12 hours since last state change** → no call
- **12 hours to 3 days since last state change** → triggers the daily automated outbound call; while the vendor is on the line, the AI will also ask about any other open WOs whose last state change was within the past month
- **Needs Approval status** does NOT trigger a call (it doesn't require vendor action)

<Warning>
  **Bid Requests & Estimates**

  The AI does NOT follow up on outstanding bid requests or estimates via outbound call, but it can receive estimates over voice.
</Warning>

### Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Click-to-Call and Automated Daily Calls?">
    **Click-to-Call** only happens when someone on your team clicks the call button — Latchel will never place that call on its own. Automated Daily Calls run independently: once turned on, Latchel calls each vendor once a day (at their preferred time, if they've set one) to check on every open job, with no click required from your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I turn on Automated Daily Calls, does every vendor get called every day?">
    Every vendor with relevant open work orders gets one call a day, all around the same time. If there's nothing new to check on for a vendor that day, no call goes out — Latchel only calls when it's worth the vendor's time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I exclude specific vendors from automated calling?">
    Yes. If a vendor already manages their jobs well through the portal, you can turn off automated calling for just that vendor in their individual settings while keeping it on for everyone else.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can vendors choose what time they get called?">
    Yes. Vendors can tell the AI their preferred time of day (or days of the week), and Latchel will call them as close to that time as possible going forward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the AI know if a vendor already has other jobs scheduled that day?">
    Yes. When the AI calls about a job, it has context on everything else that vendor has scheduled and will walk through the full list of relevant work orders in that same call.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the AI can't resolve something on the call?">
    It creates a task inside Latchel for your team with the details, so a person can pick it up — nothing falls through the cracks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does this work in Spanish?">
    If a vendor responds in Spanish, the call automatically switches to Spanish. A setting to default the call to start in Spanish for specific vendors is on our near-term roadmap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see what was said on the call?">
    Yes. Every outbound call — click-to-call or automated — comes with a full transcript and summary, and you can rate it with a thumbs up or down. That feedback helps train your company's AI model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit to how many times a vendor can reschedule before Latchel escalates?">
    Not currently — there's no hard limit on reschedules. If you're on the Analytics\+ package, you can request custom reports to track reschedule counts per vendor and flag repeat offenders.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a vendor is already headed to a property and a new compatible job comes in?">
    That's a related feature already live today: if a service provider is scheduled to visit a property and a new issue comes in that they can also handle, Latchel can automatically attach it to that visit (when auto-approval is enabled in your settings) — cutting down on extra truck rolls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the vendor doesn't answer, or it goes to voicemail?">
    Latchel logs a short "no answer" note on the relevant work order(s) instead of generating a full call summary — so your activity feed stays accurate about what actually happened, and no time is wasted trying to summarize silence. This applies to both Click-to-Call and Automated Daily Calls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see why a specific work order wasn't brought up on a given day's automated call?">
    Yes. When Latchel builds a day's call agenda, it sometimes leaves a work order off — for example, if it was already covered on a call a couple of days ago or there's nothing new to raise. When that happens, a note is added directly to that work order explaining why it was skipped, so you can see the reasoning instead of wondering if it was forgotten.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does AI Voice only make outbound calls, or can it also answer when a vendor calls in?">
    Both. If a vendor calls your Latchel line and their number is recognized, AI Voice can also handle that inbound call — so the experience is consistent whether Latchel calls the vendor or the vendor calls in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When the AI collects an invoice on a call, how is it delivered to us? We route invoices into our accounting system and need to know where they land.">
    We don't collect invoices over voice, only estimates. Building an invoice is too involved for a voice call (before/after pictures may be required, separating labor and materials and breaking out line items). The routing for invoices remains unchanged (and if we ever do solve creating a full and complete invoice over voice, we'd keep the same invoice routing. wehave today)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the autopilot only call on dispatched and approved work orders? We need confirmation it will not schedule work sitting in Needs Approval or estimate-only status.">
    Yes, it doesn't take work that isn't approved and send it out. Needs Approval is NOT a status that requires vendor action so it wouldn't trigger a vendor call or be part of its call script. If a vendor asked about it, they'd be told it's in Needs Approval. In addition, the same budget limits and rules apply for voice calls as any other work order.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We are in California, a two-party consent state. Please confirm how recording consent is handled on these calls beyond the opening disclosure.">
    In California, consent is legally achieved if a participant stays on the line after hearing an automated or verbal disclosure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
