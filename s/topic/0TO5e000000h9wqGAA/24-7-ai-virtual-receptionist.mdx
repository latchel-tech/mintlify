### **What Is the 24/7 AI Virtual Receptionist?**

The 24/7 AI Virtual Receptionist is an opt-in upgrade to your existing Latchel-assigned phone number. It is **not a new or separate phone number** — it simply expands what happens when someone who isn't a current resident calls your Latchel line.

### **How to Opt In**

Email [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) to request activation for your account.

### **How Calls Are Routed**

If a caller isn't already registered in Latchel, they'll hear a short prompt: "If you are a current resident, press 1. For all other callers, press 2."

- Residents who press 1 continue through Latchel's normal maintenance intake process.
- Everyone else — prospective tenants, owners, vendors, and other non-resident callers — is routed to the AI Receptionist flow.

### **What Gets Captured**

For every call handled by the AI Receptionist, Latchel stores:

- A summary of the call
- A recording of the call
- A full transcript of the call

### **Call Category Routing**

You can configure separate routing rules for different call categories — for example, routing leasing inquiries to one destination and rent payment questions to another. The AI classifies each caller's topic and applies the matching rule automatically.

This means a single Latchel-assigned number can intelligently handle many different caller types without manual intervention, sending each to the right place based on why they called.

Category routing is configured in your non-resident intake settings. Contact [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) to set up or update your categories and routing rules.

### **Routing Options**

For each call category, you can choose how it is handled. Available destinations include:

- **Post-call delivery** — After the call ends, the summary, recording, and transcript are sent to your team via email, SMS, Latchel task, Slack notification, or webhook.
- **Live-line forwarding** — The AI live-transfers the caller to a designated phone number during the call (see below).

### **Live-Line Call Forwarding**

Live-line forwarding is now available as a routing destination. When a call's category matches a rule configured for live transfer, the AI forwards the caller to your designated phone number mid-call — no need to end the call first.

<Callout icon="phone-arrow-up-right" iconType="duotone" color="#b19cf8">
  **When it triggers:** Forwarding happens automatically when the AI classifies the call into a category you've set up with a live-transfer destination.

  **Configurability:** Live-line forwarding is configured per account in your non-resident intake settings. Contact [success@latchel.com](mailto:success@latchel.com) to set up or update your forwarding number and category rules.

  **Transfer notifications:** The system sends a notification indicating whether the live transfer succeeded or failed, so your team always knows what happened.
</Callout>

### **Giving Feedback**

Just like maintenance intake, it's easy to let us know how the AI Receptionist did — you can give feedback right from the call itself.

### **Supported Languages**

The AI Virtual Receptionist supports both **English** and **Spanish**.

### How to Set Up Call Category Routing & Live-Line Forwarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your non-resident intake settings." icon="1" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    Navigate to `[Admin → PM Company → {Company Name} → Non-Resident Intake Settings]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your default call categories." icon="2" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    Every account comes pre-seeded with 4 default call categories (e.g., Leasing, Rent Payment, and others). There is also a catch-all category for callers whose purpose doesn't match any configured category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3: Add or edit a call category (optional)." icon="3" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    To create a custom category, click **Add Category** `[or equivalent button label]` and provide:

    - **Title** — a short name for the category (e.g., "Maintenance Inquiry")
    - **Description** — a brief explanation of what callers in this category are calling about. The AI uses this description to match against what the caller says.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4: Set a destination for each category." icon="4" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    For each category, select how calls in that category should be handled. Options include:

    - Post-call delivery via email, SMS, Latchel task, Slack notification, or webhook
    - **Forward the live call** — transfers the caller to a phone number during the call
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 5: Configure live-line forwarding." icon="5" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    If you select **Forward the live call** as the destination, enter the phone number you want calls transferred to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 6: Configure notifications." icon="6" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    For each category, you can also set up one or more notifications (e.g., a Latchel task or email) to alert your team after the call. If a live-transfer attempt fails, the notification will indicate that it was unsuccessful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 7: Save your settings." icon="7" iconType="duotone" titleSize="h4">
    Click **Save**  to apply your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
