---
title: "Redirect Tenant Maintenance Submissions From Buildium to Latchel"
---

Complete the following steps to redirect Buildium maintenance requests to the Latchel maintenance URL.

<Note>
  This is only a viable option if Latchel handles maintenance for ALL units in Buildium, as this is a global change.
</Note>

### **Setup Maintenance Submission to Latchel**
