---
title: "Redirect Tenant Maintenance Submissions From Buildium to Latchel"
---

Complete the following steps to redirect Buildium maintenance requests to the Latchel maintenance URL.

<Note>
  This is only a viable option if Latchel handles maintenance for ALL units in Buildium, as this is a global change.
</Note>

### **Setup Maintenance Submission to Latchel**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Resident Center Settings." icon="1" iconType="sharp-solid" titleSize="h4">
    From your dashboard, go to `Communication > Resident Center Settings.`

    <Frame>
      ![Buildium 1](/images/Buildium-1.png "Buildium 1")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Setup Maintenance request redirect." icon="2" iconType="sharp-solid" titleSize="h4">
    Select **Requests**, then under Maintenance request redirect, click **Edit**.

    <Frame>
      ![Buildium 2](/images/Buildium-2.png "Buildium 2")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Latchel Residents Work Order Submission URL." icon="3" iconType="sharp-solid" titleSize="h4">
    Go to your **portal settings**, then copy your **Residents Work Order Submission UR**L. Paste the URL into the Redirect URL field.

    <Frame>
      ![Buildium 3](/images/Buildium-3.png "Buildium 3")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finally, click Save." icon="4" iconType="sharp-solid" titleSize="h4">
    **Not sure where you can find your Resident Work Order Submission URL?** Check how you can find it through the help guide below!

    <Card title="Configuring Portal Settings | Residents Work Order Submission URL" color="#ff9b8a" icon="person-to-door" iconType="duotone" href="/s/topic/0TO5e000000h9wqGAA/Configuring-Portal-Settings#residents-work-order-submission-url">
      Manage settings that customize your team's experience in the Latchel portal.
    </Card>
  </Step>
</Steps>
