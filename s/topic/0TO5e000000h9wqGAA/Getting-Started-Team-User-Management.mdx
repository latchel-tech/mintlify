Watch the video below to learn more how you can manage your team more effectively with Latchel!

<Note>
  **Teams** (formerly called "User Groups") can now also be set at the individual Work Order level, overriding the Team inherited from the property, portfolio, or company. Team is now managed from the "Property Manager" card on the PM Work Order Details view — the old "Notification Settings" card has been retired. You can also filter the Feed and Work Order Kanban views by Team and Team Member.
</Note>
